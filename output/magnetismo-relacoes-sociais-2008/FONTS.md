# Fonts: Magnetism in Social Relations

| Use | Font | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| All text | Liberation Serif 2.1.5 (Regular, Bold, Italic, Bold Italic) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | `fonts/liberation/` (license in `fonts/liberation/LICENSE`) |

Liberation Serif is metric-compatible with Times New Roman, the font of the
Portuguese original, so line lengths and spacing match the original design.
All fonts are embedded (checked by `tools/check_pdf.py`). The OFL allows
embedding in documents and commercial distribution of the PDF.
