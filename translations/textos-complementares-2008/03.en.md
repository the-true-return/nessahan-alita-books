2. Accepting and getting around the barriers

<!-- seg 03-0002 paragraph h=c016d66c -->
Barriers to approach combined with inviting behaviors constitute the ambiguity or incoherence in female behavior that torments us so much. It is more advisable to look for alternative paths (openings in the armor) than to try to push against the resistances imposed. Let us ally ourselves with the resistances, agreeing with them and reinforcing them, while at the same time we slip in through openings not visible at first sight. In this way we will obtain good results.

<!-- seg 03-0003 paragraph h=3e9d82ba -->
The attitude of accepting the unpleasant side of women, while also handing back to them all the undesirable consequences of this side, can pull them out of the behavioral neoteny that drives them to throw onto our backs blame and responsibilities that do not belong to us. In other words: through a correct psychological state, we corner them so that they either grow up or suffer the bad consequences of their own sly tricks, leaving them no way out other than to act like an adult. Playing with other people’s feelings is a childish irresponsibility, typical of idle people.

<!-- seg 03-0004 paragraph h=638985d3 -->
Let us not force definitions: let us take advantage of the openings offered by the favorable signals contained in ambiguous behavior. Let us choose the correct forms of insinuation. Let us avoid qualitatively wrong insinuations. Qualitatively wrong insinuations provoke explosions.
