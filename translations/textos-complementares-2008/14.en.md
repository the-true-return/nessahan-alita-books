13. Affective aggression

<!-- seg 14-0002 paragraph h=8804ac29 -->
Besides the power to attack physically, people of both sexes can attack each other psychologically. Among the forms of psychological aggression, we have aggression by means of romantic passion. Its power cannot be neglected, and it is capable of raising stress to extremely high and even lethal levels. It becomes, then, important to know the means of defending ourselves emotionally and of dismantling such aggressions.

<!-- seg 14-0003 paragraph h=92b099d0 -->
I have been writing about the affective aggressions perpetrated by women against men and about the possible means that would allow such aggressions to be dismantled. The reason is that I am a man, and experience is my first source of inspiration.

<!-- seg 14-0004 paragraph h=7a7e02ba -->
My hypothesis is that women have a greater ability to instrumentalize love as a weapon, while male aggressions tend to be of another kind, though no less worrying for that. Let them prove me wrong and I will modify my hypothesis, as well as the ideas that follow from it.

<!-- seg 14-0005 paragraph h=0f5e75b5 -->
Much of what I have written can be applied in reverse, to women’s advantage, but not all of it. A woman can read my texts and learn a great deal about the art of fighting[^14] in the war of passion, but she must take care not to attribute to the supposed “stronger sex” only the female tactics of attack, because men often use different tactics. In other words: the tactics of cads, about which I have written almost nothing, are not always the same as those used by the sly ones.

<!-- seg 14-0006 footnote n=14 h=8cf2cd56 -->
The fight is completely inward. He who conquers himself wins the war of passion. It is a peaceful fight, in which we boycott insincerity through abstention, adapt ourselves to difficulties, and dismantle and unmask cheating and wiles by means of strategic skills. None of this would be possible without the proper inner condition.

<!-- seg 14-0007 paragraph h=31c28c35 -->
All my reflections must be situated <b>only</b> within the affective, romantic, and passionate sphere of relationships between men and women. I have no political position whatsoever, and I do not believe in politics as a means of liberating man. I can rightly be considered politically “alienated,” since I am a stranger to such questions. I am completely disillusioned with politics, and the proposal I make to the reader is to concern himself only with his inner liberation. I believe that the only true freedom is the freedom of the soul, and this is not achieved through social struggles, political reforms, or economic measures. The “isms” have never liberated man from suffering and certainly will not. I consider that we must free ourselves inwardly from all these ties. However, if someone wants to campaign for some cause, well... that is not my problem.

<!-- seg 14-0008 paragraph h=435acf66 -->
As far as possible, we must free ourselves inwardly, not only from the affective oppression caused by the opposite sex, but also from the emotional oppression that all the things of life exert on our heart. Negative emotions cause great damage to our physical and mental health, harming the quality of our life.

<!-- seg 14-0009 paragraph h=0866b3ff -->
Because it lies at the essence of the affective aggression I discuss, I understand romantic passion to be an evil in an idyllic and wonderful disguise. My view is that it is an obsessive-compulsive disorder incessantly worshipped in our society, to the detriment of all. There is an immense distance between true love and romantic love. The first is a stance resulting from the will, and the second an emotional and exalted possession resulting from desire. Understand me well: it is not that we must be cold; what we must do is give shelter to sublime emotions within us.
