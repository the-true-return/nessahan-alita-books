9. Meanings of the sexual act

<!-- seg 10-0002 paragraph h=49750875 -->
While the female sexual organ is associated with life and birth, the male sexual organ is associated with invasion, pain, penetration, and aggression.

<!-- seg 10-0003 paragraph h=a3c1aea3 -->
The male sexual act, unlike the female one, is not an act of love or of affection but rather an act of aggression and fury, in a certain sense[^10]. It is for this reason, among various others, that the woman is reluctant, selects her partners so carefully, and demands that men hide their true intention, which is to penetrate her, until the moment she decides whether or not she will accept being “attacked” in this way. If she concludes that she will not be able to control the aggressiveness of the act, she will reject the suitor, considering him too impulsive, and will relegate him to the level of the unsatisfied desirers who must wait for her for the rest of their lives. She will not cut the bond definitively, by clarifying things, but will keep him captive without allowing him to satisfy his desire to possess her.

<!-- seg 10-0004 footnote n=10 h=bcd98293 -->
By “fury” understand force applied intensely. What I am asserting is that, because it is an act of penetration, it is not possible to separate it from its invasive and, by extension, aggressive aspect. This “aggression” will be consented to only if the woman feels safe and trusts that the man will not go beyond the permitted limits.

<!-- seg 10-0005 paragraph h=b948c6bf -->
The most curious thing is that, despite being, in a certain sense, an aggression, wild sex can, in some cases, make the woman feel “more female,” bind her, and make her dependent[^11]. The contradiction is explained by the fact that aggressive fury, though feared, makes an impact and transmits virility. At the same time, it can turn into a misfortune if the animal male escapes control. It is a complex problem and difficult to solve.

<!-- seg 10-0006 footnote n=11 h=e6509d30 -->
This happens when the aggressiveness is controlled and conducted correctly by the man in a way that does not harm the woman.

<!-- seg 10-0007 paragraph h=d13933d7 -->
From this aggressive character derive the preventive social measures against the <i>phallus erectus</i>, including punishments for certain exhibitionist or harassing conduct, as well as the rules that restrict and condition the expression of male sexuality. Exhibitionists cause disgust.
