7. Family decline

<!-- seg 08-0002 heading h=01afaa03 -->
Selection criteria

<!-- seg 08-0003 paragraph h=df3ce6f9 -->
Nowadays, the criteria for choosing husbands tend to be usefulness, convenience, pragmatism, salary, docility, fondness for children, companionship. The “good guys”—sincere, hardworking, and honest—fit this criterion.

<!-- seg 08-0004 paragraph h=e2358e74 -->
The criteria for choosing lovers are sexual attraction and emotional impact. Those of bad character fit this criterion, including those with the traits of Jonason and Schmitt’s “Dark Triad.”

<!-- seg 08-0005 paragraph h=d8ed62a4 -->
Therefore, husbands are generally not chosen for the degree of excitement and passion they arouse in their wives, unlike lovers. That is why they are left with the worst and most uninteresting part: obligations, work, expenses, worries, and commitments, among them the commitment to fidelity, besides sexual dissatisfaction. It is also for the same reason that their wives feel no great sexual attraction or passion for them, since they did not choose them for sex but only to help them through difficulties, while the lovers, on the other hand, make their knees go weak. That is what marriage has been turned into!

<!-- seg 08-0006 paragraph h=e9cade64 -->
Exceptions do not invalidate this widespread tendency. Marriage, in the form it takes today, has lost its meaning and brings no advantage whatsoever to the man. That is why I am a defender of the perfect matrimony and condemn the ridiculous caricatures of matrimony that modern unions have turned into. In today’s unions, adultery is the rule and not an exception.

The family of the future

<!-- seg 08-0008 paragraph h=e6a968a3 -->
According to the values currently imposed by the mass media and by the governments of Western countries, the ideal husband must be a resigned and happy cuckold (the much-touted “understanding husband”), the ideal wife must be an adulteress (they call this “women’s sexual freedom”), and the children must be induced from childhood to invert their natural gender identities. Those who rebel against these values are labeled prejudiced and oppressors, if not criminals. From there to the creation of repressive laws that force the adoption of these behaviors there is only one step.

The shamelessness of some

<!-- seg 08-0010 paragraph h=53139dcd -->
There are women so shameless that they voluntarily marry one man and remain in love with another, even having the gall to demand fidelity and monogamy from the unfortunate husband, who will live through the hell of not feeling loved for an entire lifetime.
