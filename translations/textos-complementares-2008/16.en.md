15. Some types of women who do not deserve trust

<!-- seg 16-0002 paragraph h=5fa1cc39 -->
It is worth cataloging some types of sly ones who like to cheat in love. We have to guard against them, always staying ready to dismantle their wiles. Let us look at some types:

<!-- seg 16-0003 paragraph h=8875f707 -->
1. The woman who gives you her phone number but does not answer when you call, or has someone say she is not in;

<!-- seg 16-0004 paragraph h=1bfbf94f -->
2. The woman who asks for your phone number but does not call you;

<!-- seg 16-0005 paragraph h=37adf63e -->
3. The one who likes to flirt with other guys but does not tell you so right at the beginning of the relationship, letting you believe that she has a monogamous vocation and only has eyes for you;

<!-- seg 16-0006 paragraph h=27b16cf9 -->
4. The one who likes to “give the time of day” to harassers;
<!-- QUERY: "dar trela" (colloquial: to encourage, give rope to someone’s advances) rendered "give the time of day"; alternatives: "lead on", "encourage". -->

<!-- seg 16-0007 paragraph h=7fc819f8 -->
5. The one who wants to receive love but does not want to give it;

<!-- seg 16-0008 paragraph h=3b3b4c44 -->
6. The one who suddenly disappears from your life, exactly when you are liking her the most;

<!-- seg 16-0009 paragraph h=499884cf -->
7. The one who spends all your money;

<!-- seg 16-0010 paragraph h=78226d85 -->
8. The one who wants to turn you into a slave;

<!-- seg 16-0011 paragraph h=38c18f9d -->
9. The one who abuses the good faith of sincere men;

<!-- seg 16-0012 paragraph h=f565c8cc -->
10. The one who reacts to affection with contempt;

<!-- seg 16-0013 paragraph h=93dad449 -->
11. The one who adopts ambiguous, incoherent, and indefinite behaviors;

<!-- seg 16-0014 paragraph h=65d7c35b -->
12. The one who labels as “insecure” or “jealous” the men who demand transparency and coherence of attitudes;

<!-- seg 16-0015 paragraph h=aa4ebc8e -->
13. The one who demands commitment and trust from her partner but behaves in a dubious way;

<!-- seg 16-0016 paragraph h=389bb26e -->
14. The one who pretends to be interested in you in order to repel you when you try to approach;

<!-- seg 16-0017 paragraph h=b464f178 -->
15. The one who shows herself attractive and receptive in order to make false accusations of sexual harassment;

<!-- seg 16-0018 paragraph h=0756c4fd -->
16. The one who enjoys watching males fight it out over her;

<!-- seg 16-0019 paragraph h=f5b5800d -->
17. The one who, out of pure spite, goes against all her partner’s wishes;

<!-- seg 16-0020 paragraph h=d193ca36 -->
18. The woman who provokes your irritation to see you furious, calls the police, and tries to get you to hit her before the police arrive.

<!-- seg 16-0021 paragraph h=0a53bf67 -->
There are many other types that I do not remember right now, but these I have pointed out will serve for you to begin reflecting. Prevention is better than cure: before we fall into the traps of these sly ones, we must get ahead of them and take prophylactic measures for protection. A preventive measure that usually works is to confront them right off the bat, firing point-blank questions about their sentimental honesty and in this way obtaining guarantees of sincerity. This inhibits the cheating somewhat (but not entirely), since they realize they are not dealing with an idiot. A second measure is to observe them and adapt ourselves to their behaviors, avoiding believing what they say. A third (and practically infallible) preventive measure is to avoid them for good, preferring only women who are sincere, coherent, transparent, and who act in a definite way in love. But it is not easy to find women like that these days...
