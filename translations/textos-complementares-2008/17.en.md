16. Male self-power

<!-- seg 17-0002 paragraph h=780128bc -->
It is one thing to master oneself. It is another thing to dominate the other. He who masters himself is highly adaptable, and ends up leading the other through the other’s own desires and passions, without forcing his will or his free will. Because he accepts and adapts, he is able to follow, without identification, the course of other people’s passions.

<!-- seg 17-0003 paragraph h=cc310360 -->
There are men who let themselves be taken over by the desire that women do what they want. They are possessed by a passion: their will is imprisoned in this desire. If they do not eliminate this weakness, they may be led and manipulated all over the place: women will pretend to go along with them and will drag them wherever they please. It is important, for this reason, to renounce the desire to force them to fit the idealized molds we create. We have to destroy such molds in order to adapt ourselves to what they really are, to what they actually present and offer us[^15]. This is not submission but adaptation.

<!-- seg 17-0004 footnote n=15 h=64293bee -->
The strategies of “mental cornering” and of the “ultimatum” aim to reveal reality so that we can adapt. They do not aim to force the other’s free will. Nor would they aim to force the woman to love us, because that would simply be impossible.

<!-- seg 17-0005 paragraph h=460dd377 -->
Male self-power should be understood thus: man’s power exercised over himself. Man must be master of his own feelings. Women’s frustration of male expectations is possible, in part, because men let themselves be dominated by the desire that women do what they want. This is the main expectation that suffers frustration. To dismantle the process, it is enough to have no expectation whatsoever. How? Through the death of the defects.

<!-- seg 17-0006 paragraph h=f38ec6aa -->
To accept everything, to adapt, and to take advantage of whatever is possible for as long as it is possible: that is my suggestion. Not to waste time punching the knife, stupidly fighting it out against the inevitable. The reader will ask me whether there is a limit. I will say: the limit is the damage caused to our life. A woman has every right to do whatever she wants with her own life, but not with ours. The limit is abuse of our person. Got it?

The reason some women detest what I write is that I suggest to man psychological rebelliousness in his relationship with the opposite sex. I suggest that man stop being affectively submissive and seek emotional independence. Many women do not accept this because they want to keep men under their feet.
