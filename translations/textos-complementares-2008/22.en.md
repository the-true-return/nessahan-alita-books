21. Passionate love and lust

<!-- seg 22-0002 paragraph h=bbf825c0 -->
They say that passion is something beautiful and that it does us a great deal of good. Lust is encouraged everywhere. Everywhere one hears, as Alberoni observed:

<!-- seg 22-0003 paragraph h=758931f2 -->
“Seduce everyone.”

<!-- seg 22-0004 paragraph h=64539211 -->
This is a disease of our civilization: the cult of lust and of sentimentalism. People go so far as to believe that sentimentalism is the most sublime spirituality, without heeding the fact that there are horrible and destructive feelings that alternate, in bipolar succession, with “benevolent” passionalism.

<!-- seg 22-0005 paragraph h=42f38e42 -->
Romantic passion is felt in the heart. The lustful impulse is felt in the sexual organ. Nevertheless, both have a mental origin; they are in the head. They are grounded in mental forms, thoughts, memories, morbid and romantic mechanical imaginings. That is why philosophical reflection frees man and teaches him how to live: lust and romantic passionalism are in the mind, and it is from there that they must first be uprooted. They are mistaken visions, distorted understandings, subjective and erroneous perceptions. He who tries to overcome his weaknesses without cleansing his mind will get worse and worse and will fall ever deeper. The way is to understand reality, and this is achieved first of all through educating the mind for correct, silent, and concentrated thinking. Only this kind of thinking makes possible the self-analysis, self-reflection, and self-observation taught by Erich Fromm.
<!-- QUERY: Original spells "Eric From"; corrected to "Erich Fromm". -->

<!-- seg 22-0006 paragraph h=03adeb37 -->
The silent mind gives no shelter to the despotism of bestial mental forms. The more one thinks, the worse one makes the situation. On the other hand, concentrated thought leads, over time, to not-thinking and to pure understanding.

<!-- seg 22-0007 paragraph h=bcba751c -->
The man in love thinks of his beloved constantly. The lustful man thinks of the woman he desires without stopping. Their minds are, in a certain sense, obsessed. Therefore, if you want to overcome your affective and sexual weaknesses, you must begin by taming and educating your mind.

<!-- seg 22-0008 paragraph h=17afd48e -->
Both romantic passion and the lustful impulse can invade the mind. Obsessed[^19], the individual no longer has control over himself and is not the master of his own acts. The non-satisfaction of the obsessing impulse causes suffering, and this pain drives the person toward satisfaction. To overcome this suffering, the obsessed person must separate himself from the invading impulse, observing it “from outside,” as something foreign, regarding it as a stranger (another “I” within himself)[^20]. With his observation he must penetrate, first of all, his mind (his thoughts, imaginings, fantasies, memories, reasonings, and recollections). It is mainly there, in the mind, that the obsessing and undesirable psychic elements are found. The problem is, therefore and above all, in the head (intellectual center). When we resolve it there, we can easily resolve it in the heart (emotional center) and in the other centers (sexual, instinctive, and motor).

<!-- seg 22-0009 footnote n=19 h=421c028c -->
I am not using this word to designate people who have definitively lost control of themselves, for in such a case every attempt at self-overcoming is impossible. I am referring to cases in which there is still enough lucidity to drive the person to fight against the despotism of destructive inner impulses.

<!-- seg 22-0010 footnote n=20 h=794e8548 -->
Only he who possesses a lucidity that allows him to understand that he has been invaded, and to feel the need for self-overcoming, is capable of such a break with identification.

<!-- seg 22-0011 paragraph h=3745ec38 -->
A serious mistake made by the obsessed is trying to stop the impulses without stopping the corresponding thoughts. Such an attempt causes the impulses to assail them with even greater force.

<!-- seg 22-0012 paragraph h=843ae501 -->
Concentration of thought and control of the mind are also useful in overcoming premature ejaculation, which results from mental overexcitement caused by unsatisfied erotic fantasies that invade the head during the sexual act, and in eliminating any other defects of a psychological nature. It is a specific care that one needs to take during the death of the Ego[^21].
