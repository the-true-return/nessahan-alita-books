17. Why they are so fascinating

<!-- seg 18-0002 paragraph h=b73264a2 -->
What makes women so attractive in men’s eyes is femininity. And what is femininity? In my way of thinking, it is fragility, delicacy, and sweetness. It is these characteristics that make a woman more feminine, and the more feminine a woman is, the more attractive she will be. The woman who wants to be attractive in male eyes shows herself feminine in every respect, as far as she is able. Conversely, the woman who does not want to activate men’s sexual desire shows herself similar to them. Of course men will then repel her, since she gives them the same sense of strangeness given them by other men. From the point of view of sexual affinity, the male is strongly attracted to what is opposite to him as a gender and repels what is similar to him.

<!-- seg 18-0003 paragraph h=798cc5f7 -->
Women are imbued with the universal feminine. When they enter our field of perception, their unpleasant characteristics and defects are excluded from our consciousness, outshone by the archetypal feminine traits. In other words: we see only the adorable femininity and become blind to everything else. Because it is so wonderful and paradisiacal, the feminine masks the uninteresting side, which they also bear. However, this idyll is a fantasy and is very far from the real woman. She is rarely perceived. When the fantasy is suddenly undone, against our will, it can turn into the opposite fantasy: that woman is a terrible being with immense powers. Both fantasies are mistaken, because their power is conferred by our own psyche. It is within us, therefore, that the problem lies.

<!-- seg 18-0004 paragraph h=f8540444 -->
The feminine is a force of nature and not something exclusive to women, as everyone thinks. Everything in nature develops according to polarities. Long before women existed on Earth, the feminine already existed. The feminine is eternal and universal.
