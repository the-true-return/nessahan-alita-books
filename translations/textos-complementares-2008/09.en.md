8. Generating attraction

<!-- seg 09-0002 paragraph h=1d8944e4 -->
To produce positive results, provoking admiration, impact and, consequently, attraction, our prominence should preferably occur in the circles where the female presence is strong.

<!-- seg 09-0003 paragraph h=a9b64e15 -->
Attraction arises from our standing out positively in relation to other men, before whom we “show up more,” and it brings together multiple combined factors. They always compare us.

<!-- seg 09-0004 paragraph h=69a4a128 -->
Intense feelings are provoked by the following paths: decisive speech, with an unerring aim and topics that are not repetitive; behavioral alternation; good-quality wild sex with great intensity; fearless and fair unmaskings; prominence in the male hierarchy; a penetrating gaze; confrontation of convictions; severity; confidence; masculinity; coldness; calm; objectivity; ease; mastery of oneself and of situations; leadership; protective command; skill at dismantling; initiative; activity; penetrating intelligence; virility[^9].

<!-- seg 09-0005 footnote n=9 h=dd7b7969 -->
It is important to remember that adopting some of these behavioral traits incorrectly, improperly, or in inappropriate contexts is counterproductive and can even be extremely dangerous.

<!-- seg 09-0006 paragraph h=c31c724f -->
One does not provoke fear in the enemy when one fears him. One does not provoke anger in the scoffer when one is enraged. Similarly, one does not make a woman fall in love when one is in love. In social relations, feelings complement each other by opposition: what I feel for the other is, in some way, the opposite of what the other will feel for me. It is a matter of logic. Cads provoke in women intense feelings of surrender and devotion. Human passions follow the principles of universal magnetism.

<!-- seg 09-0007 paragraph h=8a2138d3 -->
Unfortunately, braggarts, show-offs, liars, and narcissists provoke more emotional impact, and consequently make a greater impression, than the sincere, transparent, humble, and honest. We have to surpass, in the power to impress, those who bear Schmitt’s “Dark Triad,” but without actually turning into what they are.

<!-- seg 09-0008 paragraph h=5a6234d1 -->
Driven by competitiveness and curiosity, defects that tend to swallow them alive, a woman will become specifically interested in a man if she perceives that many other women feel attracted to him. The more beautiful and desirable these rivals are, the more intense the interest will be.

<!-- seg 09-0009 paragraph h=b439b2e9 -->
To generate attraction in women is to act in the correct way (which is normally the opposite of what we were taught) and not to try to arbitrarily force their will, an act that strips us of all reason and gives them reasons to spare to accuse us and to manipulate us through our own feelings of guilt. An example of an arbitrary attempt to activate female desire by mistaken paths is the uncontrollable impulse, very common among the uninformed, to show interest, chase, and harass.

<!-- seg 09-0010 paragraph h=1463e9ee -->
If she does not show herself attracted, it is because the information you (in)voluntarily transmit presents nothing interesting. Change, then, your way of being and acting. Do not persist on the same mistaken path against all the evidence.
