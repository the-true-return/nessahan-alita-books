9. Preemptive dismantling
<!-- QUERY: "Desarticulação antecipada" → "Preemptive dismantling" ("desarticular" is rendered "dismantle" throughout). Alternatives: "Advance dismantling," "Dismantling in advance." -->

<!-- seg 10-0002 paragraph h=85afb248 -->
Let us imagine a hypothetical case. Suppose you happen to come across a woman who pleases you very much. She seems perfect to you, and you really liked her a lot!

<!-- seg 10-0003 paragraph h=b922eeae -->
So you ask for a phone number and she gives it to you with all kindness. Everything indicates that things are going very well: smiles, glances, wonderful conversations! Your hopes grow! You call her on the agreed day and then... she does not answer, leaves her phone switched off or something like that!

<!-- seg 10-0004 paragraph h=fd2255b1 -->
Or else suppose you manage to set up a date and she reciprocates completely. Full of feigned enthusiasm, she agrees to see you on such-and-such a date. But on the day of the date, she simply does not show up.

<!-- seg 10-0005 paragraph h=1ed83758 -->
In both cases, you were the victim of a romantic swindle. Have things like this happened to you? Certainly they have, for such behavior is a rule and is predictable.

<!-- seg 10-0006 paragraph h=379b051c -->
What were your mistakes? First: you believed her. Second: you got carried away with enthusiasm. Third: you did not create a definitive cornering situation that would force her to show her true face.

<!-- seg 10-0007 paragraph h=681c14cc -->
Breaking romantic commitments, kindly entered into, is a rule adopted by many women, and cornering situations are the conditions we create so that their true intentions are revealed. Since this is a dominant tendency, frustration is something predictable and expected, and it is much easier to anticipate it so as to prevent it than to remedy it.

<!-- seg 10-0008 paragraph h=58e2ea49 -->
Since swindling and frustration are the rules, whenever we set up a romantic engagement with a woman (a phone call, a date, an outing, etc.), it is indispensable to “pin her down” by means of cornering situations that define the situation before the sly one tries to frustrate us.

<!-- seg 10-0009 paragraph h=ea136f43 -->
Since swindles are a rule, they should come as no surprise and, truth be told, they are merely something expected and predictable. Hence, the most sensible means of combating them is the prophylactic route: dismantling them before they begin. The most propitious moment to dismantle them is the one when everything is going wonderfully well (the moment when our hopes begin to grow). No enthusiasm! The moment she dangles a wonderful future (near or distant) before us is the moment to demand guarantees of sincerity, honesty, consistency and transparency. It is also the moment to communicate clear rules that prevent indefiniteness, sudden disappearance and other torments. It usually works to demand in advance that the commitments made be kept. What matters is to get out in front and arrive first, not letting the swindle happen, that is, aborting it before it is born.
