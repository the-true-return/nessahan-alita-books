20. Going further

<!-- seg 21-0002 paragraph h=f8a16933 -->
As time goes by, experience accumulates. The accumulation of experience transforms one’s conceptions. My conceptions of today differ from those of yesterday and will differ from those I will have tomorrow.

<!-- seg 21-0003 paragraph h=fad0342a -->
In my books I detailed the dark side of the feminine. Today I think that perhaps I exaggerated a little, since some readers seem to have gone blind to the other side. However, there is also a sublime side to the feminine that we cannot ignore.

<!-- seg 21-0004 paragraph h=69282e81 -->
The state that is superior to the emotional hells is not a polarized state, whether against or in favor. Let us understand that polarization will always bring bad consequences. If we are blind to either side, we will be under a partial, conditioned and stubborn view that will be very harmful to us.

<!-- seg 21-0005 paragraph h=ee71f797 -->
The fact is that both sexes need each other. Woman cannot live without man, contrary to what people usually say out there. And man, as everyone knows, cannot live without woman. So, no trying to become “self-sufficient”! We have to learn to dismantle wiles and dishonesty with the sole aim of legitimate self-defense, and not in order to polarize ourselves against the opposite sex. We have to learn to dismantle the war of passion and refuse to fight in this war.

<!-- seg 21-0006 paragraph h=702a3c46 -->
Today I think that we should in no way enter into a “tug-of-war.” More than ever, I believe that we have to transcend and overcome the wiles and swindles, not reinforce them further with attitudes that justify them. Repaying dishonesty with dishonesty is reinforcing the game and feeding the endless cycle of swindles in love, precisely what we must destroy.

<!-- seg 21-0007 paragraph h=4b2866c6 -->
We have to break the chain, break the cycle. We only manage this through an inner state superior to the situations. To vibrate on the same wavelength is to chain oneself to the endless circle of suffering.

<!-- seg 21-0008 paragraph h=2acd446f -->
Love also presents itself in a dual form: it can wound and kill, but it can relieve and heal. Let us distinguish conscious love (altruistic) from emotional love (wrongly considered true love).

<!-- seg 21-0009 paragraph h=af935c7d -->
We should remember that, although there is a dark side to the feminine, there is also a dark side to the masculine, and that both reinforce each other in an infernal dance. I do not advocate that we reinforce our dark side as a way of fighting the other party’s dark side. Opposition dams up the contrary forces. We need to be superior to evil and not equal to the opposite side.

<!-- seg 21-0010 paragraph h=92cd2d48 -->
Even while aware that human beings (including women, obviously) are selfish and tend to be users, we have to refrain from harming them. We have to learn to protect ourselves without causing them harm or, at most, causing them the least harm possible. If she provokes your hatred, do not hate. If she tries to deceive, manipulate, betray you, etc., sabotage, boycott, dismantle, but do not return the same thing, because, as I see it, you will be wounding yourself. Return something superior.

<!-- seg 21-0011 paragraph h=768f7bb1 -->
Today I think I ought to rewrite a good part of the books, for a more exact understanding. Perhaps I failed to reinforce certain points that are missed today. When I sometimes recommend, for example, returning women’s wiles, I am referring to a mirror effect (bouncing off and coming back) and not to revenge. It is not a matter of repaying, out of mere vengeful desire, some wiles with other, different and additional wiles; it is only a matter of isolating the sly one in her own trap, so that she plays alone and experiences what had been intended for us. “Returning the wile” is creating a refractory barrier that carries the wiles and their undesirable effects back to whoever launched them; that is, it is refusing the unpleasant “gift” that was offered to us, as the legend of the Buddha tells.

<!-- seg 21-0012 paragraph h=b1101363 -->
It bothers me a great deal to see that there are people who get it all wrong and suppose that I endorse romantic revenge. What I endorse is the boycott of dishonesty in love and also certain just retaliations. If at some point I wrote something that allowed another interpretation, it was out of mere conceptual inexactness. I sympathize with the just indignation (and even with the anger) of whoever is swindled, but I do not believe that the best thing for our health is to fixate on these feelings. It is understandable that we feel anger, but it is not healthy to cultivate it.

<!-- seg 21-0013 paragraph h=e69eb131 -->
True happiness does not lie in the other person; we have to seek it in ourselves. We have no reason to demand that women “make us happy,” for this is not within their reach. If they are not capable of making even themselves happy... much less could they make us so!
<!-- QUERY: "no(a) outro(a)" marks both genders in Portuguese; English "the other person" is already neutral, so the marking was not reproduced. -->

<!-- seg 21-0014 paragraph h=6a90575b -->
Once the correct inner state has been attained, we will be fully aware of the condition in which present-day women find themselves but, at the same time, we will accept that state as something that is theirs by right of free will (just as our emotional defense is also a legitimate and inalienable right). We will not nurture resentments or negative emotions of any kind, for we will understand that we all have our own defects. Whoever does not have them in one field has them in another, since there is no one who is a little saint.

<!-- seg 21-0015 paragraph h=079dc0af -->
If she does not understand the value of true love and scorns the sincerity you devoted to her, so be it! Remember that people are the children of their own deeds. If she made you very angry unjustly, remember that she did so because she was deluded by the lie that swindling others is an advantage, but that the days of that joy will also come to an end, since in the end we will all be equal. There is no sly one who can swindle time...
