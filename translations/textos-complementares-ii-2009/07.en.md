6. Two possible paths

<!-- seg 07-0002 paragraph h=3b2c274e -->
In the work of winning the woman we long for, I see two basic paths:

<!-- seg 07-0003 paragraph h=b7511e58 -->
1. A short and direct path;

<!-- seg 07-0004 paragraph h=6d25ab6a -->
2. An indirect and long path;

<!-- seg 07-0005 paragraph h=fd74e699 -->
One path is positioned at one extreme and the other at the opposite extreme. Between the two, there are various possibilities and nuances.

<!-- seg 07-0006 paragraph h=41c196de -->
On the first path, we make our real intention explicit; we create a clear and definitive situation immediately after meeting her. This route does not work with all women, nor does it suit all men. My view is that it only yields results if the woman is already interested beforehand, to a very high degree, in something we have, whatever the interest that motivates her. Here, tolerance of indefiniteness is zero from the start.

<!-- seg 07-0007 paragraph h=7ad4d4e5 -->
On the second path, we conceal the intention and at the same time progressively draw closer in intimacy through ever more compromising attitudes, until the moment we ask her out or something of the kind. I believe this route is the most workable for normal men and the one that has the most effect with so-called “difficult” women.

<!-- seg 07-0008 paragraph h=9e2db23f -->
Many women take offense (<i>“What does he think I am?”</i>) when approached directly by normal men (<i>“Who does he think he is?”</i>). They become indignant and tend to rebuff them, even if deep inside they are gratified by the satisfaction of the desire for continuity. Only if the man makes a strong impression, through the right emotional impact, does the short and direct path yield positive results. When one lacks this ability, whether for economic, physical or other reasons, it is more reasonable to opt for the indirect route. It is longer and demands a great deal of patience. It is on the long path that we are most exposed to the unpleasant effects of the little games.

<!-- seg 07-0009 paragraph h=60e99e3a -->
When we are not the object of intense prior interest, we are required to behave in a non-sexual way specifically toward the woman who interests us. This must be well understood: it is not a matter of general asexual behavior (toward all women) but of a stance of disinterest, or of little interest, only toward the woman who is blocking the approach. This means: acting in a highly masculine way but, at the same time, acting as if she did not interest us much, which is totally different from being a mere penis-less “bestie.” Of course, if we proceed correctly, things will evolve to a point where concealment will no longer be possible, nor necessary: the moment of extreme intimacy.

<!-- seg 07-0010 paragraph h=b5c7ff9e -->
If there is no pre-existing interest, the tendency is for us to be rebuffed, because the woman tries to keep us at a distance and does not allow us to reveal our attractions. If you are not the bearer of attributes that make an impact at first sight (height, a big car, fame, social prominence, etc.), you will need more or less close and prolonged contact to reveal your interesting side. Without entering the woman’s field of consciousness, it is absolutely impossible to activate attraction. How could a person feel attraction for someone whose existence is not perceived?

<!-- seg 07-0011 paragraph h=2fdad32d -->
But approach and contact will not be possible if the man lets his tongue hang out and stands there drooling like a wolf. The most one achieves with this behavior is to come up against a wall. Hence the need to conceal one’s intention while revealing attractive masculinity and gradually drawing closer in intimacy. It is unnecessary to reveal our specific interest, because women already tend to believe that they are desired. So why reveal something that is already known? To disconcert them, it is much more effective to generate doubts than to reinforce certainties (and isn’t that what they do to us?).

<!-- seg 07-0012 paragraph h=70ad38c5 -->
I have seen that men, when they let themselves be seized by desire and grow desperate, tend to skip stages and rush through phases, which results in failure. This happens because they should have opted for the indirect path but could not bear it. They imagined themselves in the middle of the sexual act with the woman, were seized by desire and did not concentrate on the earlier stages.

<!-- seg 07-0013 paragraph h=47f96463 -->
Between the two paths described here, there are various nuances or shades, which can be chosen as real situations present themselves.
