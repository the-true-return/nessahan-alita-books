5. Main wiles and the corresponding solutions

<!-- seg 06-0002 paragraph h=deff2c2e -->
Here follows a list of the most common wiles in women’s little games and of the main means of guarding ourselves against them and dismantling them.

<!-- seg 06-0003 table_row h=d140ba5a -->
Wile ¦ Solution

<!-- seg 06-0004 table_row h=1114fcac -->
Sudden disappearance ¦ Anticipation[^3]

<!-- seg 06-0005 footnote n=3 h=68215cb4 -->
This strategy consists in informing the woman in advance that we are aware of the unpleasant facts to come, so as to show her that we already expect them and will not be caught by surprise. Communicating in advance empties the wile of its meaning, because it needs surprise to have its frustrating effect.

<!-- seg 06-0006 table_row h=4c9d4840 -->
¦ Cornering[^4]

<!-- seg 06-0007 footnote n=4 h=56e87444 -->
It consists in creating a definitive situation in which even highly ambiguous conduct reveals the real intentions hidden behind dissimulated behavior. Example: “If you don’t phone me within three days, you needn’t ever call me again.”

<!-- seg 06-0042 paragraph h=ede5c127 -->
Let us not consider the solutions above as ready-made recipes to be applied mechanically. They are only suggestions that require judgment and good sense when used. There is no total guarantee of effectiveness, for in the little games of the war of passion there is never a total guarantee of anything. We can only say that the chances of solving our problems increase when we begin to consider the solutions proposed here as possible means of dismantling the situations indicated.

<!-- seg 06-0043 paragraph h=38113efa -->
For each problem there are multiple possible solutions. Obviously, I have not listed all the problems, nor all the existing solutions; I have only pointed out a few.
