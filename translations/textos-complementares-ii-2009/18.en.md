17. War of minds

<!-- seg 18-0002 paragraph h=54deb6e4 -->
When a man irritates or enrages a woman, he is invading her mind through a door that almost always remains open. He will be the constant object of her thoughts, the target of her vengeful spite and of her hatred, but at the same time he may become the object of a gradual passion, since love and hatred, both being absurd, tend to follow one another and even to coexist. However, if this same woman dissolves the egos related to the negative feelings in question (irritation, hatred, feelings of inferiority, the need for revenge and many others), she will become armored and invulnerable.

<!-- seg 18-0003 paragraph h=6da037c7 -->
Women usually armor themselves very well against enchantment (that is why letters, flowers and declarations of love do not work), but they seldom guard themselves against the bewitchment that takes place through negative emotions.

<!-- seg 18-0004 paragraph h=3f7b5853 -->
In the war of romantic conquest, what matters is to penetrate the other’s mind, invade it and take root there. If they try to invade our mind, it seems legitimate to me that we turn their own spell back on them.

<!-- seg 18-0005 paragraph h=9a8c307b -->
The woman will change her attitude if she is led to have opposite feelings. She will be led to opposite feelings if her beliefs undergo an inversion. And her beliefs will undergo an inversion if we work our way in and reach her mind with the right attitudes. It is not a matter of manipulating, persuading or convincing: it is a matter of making her aware of what we are capable of by means of right, impactful attitudes that destroy her mistaken beliefs about us. By destroying the beliefs, we also destroy the resistance.

<!-- seg 18-0006 paragraph h=7aae337f -->
In the war of passion, the one who constantly thinks about the other party is the defeated one.

<!-- seg 18-0007 paragraph h=ec3df064 -->
Women, if you don’t want us to come in through the back doors, close them!
