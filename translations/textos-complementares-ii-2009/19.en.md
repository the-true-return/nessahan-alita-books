18. The cad’s devotion

<!-- seg 19-0002 paragraph h=1a6ac98a -->
I have observed that cads are not negligent toward the relationship. They are highly devoted, but they are so only to achieve their selfish goals.

<!-- seg 19-0003 paragraph h=ef019e8e -->
The cad lies, deceives, cheats. He promises the moon and the stars in order to defraud the women who trust in him. He is a great braggart. Through his feigned conduct, stuffed with lies, he manages to impress women intensely, striking them unerringly in their feelings and leading them into an exaltation of the imagination.

<!-- seg 19-0004 paragraph h=1b6e8790 -->
The cad excites the female imagination and, by extension, the feelings.

<!-- seg 19-0005 paragraph h=c7bd4813 -->
It is not correct to say that the cad is not devoted. He is devoted, and very much so. He is just devoted in a selfish way. The cad is the exact male version of the tramp.

<!-- seg 19-0006 paragraph h=7674abec -->
The cad dangles the possibility of turning into a faithful man. He dangles this possibility but does not make it real, exciting, but not satisfying, the female desire for continuity.

<!-- seg 19-0007 paragraph h=9b9a4508 -->
Women seem to me highly vulnerable to cads because they are impressionable. Cads tend to be braggarts and theatrical in order to impress. They lie a lot; they say exactly what women want to hear. They are forever boasting and singing their own praises! They normally extort a good deal of money from their lovers.

<!-- seg 19-0008 paragraph h=08c1be60 -->
The cad’s various lovers suffer over him, because of their unsatisfied desires for continuity and domination. The cad, however, does not suffer over any of them. His devotion consists in adopting the best means of deceiving them. When the cad falls in love, he is struck down by his own spell.

<!-- seg 19-0009 paragraph h=4ebfcc6e -->
I believe that women should work on their weaknesses so as not to be so vulnerable. They should learn to value virtues and stop rewarding wicked men, for this vice does them no good whatsoever.
