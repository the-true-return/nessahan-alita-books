7. A type of woman who deserves trust

<!-- seg 08-0002 paragraph h=500cf36d -->
In the books I did not describe the virtuous woman much, and I will now try to fill this gap. I will seek to outline the female profile that is excluded from my criticisms and that was left out of most of my observations.

<!-- seg 08-0003 paragraph h=1c34ec10 -->
The trustworthy woman who deserves great consideration has the following characteristics:

<!-- seg 08-0004 paragraph h=5c534cdc -->
1) She is sweet, affectionate and feminine;

<!-- seg 08-0005 paragraph h=2b8846dd -->
2) She does not play little games;

<!-- seg 08-0006 paragraph h=5774c1a8 -->
3) She does not dissimulate;

<!-- seg 08-0007 paragraph h=28ae9b1d -->
4) She is sincere;

<!-- seg 08-0008 paragraph h=5e9d75cf -->
5) She does not lie; she tells the truth;

<!-- seg 08-0009 paragraph h=e6a50398 -->
6) She does not cheat in love;

<!-- seg 08-0010 paragraph h=011a7aa3 -->
7) She does not act in a dubious (indefinite) way;

<!-- seg 08-0011 paragraph h=83b1f1a1 -->
8) She does not try to manipulate situations to receive love without giving anything in return;

<!-- seg 08-0012 paragraph h=244f3aca -->
9) She does not pretend to love;

<!-- seg 08-0013 paragraph h=5ba02756 -->
10) She commits no betrayals of any kind, whether minor or serious;

<!-- seg 08-0014 paragraph h=078142f7 -->
11) She does not flirt with other males while pretending to have eyes only for us;

<!-- seg 08-0015 paragraph h=1b8324b0 -->
12) She has no little male friends;

<!-- seg 08-0016 paragraph h=fb1ab16b -->
13) She does not condone or pity harassers (including the ones who put on a nice-guy face);

<!-- seg 08-0017 paragraph h=bc25a11a -->
14) She is not forever going out with her girlfriends;

<!-- seg 08-0018 paragraph h=0133f81e -->
15) She does not give us false hopes;

<!-- seg 08-0019 paragraph h=5bf58c5c -->
16) She does not provoke our interest unless she really wants something with us;

<!-- seg 08-0020 paragraph h=e53288cf -->
17) She does not pretend to be interested only to run away afterward;

<!-- seg 08-0021 paragraph h=7274d99a -->
18) She does not attract in order to repel, nor in order to accuse of harassment;

<!-- seg 08-0022 paragraph h=bc7ba8da -->
19) She does not torment us with doubts;

<!-- seg 08-0023 paragraph h=20516102 -->
20) She takes responsibility for the consequences of her acts and does not try to hide them;

<!-- seg 08-0024 paragraph h=96cf2736 -->
21) She makes very clear what kind of person she is from the beginning of the relationship; 22) She does not pretend to be a saint when she chooses to have more than one partner;

<!-- seg 08-0025 paragraph h=96436f78 -->
23) She gives more importance to us than to other women;

<!-- seg 08-0026 paragraph h=72830577 -->
24) She dresses decently or takes responsibility for the consequences of dressing in a way we do not like;

<!-- seg 08-0027 paragraph h=74127dc6 -->
25) She does not repay love and devotion with coldness, contempt or rejection;

<!-- seg 08-0028 paragraph h=9aa73a14 -->
26) She does not repay companionship with horns or with sexual disinterest;
<!-- QUERY: "chifres" (horns) is the Portuguese idiom for cuckolding; the image was kept, as in How to Deal with Women. Alternative: "with cheating." -->

<!-- seg 08-0029 paragraph h=576e0d0c -->
27) She never tries to sneak out of the relationship;

<!-- seg 08-0030 paragraph h=a2959e3f -->
28) She does not suddenly disappear, out of nowhere;

<!-- seg 08-0031 paragraph h=e93594a3 -->
29) She does not ask for our phone number if she does not really want to call;

<!-- seg 08-0032 paragraph h=50499929 -->
30) She does not give out her phone number if she does not really want to answer;

<!-- seg 08-0033 paragraph h=1fc3ec0e -->
31) She does not provoke our anger;

<!-- seg 08-0034 paragraph h=fef135df -->
32) She does not kindle male desire with the intention of frustrating it;

<!-- seg 08-0035 paragraph h=c4ef25fc -->
33) She plays fair in love;

<!-- seg 08-0036 paragraph h=7acaf5a1 -->
34) She does not try to bend us through infatuation;

<!-- seg 08-0037 paragraph h=d7d4682b -->
35) She does not toy with our feelings;

<!-- seg 08-0038 paragraph h=0375204f -->
36) She does not marry one man while loving another;

<!-- seg 08-0039 paragraph h=d7ca79c5 -->
37) She likes us for what we are and not for what we have;

<!-- seg 08-0040 paragraph h=523388b1 -->
38) She does not want our money;

<!-- seg 08-0041 paragraph h=e067f590 -->
39) She values virtues;

<!-- seg 08-0042 paragraph h=27b4148c -->
40) She feels a strong sexual aversion to wicked men and cads;

<!-- seg 08-0043 paragraph h=0a66146c -->
41) She feels a strong sexual attraction to a kind man (sorry, but I can’t help having a good laugh as I write this item!!!!);

<!-- seg 08-0044 paragraph h=04cf5b85 -->
This is the woman who does NOT disappoint us and who does NOT fit the profile that leaves us discontented and against which we rebel. A woman like this deserves all our love, fidelity and devotion.

<!-- seg 08-0045 paragraph h=8f535acc -->
Women who are decent and honest in love and in feelings, where are you? Show yourselves!

<!-- seg 08-0046 paragraph h=0ad0943e -->
Jokes aside, there are no perfect women, just as there are no perfect men. But there are women who are a little better. The Feminine is indispensable to all of us (at least it is to me, and I think to you too!). However, like everything in life, it is dual; it has two natures: a superior one and an inferior one. The inferior aspect is the one that torments us, and the superior aspect is the one that gives us relief. Because of this duplicity, it is not at all easy for us to deal with women.
