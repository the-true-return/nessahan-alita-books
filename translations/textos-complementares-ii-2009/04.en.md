3. Premature and delayed ejaculation

<!-- seg 04-0002 paragraph h=ba3dd1cc -->
Ejaculation is a reflex act resulting from mental overexcitement. On reaching an extreme degree of excitement, the brain sends the sexual organ the message that the moment to ejaculate has come. What leads the brain to send such an order are lustful mental images. If we suppress them from the mind, we also suppress the overexcitement. Hence, the key to keeping ourselves short of ejaculation is a mind clean of morbid thoughts.

<!-- seg 04-0003 paragraph h=a667c667 -->
When erotic fantasies excite the sexual organ to an extreme point, which is the point of ejaculation, the brain “understands” that the moment to ejaculate has come and sends the order. Thus, mental silence acts as if it “tricked” the brain.

<!-- seg 04-0004 paragraph h=09d4b12f -->
During the sexual act, many memories, images and fantasies related to unsatisfied desires arise in the mind. The most secret erotic fantasies take hold of the mind and lead it into a state of growing excitement. When the excitement reaches a certain level, ejaculation follows. Those who suffer from premature ejaculation normally have a very excitable mind. However, by quieting the mind we can delay ejaculation and even suppress it. Suppressing ejaculation means suppressing orgasm, and not enjoying orgasm while trying to retain the semen (which is harmful to health). It is very important to distinguish the two things.

<!-- seg 04-0005 paragraph h=f38aa8f5 -->
Suppressing orgasm means suppressing ALL erotic fantasies and lustful images from the mind, which includes: memories of beautiful women, plans to obtain a coveted female, images of women we would like to have but never managed to, specific kinds of caresses we enjoy, and anything related to all of this. The arousal of the sexual organ should originate only from the reality perceived by the physical senses (caresses and touches, contemplation of the woman’s beauty, etc.), never from mental forms that are in our heads. The masturbator climaxes with mental images, and that is why masturbation is not recommended. Viewing pornography likewise strengthens this mental vice, conditioning the imagination. Ejaculation comes from morbid imagination. Ejaculation is avoided when the mind is kept quiet during the sexual act. If the slightest lustful thought enters the mind, an ejaculatory process is set off.

<!-- seg 04-0006 paragraph h=3284d027 -->
The fornicator is fascinated by the female figure and by pleasure, opens his mind to erotic mental images, identifies with them, savors them. He lives the illusion of his sexual fantasies as if it were reality. He places all his attention on the sexual organ, waiting for orgasm, while his mind, unguarded, is invaded by multiple mental forms[^1] that dominate him and drag him along.

<!-- seg 04-0007 footnote n=1 h=9633a1ce -->
The incubi and succubi described by Paracelsus. Such mental forms are autonomous, living psychic creatures, created by morbid imagination, and they grow stronger each time our thinking is drawn into lust. However, such mental creatures weaken rapidly when we learn to withdraw their food. If we do not dissolve them, on the other hand, they progressively distort thought and perception, slowly leading their progenitor, through ever greater depravity, to total ruin.

<!-- seg 04-0008 paragraph h=c6629d3c -->
We have to invert the lustful psychological process of ejaculation. If we are seized by the desire to ejaculate and want to get out of the quagmire, the first thing to do is to focus our attention on the head and not on the sexual organ. The second thing is to stop the mind, that is, to interrupt the lustful processes of thought. The third measure is to stay calm, not to get carried away. Then we can begin observing ourselves, without identification, and consciously follow what we are doing: the conscious sexual act. Conscious observation of oneself is not possible if we keep thinking millions of silly things. The millions of thoughts we have are, for the most part, useless.

<!-- seg 04-0009 paragraph h=46ded5ef -->
Seminal loss, whether it comes from fornication or from masturbation, can be avoided by staying vigilant and striking down lustful thoughts as soon as they begin to sprout. The same tactic serves to protect oneself against possession by any other defect. When we are negligent and allow thoughts to sprout and settle in, they soon grow stronger and it becomes almost impossible to remove them. Hence the importance of anticipating.

There are Hindu yogis who manage to avoid orgasm, retaining the semen, for years on end, and they achieve this through the power of concentration. True chastity is achieved when one is capable of having sexual relations for a long time with a single woman. It is not a matter of having one or another sexual encounter that lasts for hours, but of keeping far from orgasm for years and, preferably, even for one’s whole life. Both premature ejaculation and delayed ejaculation should be avoided, replaced by virile non-ejaculatory penetration[^2].

<!-- seg 04-0012 footnote n=2 h=545b38e3 -->
I am referring to a state in which the man keeps himself very far from the point of ejaculation, and not to some kind of orgasm in which the ejaculation is contained or “held back.” Trying to stop ejaculation without renouncing orgasm is extremely harmful to health.

<!-- seg 04-0013 paragraph h=b4365bf8 -->
The ordinary person is not capable of living without orgasm. He has turned it into a necessity. Some even go so far as to consider an anti-orgasmic life an unhappiness, for they know nothing of spiritual pleasures. They are souls that enjoy only the illusory happiness of the senses and are enamored of matter. However, if we strive to transform ourselves, little by little we understand that there is something more beyond orgasm and that it is perfectly possible, and also preferable, to live without it.

<!-- seg 04-0014 paragraph h=9e92b99b -->
I see no opposition between sex and chastity. In my view, chastity is incompatible with promiscuity and with orgasm, but not with the pure sexual act with a single woman.
