15. On compliments and attraction
<!-- QUERY: "galanteios" (gallant compliments, flirtatious flattery) → "compliments"; "cantadas" → "pickup lines." Alternatives: "gallantries," "flattery." -->

<!-- seg 16-0002 paragraph h=44b1b8b6 -->
The vice of paying compliments is a terrible habit that drives women away instead of drawing them closer. It is a plague: all a woman has to do is look at us and send a faint favorable signal, and there we are, tossing out the wretched pickup lines. We flatter, we pay compliments, we court, we take risks and we humiliate ourselves in every possible way, almost always without any result. If, by chance, we get some positive result after such notorious conduct, it is much more likely to have been due to some factor other than the complimenting conduct.

<!-- seg 16-0003 paragraph h=447f0448 -->
The function of the compliment is to inflate the female ego. The uninformed believe that it has a sexually attractive effect, which is false.

<!-- seg 16-0004 paragraph h=73b40480 -->
Female sexual attraction does not result from compliments, but from the evidence of male sexual characteristics, chiefly conduct. The more masculine a man is, not only in physical appearance (because they are not as visual as we are) but also in conduct (that is, in his clothing, his speech, his gaze and his manners), the more attractive he will be. The same happens with women: the more feminine they are in everything, the more attractive they will be in our eyes.

<!-- seg 16-0005 paragraph h=78864d57 -->
It follows, then, that a man who wants to be attractive to a woman must curb the impulse to make advances and to harass, while at the same time highlighting his masculinity, bringing to the fore a virile, leading and protective conduct. A virile conduct is the conduct of a male. A leading conduct is the conduct of one who guides and commands. A protective conduct is the conduct of one who wards off dangers. When a man replaces harassing conduct with this synthetic, threefold conduct, women usually allow him to take care of them and gradually open up, since not doing so would go against their needs and interests. Female sexual attraction is pragmatic.
