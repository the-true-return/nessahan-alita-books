Introduction

<!-- seg 01-0002 paragraph h=307c363b -->
Let us continue the clarifications begun in the previous volume.

<!-- seg 01-0003 paragraph h=28f8ea84 -->
If you have not yet read the books, I suggest you do so to avoid distortions.

<!-- seg 01-0004 paragraph h=d3962641 -->
The content of these texts was not introduced here to be turned into dogma. They are only starting points for later reflection. Although some ideas are aimed specifically at those sympathetic to spiritualist studies, nothing prevents other people from learning about them.

<!-- seg 01-0005 paragraph h=cab90d54 -->
I reiterate, once again, that I am mentally free and have no ideological commitments to anyone. I am the author of my books and may very well modify them and even renounce their content, partially or totally, if I so decide. It is not my fault if people do not want to think dialectically.

<!-- seg 01-0006 paragraph h=1c02b7ab -->
Never follow anyone; follow only yourselves. And even then, be careful!

<!-- seg 01-0007 paragraph h=eb9a2f02 -->
Happy reflecting.
