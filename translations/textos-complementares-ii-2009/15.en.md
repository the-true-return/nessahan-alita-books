14. Enemy love

<!-- seg 15-0002 paragraph h=2324dec9 -->
As long as passion has not taken hold, it is very easy to repudiate it. At that stage, it is enough for us to stay vigilant against everything that may sprout within us as regards enchantment.

<!-- seg 15-0003 paragraph h=e4f52bfe -->
Very different is the situation of the man who makes the mistake of letting himself be carried away by enchantment/spell. When we make this mistake, we fall into an oscillating game of love and hatred that causes us immense suffering and does great harm to our health.

<!-- seg 15-0004 paragraph h=d9774655 -->
Emotional swindles are true frauds of love. They are traps for obtaining the other person’s feelings without giving anything in return. They consist in kindling expectations and hopes only to frustrate them after the heart has been handed over. To swindle the other is to deceive him, inducing him to believe that he is loved when in reality he is not. In the romantic swindle, love and sincerity flow unilaterally and in one direction only, since one of the parties aims only to take advantage and exploit.

<!-- seg 15-0005 paragraph h=58cb60c0 -->
Women who cheat in love are very similar to any con artist and adopt the same <i>modus operandi</i>: they count on the victim’s credulity and turn his sincerity into an instrument. They also operate like charlatan salesmen, who fascinate their customers and sell products that do not exist. They are walking frauds. The way is to guard ourselves with foresight and skepticism. We can say that the sexual and romantic market is controlled by them; they make the laws and dictate the rules.

<!-- seg 15-0006 paragraph h=e4fb001b -->
The naive fall again and again for dishonest scams, until the day when, after many unpleasant experiences, they learn to guard against them before they happen. The experienced man does not fall for confidence tricks.

<!-- seg 15-0007 paragraph h=c7b4d991 -->
One feeling inevitably provoked by swindles is hatred. It is very difficult not to hate someone who plays with our feelings and uses our sincerity. This terrible feeling can become very intense and is usually proportional to the passionate love we felt before, that is, before we discovered that we had been double-crossed by the sly one. These are negative emotions provoked by the dirty games: anger, jealousy, the feeling of loss, of feeling defeated, double-crossed. It is no use at all trying to smother them: they explode and kill us. Nor is it any use surrendering to them: they will destroy everything around us, starting with our peace and our life. The only possible solution is understanding.

<!-- seg 15-0008 paragraph h=e9209bca -->
But it is not at all easy to understand reality when we are mad, and that is exactly what the accursed little games do to us: they make us irrational. The sly ones drag us onto the terrain of irrationality because that is precisely where they reign. It is on the terrain of the non-rational that the sly ones move with ease. On the terrain of mad emotions we have immense difficulty thinking and understanding[^12]. In the whirlwind of chaotic emotions we become vulnerable, a fact that favors them. Then we are dragged in every direction. The emotional whirlwind clouds the mind.

<!-- seg 15-0009 footnote n=12 h=ce8b9456 -->
As Chris Rock says, the whole problem is that, for us, there is always a need for things to make sense.

<!-- seg 15-0010 paragraph h=57c34f6c -->
As a man grows older, he must guard against the hatred resulting from his painful experiences with women. Otherwise, this hatred will devour him. He will need to transcend low and despicable, yet powerful, feelings.

<!-- seg 15-0011 paragraph h=922b383c -->
Everything in nature develops in duality, and women are no different: they have a good side and a bad side. The good side enchants and the bad side bewitches. To let oneself be dragged along by the magnetism of one side or the other is to expose oneself to destruction by the opposite side.

<!-- seg 15-0012 paragraph h=0c3d25f1 -->
In situations of defeat, it does not always seem advisable to me to seek out the woman to tell her everything that is stuck in one’s throat (but sometimes it is!). Sometimes the best way to strike her is to say nothing, to isolate her forever, leaving her in perpetual doubt. It is not a matter of fighting hatred with hatred, but of taking precautions to protect oneself. And defending oneself is a just and legitimate right. A simple look, cold, penetrating and direct, can often communicate much more than hundreds of words.

<!-- seg 15-0013 paragraph h=21cc271e -->
In every war there are two sides, and the war of passion is no different. On our side are the sincere women, who do not deceive and do not resort to selfish wiles in order to be loved. On the other side are the insincere ones, who try to steal our love by dishonest means. Those who try to deceive us choose the enemy side and voluntarily place themselves in the line of fire. It is a war we would rather not fight, for it is not at all healthy, but one from which we are not allowed to flee. No man manages to escape this sentimental war; women do not allow anyone to flee, they give no chance at all. The only solution left to us is to sabotage, boycott, pull out the foundations. We dismantle the war when we stay out of reach, when we become inaccessible to understanding by the other side. The only means I know of achieving this is by attaining an inner state of total detachment, something only a few manage. Entering the oscillating game of love and hatred is the worst thing we can do. We should not hate the “enemy” but rather understand the state she is in. If we fight, it is only because we are forced to and not because we like it or because it gives us pleasure. There is no fun at all in living in hell.

<!-- seg 15-0014 paragraph h=4b311021 -->
In order to swindle, the sly one needs to know our feelings to find her bearings. When we detach ourselves completely, we are able to prevent our feelings from being probed.
