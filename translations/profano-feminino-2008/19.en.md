Conclusions

<!-- seg 19-0002 paragraph h=f53fb42b -->
The Sacred Feminine (the part that expresses itself in sincere women) deserves all respect and reciprocation; that is, the sincere woman must have her merits recognized and reciprocated.

<!-- seg 19-0003 paragraph h=6c69754e -->
To state that women possess a sublime and wonderful aspect that corresponds to the Sacred Feminine is to state that there are sublime and wonderful women alive and walking upon the Earth. However, except in truly unequivocal cases, we cannot count on them very much, even if we want to, for their opposites, the sly ones, are not few and simulate sincerity to perfection.

<!-- seg 19-0004 paragraph h=7731a108 -->
In love, tricks are nowadays the rule. That being so, it is better to be realistic, prepare for the worst, and not waste time waiting for what is unlikely.

<!-- seg 19-0005 paragraph h=b2ad05d7 -->
Inner silence, non-action, acceptance, and certain just actions—specular, returning, refractory, or unmasking—are ways of countering the wiles of the Profane Feminine.
<!-- QUERY: "certas ações justas especulares devolutivas refratárias ou desmascarantes" is an unpunctuated string of adjectives; read as just actions that mirror (cf. "movimento especular ou de retorno", 18-0065), give back, are refractory, or unmask. Alternative: "certain just mirror actions that give back, refract, or unmask." -->

<!-- seg 19-0006 paragraph h=07fab5fe -->
Masculine desire is continually sought by women.

<!-- seg 19-0007 paragraph h=af2333be -->
Impartial observation is a means of understanding them.

<!-- seg 19-0008 paragraph h=d734af2e -->
Feminine feelings, thoughts, actions, and opinions are seen as absurd, illogical, and incoherent by us men, because they are paradoxical (they articulate mutually exclusive opposites that, from our perspective, could not coexist). Trying to force them to adopt a masculine worldview is a mistake. Trying to compel them to see the world from the perspective of masculine rationality, linear and focal, is equally a mistake.

<!-- seg 19-0009 paragraph h=8a3f00f4 -->
Illogicality and non-rationality are other forms of logic and rationality: of an emotional, intuitive, comprehensive, non-linear, non-focal, and paradoxical kind.

<!-- seg 19-0010 paragraph h=193ba656 -->
We should not try to change the world or other people, but rather ourselves.

<!-- seg 19-0011 paragraph h=c21ae95c -->
We should see women naturally and not as “beings from another world.” The feminine world of love is the world of indirect, ambiguous, and subliminal communications.

<!-- seg 19-0012 paragraph h=388e2227 -->
Sex is not the main goal of the feminine world.

<!-- seg 19-0013 paragraph h=0d4a7f48 -->
Sex is the main goal of the masculine world.

<!-- seg 19-0014 paragraph h=98be7eba -->
Feminine sexual desire is not as genitalized as masculine desire and is fused with desires of other orders.

<!-- seg 19-0015 paragraph h=7de391e4 -->
Getting irritated by feminine sarcasm is reinforcing it.

<!-- seg 19-0016 paragraph h=1e0421a6 -->
Arguing with women only makes the relationship worse.

<!-- seg 19-0017 paragraph h=46ec36d4 -->
Men ARE NOT useless beings.

<!-- seg 19-0018 paragraph h=aace9d24 -->
Men are desperate for women and sacrifice themselves for them, being incapable of living without feminine affection, with rare exceptions.

<!-- seg 19-0019 paragraph h=202393c7 -->
The knowledge developed in this book, and in the previous ones, constitutes only a provisional “theoretical model” that aims to help (adult) men by making the stressful behavioral contradictions of women in the realm of love comprehensible. Theoretical models must always be replaced by more refined theoretical models, and I hope this one will be too. It is not my wish to form fanatical, dogmatic, and extremist readers, devotees of stupidity and fixed conceptions, which would be anti-philosophical. If you have this tendency toward ignorance, I suggest you throw this work in the trash and never read it again. I do not write for the stupid.

<!-- seg 19-0020 paragraph h=e7051afe -->
All the criticisms developed here are limited to the terrain of love and do not extend to others. I would very much like to be wrong about the reality of love and of the human soul, but, unfortunately, my commitment to the truth does not allow me to write the opposite of what I observe, verify, and confirm daily, at least for now. If my ideas are refuted one day, I will be very happy about it.
