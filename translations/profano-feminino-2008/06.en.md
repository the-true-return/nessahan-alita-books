5. Why they are the opposite of what they confess

<!-- seg 06-0002 paragraph h=ebc9c642 -->
The unconscious stands in a relationship of opposition and complement to the conscious psyche. This is one of the reasons why people do not practice what they preach.

<!-- seg 06-0003 paragraph h=5df7c2aa -->
We do not know a person just by listening to what she says; we have to observe her to verify how she acts and reacts in various situations. Besides that, there are also conscious and purposeful inconsistencies. Human beings, including the females of the species, are feigning, false, lying, deceiving, and hypocritical.

<!-- seg 06-0004 paragraph h=7e6122ba -->
It is common for women to badmouth a man they feel attracted to. Almost all of them are unanimous in condemning the attitudes of cads and playboys, swearing up and down that they could never desire them, but when they are in front of them, they swoon and give themselves. All of them guarantee that they desire good, hardworking, honest men, but when they are in front of them, they find them tiresome and boring.

<!-- seg 06-0005 paragraph h=f3a85dcc -->
It follows that he who tries to be guided by what the sly ones say will be caught by surprise and come off badly. In the same way, almost all those interviews and polls that claim to demystify the disconcerting female imagination are ridiculous and false; they only muddle and disconcert our understanding.

<!-- seg 06-0006 paragraph h=715760e5 -->
Be very careful when you hear them saying: <i>“I like men who are like this and like that, who do this or that.”</i> They will very probably be lying, for in the field of love and sex they tend to be the opposite of what they confess.

<!-- seg 06-0007 paragraph h=8e1ebd94 -->
Do not try to fit the confessed ideal male model. Notice that they decide in favor of the absurd. Those who “bend” them are precisely the opposite of what is announced.
<!-- QUERY: "Aqueles que as 'dobram'" (those who "fold"/"bend" them, i.e., who win them over and make them yield) → “bend.” Alternative: “win them over.” -->

<!-- seg 06-0008 paragraph h=2f517520 -->
Do not waste time questioning them about what they want: observe them and you will find out. If you ask, you will be deceived. It is not rare for them to ask for precisely what will be rejected when it is received. They tend to repay affection with indifference and protective dominance with affection.

<!-- seg 06-0009 paragraph h=ad277578 -->
Understanding the female psyche is fundamental for dealing correctly with these delicious sirens. However, true understanding comes from observation and not from conversations.

<!-- seg 06-0010 paragraph h=ea4428a0 -->
In schools and high schools, we commonly see them scorn the good, polite, studious boys. They band together to admire and competitively chase the rowdiest and most undisciplined ones, who prefer drinking, getting hooked on drugs, having irresponsible fun, and avoiding study. Even so, when questioned, they deny, against all evidence, that they prefer the worst. But impartial observation reveals that the selection criteria are the style of the clothes, the motorcycles, the cars and, depending on age, the size of the salary, of the bank account, and of the material possessions. In short: social prominence is all that matters.

<!-- seg 06-0011 paragraph h=cd40c497 -->
The preference for the worst makes them naive and means they can easily be fooled by anyone. I know young men who rent motorcycles and cars to show them off at the doors of schools, bars, or dance clubs as if they were their own. They also often pay beautiful prostitutes to accompany them as if they were their girlfriends. The result is not long in coming, and several foolish women are quickly taken to a motel. Hardly rational, these females cannot resist the fascination of false signs of power that are convincing. Afterward, when they are abandoned after losing their virginity, they go around complaining and cursing every male on Earth, as if they themselves were not responsible for having offered themselves to precisely those they should not have. And they go on lying without the slightest shame when they say they feel attracted to nice, honest guys.

<!-- seg 06-0012 paragraph h=e27d740b -->
As I read the other day, I don’t remember where: women do not like the romantic dinner, as they say, but rather the high price of the whiskey, the diamond in the ring they are given, and the gold of the jewelry... They use love to camouflage their shameful greed.

<!-- seg 06-0013 paragraph h=004f4052 -->
Despite the lies that all of them insist on naively trying to keep up, the truth is that a female’s attraction is determined by the social value of the male. If he is considered “the hunk” or “the top dog” by the social circle and circle of friends in which <b>she</b> lives, he will arouse attraction even if he is mediocre, stupid, infantile, and promiscuous, as we see in the film “Kids.” He can have several girlfriends at the same time, for all of them will find his promiscuity lovely and will consider it a legitimate right. So what, under such conditions, they dare to call love is garbage, a ridiculous sentimentalism activated by social prominence.

Most males are not able to seduce beautiful women simply because they believe that these women are what they openly confess. Falling for these lying con games, they act according to what they were induced to believe and obtain results opposite to those they aimed for. Meanwhile, the worst good-for-nothings have no qualms about lying and pretending to impress them and fool them without the slightest scruple, and they are rewarded with good-quality sex and affection. This is repeated incessantly everywhere and, even so, they never change. Irrational female passionality colludes with lies, with vice, and with pretense, rewarding mediocrity and contributing to social degeneration[^15].

<!-- seg 06-0015 footnote n=15 h=6ccbfa27 -->
Just as irrational male passionality colludes with degenerative promiscuous fornication. All passionality is irrational, and that is why we cannot give ourselves over to it. Passionality is the cause of the wars that destroy peoples, of the consumerism that destroys the planet, and of the folly that destroys couples and families.

Try to see them as robots with intense but conditioned feelings[^16]. The female posture is a reflection of the male one[^17]. Change your behavior and they will change. Give them what they need and not what they ask for or claim to desire.

<!-- seg 06-0017 footnote n=16 h=b37b4f0e -->
The human being is a humanoid robot who lives in the incoherence of the automatism of his illusions. Men are also robots, but here we are concerned with the automatism of women.

<!-- seg 06-0018 footnote n=17 h=95265f83 -->
Just as the male posture is a reflection of the female one. I propose overcoming this automatism.

<!-- seg 06-0019 paragraph h=0d659515 -->
Try being protective, guiding, and caring on the one hand, and at the same time mysterious, distant, commanding, and cold on the other. You will see for yourself that they lie when they swear they prefer men who are in love, affectionate, mushy, tender, and needy.

<!-- seg 06-0020 paragraph h=ab8900d1 -->
The opposition between what they say and what they actually feel lies at the origin of the many confusions that disconcert the male sex. Let us look at some examples.

<!-- seg 06-0021 paragraph h=af04bd6e -->
Women say they like romantic men. This is partly true. They really do like romantic, lovestruck men as slaves, but they feel no sexual attraction to them at all. They say they detest and do not accept male infidelity. This is another partial truth. The infidelity of the emotional slave is indeed not accepted, but, curiously, the infidelity of the inaccessible ones (the powerful, the famous, and, in general, all the socially prominent) is not only accepted but also works as an attraction. The number of women of those who “are most in the spotlight” is, unfortunately, something that makes such a man interesting. They state in no uncertain terms that they do not want to be dominated, but when they date a democratic man, they feel bored and consider him a “wimp,” passive and weak, because the poor guy does not trample on their opinions. We thus confirm that almost everything we hear them say about themselves is a lie.

If you do not do this, she will have fun deceiving you.

If you waste time running after the nonsense they talk, they will refuse you the smile full of life, the passionate look, the ardent kiss, the warm embrace, and the maddening sex. That is why it is lawful and fair for you to “arm” yourself and defend yourself. Always stay on the side of truth and reason. Do not play dirty; leave that task to them.
