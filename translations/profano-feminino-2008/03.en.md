2. Why they don’t look at you

<!-- seg 03-0002 paragraph h=77a69569 -->
It bothers us to notice that we are continually looking at them in the streets, desiring them insanely, while they could not care less about us; they ignore us. Why does this happen?

<!-- seg 03-0003 paragraph h=538a5913 -->
The answer is that nature made females indifferent to males and made males desperate for females. Female sexual desire is much less intense than male desire.

<!-- seg 03-0004 paragraph h=480f166c -->
Note that a human female can be impregnated by only one male in the space of a year. By contrast, how many females could that male impregnate in the same period?

<!-- seg 03-0005 paragraph h=42eb7b4a -->
If you go on a sex strike, you will see that your companion will be affected only after a few weeks. And even then it will not be from the lack of sex itself, but from the disturbance of doubt about what is going on.

<!-- seg 03-0006 paragraph h=bcbb5e54 -->
Those who present themselves as fatal females are in fact pretending. Even nymphomaniacs, prostitutes, and porn actresses are not moved by simple genital desire, as the uninformed suppose. They are driven to the act by other motives: money, acceptance, damaged self-esteem, competition, curiosity, etc. They grow used to sexual hyperactivity and accept it, taking it on as in a stage play. Know that women do not much like sex itself, and they deceive you. There are anorgasmic women who are extremely promiscuous.

<!-- seg 03-0007 paragraph h=10d7626d -->
Many are those who pride themselves on their anorgasmia. Many are those who state in so many words that they do not need males for anything. Cases of women who rape men practically do not exist, and one does not hear of women who sexually harass their sons or brothers. We do not see women groping our virile member without our permission when we are on buses or in the streets. I have never heard of a single woman who spied on a man in the shower. There are no proven cases of women who make war on other peoples in order to take their males. The reverse, however, has been well known since time immemorial. These facts prove, in an absolutely irrefutable way, that the female sexual appetite is a sham. In truth, a woman’s sexual appetite is weak, which gives her immense endurance in the act of copulation by minimizing energy loss. Even so, the belief persists that women desire sex as much as we do. Why? Simply because their desires and feelings of various kinds take on a sexual appearance and express themselves that way, confusing us. What is expressed under the appearance of sexual desire is, in truth, fear, greed, envy, competitiveness, sadness, revenge, gratitude, pride, etc. If it were not so, females would be after us all the time, they would pursue us, and the situation would be reversed.

<!-- seg 03-0008 paragraph h=58ffbf9c -->
If someone still goes on doubting this evident truth, we unmask and destroy the lie that has victimized him with one more observation: prostitutes and porn actresses will not offer their favors unless they are paid; the most coveted hotties of the schools, the dance clubs, the dances, and the street corners do not offer themselves to shy, needy, dull, penniless males, but to playboys. Is there any objection left?

I will, even so, stamp on and grind them a little more until the remains of these lies disappear completely. In a survey published by Marie Claire magazine this month (July 2006), 74% of the women interviewed said they would rather go shopping at a mall than have an orgasm. I do not believe the editor faked the survey... Those who must have faked it were the interviewees themselves. Liars that they are, my first suspicion is that the remaining 24% also preferred shopping and did not tell the truth!

The climax of rose-colored romances is dull kisses on the mouth, not wildly passionate sex.

<!-- seg 03-0011 paragraph h=2611c3ca -->
People are reluctant to admit that coitus in and of itself is of little interest to females, because the idea is unpleasant to both sexes. Feminists then take the opportunity to say that we repress and castrate female sexuality, for the hypothesis that they are inherently apathetic and cold displeases them greatly.

<!-- seg 03-0012 paragraph h=21bef4c4 -->
But, our naive opponents will say, then why do they howl and hallucinate during the sexual act, even to the point of losing their sensitivity to pain? The answer is this: because they are melodramatic and theatrical, and possess the rare ability to believe in their own simulations and pretenses without losing awareness that they are pretending. They achieve this feat by dividing themselves into two parts: one that believes in the pretense and another that preserves the knowledge of the pretense. Excitement that appears exclusively sexual is, in truth, much more emotional and passionate in nature than properly erotic. It is adrenaline raised by an economic, sentimental, and social context without which sex will not happen. The fatal female is a sham.

<!-- seg 03-0013 paragraph h=073047d8 -->
My opponents will then say that this is due to the sexual repression exercised by men over women. What a fallacy! Such repression does not exist or, if it does, it is exercised mainly by fathers and husbands, with the specific interest of concentrating female libido on a single man, and it does not cancel out the attempts at stimulation on the part of most other human males. Let us do a test: let us leave a woman in the middle of several men, preferably half-undressed or in provocative clothing, and see whether they try to repress her or to encourage her sexually... In truth, human males try more to stimulate than to repress female sexuality, for they think about mating full-time.

<!-- seg 03-0014 paragraph h=99c966a3 -->
We have to understand, once and for all, that the desire to receive sex and affection is <b>NOT</b> the main weakness of the female sex. The main weaknesses are others:

<!-- seg 03-0015 paragraph h=44f65bd7 -->
• Fear, which moves them to seek men who offer protection, guidance, and leadership;

<!-- seg 03-0016 paragraph h=1991a24d -->
• Greed, which moves them to seek males who have material possessions and wealth;

<!-- seg 03-0017 paragraph h=79a7b09c -->
• Curiosity, which moves them to try to seduce disconcerting, intriguing, and mysterious men;

<!-- seg 03-0018 paragraph h=f7432eec -->
• Envy, which moves them to try to take men away from beautiful women or men who are desired by many;

<!-- seg 03-0019 paragraph h=ea18f58f -->
• Pride, which moves them to try to seduce in order to prove to themselves that they are attractive;

<!-- seg 03-0020 paragraph h=4d8213c2 -->
• Revenge, which moves them to try to seduce and subjugate a man who has wounded their self-esteem and pride.

<!-- seg 03-0021 paragraph h=da786122 -->
The motives that lead a woman to come on to a man can be various: to enslave him through love (so that he works or supplies money without getting anything in return), to make her rivals envious, to take revenge, to discover what is hidden behind his intriguing behavior, to be protected against threats of all kinds, to prove to herself that she has the power to attract the opposite sex, to test her own power of seduction, to obtain a substitute for the vanished biological father of the child she is carrying in her womb, to check whether men consider her ugly and uninteresting, etc. Never will the advances made by a human female be due to an exclusively sexual desire or to a disinterested love, as everyone wants it to appear.

<!-- seg 03-0022 paragraph h=5886a8e9 -->
Therefore, they do not like sex as much as they seem to, and they do not notice our presence in the streets simply because they do not have the act of copulation as an existential goal, they do not miss us, and, to make everything worse, they believe themselves continually desired by everyone[^13]. We, on the contrary, live only to have sex and are desperate to have them in our arms, to invade them, penetrate them, and lose ourselves inside them.

When they feel desired, there is no discomfort of any kind and, therefore, no need whatsoever to take action. Men keep women in inertia by treating them like queens, goddesses, and offering themselves as slaves.

Males dream of females who fall in love with their <i>phalus erectus</i> and pursue it tirelessly, but such a dream is absurd and crazy. For a woman to look at a stranger and come on to him, other motivations of a nonsexual nature are needed. It is a waste of time to spend energy on such foolish hopes. No woman dreams of a four-meter penis, but many lie and say they do.

Women will go on not noticing your presence, they will ignore you, and they will not come out of their inertia unless you display some visible symbol of power that shows social superiority over rival males.

<!-- seg 03-0027 paragraph h=c9026626 -->
They will not look at you for nothing. For your utopian dream of being chased by gorgeous females to be satisfied, you would have to have the attributes of the males they chase, and that is the problem: gorgeous females are monopolized merchandise and chase only the powerful, the famous, and the mobsters. They chase only those who stand out at the top of the male hierarchy. So, if you want to have or keep a beauty in your arms, take the initiative and do not stand waiting passively. Get moving, do something. However, do not forget that you will have to act by alternative routes.

You, I, or any other ordinary male will be the object of a spontaneous glance only from those who feel incapable of attracting males more socially prominent than we are.

Elements that easily draw the female gaze of admiration are: big flashy cars, a display of luxury, and expensive clothes (not for the beauty they possess in themselves but because they are signs of power). We ordinary men, however, have to resort to other means: the fearsome gaze, selective indifference, calculated horrification, intriguing conduct, masculine posture, distinctive speech, a commanding tone of voice, a protective, distant, and non-insistent attitude, mysterious behavior, etc.
