12. When they leave

<!-- seg 13-0002 paragraph h=836ee5df -->
You must already have gone through the bitter experience of being caught by surprise by a sudden, unexpected, and unexplained abandonment or loss of interest on the part of some girlfriend. Then you found that all that remained were questions, doubts, unresolved issues: “Why did she suddenly want nothing more to do with me? Why did she change? What did I do wrong?” Then you must have imagined that you mistreated her or did some other stupid thing without realizing it. In short: you considered yourself to blame for losing her.

<!-- seg 13-0003 paragraph h=1f53b446 -->
Experience shows that these sudden losses of interest on the part of women are not random but follow a mechanical, automatic, and conditioned logical principle. There are psychological motives for the sudden and treacherous loss of interest: the sick desire that you suffer from the crisis of absence or, at least, that you remain longing for your beloved’s return.

<!-- seg 13-0004 paragraph h=66970490 -->
When the woman believes that you will regret her absence or will wait for her return, she will abandon you. The emotion of waiting does not need to be very intense, just enough for you to suffer a little from the unsatisfied expectation. The sly ones are lying in wait for the best moment to leave the relationship “on top,” as they say, that is, victorious in the war of passion. As long as your partner suspects that you will not feel her absence if she withdraws, she will stay by your side, insisting on trying to break down your defenses to bend you and induce you to fall in love.

<!-- seg 13-0005 paragraph h=db763197 -->
Therefore, the woman abandons you because she believes that you will suffer, and she stays by your side when she suspects that you want her to go, for all that matters to them is to contradict and frustrate; they want to do the opposite of what we want in order to hurt us.

<!-- seg 13-0006 paragraph h=946f2287 -->
Once she has really gone, there will be no alternative but to focus on the present, on other people and matters, getting involved with a new life. If you stay locked inside your house, feeling sorry for yourself and remembering the past, you will not arouse anyone’s pity, much less that of the cheat who abandoned you.

<!-- seg 13-0007 paragraph h=7f3b63e4 -->
I know a young man who let himself fall madly in love with a woman who enchanted him. She induced him to fall in love by behaving like a fairy out of our loveliest dreams, but in truth she was a terrible sorceress. When he was at the height of his passion, he brought her flowers. The fairy-sorceress then feigned surprise, pretending to be indignant, so as to have the pleasure of breaking his heart. Next, she showed up in front of him with another man, immensely superior to him in several respects. My friend was simple, poor, sensitive, and, on top of that, wore glasses. He felt lower than a worm and, in the following months, shut himself up in his room and did not come out, until one day his mother convinced him to forget everything. It seems he is better now, but I never saw him again. I hope he has been cured.

<!-- seg 13-0008 paragraph h=cc7e3c1a -->
When our hopes in love suddenly come to an end, as when we are abandoned, betrayed, or deceived by the woman to whom we devoted our best feelings, we become completely disoriented and destroyed. Life no longer has any meaning and, having no meaning, many even think of suicide (and unfortunately commit it). Other women become uninteresting and the whole world loses its charm. It is at that moment that we can lose ourselves for good or transform ourselves into something higher, for, as Nietzsche said, what does not kill us makes us stronger.
