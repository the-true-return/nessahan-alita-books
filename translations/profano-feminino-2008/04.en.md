3. The privileges

<!-- seg 04-0002 paragraph h=a35e3d92 -->
It is commonly said that females are poor victims of society, that they have been exploited and oppressed in every culture since prehistory and only in the twentieth century achieved emancipation, that men are their enemies, etc. Let us unmask this untruth as well.

<!-- seg 04-0003 paragraph h=8af1933d -->
All those studies claiming that women have always been poor victims of males are exclusionary and biased. Exclusionary because they do not take into account the privileges that have benefited women in the various eras. Biased because they do so on purpose. If we study the different societies, we will see that, in truth, what happens is exactly the opposite, as Van Creveld denounces. Men have always been assigned the worst, most difficult, heaviest, and most dangerous work. In situations of danger, as in natural catastrophes, males have the duty to protect women and children, and are therefore the last to gain access to safety and to the guarantees of life. Obviously, these rights of women and children are not wrong; they are sacred. What is wrong is to lie by saying that we oppress and exploit them when, in truth, we were born to protect them. If we ceased to exist, as some feminists would like, who would go into the fire to rescue the victims of blazes? Who would do the dangerous work on high towers and on the tops of buildings? Who would face the currents to rescue flood victims? Who would go into the underground sewers to unclog them? Who would carry the sacks of cement and other heavy loads? Who would unload the trucks? Who would slaughter the cattle so that the meat could go to the butcher shop?
<!-- QUERY: "Van Creveld" is presumably Martin van Creveld, the Israeli military historian; the argument matches his "Men, Women and War" (2001), later expanded in "The Privileged Sex" (2013). Not listed in the bibliography; kept as in the original. -->

<!-- seg 04-0004 paragraph h=9d51fed0 -->
We flatter and fawn over females in the hope of receiving crumbs of sex. The opposite has never happened. We compete fiercely among ourselves for them. Mobsters, celebrities, and the powerful do not want fame, money, and power just so they can sit and contemplate them... In the competition among males, which is socioeconomic, the winners get the most desirable women, the beautiful and perfect ones, while the losers content themselves with those the former turned down.

<!-- seg 04-0005 paragraph h=6ee1aa23 -->
All of this privileges females and not us. We do not see them killing themselves for a place in the shade of our protection. After we have slaughtered one another, they simply wait and settle into the places hierarchically reserved for them beside those who are left.

<!-- seg 04-0006 paragraph h=f9521db3 -->
Females manage to make certain privileges that they themselves provoke and incessantly seek look like sad sufferings so that, on the basis of this forged appearance, they can claim more privileges. E.g.: male polygamy (they themselves are the ones attracted to men who have many girlfriends), motherhood, staying at home and being supported by a rich man, etc.

<!-- seg 04-0007 paragraph h=ef7be208 -->
Privileges make them snobbish and sure of being desired. That is why they feel free to abuse male feelings. Besides, they believe themselves continually pursued and harassed. You need only stop and stare at one of these privileged beings and you will immediately be seen as a harasser or a flirt. And if you are poor, black, or of mixed race, it will be even worse. They never imagine that we might be finding them uninteresting. That is why we surprise and disconcert them when we ignore, avoid, and reject them.
