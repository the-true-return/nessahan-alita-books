About the author

<!-- seg 21-0002 paragraph h=9eda1988 -->
The author of this work IS NOT A PSYCHOLOGIST, NOR IS HE ANYONE’S MASTER. He FLATLY REFUSES DISCIPLES and does not want followers of any kind. He simply exercises his right to think freely about suffering in love and wishes people to think for themselves. His ideas were published to be discussed so that they might be improved critically, and they are not intended for immature, dogmatic, or extremist people. This is not a recipe book, nor is it a manual!

<!-- seg 21-0003 paragraph h=e945f4a9 -->
The author DISAPPROVES of the formation of any doctrinal groups based on his ideas, which he wishes to be only starting points for deeper reflections and bridges to other authors and other points of view. Being a free thinker and a true lover of the continuous construction of knowledge, he DOES NOT ALWAYS AGREE WITH HIMSELF and considers that many of his ideas may be wrong! His ideas were published to be questioned and discussed rather than praised or disparaged. They are just one more point of view among the possible ones (perspectivism).

<!-- seg 21-0004 paragraph h=2f6524fa -->
The author considers that all human beings have the right to philosophize, that is, to think in a critical, deep, careful, and concentrated way. All those who say they are his disciples are IMPOSTORS!

<!-- seg 21-0005 paragraph h=765953ee -->
There are no groups representing this author’s ideas anywhere in the world, virtual or otherwise. There are only groups with points of view similar to his in certain respects.

<!-- seg 21-0006 paragraph h=039ba91c -->
Those who are not capable of continually modifying their points of view should not meddle with deep thoughts!
