10. The strategy of attacking masculinity

<!-- seg 11-0002 paragraph h=adc39538 -->
When they lose a sexist debate to an experienced and determined chauvinist who relentlessly demolishes their arguments and resists all provocations, some defenders of feminist prejudices[^22] tend to resort to one last, extreme measure: attacking his masculinity by calling him a homosexual.

<!-- seg 11-0003 footnote n=22 h=804d0f83 -->
I am not referring to conscious feminists but rather to dogmatic and fundamentalist feminists.

<!-- seg 11-0004 paragraph h=4d9614b4 -->
This low and cowardly wile is the most extreme among the socially acceptable ones. It clearly indicates that the woman he is talking with has been cornered in the argument and is desperate. It is a cheap strategy that aims to strike the opponent’s self-esteem at its most vulnerable point: his masculinity.

<!-- seg 11-0005 paragraph h=6dd839ee -->
When, in a sexist argument, a woman calls a heterosexual man “gay,” she is only trying to hit him emotionally through shame, wounding his self-image to force him to back down. It is mere pretense: the sly one pretends really to believe it but remains fully aware that she is interacting with an authentic straight male. The proof that this awareness is preserved lies in the fact that she never dares to challenge him to prove that he likes sex with females.

<!-- seg 11-0006 paragraph h=9d90eceb -->
Generally, this manipulation strategy works and disconcerts even experienced debaters, because this really is one of our weakest points. Few things infuriate us straight men as much as such labels. Since our masculinity and heterosexuality are basic points in our lives, it follows that such provocations are very effective at causing anger, confusion, and disturbance.

<!-- seg 11-0007 paragraph h=29d8a183 -->
Therefore, we have to achieve psychic armor in this respect as well. Besides the armor, it is essential to have a battery of disconcerting, unmasking replies and reactions ready to pour out on the sly ones who dare to provoke us by this route.

<!-- seg 11-0008 paragraph h=7b431ec1 -->
The attack on heterosexuality is nothing more than a variation of the well-known manipulative wiles that aim to provoke specific emotional states by inducing beliefs.

<!-- seg 11-0009 paragraph h=e7a444f8 -->
This behavior is mechanical, recurrent, and predictable. Therefore, if you intend to engage in debates with feminists and their kind, be prepared beforehand. The prediction almost never fails: in the middle of the heated debate, inevitably one of them will resort to the low strategy of calling you gay (I am not condemning gays as people but merely pointing out the disconcerting effect of this wile on straight men) or saying that you do not like women, then cackling like a witch to try to disconcert you. The curious thing is that they are not ashamed of supplying proof of intellectual mediocrity with such an attitude.

<!-- seg 11-0010 paragraph h=be8f5c78 -->
By diverting an argument to the merely provocative-passionate level, such women supply irrefutable proof that they are irrational but, at the same time, they efficiently dodge the path of criticism that would expose their wiles. The adoption of low strategies is one more sign that they get flustered when they are obliged to deal with objectivity and with uncomfortable coherent reasoning. Our dear friends have difficulty dealing with the logical-rational but feel very much at ease in the field of emotional manipulations.

<!-- seg 11-0011 paragraph h=89187de0 -->
Experience shows that gender debates are fruitless because of the chaos that females install. Since the impossibility of an objective and impartial study is established beforehand and there is no hope of change, any attempt to the contrary will be a loss. Therefore, the solution is not to argue and simply to unmask the sly tricks unilaterally, keeping yourself armored against everything. Be deaf to the provocations, fallacious questioning, and trick questions. Simply ignore all the garbage they say. But be prepared for the outbursts of fury, the shouting, and the crying.
