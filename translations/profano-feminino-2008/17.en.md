16. Preserving the thin thread and moving forward

<!-- seg 17-0002 paragraph h=45f5049c -->
When we cannot bear to lose a woman because we consider her exaggeratedly important (which happens when the man is in love), it is not advisable to force her out of ambiguity. The best course in this case is to use the ambiguity in our favor.

<!-- seg 17-0003 paragraph h=46d3c11a -->
The positive and desirable aspect of ambiguous behavior constitutes a link that keeps us hooked, a door that preserves our hopes. Whatever the favorable signals may be (smiles, friendly behavior, etc.), they constitute an opening for approach (or they would not be favorable signals) and can be used as a path toward the progressive deepening of intimacy. In this case, we simply ignore the unfavorable signals, adopt an ideal masculine profile, and keep making our way through the openings with a certain naturalness. For that, it is enough to take advantage of the favorable signals whenever they are shown, going a little further with each one, but always concealing our real intentions perfectly[^24]. Let us take advantage of the openings, but let us not dare too much.

<!-- seg 17-0004 footnote n=24 h=69c0566e -->
Which is a demand of the woman herself, who will start avoiding us if we show too much interest.

<!-- seg 17-0005 paragraph h=ecb6fb87 -->
It does not seem right to me to destroy the thin thread if we do not really want to “move on to someone else.” It seems more sensible to me to strengthen it.

<!-- seg 17-0006 paragraph h=cab25963 -->
When we want to take advantage of the ambiguity instead of ending it, the right course is not to force a definition but, on the contrary, to take advantage of the openings (favorable signals) that are offered to us, however few and in however confused a context, choosing the qualitatively correct forms of approach, of making a move, and of insinuation, and avoiding the qualitatively wrong forms, which legitimize reactions of outrage and accusations of harassment.

<!-- seg 17-0007 paragraph h=79e5695a -->
Therefore, in the case we are now dealing with, the ambiguity must be allowed, because it contains within itself a “thin thread” that can sometimes be considered a hope of union to be fed and strengthened. For the unfortunate man in love who will not bear the pain of loss, adapting to the ambiguous behavior may be better than forcing a definition. Adapting to ambiguity is acting implicitly, “with a certain dose of hypocrisy, as if one were not thinking of it” (ha ha), as Eliphas Lévi would say. This is how we give back the hypocritical denial of intentions and the irritating undefined behavior, moving forward without admitting the obvious and denying the evident. The overall reading of female reactions (and not of what is said explicitly) will tell us whether we should move forward, stop, back off, or give up.

Preserving masculine severity almost all of the time will increase the chances of success in the undertaking of taking advantage of the favorable signals.

<!-- seg 17-0009 heading h=6f26c898 -->
Daring with prudence

<!-- seg 17-0010 paragraph h=e61dbef8 -->
The advances (insinuations) that aim to take advantage of the openings (favorable signals) require a certain dose of risk and daring combined with prudence and good sense. It is counterproductive to make a move indiscriminately and in just any way. There are several levels or degrees of intimacy, and skipping steps when deepening it may be inadvisable.

<!-- seg 17-0011 heading h=95aa69ef -->
The decisive moment

<!-- seg 17-0012 paragraph h=b0b00ca0 -->
If a man does not know how to identify and seize the specific moment of favorable opening for an immediate and daring insinuation, he may cause the woman to close herself off totally and definitively. This moment constitutes a “psychic standstill” in which female reactions freeze because of intense emotions, doubt, and uncertainty. Once the moment has passed, the fickle feminine being recovers her normal emotions and begins to see the man as someone uninteresting and dull. The “hypocritical” character (ha ha), that is, the denial of evident intentions, will protect the man during his insinuations, allowing him to back off if necessary. There must always be a good excuse for the contact and the insinuations.

<!-- seg 17-0013 paragraph h=cbb9706a -->
At the exact moment, which is not always easy to calculate, the man must embrace her and kiss her so as to disarm her completely, or he will cause a disappointment.
