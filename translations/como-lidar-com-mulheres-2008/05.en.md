<!-- seg 05-0001 heading h=1cbc6add -->
4. How to survive the difficult game of the magnetic forces of seduction that surround female[^1] cheats[^2]

<!-- seg 05-0002 footnote n=1 h=98a3ee35 -->
The expressions “female,” “human female,” “male,” “human male,” etc. are used in a biological and anthropological sense, just as Desmond Morris, Theodosius Dobzhansky (1968), and other authors use them. I understand that human beings belong to the animal kingdom and are part of the class of mammals (mammalia) and of the order of the hominids.

<!-- seg 05-0003 footnote n=2 h=d06b86c4 -->
Once again, I am referring only to romantic cheats and not to other women.

<!-- seg 05-0004 paragraph h=8c8f9be5 -->
1. Do not be possessive. Get the idea out of your head that she is yours, especially if she says she is faithful, that you are the best guy she has ever met, the only one, etc.

<!-- seg 05-0005 paragraph h=666d2eb3 -->
2. As long as you have no proof to the contrary, try to see her as a marvelous woman with many partners who does not admit it for fear of social repression but who needs a great friend who understands why she goes out with everybody.

<!-- seg 05-0006 paragraph h=ff178b2c -->
3. Do not fall into the temptation of seeing her as a heavenly being. Never believe in her faithfulness or that she flirts with no one but you[^3].

<!-- seg 05-0007 footnote n=3 h=3a3adc97 -->
For human beings of both sexes, including those of the female sex, are inherently unfaithful. Infidelity arises from an imbalance between the forces of the Id and the Superego, that is, between the impulses of the unconscious and the capacity of the (ordinary) ego to resist them.

<!-- seg 05-0008 paragraph h=f7ba5e0c -->
4. Be indifferent to her games of contrary and inconsistent attitudes.

<!-- seg 05-0009 paragraph h=bad6a95c -->
5. Kiss her ardently, as if you were feeling a great deal of feeling.

<!-- seg 05-0010 paragraph h=14dd657a -->
6. Get the worry about faithfulness out of your head. If she wants to put out for another guy, nobody is going to stop her.

<!-- seg 05-0011 paragraph h=794bae64 -->
7. Do not irritate or smother her with continual displays of love.

<!-- seg 05-0012 paragraph h=dbfce011 -->
8. Do not be a dependent crybaby screaming for his mother.

<!-- seg 05-0013 paragraph h=6449fecc -->
9. When she stands you up, accept the lying, flimsy excuses you get the next day and pretend you believed them, ignoring the matter, or else go to the other extreme and unmask her.

<!-- seg 05-0014 paragraph h=d91be30e -->
10. Never delude yourself into believing that you will find out what she feels by asking questions or having direct conversations about it.

<!-- seg 05-0015 paragraph h=81fcc83c -->
11. Be indifferent to the games of drawing near and pulling away that they play to drive us crazy.

<!-- seg 05-0016 paragraph h=08aca1e3 -->
12. Be a man and be prepared for the unexpected: being traded for another, being abandoned for good or for a while, being let down on dates, etc.

<!-- seg 05-0017 paragraph h=91abd19a -->
13. Do not become attached. Love her disinterestedly, even if from a distance.

<!-- seg 05-0018 paragraph h=4feba957 -->
14. Never forget that the cruel historical reaction of chauvinist culture to their wiles has forced them to mix truths with lies in everything they say[^4]. Never believe or disbelieve what they say: limit your conclusions to what you see.

<!-- seg 05-0019 footnote n=4 h=51b6b0dd -->
This characteristic is also present in men, but for other reasons and in other guises. I believe that there is, in every ordinary human being, a limit to the capacity to bear the truth, from which arises a limit to the capacity to express it.

<!-- seg 05-0020 paragraph h=45e38a37 -->
15. Write her love lines very rarely.

<!-- seg 05-0021 paragraph h=148325d8 -->
16. Achieve total emotional independence.

<!-- seg 05-0022 paragraph h=83e81409 -->
17. When you are compared with some other male, remember the points on which you are superior to the guy and forget the matter. Remember: although it may not seem so, in the long run she is the one who will have lost, not you.

<!-- seg 05-0023 paragraph h=1dbd8d1f -->
18. Consciously adopt a behavior that pleases her, but do not become conditioned.

<!-- seg 05-0024 paragraph h=6bd4efe1 -->
19. Melt into rare and false passionate declarations[^5].

<!-- seg 05-0025 footnote n=5 h=787a4157 -->
Which is legitimate because, remember, we are dealing with a woman who cheats in love and not with a sincere woman.

<!-- seg 05-0026 paragraph h=66387264 -->
20. Be firm and kind at the same time.

<!-- seg 05-0027 paragraph h=9ef9bc42 -->
21. Do not mind when she fails to keep her commitments to dates and phone calls.

<!-- seg 05-0028 paragraph h=dc467673 -->
22. Do not believe her when she promises to call or see you.

<!-- seg 05-0029 paragraph h=3598d794 -->
23. Be willing to lose her at any moment.

<!-- seg 05-0030 paragraph h=cbfdba97 -->
24. Do not see her as the only one.

<!-- seg 05-0031 paragraph h=7e2f417b -->
25. Do not try to impress her with your talents.

<!-- seg 05-0032 paragraph h=848ae6a6 -->
26. Do not show off your talents for no reason, but let her notice them little by little.

<!-- seg 05-0033 paragraph h=b6ba5679 -->
27. Do not chase after her all the time.

<!-- seg 05-0034 paragraph h=892fc4ce -->
28. Do not wonder whether or not she is going out with someone else.

<!-- seg 05-0035 paragraph h=abeba4a7 -->
29. Do not always be rude or ill-mannered in your ways and reactions; only a little, and now and then[^6].

<!-- seg 05-0036 footnote n=6 h=fba0b104 -->
However, we must never give in to provocation and attack the woman, because that takes away all right from our side. And once we no longer have right on our side, how can we complain or demand anything?

<!-- seg 05-0037 paragraph h=8bcfd874 -->
30. Do not be possessive[^7].

<!-- seg 05-0038 footnote n=7 h=5bf1fa4a -->
That is, do not be her owner or feel like her owner.

<!-- seg 05-0039 paragraph h=808c2df4 -->
31. Do not feel her as if she were yours.

<!-- seg 05-0040 paragraph h=0e9ecdb5 -->
32. Define the nature of the relationship solely on the basis of what behaviors and attitudes show.

<!-- seg 05-0041 paragraph h=f0835cff -->
33. Do not dive headfirst into the relationship, EVER!

<!-- seg 05-0042 paragraph h=dcaff039 -->
34. Do not be fascinated by smiles, glances, and passionate words, but behave as if you were a little fascinated, just a little[^8].

<!-- seg 05-0043 footnote n=8 h=84c2c089 -->
It is an emotional demand of their own. If they discover that they are not capable of fascinating a man, they become sad (ALBERONI, 1986/n.d.).

<!-- seg 05-0044 paragraph h=66aa2b69 -->
35. Do not chase after her or let yourself be drawn in. Be fascinating, so that she will chase after you.

<!-- seg 05-0045 paragraph h=b65748ec -->
36. To attract, combine in homeopathic doses seriousness, disinterest, loyalty, altruism, sincerity, minimal care for your appearance, eloquence, determination, economic independence, material independence (at least a house and a car), an image of a man pursued by women who does not boast about it (it can be false; just tell a very gossipy female friend that there are several beautiful women after you and ask her not to tell anyone, and she will take care of the rest...[^9]), virility, intense masculinity, sensitivity, kindness, level-headedness, and intelligence.

<!-- seg 05-0046 footnote n=9 h=2923e54c -->
Do not get furious, reader; it is just a joke...

<!-- seg 05-0047 paragraph h=a2d657fd -->
37. Detect the contradictions in her behavior.

<!-- seg 05-0048 paragraph h=4189ef11 -->
38. Do not expect good sense or understanding.

<!-- seg 05-0049 paragraph h=e6ee41dd -->
39. Resist negative feminine magnetism.

<!-- seg 05-0050 paragraph h=19f796b1 -->
40. Do not argue.

<!-- seg 05-0051 paragraph h=dbb4a611 -->
41. Do not cultivate conflict.

<!-- seg 05-0052 paragraph h=48a57d42 -->
42. Observe her “from outside” (without identification), trying to grasp her feelings.

<!-- seg 05-0053 paragraph h=9c2207c8 -->
43. Be silent; listen to her.

<!-- seg 05-0054 paragraph h=5e34c857 -->
44. Be distant, to give wings to mystery.

<!-- seg 05-0055 paragraph h=88b7d79a -->
45. Do not let what is going on inside you show.

<!-- seg 05-0056 paragraph h=234f7371 -->
46. Train her[^10] gradually, rewarding her for good behavior.

<!-- seg 05-0057 footnote n=10 h=52977455 -->
This is exactly the expression used by the feminist writer Karen Salmansohn (1994), who literally and explicitly recommends that women train men as if they were dogs.

<!-- seg 05-0058 paragraph h=4209927a -->
47. Let her steer the course of the conversations.

<!-- seg 05-0059 paragraph h=502e1ec9 -->
48. Encourage her to talk about what she likes most.

<!-- seg 05-0060 paragraph h=683daf11 -->
49. Always agree, except when she wants to be contradicted.

<!-- seg 05-0061 paragraph h=ff4bc97d -->
50. Exalt her imagination.

<!-- seg 05-0062 paragraph h=34634200 -->
51. Embody the principles of higher love.

<!-- seg 05-0063 paragraph h=9f1b5284 -->
52. Do not waver in your positions.

<!-- seg 05-0064 paragraph h=b0c980fc -->
53. Treat her like a girl.

<!-- seg 05-0065 paragraph h=96a40660 -->
54. Play with the binary, the alternation of opposites.

<!-- seg 05-0066 paragraph h=7c5c56cd -->
55. Hand back to her the responsibility for her acts, silly little games, etc.

<!-- seg 05-0067 paragraph h=ae9f3c80 -->
56. Do not speak in a pleading or begging tone, but in a commanding tone.

<!-- seg 05-0068 paragraph h=f5030f70 -->
57. Perform small romantic rituals now and then.

<!-- seg 05-0069 paragraph h=334198c7 -->
58. Be a mirror without giving her much of an opening.

<!-- seg 05-0070 paragraph h=a92a0fcb -->
59. Make her laugh only rarely.

<!-- seg 05-0071 paragraph h=3cb2bc02 -->
60. Point out her virtues when they show themselves.

<!-- seg 05-0072 paragraph h=4fea567e -->
61. Alternate severity with sweetness.

<!-- seg 05-0073 paragraph h=90eb2bba -->
62. Alternate silence with brief remarks that stimulate and calm her.

<!-- seg 05-0074 paragraph h=f5c86114 -->
63. Kiss her suddenly on the mouth.

<!-- seg 05-0075 paragraph h=b9519f47 -->
64. Tell her now and then that you love her (but not always).

<!-- seg 05-0076 paragraph h=76c04690 -->
65. Do not let yourself be possessed by a feeling of inferiority in relation to other men.

<!-- seg 05-0077 paragraph h=15f20776 -->
66. Agree with her mistaken behavioral tendencies and encourage them, pushing her in their direction[^11]. For example: when she wants to go out with an exaggerated neckline, say that the neckline is still too closed and that she should open it more; when she wears a very short skirt, say that it is long and should be shorter. Go with her to the extreme limit to find out what kind of woman you really have at your side. If she refuses and backs down, she is suitable for a more serious commitment.

<!-- seg 05-0078 footnote n=11 h=f8acaf97 -->
Therefore, never try to repress her. Any attempt to forbid or repress feminine behavior offers immediate grounds for effective victim-playing protests. You will be branded cruel, a dictator, an oppressor, etc. Do not give grounds; just hand back consequences. Make absolute adaptation to reality your personal goal.
