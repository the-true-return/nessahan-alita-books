Introduction

<!-- seg 01-0002 paragraph h=9bd2a8a9 -->
In this work I will portray the negative side, the dark and destructive face of the feminine, which, unfortunately, in these decadent present days corresponds to a good part of the women in existence. I will not address its divine and celestial side, which is equally real, but only the negative aspect, which must be overcome so that woman will voluntarily hand us the keys to paradise. Purely as a matter of focus, only this side will be criticized.

<!-- seg 01-0003 paragraph h=f0c6724c -->
Whoever opens this book must always keep in mind the fact that I am describing a specific type of woman—the sly romantic cheat—and that the characteristics pointed out are, most of the time, unconscious. The signs of this unconsciousness are women’s strong reactions of resistance against every attempt to communicate this reality to them: indignation, surprise, fury, or summary denial. In this book I am not concerned with sincere women, and everything I explain, detail, and describe is nothing more than one great hypothesis and nothing else. It is not an absolute and immutable truth that cannot be questioned or that it is forbidden to doubt. What I describe here is the feminine form taken by human characteristics that belong to both sexes. If I do not concern myself with the masculine form those characteristics take when they manifest, it is simply because that is not the goal of this book, and also because a great many books have already been written about it. I hope I will not have to repeat this a million times. I am already tired of stressing these points so much.

<!-- seg 01-0004 paragraph h=cba605f7 -->
Skill in dealing with the dark side of women consists in assimilating a body of knowledge that almost amounts to a science. I disagree with the thinkers who considered them incomprehensible.

<!-- seg 01-0005 paragraph h=df125f12 -->
Women are deliciously terrible, two-faced beings who ease our pains and, at the same time, make us suffer terribly. Sometimes they torment us with their contradictory games and inconsistencies, driving us mad. When we conquer them, they present us with the marvelous secrets and delights they reserve for the chosen few. They are not inherently evil; they are only human, like us.

<!-- seg 01-0006 paragraph h=753e3c39 -->
Since I have seen many men suffer at the hands of these delicious creatures, I decided to share the knowledge I acquired through hard experience.

<!-- seg 01-0007 paragraph h=bbbb429e -->
When I was young, I did not understand why certain philosophers and writers said that we needed to detach ourselves from women. I considered them unfair and disagreed. Today I understand them perfectly and agree with a good part of what Nietzsche, Schopenhauer, Kant, Eliphas Lévi, and other sages said. The warnings of the Church in the Middle Ages, of the Koran, of the Bible, and of other sacred books about these beings, at once marvelous and wicked, are not unfounded.

<!-- seg 01-0008 paragraph h=6a0f2249 -->
The game of passion is a battle of feelings in which woman tries to win by using man’s affective and sexual needs. The intention is to conquer our heart in order to command, in this way, the subservience that arises from the state of being in love.

<!-- seg 01-0009 paragraph h=9fd74f35 -->
The principles I point out apply in general to stable relationships between the sexes: to courtship, dating, and marriage, among other “modalities” (and they are therefore intended for adults only). The information was gathered from the works of respectable authors and from contact, observation, and personal experience. I can state nothing about what does not belong to the context I have experienced, since obviously I do not know all the women on Earth. In no way do I deny that the superior and the inferior coexist, or that there is a marvelous, sublime, and divine aspect in women. However, I suspect that, in these decadent times, there are not many who seek to fuse with their positive and superior part. This portion seems to have been banished to the unconscious[^1]. Many seem to identify with their sinister side, with the dark face clearly pointed out in mythologies, and that is what caught my attention. We can say that the blame for our suffering is ours alone, and the blame for their being this way is theirs alone. Other paths might exist if we were different... Unfortunately, humanity prefers evil. Our share of responsibility for suffering at their hands lies in the weakness of surrendering ourselves to the unbridled excess of our animal passions and to sentimentalism. Therefore, we do not have, nor should we have, anything against women, but rather against ourselves: against our naivety and ignorance in not seeing reality and in deluding ourselves.

<!-- seg 01-0010 footnote n=1 h=a96f0e9f -->
In the strictly romantic sphere, obviously.

<!-- seg 01-0011 paragraph h=02651201 -->
Basically, I set out to describe the feminine strategies for deceiving man in the romantic sphere and chaining him, the mistakes we usually make, and the ways of defending ourselves emotionally (in the cases where defense is legitimate and justified). I hope I have not shocked the reader by having, like Machiavelli, dealt only with things as they are and not with things as they ought to be. The reality of what we usually understand by love is not so beautiful and tends to differ from what we would like it to be.

<!-- seg 01-0012 paragraph h=caca40c2 -->
My intentions in preparing this work were: 1) to provide a model that would make the apparently contradictory behavior of women understandable; 2) to provide a body of knowledge that would allow men to protect themselves from emotional aggression and that would therefore have the effect of minimizing conflicts between the sexes[^2]; 3) to dismantle trickery, wiles, and cunning in love[^3]. It was not my intention simply to “badmouth” this or that gender. I do not curse women: I judge and condemn <b>their negative attitudes in the romantic sphere</b>, because I know that in the war of love, unfortunately, mercy does not seem to exist. As for their positive side, it will not be dealt with in this book, although it exists and is very important, simply because it would divert the focus of our interest. I did not create them; I only describe them as they appear to me, without masks or evasions. The complex and confusing feminine world needs to be approached in a raw, direct, realistic, and objective way in order to be understood. However, let the reader remember that this is only one more personal point of view and nothing beyond that. It is not a finished truth, unquestionable or beyond doubt; these are ideas put forward for discussion and continual improvement, not dogmas. The various discussions sparked by the previous editions made great progress possible and pointed out paths for going deeper. Criticism is always welcome.

<!-- seg 01-0013 footnote n=2 h=23eadaf9 -->
The reduction of intrapersonal conflicts has repercussions in the reduction of interpersonal conflicts between the sexes, which in turn will help to weaken violent behavior between couples.

<!-- seg 01-0014 footnote n=3 h=9a5bac1c -->
Which means that only women who fit the profile described here would have any reason to feel alluded to.

<!-- seg 01-0015 paragraph h=7b79543c -->
There are no arguments in this book in favor of negative sentimentalism. We argue against passion.

<!-- seg 01-0016 paragraph h=c261ba1e -->
I hope not to be mistaken for a mere extremist and dogmatic male chauvinist[^4]. Nor do I recommend resentment, promiscuity, or polygamy. The real man does not need to cheat; he does not need several women, because he is capable of conquering a woman who completes him, of wresting from her everything he needs in order to be faithful. Promiscuous men seem weak to me, unable to bear the torments of a single wife without resorting to other lovers as crutches. If you need several lovers, this may indicate that you are incapable of wresting satisfaction from just one. The superior male turns his companion into wife, lover, and girlfriend at the same time, leaving her no other way out but to become a superwoman—sincere, complete, and perfect—or to decide to end the relationship.

<!-- seg 01-0017 footnote n=4 h=86437e27 -->
Enlightened male chauvinists are totally different from dogmatic male chauvinists. The latter were responsible for various distortions of my texts. When they came across my playful and ironic language, whose only intention was to lighten the description of a painful reality by minimizing the impact of its tragic nature, they believed they had found a writer who would back their absurd and traumatized views. A misogynistic male chauvinist and an androphobic-misandrist feminist are, deep down, identical and fall into the same errors: they practice intellectual and gender intolerance, besides adopting a one-sided, fixed, and uncritical stance. I have never written for these people.

<!-- seg 01-0018 paragraph h=8af809a8 -->
This is not a seduction manual, but rather a philosophical reflection on living together and on the power of the (adult) man over himself. It is a good-humored essay, though at times it gives free rein to venting, about feminine behavior and about masculine self-power. If at certain moments I provide strategic information about courtship, I do so simply to help those who have difficulty finding or keeping a suitable companion, since women often have an inverted system of values that leads them to prefer the worst men, a fact that harms them.

<!-- seg 01-0019 paragraph h=9831c81e -->
Ultimately, we suffer through our own fault and not through theirs. What weakens, destroys, subjugates, and annihilates us are our own desires and feelings. Woman simply takes advantage of them, using them as tools to strike at us. Hence the solution is to fight ourselves, “dissolving ourselves” psychically through the death of the egos, instead of trying to force women to fit the standards we desire. I am radically opposed to each and every form of mental manipulation of one’s neighbor. Instead of manipulating others, it is better for us to learn to manipulate ourselves.

<!-- seg 01-0020 paragraph h=f52623f2 -->
People of both sexes behave in a mechanical and conditioned way, and those capable of rebelling against themselves to the point of escaping animal patterns of conduct entirely are very rare. Therefore, unfortunately, there do not seem to be many women on Earth who show that they depart very far from the behavioral profile pointed out here.

<!-- seg 01-0021 paragraph h=f6483c9e -->
The ideas developed here DO NOT APPLY to any sphere other than that of ROMANTIC relationships between adult heterosexual men and women. They are permanently under construction, undergoing readjustments and modifications as the discussions evolve and the facts reveal new truths to us[^5]. They are not a mere set of inductive conclusions (generalizations from a few particular cases). I preferred to take the path of deduction, drawing provisional conclusions from inferences based on premises that are socially accepted, verifiable by common experience, or defended by authors I have always admired and who strongly influenced my worldview. They are not scientific hypotheses but hypotheses of a more philosophical kind, of spiritualist and religious inspiration. The concepts adopted in building the model and the conclusions, always provisional, were and will continue to be formed <i>a posteriori</i> (post-concepts) and not <i>a priori</i> (pre-concepts). Remember that prejudices are nothing more than harmful, hostile, fixed, and immutable pre-concepts. Prejudice is entirely distinct from criticism. The latter aims to point out and denounce errors; the former aims to harm.

And it may even turn out that one day the entire hypothesis is abandoned, if reality so demands.

<!-- seg 01-0023 paragraph h=f6a88553 -->
The reader should bear in mind that I am not an adherent of rationalism and that, when I criticize feminine rationality, I do so from the point of view of someone who considers emotional intelligence and intuition superior to the linear and cold rational intellect, which is typically masculine.

<!-- seg 01-0024 paragraph h=10215cc0 -->
This book is intended only for mature people who are in, or wish to be in, stable relationships (and therefore for adults). It is meant only for people who think for themselves. If you are one of those who go around looking for leaders to tell them what to do, masters who gather groups of fanatics, strategies for manipulating others, etc., throw this book in the trash, because the message is not for you.

<!-- seg 01-0025 paragraph h=115f6c68 -->
This work DOES NOT SUGGEST the manipulation of beliefs, but rather real (not simulated) behavioral changes in the man, which have the effect of altering the woman’s beliefs and opinions about him. The change in behavior arises from inner changes, in the soul, and its expected effect is to reduce the incidence of negative feelings and romantic conflicts between the two sexes through a change in the masculine stance. This book, therefore, is entirely focused on the inner state of the man, and that is how it needs to be read.
