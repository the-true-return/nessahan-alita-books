14. Why It Is Necessary to Hide Our Feelings and Our Conduct

<!-- seg 15-0002 paragraph h=13af5d0d -->
Women are imaginative and intuitive beings, very little rational, who are guided by feelings and not by logic or reason.[^1] Thus, they show little resistance to the truth and need to live in illusion and lies (SCHOPENHAUER, 2004). This is proper to the feminine nature.[^2]

<!-- seg 15-0003 footnote n=1 h=c7bc6fd2 -->
This does not imply inferiority but only difference. As Kant (1993/1764) says, they have a greater vocation for the beautiful than for the sublime. What defines beauty is agreeableness to the feelings. Exhaustive logical work is aggressive to femininity. When a woman becomes excessively rational and cerebral, she stops being attractive to men. Human males are not sexually attracted by a logical brain (in the rational sense of the word), because its owner does not provide them with the pleasant sensation provided by a woman whose brain makes her delicate, sweet, and intuitive (KANT, 1993/1764). If a woman wants to attract men, she should differentiate herself from them as much as possible, becoming as feminine as possible. Her thoughts, feelings, movements, facial expressions, tones of voice, clothing, etc., should be typical of a woman, and this advice should help those who do not fit the dictatorial stereotypes of beauty. This feminine typification of conduct has two poles: the positive and the negative. The feminine world is the world of light and pleasant things and not that of logical things, which are exhausting and heavy for the mind. For a woman, “wrong” or “bad” is whatever causes unpleasant feelings. This does not mean that they are illogical in the broad and absolute sense of the word, but only in its usual sense, which implies linear rationality. In the common sense of the word logic, that is, of logic as linear, focal, heavy, exhausting, and rigorous mental processes, women are illogical. However, in the emotional sense of the word logic, meaning the coherent chaining of feelings in relation to certain ends (not always altruistic ones), they are entirely logical and coherent. In other words, women are logical in an emotional sense and not in the common rational sense. Being rational is not synonymous with being intelligent (GOLEMAN, 1997). Emotional intelligence is much faster than rational intelligence in solving its problems and penetrates fields impenetrable to the intellect. Feminine illogicality disconcerts and confuses the masculine intellect, which is illogical from the emotional point of view, and brings them successive victories in the war of passion. Considering woman as different from man and more inclined to the emotional, the beautiful, and the pleasant than to the rational and the logical (in the usual sense of the word) contains no idea of inferiority except for those who mistakenly deify the intellect as the means of cognition par excellence. Intellect and intelligence are not identical. There are people who are extremely intellectual and, at the same time, stupid, incapable of finding solutions to simple problems of real life. I once knew a great scholar, one of those who seem like living libraries, who was highly limited in intelligence, being incapable of grasping obvious and simple everyday things. Therefore, any accusation of prejudice that may be imputed to this point of view will in fact be the reflection of the prejudice that the accuser himself carries within and projects, most likely in a victimist form. Likewise, the accusation of misogyny imputed to Kant is totally unfounded and is nothing more than a fallacious wile to spare the feminine profile from realistic, incisive, direct, and absolutely sincere philosophical criticism, a decoy to prevent dialectical reflection about the feminine. There are light and non-rational forms of intelligence. The statistical data presented by Van Creveld (SCHELP, 2006), which show men with a higher IQ than women, were most likely gathered taking into consideration only the usual rational intelligence. Put another way, so as not to scandalize so much, we could say that feminine rationality and logic are paradoxical, in the sense given to this word by Fromm (1976). In fact, what modern Western civilization considers “irrational” and “illogical” corresponds simply to forms of reasoning and logic that are nonlinear, nonfocal, and do not exclude opposites. While the masculine mind excludes, focuses, penetrates, and deepens, the feminine mind encompasses and articulates.

And of the masculine nature too. However, here we are dealing with the feminine form in which the human tendency, which seems to be universal, to lie and not to bear reality expresses itself.

They cannot bear raw reality, and they despair or become enraged when we are absolutely direct, unmasking them, but at the same time, curiously, they admire us for such qualities, because they are highly contradictory in themselves and in relation to their own opinions.[^3]

When we excite and exalt their imaginations in the right directions, we can dominate them.[^4] But if we are not strong enough, we will be the ones dominated. Therein lies the danger and the need not to fall passionately in love. The tendency toward vehement denial of reality creates a hell in the masculine mind because we males are logical (from the point of view of what the Western mentality considers to be “logical,” that is, linear causal rationality that excludes opposites). Therefore, the desire to always know the truth about the woman (whom she goes around with and what she does when she is away from us, what she really feels, etc.) is a weakness.

<!-- seg 15-0008 footnote n=4 h=d4800a29 -->
By leading them in the direction of their own feelings and desires. As has already been explained, this is not selfish coercive domination but the guiding of pre-existing feminine desires, without any violation of free will. Why the word “dominate”? Because what the woman desires can become our goal too when we destroy the I’s, although without the previous libidinal conditioning. When we no longer have desires, feminine desires are accepted by us without resistance and even come to be part of our objectives. This implies a dominance because, from that moment on, what the other party wants to do is exactly what we want her to do. It is a dominance that reflects the mastery of oneself and the renunciation of the domination of the other. Although it seems contradictory, when we leave a person absolutely free to do whatever she wants and prefer to change ourselves, dissolving our desires in relation to her, we are exercising a dominance, in the sense that we are in total control of the situation and that we are allowing, and even encouraging, the other person to be led by her own desires. The death of the egos makes a man free of volitional conditionings and develops an extreme capacity for adaptation. Since, after this death, there is no opposition or conflict between their desires and ours, because we no longer have desires to conflict, it then follows that feminine desires come to be accepted without resistance on our part. Therefore, what the woman will desire to do will coincide completely with what we accept, and even desire, that she do. However, since we will be free of volitional conditioning and the partner will not, she will be conditioned to do what she desires while we will be unconditioned. Our act of approving and accepting the feminine conduct that was once undesirable, and is now acceptable, will be a conscious and voluntary act. The final result is that we will be exercising a dominance over the situation involving the partner, and through her own desires, without in any way violating her freedom. Let us imagine that a man has a strong desire for his wife to walk to the left, although her desire is to head to the right. If this man dissolves his desire, he will no longer resist his companion’s tendency to go to the right. If this desire, which is a defect, is really dissolved, the movement to the right will be not only accepted but, depending on the degree of dissolution of the I in question, even encouraged. The man will be free of volitional conditionings and will be able to make the woman’s acts converge with his determinations and decisions. He will not try to modify or repress his partner’s acts but rather his own determinations and decisions, by means of the dissolution of his defects. From then on, there will be a masculine dominance, because the partner’s act and the man’s will are turned in the same direction. The free masculine will can be employed in the same direction toward which feminine behaviors tend. The dominance in such cases is said to be masculine, and not feminine, simply because the one who will be volitionally unconditioned is the man and not the woman. If the opposite occurs, that is, if the woman dissolves her desires and the man does not, the dominance will be feminine, because the one who masters himself is the one who has the best chance of controlling the situation. Observing everyday situations, it seems to me that women put up with men’s undesirable behaviors and dominate them more than the other way around. Men are dominated and dragged by them in every direction, hooked by their own desires like a fish on a hook.

Through contradictory attitudes and words, the sly ones prevent us from taking definite positions in the relationship, yet they ceaselessly demand such positions from us, accusing us of being indecisive, insecure, etc. Younger men generally fall into these traps and suffer a great deal. Since they never let us know what they feel and what they do when they are out of our sight, the only alternative left to us is to consider them “tramps[^7]” and liars until they prove otherwise, if they are able to.

The sly ones hide how much they really need us and reveal it only in extreme situations, and even then as little as possible, to preserve their dissimulations. The reason is that whoever hides his emotions leaves the other without a reference point for behaving in a way that would dominate him.[^8] In love relationships, our behavior is defined by the other’s feelings. That is why these women reveal how much they need us only in extreme situations, under the real imminence of losing us, or when they feel that we are inaccessible. Paradoxically, they return to their initial haughty indifference when we give ourselves over after they have revealed themselves. Love, sex, and affection will be offered only as long as we do not give them much importance, receiving them as something natural that is obviously owed to us, without identifying ourselves. The reason for this is that they are tools of dominance,[^9] that is, offering them is absolutely hypocritical and aims to domesticate us, tame us, subdue us, weaken us, and soften us by means of passion, so as to induce us to reveal what we feel. That is why they are offered only to the good-for-nothings[^10] or to the superior men[^11] who have eliminated from their souls all the shadows of passionate love, attachment, and sentimentalism.
