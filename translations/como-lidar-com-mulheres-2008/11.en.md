10. Alternation

<!-- seg 11-0002 paragraph h=050fbc7e -->
The relationship should never become polarized in continuous coldness or continuous affection.

<!-- seg 11-0003 paragraph h=5c09fc76 -->
We have to be indifferent and, at the same time, ardently romantic.

<!-- seg 11-0004 paragraph h=7bae7c75 -->
The exclusively affectionate man becomes repulsive, and the woman comes to consider him clingy. On the other hand, prolonged distance and indifference cool the relationship[^1]. Consequently, we have to alternate our conduct, leaving her confused, not knowing what we really feel, exactly as she does with us. Cultivate the coldness of Zen Buddhism combined with the warmth of the Kama Sutra.

<!-- seg 11-0005 footnote n=1 h=f8936921 -->
“The perpetuity of caresses soon breeds satiety, boredom, antipathy, just as a constant coldness or severity gradually distances and destroys affection” (LÉVI, 2001, p. 226).
<!-- QUERY: Lévi, translated from the author's Portuguese; compare Waite's public-domain "Transcendental Magic". The original misspells the author as "LËVI"; corrected. -->

<!-- seg 11-0006 paragraph h=96ef364c -->
We have to surpass the woman[^2] in her opposite, bipolar tendencies. We have to steer the relationship and manage feminine feelings as they appear, instead of trying to change them or subdue them.

<!-- seg 11-0007 footnote n=2 h=ca565839 -->
I mean the competition for self-mastery already explained.

<!-- seg 11-0008 paragraph h=895ee2d8 -->
Because they know emotional mechanisms well, women tend to play games of alternation (LÉVI, 1855/2001). These are games that vary greatly in form and are marked by oscillation between opposites: they draw near and then pull away, they behave as if they were faithful and then admire other males, etc.

<!-- seg 11-0009 paragraph h=11a5a68d -->
The best way to tear apart these odious emotional games with opposites consists in “pushing” the woman precisely in the unexpected direction[^3]. The responsibility and the blame that are hers, and that she tries to transfer to us, must be handed back very amicably.

<!-- seg 11-0010 footnote n=3 h=a8248446 -->
That is, toward what she is trying to do, toward the undesirable attitude or behavior she is trying to adopt. We will not, therefore, be forcing feminine free will by adopting this posture.

<!-- seg 11-0011 paragraph h=96417368 -->
Example: when a woman makes a complimentary remark about another man in front of her husband or boyfriend, or spends an hour talking to some nice-guy-looking imbecile at a party, she generally expects her husband to react with jealousy and suffer, giving her satisfaction[^4]. If the husband, on the contrary, forces (with real actions) her to get closer to the guy, he will have two advantages:

<!-- seg 11-0012 footnote n=4 h=7c0203a5 -->
The satisfaction that comes from jealousy is due to the realization that the husband suffers from the feeling of attachment and that he still feels for the woman the emotional love of romantic passion.

<!-- seg 11-0013 paragraph h=1b2ef283 -->
1) he will find out whether the woman is faithful or is really the “tramp[^5]” she is showing herself to be, since she keeps giving another male the time of day and does not admit it[^6];

<!-- seg 11-0014 footnote n=5 h=c5a1eab4 -->
In the sense given by the Michaelis (1995) and Aurélio (FERREIRA, 1995) dictionaries.

<!-- seg 11-0015 footnote n=6 h=9c208e28 -->
In general, the sly one totally denies any possibility of involvement with the “jerk” to whom she devotes her exclusive attention, but deep down she knows very well the implications of giving a great deal of attention to a sexually active adult heterosexual male. Although she feigns and claims ignorance, she is not unaware that, if she were to get naked, he would jump on her.

<!-- seg 11-0016 paragraph h=485ade9f -->
2) he will leave her disoriented.

<!-- seg 11-0017 paragraph h=b1a766d3 -->
Here, then, is one more good reason to eliminate jealousy. Jealousy, the baleful consequence of being in love, is an important tool in the games of alternation they play to torture us and drive us crazy.

<!-- seg 11-0018 paragraph h=4b07c586 -->
The sly woman does not want to take responsibility for her attitudes. She wants a “serious commitment” but does not want to give up her “little friends,” she wants to have male friends but does not want to be treated as a “tramp[^7],” she wants to hang out with unhappily married girlfriends or ones of doubtful conduct, etc. Therefore, we have to develop mechanisms to force them to take on the consequences of what they do. Obviously, we have nothing against prostitutes or the like (and we even hold them in special esteem!!!!), but rather against the attitudes of other women who act in bad faith and play with our feelings, feigning faithfulness of feeling without giving it, letting us build false expectations. It is these who deserve no pity. The problem focused on here lies in the lack of sincerity in toying with other people’s feelings while trying to dodge the consequences, and not in opting for a multiplicity of partners, nor in the choices people make for their lives. To reinforce the point: prostitutes are not “tramps” in the sense we are dealing with here, because they seem to be the most sincere women there are, since they do not try to deceive us by pretending to be prudish, and, besides, they keep busy full-time. Prostitutes do not live in idleness. In Ancient Greece there were even professional courtesans, the hetaerae, who cultivated physical beauty and psychological refinement, rising above ordinary women (JOHNSON, 1987).

<!-- seg 11-0019 footnote n=7 h=c5a1eab4 -->
In the sense given by the Michaelis (1995) and Aurélio (FERREIRA, 1995) dictionaries.

<!-- seg 11-0020 paragraph h=ac260d9a -->
To continue... Do not feed the illusion of discovering through questions what they really feel for you, or that it might be confessed. You only find out what goes on in these women’s hearts in extreme situations. Give no importance to anything they say, for their useless words are contradictory, vague, deceptive, and incoherent, serving only to mislead. The degree of her emotional dependence on you will only be revealed by force, in an extreme situation such as, for example, total withdrawal on your part because of some serious mistake she has made. Hence the importance of being free of passion, so as to have the capacity to remain indifferent for a long time, if necessary. However, we should not become polarized in coldness, but rather alternate. Let us look at this more closely.

<!-- seg 11-0021 paragraph h=9ef3559d -->
In dealing with a woman, there are only two basic options:

<!-- seg 11-0022 paragraph h=fb7ef07e -->
1) to be cold, indifferent, and sometimes somewhat aggressive[^8];

<!-- seg 11-0023 footnote n=8 h=fa4c8cdc -->
I mean a slight aggressiveness in manner, typical of males, and not violent conduct.

<!-- seg 11-0024 paragraph h=cc02b32b -->
2) to be affectionate and kind.

<!-- seg 11-0025 paragraph h=b8c6182e -->
If we become polarized exclusively on either side, we will lose her. The ideal is to alternate according to the fluctuations of mood and the deliberate oscillations of the feminine little games: when your girlfriend’s behavior does not please you, give her the cold shoulder and ignore her. You will then see her desperately trying to find out what is happening. Do not reveal it, or you will lose dominance of the situation. Find a way to show her that she is being rejected for her bad conduct, and hold out until the change happens the way you want[^9]. Then reward her with lots of affection and little notes; be a friend, understanding and protective, but stay on the lookout, on alert, because the problem will soon return. Train her[^10] in this way, little by little, but change the pattern now and then so as not to become predictable, or you will be the one dominated.

<!-- seg 11-0026 footnote n=9 h=c71ea0f2 -->
Provided this is fair, obviously.

<!-- seg 11-0027 footnote n=10 h=1f6cda92 -->
As Salmansohn (1994) recommends that women do with men.

<!-- seg 11-0028 paragraph h=587757e7 -->
When we are cold and distant, two possibilities open up: the woman becomes desperate[^11], growing insecure, or she forgets you for good. Either way, you will find out the real content of the feelings that were hidden behind the deceptive words. If she is really in love, she will not let you go; she will come after you. If she does not come, it is because she never loved you before and only wanted to string you along[^12]. Do not be afraid of the truth. Be cold without fear, but not continually indifferent.

<!-- seg 11-0029 footnote n=11 h=bfe393e2 -->
Because her manipulative wile has been dismantled.

<!-- seg 11-0030 footnote n=12 h=b67d5445 -->
Possibly because she had ulterior motives and was interested in other things (a car, money, showing off, having an emotional slave, etc.) and not in the man himself.

<!-- seg 11-0031 paragraph h=dd56b3c9 -->
When we are affectionate and attentive, two other possibilities likewise open up: the woman grows tired, considering us clingy, or she likes this protective affection and becomes dependent. If the lady becomes bored, it means she never gave you real importance and only saw you as a sucker. If she does not grow sick of it and does not avoid you, it is because she really is becoming dependent. Watch out for pretense. Do not always be affectionate; alternate to confuse her[^13].

<!-- seg 11-0032 footnote n=13 h=0f5d1f00 -->
As they do with us.

<!-- seg 11-0033 paragraph h=c7bba04c -->
Some females appreciate virile attitudes in males and provoke them to see them enraged and threatening (JUNG, 1991). I suggest that you not fall for it unless you want to feign a state of fury, because it is a form of test that confirms to them our degree of submission to their manipulations.

<!-- seg 11-0034 paragraph h=f96aac47 -->
Be unpredictable, offering love and affection at the most unexpected moments. Surprise her by calling when everything suggests you will not, but do it rarely, in a disconcerting way.

<!-- seg 11-0035 paragraph h=c00b7b00 -->
Be alert to perfect simulations of submission, passion, and surrender that conceal indifference. This is an originally feminine gift, but it can be developed by man to unthinkable levels, even surpassing the peak of feminine dissimulation. We can say that this is the supreme secret of seduction and dominance: to simulate perfectly an intense and submissive passion without really having this feeling. It is this power that gives females the ability to pass suddenly from one extreme to the other without the slightest disturbance, leaving us crazed in the middle of the confusion.

<!-- seg 11-0036 paragraph h=8b130c3a -->
The rite of enchantment strikes the “victim” full on when it is performed in a situation that makes it unexpected, because it is the opposite of the situations in which it would normally occur. An intense declaration of love made after days of coldness, distance, or hostility has more effect than if it is made during long romantic periods. The same holds for reproaches and “punishments.”

<!-- seg 11-0037 paragraph h=c8541b15 -->
The impact of a melting declaration of love will be more intense if it is preceded by a period of distance and coldness, and vice versa. Therefore, when your partner stubbornly refuses sex and affection, hold out. Wait until she comes looking for you. Then go to the opposite extreme, having sex intensely until she is exhausted, and pulling away right afterward.

<!-- seg 11-0038 paragraph h=ee6cf52a -->
The more exalted and intense the rite of enchantment (of love or of hatred), the more effective its power will be. However, the greater also will be the risk we run of being victimized by it, dragged along by the passion unleashed (LÉVI, 1855/2001). To intoxicate your female with love, you must feign being absolutely mad with passion, yet at the same time you must not really be so. The danger here lies in feigning the madness of passion and actually falling in love in the course of the simulation, which throws the would-be seducer into a ridiculous situation:

<!-- seg 11-0039 paragraph h=f469331a -->
“Thus the ignorant operator is always astonished to obtain results contrary to those he set out to achieve, for he does not know how to cross or alternate his action; he wishes to bewitch his enemy, and it is he himself who brings misfortune upon himself and falls ill; he wants to make himself loved, and he falls madly, miserably in love with women who mock him (...).” (LÉVI, 2001, p. 227, emphasis mine)
<!-- QUERY: Lévi, translated from the author's Portuguese; compare Waite's public-domain "Transcendental Magic". "LËVI" in the original corrected to LÉVI. The author's emphasis is not marked in the extracted text. -->

<!-- seg 11-0040 paragraph h=30f1ded4 -->
On the other hand, a fearsome man who softens his extreme severity by tempering it occasionally with acts of kindness, using it to protect the woman and give her security, becomes fascinating, for he will be alternating and crossing his action.

<!-- seg 11-0041 paragraph h=5b49fad7 -->
Alternation is possible only when we develop the opposite characteristics latent in our psyche and integrate them, achieving what Jung calls “conjunction” (conjunctio)[^14]. We all have opposite, complementary inner possibilities that need to be developed: delicacy and strength, fury and tranquility, coldness and ardor, etc. We need to be simultaneously good and bad, merciful and cruel, malleable and firm, using these characteristics according to the needs that arise[^15].

<!-- seg 11-0042 footnote n=14 h=c722d3f2 -->
Jung deals with this in his book “Mysterium Coniunctionis.”
<!-- QUERY: The original spells the title "Misterium Conjunctionis"; corrected to Jung's Latin title "Mysterium Coniunctionis" (also the title of the English edition). -->

<!-- seg 11-0043 footnote n=15 h=87c60c04 -->
I mean modulated furies, malices, and cruelties, consciously directed against the wrong things in life. What we normally call “evil” are animal instinctive forces displaced from civilized human reality because they are autonomous and are not under the control of consciousness. This maladjustment disappears when we assimilate them fully into consciousness. On this point, see Sanford (1988) and Nietzsche (1886/1998).
