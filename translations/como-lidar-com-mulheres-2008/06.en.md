5. On woman’s desire[^1]

<!-- seg 06-0002 footnote n=1 h=f1f26883 -->
This chapter refers to desires that are unconscious but that make themselves painfully felt in a man’s consciousness through their concrete effects. Once again, we must not generalize. The conclusions described here are limited to one more perspective on reality to be considered. I must remind the reader that the unconscious, in both sexes, is the source from which spring the nightmares of hell and the marvelous dreams of heaven.

<!-- seg 06-0003 paragraph h=b7d3474d -->
Feminine desire is something very controversial and disconcerting. Much confusion reigns about it. This is due mainly to the opposition between what is conscious and what is unconscious. This opposition leads women to say the opposite of what they feel and of what they are[^2]. One cannot discover the factors that bewitch and subdue them through questions, interviews, etc., because we will be deceived. Know that almost everything we hear the sly ones say about what they are looking for in a relationship is a lie and, moreover, is usually exactly the opposite of what they really desire. I will now set out what they try to hide and never admit[^3].

<!-- seg 06-0005 footnote n=3 h=1850b44d -->
However, this concealment is not always conscious. It seems to me that most of the time the woman herself denies these things to herself.

<!-- seg 06-0006 paragraph h=f14fe23a -->
Human sexuality is similar to that of horses, zebras, and wild asses. The females spontaneously go to the territory of a stallion, who settles near the best sources of food and water (material resources), and offer him their sex at will. The other, secondary males are forced to wander in bands made up only of individuals of the male sex, going without mating for years on end, until they manage to replace some stallion that has grown old. The females do not compete with one another and accept the stallion’s infidelity as natural (as happens with the fans of any famous artist, mobster, billionaire, or politician). The stallion can mate with any mare in his harem without the slightest problem as long as he is able to keep predators and harassing secondary males away. In other words: the men considered “alpha males” act like wild stallions, and the women who chase them act like their females[^4]. On the other hand, the men excluded by women’s selective criteria are like the rejected horses that never mate. Something very similar happens among lions, among gorillas, and among other animals.

<!-- seg 06-0007 footnote n=4 h=0ce577b9 -->
The comparison with other mammals seems inevitable to me. We can identify similarities in gender behavior among the various mammals, particularly between the primates and man. The same behavior described here among equines is attributed by DOBZHANSKY (1968) to the hominid ancestors of man. Dobzhansky further adds that, in these cases, the beta males stay on the margins of the group, waiting for the moment when they can attack the alpha male and dethrone him. A very similar hypothesis was defended by Freud (1913/1974).

Being man’s complement and opposite pole, woman has an inverse psychic structure.

We want as much sex as possible and try to have sex as long as we have any strength left, up to the last moment. For us, sex comes first and love second. For them, the opposite occurs: love comes first. But let this be well understood: most of the time, they do not want to give love; they only want to receive it, giving in return only the minimum necessary to keep us trapped by desire, by feeling, and by passion. They have a double desire. They desire the servitude of the weak and the protection of the strong. They want to dominate the feeble and needy in order to exploit them as husbands who raise their offspring, while at the same time they dream of winning the affection of the insensitive men who have harems and stand out in the male hierarchy. The weak, once imprisoned, receive sex, tenderness, and love in minimal quantities, just enough to keep them trapped.

<!-- seg 06-0010 paragraph h=cadb6ba1 -->
They do not love us as a simple automatic return for our love, that is, simply because we love or desire them. They desire our attractive characteristics and not our person in itself. This is explained by the fact that their needs go far beyond mating: they need to raise and protect their offspring. Hence they do not miss males in themselves, but only their attitudes in utilitarian contexts. We, on the contrary, love them in themselves, that is, directly, because our existential goal is to mate. We want to pass on our genes against the genes of others. We love them bodily, directly. We are loved indirectly, in terms of function and usefulness. Our absence is not felt outside a utilitarian context.

The feminine existential goal is the raising of offspring, which passes directly through the formation of the family. For us sex is an end, and for them it is a means, because the end is raising the young. In other words: feminine love is meant for the children and not for the males. They want the best male in the herd, the best breeder and protector: the winner, the rich one, the famous one, the one who stands out from the other males. In this respect, they do not differ from female monkeys, wild mares, and other females. Just as among certain herds of mammals and flocks of birds the leading males are preferred by the females for mating and the second-rate males are rejected, among human groups the most outstanding are the most desired. Heartthrobs, artists, idols, etc. are chased and adored because they stand out and not for what they are in themselves. So if you want to attract the attention of some woman who ignores you, you must be different from the imbeciles. First of all, you must <b>not</b> do what everybody does: chase them, try to attract attention, talk a lot, talk loudly, clown around, rush to please, harass, pressure, etc. Learn to impress without making noise or effort, as if you did not want to. Be more feared than loved[^5]. Impress her without fanfare, by paths contrary to those everyone treads. Approach without fear but with indifference, look her steadily in the eyes to intimidate[^6] and then give some protective order, ignore attractive parts of her body on display, disagree, attack her mistaken points of view, startle her, “horrify her”[^7] with your solid arguments, scandalize her, leave her emotionally defenseless[^8], and surprise her by protecting her with indifference. Do not fear approaching or losing. Take risks. Know how to measure out your exposure to loss masterfully. Bind her[^9]; make her think about you continually. Inhabit her thoughts and memories like a ghost,[^10] as she does with you. Do not try to cross the barriers by the paths everybody tries; break into the fortress through the passages left unguarded because idiots do not notice them. Know how to perceive the moment to approach and to withdraw, to show disinterest and interest, to rebuff and to welcome. Do not become mechanized in a pattern as if you were a robot. Above all, be self-assured and love yourself.
