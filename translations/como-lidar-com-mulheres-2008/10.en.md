9. On the impossibility of dominating the “weaker sex”

<!-- seg 10-0002 paragraph h=4b538b39 -->
Our dear and dangerous females tirelessly try to dominate the relationship in order to impose on us the patterns they desire, which concern the frequency, intensity, and quality of the dates, the phone calls, the sex, the affectionate treatment, the talk, etc. Whoever loves more, that is, needs the other’s love more, will give in and submit for fear of losing the beloved. Whoever loves less will come out victorious and will dominate the relationship.

<!-- seg 10-0003 paragraph h=5b6c8756 -->
The power to exercise dominance or to be dominated is closely bound up with physical beauty[^1], in the woman’s case, and with social prominence, in the man’s case, although not only with these elements.

<!-- seg 10-0004 footnote n=1 h=1ccaa374 -->
Although beauty is relative and varies in meaning from one culture to another and even from one man to another. Regardless of his concepts of beauty, a man will tend to weaken more for those who, by his criteria, seem beautiful to him. As far as I am concerned, I do not go along with the stereotyped criteria and standards of the twentieth century (youth, body shapes, etc.)

<!-- seg 10-0005 paragraph h=4d3ab1af -->
If you have a girlfriend or wife, you must already have noticed that she tends to resist almost everything you want, especially giving you sex exactly when you need it. This resistance is natural, and we should not protest, much less force anything. These are obstacles that her unconscious places before us to see whether we can overcome them and prove our masculine worth.

<!-- seg 10-0006 paragraph h=9bd8739d -->
Although they are never admitted or acknowledged by women, the resistances never cease, not even after decades of marriage. When they resist, women in truth want to be enchanted to the point of total emotional intoxication. They want us to break the resistance by throwing them into a state of madness so that they can no longer resist. If we do not, they consider us incompetent and in time give us a fine pair of horns, because they need intense and crazy emotions[^2]. This is the reason they try to dominate us instead of submitting passively.
<!-- QUERY: "colocar chifres" (to give someone horns) is the Portuguese idiom for cuckolding; kept the image ("a fine pair of horns"), which exists in older English. Alternative: "will cheat on us in style". -->

<!-- seg 10-0007 footnote n=2 h=39dc8e0c -->
Because they are emotionally oriented beings.

<!-- seg 10-0008 paragraph h=f0a33003 -->
Some ignorant men, desperate at being unable to exercise dominance over their women[^3], attack them physically or psychologically. This attitude is unnecessary, as we will see below.

<!-- seg 10-0009 footnote n=3 h=2ca33cac -->
I mean the consented domination that results naturally from the renunciation of coercive domination.

<!-- seg 10-0010 paragraph h=a40e08a5 -->
Woman has sophisticated psychological mechanisms for getting around any attempt at domination. They are refractory to domination. They resist continually, even against brute force[^4], and can only really be influenced by the dominance of an emotional strength superior to their own. Not all is lost...

<!-- seg 10-0011 footnote n=4 h=61ccef3c -->
Machiavelli’s old and well-known sentence (1513/1977; 1513/2001) portrays this impossibility: <i>“Fortune is a woman, and to master her one must beat her and strike her.”</i>. This is not a misogynistic or violent sentence, but a comparison between woman and fortune, based on the fact that the former does not let herself be dominated. The intention of the comparison, however, is to show that fortune can be mastered only when we beat it, just as ignorant men in Machiavelli’s time did with women, moved by desperate fury. Machiavelli is not recommending that one beat woman, but fortune. He uses woman in his sentence only as a comparison, with the aim of illustrating that woman does not let herself be dominated.
<!-- QUERY: The Prince, ch. 25, translated from the author's Portuguese. Public-domain English (W. K. Marriott, 1908): "fortune is a woman, and if you wish to keep her under it is necessary to beat and ill-use her". -->

<!-- seg 10-0012 paragraph h=bc873e10 -->
There is a very effective means of protecting ourselves and at the same time dominating the relationship without going mad: it consists in renouncing the attempt to dominate the female, choosing instead to dominate our own feelings of possessiveness, jealousy, and other weaknesses through the death of our egos. This seems contradictory, but it really works, because women are contradictory and illogical beings in essence, or rather, beings who follow a logic contrary to the one we imagine[^5].

<!-- seg 10-0013 footnote n=5 h=d2734c87 -->
This refers specifically to the romantic field and corresponds only to the formal (rational) sense of logic. However, feelings also have another logic, distinct from linear rational logic, which is the foundation of emotional intelligence and intuition.

<!-- seg 10-0014 paragraph h=ee9f8d23 -->
Eliphas Lévi (1855/2001) tells us that women chain us through our desires[^6]. The desires to be together, to receive sex, affection, love, etc., are weak points through which females take hold of males and bring them down. I add that, besides desires, they chain us through our feelings. Consequently, if we eliminate feelings and desires, we throw them into their own mental dungeons. The shot will backfire owing to the mirror effect, which throws the spell back onto the one who sent it. The woman will then fall into the very mental-emotional hell into which she tried to throw us[^7]. The reason is very simple: since the sly ones need, continually and madly, to confirm that we suffer for them, they are forced to face their own frustration when they find the opposite.

<!-- seg 10-0015 footnote n=6 h=3ef0ede2 -->
This is the well-known sentence already quoted: “Woman enchains thee through thy desires. Be master of thy desires and thou shalt enchain woman.” (LÉVI, 1855/2001, p. 73)

<!-- seg 10-0016 footnote n=7 h=61e381f2 -->
Which constitutes legitimate emotional self-defense and works only if the woman is a sly one who tries to cheat us in love. If the woman is honest and sincere in the relationship, there will be no spell to be returned, and she will not be affected emotionally by anything, for reason will be on her side.

<!-- seg 10-0017 paragraph h=c11179a9 -->
From the beginning of the relationship, we should take more care with ourselves, with what we feel, than with the woman. This does not mean that we have to treat her badly, coldly, etc., but only that we need to surpass her in the fields in which we are weak and she is strong[^8]. Jealousy, fury, possessiveness, etc. are weaknesses that are part of the ego and leave us dominated.

<!-- seg 10-0018 footnote n=8 h=ae408013 -->
By fighting against ourselves and not against them.

<!-- seg 10-0019 paragraph h=e18b2826 -->
Rather than dominating the opposite sex, it is better to dominate the relationship. But to dominate the relationship we have to dominate ourselves. Consequently, everything comes down to self-mastery. Woman cannot be dominated, not even by brute force. If you ask her for something, your request will be kindly refused or postponed indefinitely. If you give an order, she will test you to find out how far you are capable of going, curious to know to what extent the relationship is vulnerable; she will refuse to comply and will watch your reactions to make sure of your capacity to displease her, obtaining by this means important information about the depth of your attachment, your degree of emotional dependence. If you attack her physically, you will have to answer to the police or to her relatives, besides proving her right. Consequently, there is no way out except to armor yourself and retaliate emotionally, with justice and in legitimate self-defense. Never let her reach conclusions and know how much you need her.

<!-- seg 10-0020 paragraph h=fe0429b6 -->
Woman will offer her treasures to the one who defeats her in her own domains:

<!-- seg 10-0021 paragraph h=f5dc1fac -->
“Let woman be a plaything, pure and fine like the diamond, in which sparkle the virtues of a world that does not yet exist!

<!-- seg 10-0022 paragraph h=0416c4d1 -->
Let the radiance of a star sparkle in your love! Let your hope say: ‘May I be the mother of the superman!’ (...)

<!-- seg 10-0023 paragraph h=ba37a0e9 -->
Let your honor endure in your love! Woman generally understands little about honor. Let your honor be to love more than you are loved and never to come in second place.” (NIETZSCHE, 1884-1885/1985, emphasis mine)
<!-- QUERY: Thus Spoke Zarathustra, "Of Old and Young Women," translated from the author's Portuguese. Public-domain English: Thomas Common's translation. <!-- seg 10-0024 paragraph h=30f2d93a -->
She will not give her treasures, and will never love more than she is loved, unless she is defeated in her resistances. But she will not be defeated unless she fears something: the loss of a special and different man.

<!-- seg 10-0025 paragraph h=b474db44 -->
Women love the strong[^9] and despise the weak, submitting only to a power demonstrated and proven unequivocally in their own domains: feelings[^10]. One must defeat them in two opposite fields: that of coldness and that of affection. We have to surpass them in strength[^11] without letting ourselves be taken over by their weaknesses; that is, we need to be colder and more indifferent than they are toward us, but at the same time more affectionate and loving than they are toward us. Contradictory? Illogical? Yes! And effective! There is no other way out: be free of passion and, to a certain extent, theatrical. Obviously, you will not dominate her directly, by means of coercion, but if you reward her at the right moments with a certain affection, you may “tame” her through her own instincts, as is done with wild animals[^12]. When she behaves badly, disappears, does not call, avoids or postpones sex, gives attention to or is nice to another guy, etc., be indifferent, scorn her, and disappear within yourself. She will resist; resist as well, until you break the resistance. Then, when the female submits, reward her with affection and other trifles, little love letters, flowers, etc., returning right afterward to your distance. Never become polarized in distance or in affection; alternate.

In spirit.

<!-- seg 10-0027 footnote n=10 h=6277eb67 -->
To make it clearer: it is as if it were a competition in which each party tries to outdo the other in the capacity for self-mastery. Whoever manages to endure more and to overcome the inner emotional hell more deeply is the winner.

Once again I return the provocation of the writer Salmansohn (1994).

<!-- seg 10-0030 paragraph h=ea936a74 -->
When we treat them in a merely affectionate way, they become bratty, rebellious, and provocative instead of recognizing the value of such wonderful treatment. When we treat them with authority[^13], they become sweet and affectionate. From which one gathers that their provocations and brattiness are in truth requests for a firm masculine posture (JUNG, 1991), very little sentimental. The slightest opening or weakness is quickly perceived through animal instinct and exploited.

<!-- seg 10-0031 footnote n=13 h=934c9c5a -->
I mean protective and consented authority.

<!-- seg 10-0032 paragraph h=59d9da51 -->
If you are not willing to be strong[^14] and are not inwardly courageous, you had better give up being a male and turn into a butterfly... or else change your mind and resolve to acquire courage.

<!-- seg 10-0033 footnote n=14 h=d77c5abf -->
In Spirit.

<!-- seg 10-0034 paragraph h=577a760b -->
I see many guys thinking that women will fall in love with them out of pity alone. They believe it is enough to give them love and that, in this way, reciprocation will be automatic. They are lost.

<!-- seg 10-0035 paragraph h=6099ff52 -->
If you think that being a nice guy is enough to be appreciated... you are lost. Stick your head in the toilet and flush, for the good of future generations.

<!-- seg 10-0036 paragraph h=21d7e9a0 -->
The main masculine weakness I have seen is the fear of loss. From it derive jealousy, sadness, discomfort, and many fights.

<!-- seg 10-0037 paragraph h=e55314f2 -->
They constantly assess our limits and the degree of power they have over our will. They observe us and measure how far they can go. They play to the extreme. All with the intention of dominating the relationship and not being dominated.

<!-- seg 10-0038 paragraph h=3d8c0726 -->
If we really ignore these games, all they will have left are their own feelings. They will have played in vain and alone. They will feel lonely, afraid of losing us forever, and perhaps they will come to us without our needing to call them. But not even this is certain in the world of these enigmatic, absurd, and illogical beings[^15] (from the point of view we have learned).

<!-- seg 10-0039 footnote n=15 h=b218ec3c -->
I mean illogicality in the romantic field and in the logical-rational sense of the word.

<!-- seg 10-0040 paragraph h=c6bdbeb6 -->
The most curious and contradictory thing is that, although they resist domination as best they can, females give themselves only to the one who exercises dominance[^16], to the best.

<!-- seg 10-0041 footnote n=16 h=5b55d49f -->
I mean the consented, protective dominance resulting from the renunciation of coercive domination.

<!-- seg 10-0042 paragraph h=c6e20127 -->
Few things give a female as much pleasure as knowing that there is a male who suffers for her. Paradoxically, this same male is considered uninteresting and weak, not providing the strong emotions that leave them fascinated. At most, he will serve as a cuckolded husband. The greater the wretch’s suffering, the greater her satisfaction and, contradictorily, her lack of interest. That is why they feel no pity for those who commit suicide over a great heartbreak. The man who kills himself for love is communicating that he is a weakling, and with that his sacrifice becomes meaningless.

<!-- seg 10-0043 paragraph h=aad4d306 -->
Instead of killing ourselves or killing her, it is better to kill our feelings and desires. Then we will be able to treat them as they treated us or are treating us.

<!-- seg 10-0044 paragraph h=1617c974 -->
The capacity to treat a woman as she treats us allows us to act as if we were her mirror. Her behaviors, and not her words, will be the elements that govern the relationship.

<!-- seg 10-0045 paragraph h=f4a0b9f6 -->
A great masculine mistake is to believe what women say. Another great mistake is to be fascinated by their affection, tears, and fragility, believing that they are signs that their heart has been given to us. Here begins our perdition. Let her say as much as she likes that she loves you, let her cry buckets, and believe only the attitudes you witness. Above all, be guided by concrete behaviors and not by useless and deceptive words. Do not run after what they say, because you will be watched as you fall into this weakness.

<!-- seg 10-0046 paragraph h=a96e4f26 -->
The world of the women we deal with in this book is a pestilent den of lies, dissimulation, manipulation, and deceit. This holds for all of them, to a greater or lesser degree, and has its origin in a remote historical past. The room for sincerity with these females seems to be nil or almost nil. Consequently, we have to treat them according to these laws, to which they are accustomed.

<!-- seg 10-0047 paragraph h=78008b14 -->
To dominate the relationship, one must be superior to the woman in her strengths. One must have the cold blood to be more dissembling and more affectionate than they are with us. It is also advisable to conceal our previous history of relationships, as they do. In this way we become mysterious.

<!-- seg 10-0048 paragraph h=63db8817 -->
When we defeat them in their own domains, that is, in the fields of feelings and emotional intelligence, which are the fields in which women move about at ease, they give themselves to us spontaneously. They begin to see us as unique, the best, and to consider us fit to guide and command them.

<!-- seg 10-0049 paragraph h=f7cae5b0 -->
There are only two possible paths when we establish a prolonged relationship with a sly partner: to dominate her[^17] completely, establishing rules, or to leave her absolutely free to do whatever she wants, encouraging her to do everything she shows to be part of her tendency. It seems more advisable to try first a patriarchal relationship, with full powers, especially with regard to contact with other males[^18], and secondly, if she resists dominance, to go to the opposite extreme, releasing her into total freedom[^19]. In both cases we cannot be in love, nor even love[^20] the woman very much. The ideal is to like her just enough to bear her company[^21] and enjoy the benefits of sex. Love, in this sense[^22], is man’s worst debasement and a serious defect.

<!-- seg 10-0050 footnote n=17 h=7b310979 -->
Provided she consents or asks for it, showing this through her attitudes.

<!-- seg 10-0051 footnote n=18 h=c5424e84 -->
This is only an attempt, to check whether this dominant posture is what she wants and seeks in us.

<!-- seg 10-0052 footnote n=19 h=a0727248 -->
Thus meeting the newly discovered request.

<!-- seg 10-0053 footnote n=20 h=9401edbd -->
I mean emotional love and not conscious love.

<!-- seg 10-0054 footnote n=21 h=e7f090af -->
In time, it may happen that she becomes a companion as pleasant and important as a great friend or sister.

<!-- seg 10-0055 footnote n=22 h=6124349a -->
Emotional love or romantic passion.

<!-- seg 10-0056 paragraph h=c03e72f9 -->
Some women submit easily when we exercise a protective authority and let us guide their lives after they have tested and confirmed our firmness of purpose and security[^23]. Others, more refractory owing to feminist influences, tend to resist more, and there are some who definitely do not submit. These last should be pushed in the opposite direction, for they have no vocation whatsoever for the role of wives, nor even for being steady companions. They serve only for casual and superficial sex, having no other use in our love life. They were born for casual sex and are not advisable for a serious relationship[^24].

<!-- seg 10-0057 footnote n=23 h=98a57c83 -->
Because that was exactly what they were seeking in us.

<!-- seg 10-0058 footnote n=24 h=aed0d09c -->
This is the case of those who demand emotional commitment and fidelity from the man but offer, in return, only contradictory behaviors and doubts about their fidelity. Doubt causes an emotional irritation, and that is why we seek certainties (PEIRCE, 1887/n.d.).

<!-- seg 10-0059 paragraph h=b5c0e3da -->
What makes the sly ones so refractory and hard to control is the chaotic and unstable nature of their intense passions and feelings. Their moods change suddenly, without any prior warning. One moment they are mad with passion and, suddenly, they simply do not want to see our face anymore. Therefore, they are beings who cannot be trusted much. Their dispositions alternate continually and do not automatically correspond to our goals, which is why we have to take advantage of the moments when they are “open,” available, and susceptible to influences in order to work on their moods. When they are closed, we have to wait until they change.

<!-- seg 10-0060 paragraph h=cc627217 -->
Your girlfriend may be unpredictable, but she will try to induce you to become mechanical, expecting one behavioral pattern, so as to surprise you with other patterns, driving you crazy. Withstand the emotional storms. Be ready for anything. Do not let her contaminate your mind with absurd alternations of feelings. Stay centered and do not let yourself be dragged to either side.

<!-- seg 10-0061 paragraph h=7a70069b -->
Time is one of the greatest feminine allies. When you are resentful with just cause, when you keep your distance, your partner will patiently count on time for you to change. She will wait and wait, patiently, for your transformation. There is even a slang expression for this wile: “letting him stew.” It is another form of dominance over our mind.

<!-- seg 10-0062 paragraph h=5c85a9e7 -->
Never forbid anything. Prohibition stimulates disobedience[^25] and provides arguments in favor of a supposed arbitrary authoritarianism on your part. Instead of forbidding, leave the little she-devil with no way out by creating situations that turn the consequences of her undesirable attitudes back onto her own head. Communicate, unilaterally, decisions that affect her as a result of her own mistakes. Tie her up with her own ideas and attitudes.

<!-- seg 10-0063 footnote n=25 h=69960f63 -->
For prohibition has the effect of stimulating desire instead of containing it.
