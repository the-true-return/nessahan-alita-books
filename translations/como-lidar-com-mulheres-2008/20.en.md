<!-- seg 20-0002 paragraph h=07edf16d -->

<!-- seg 20-0003 paragraph h=944f634a -->

<!-- seg 20-0005 paragraph h=38cf36e7 -->
2) frustrating us afterward, not satisfying the desires they kindled or allowed us to kindle, justifying themselves theatrically with true facts and data, cunningly selected and mixed with false ones to make the lie convincing.

<!-- seg 20-0006 paragraph h=dad4e96b -->
Analyze any disturbing, conflicting, or disconcerting situation from the standpoint of this model and, if you look properly, you will be able to discover the two basic behavioral traits described above.

<!-- seg 20-0007 paragraph h=893ebe8d -->
The arousal of our various desires is not always explicit. Often it is merely a silent permission that, because of the context in which it occurs, tells us “yes.”

<!-- seg 20-0008 paragraph h=ea9f75e1 -->
The combination of these two traits has the effect of irritating us and driving us mad, making us passive elements of a hypnotic process in which we are dominated by various negative feelings. They provoke us and irritate us to our limits while we stand there like fools, at the mercy of these influences. In this way, they discover a great deal about our patterns, resistances, needs, desires, fears, and weaknesses, and they turn them into instruments in their own favor.
