13. The Impossibility of Negotiation

<!-- seg 14-0002 paragraph h=50ccafd0 -->
Sly women usually resist attempts at negotiation or steer them only in the directions that suit them. When the negotiation takes a course favorable to the man, they label him “intransigent” or “radical,” even if they are completely in the wrong in their demands.

<!-- seg 14-0003 paragraph h=10a08eff -->
Malleable men, who give in on unacceptable points, are seen as weak, indecisive, and manipulable. Regardless of what they say, these women will decide in favor of the one who stands firm in his point of view to the end and shows that he will not back down for anything, not even for fear of losing them. This is especially true in cases of “innocent friendships” with other men.

<!-- seg 14-0004 paragraph h=1daf4872 -->
If we are democratic, kind, malleable, etc., this will not be recognized or seen as a reason for gratitude but, on the contrary, as a weakness to be exploited, an opportunity to use the other as an emotional slave. The smallest openings will be quickly perceived. Moreover, we will be communicating that we are not capable of protecting or guiding anyone.

<!-- seg 14-0005 paragraph h=950b643c -->
The essence of what such females are is absolutely different from what they themselves say, which is why we must be guided only by their attitudes and never by their absurd, futile talk. Talk is one of their main mechanisms of deception in negotiations.

<!-- seg 14-0006 paragraph h=64cd9480 -->
Feminine feelings and intentions reveal themselves only in the extreme situations in which they are put to the test. Outside this sphere, everything will be confused, absurd, and contradictory. For these reasons, it is better to communicate conditions to them than to count on understanding.

<!-- seg 14-0007 paragraph h=98361082 -->
When the conditions for the relationship are communicated in an absolutely clear way, there is no way out for the woman. Whichever way she tries to move, she will be revealing herself. In this way we will discover whether she is a saint, a good wife, a mere sexual friend, or a deceiving “tramp[^1].”

<!-- seg 14-0008 footnote n=1 h=c5a1eab4 -->
In the sense given by the Michaelis (1995) and Aurélio (FERREIRA, 1995) dictionaries.

<!-- seg 14-0009 paragraph h=af8143c6 -->
The conditions need to be formulated in such a way that even the refusal to take a position and indifference have a clear and definite meaning. Since one of the greatest feminine weapons is contradiction, contradictory attitudes and the absence of attitudes also need to have a precise, clearly formulated meaning.

<!-- seg 14-0010 paragraph h=c231e777 -->
There is an immense difference between asking and stating decisively. The woman will not give up her bad habits (infrequent or poor-quality sex, friendly attitudes toward other men, etc.) just because you asked. She will only do so if she is told unequivocally that those attitudes will result, with no appeal, in the end of the relationship or in the ruin of her image. If you try to negotiate, she will perceive, with her diabolical sixth sense,[^2] a fear of losing her, and she will play with this fear to its extreme limit. Therefore, the way out is not to be afraid.

<!-- seg 14-0011 footnote n=2 h=65087f16 -->
In men, the diabolical character does not operate in such an intuitive way. In some cases, this feminine capacity to intuit or sense the man’s feelings, that is, whether he is suffering from love, whether he misses her and is anxious to see her, etc., even seems to overcome the barriers of space, bordering on the paranormal.

<!-- seg 14-0012 paragraph h=a0797a8e -->
But in order not to be afraid, one must not fall passionately in love. That is why the death of the ego[^3] is indispensable. He who is emotionally dependent will be incapable of imposing conditions without wavering. The woman, through instinct, will sense his weakness and will resist him until she bends him. The more we give in, the more we will have to give in, until we go completely mad.

<!-- seg 14-0013 footnote n=3 h=bc1e0d91 -->
By death of the Ego(s) we must understand the dissolution (assimilation) of the psychic aggregates or autonomous complexes. In a certain lecture, whose reference for citation I do not recall now, Jung stated that complexes are constituted by some kind of ego, and this is exactly what the V.M. Samael Aun Weor states. The usual Ego of psychology, the autonomous complexes of the unconscious, Freud’s Ego, Superego, and Id (1923/1997), and the so-called Alter Ego or Higher Self are all, deep down, merely different forms of Ego, which need to be dissolved so that the soul may be freed and its various one-sided, compulsive, and opposing impulses may be assimilated and fused. The egoic vision is a distortion of reality, because the multiple “I’s” subjectivize perceptions (SAMAEL AUN WEOR, n.d.). Through comprehension, we gradually correct the cognitive distortion inherent in each compulsively one-sided vision, thus making objective the vision we have of the object of desire or aversion.
