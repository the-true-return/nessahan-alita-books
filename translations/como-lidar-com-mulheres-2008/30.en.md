29. Appendices

<!-- seg 30-0002 paragraph h=f3c03817 -->
Note 1. What follows are an interview and some e-mail messages sent to friends. In this third online edition, I have replaced some words with synonyms for greater clarity and to prevent intentional distortions and biased interpretations by one-sided readers. All the observations contained here, like the others in the book, refer only to women who match the behavioral profile we are concerned with.

<!-- seg 30-0003 paragraph h=2047890d -->
Note 2. This interview has never been published outside this book. It was conducted by a feminist reader, at my suggestion, in order to clarify confusing points that arose during a chaotic online discussion. After many fruitless attempts to get a small group of women to understand my points of view, and since the confusion only kept growing, I asked them to send their objections in the form of questions. The interviewer’s identity has been kept confidential.

<!-- seg 30-0004 paragraph h=837fcc30 -->
Note 3. The letters here are, for the most part, replies to messages from various hostile feminists who wrote to me attacking my points of view. There are also replies to some people who asked for my opinions about the situations they were going through. The identity of the correspondents has been kept confidential in all cases.
