8. Why we should neither argue nor debate

<!-- seg 09-0002 paragraph h=b8e66f7f -->
Women tend to have many attitudes that harm their relationship with us. Among such attitudes, I can mention a taste for male friendships, the habit of admiring or praising other men, famous or not, etc. When we catch them red-handed, they flatly deny it and say it was all something innocent and without bad intentions, <i>“nothing malicious.”</i>

<!-- seg 09-0003 paragraph h=3b4738bf -->
Because they are based on feelings and not on reason, these undesirable feminine ideas and behaviors remain unscathed after we have intellectually destroyed their arguments.

<!-- seg 09-0004 paragraph h=f0b0e25c -->
In general, feminine arguments in favor of the attitudes that destroy the relationship are very flimsy. However, there is no point in arguing or debating, for even after they have been destroyed, their motives prevail because they are emotional. They then work out other psychological paths to justify their shady attitudes without ever owning up to them.

<!-- seg 09-0005 paragraph h=3d9f9340 -->
For these reasons, it is a total waste of time to argue or debate when we catch them in crooked tricks[^1]. This habit, which I see in many men, only creates an unpleasant atmosphere in the relationship and drives us mad, to feminine delight[^2].

<!-- seg 09-0006 footnote n=1 h=ca9dfb3b -->
The more a man tries to do violence to his companion’s free will, in the vain attempt to force her to change her behavior or admit her mistakes, the more strength he will be giving her. He will open himself to being accused of coercion, emotional violence, dictatorial behavior, etc. He will lose the moral high ground and be seen as a monster by himself, by the sly one, and by everyone who witnesses the conflict. As Esther Vilar (1972) wrote: “woman has the power that man gives her.” (my trans.). For this and other reasons, granting the woman total freedom is much more advisable from the standpoint of emotional defense. The most advisable course is for the man to leave her absolutely free to do whatever she wants, never forbidding anything, but handing back to her all the consequences and responsibilities that are hers. This demands total freedom from passion and total adaptability or, at least, at levels higher than those that correspond to the ordinary man, violent, passionate, and uncontrolled.
<!-- QUERY: Esther Vilar's "The Manipulated Man" (1972) was written in German; the author translated the line himself. Rendered from his Portuguese. -->

<!-- seg 09-0007 footnote n=2 h=3b106eb7 -->
Regarding this detail, Goleman (1997) tells us that women become distressed when men shut down, refusing to take part in conflictual debates, and that they calm down when men do take part. Still according to Goleman, men shut down as a defense mechanism against emotional flooding. When a man shuts down in a conflict situation, his heartbeat calms down but his wife’s speeds up. When he opens up to the argument, the wife’s heartbeat calms down and his speeds up. Thus an oscillating game of transmitting distress from one side to the other is formed. Therefore, the woman needs masculine participation in the conflict so that her heartbeat does not speed up. If she feels blocked, boycotted by the man who refuses to continue the conflict, she will be flooded by feelings of powerlessness, frustration, anger, etc. (GOLEMAN, 1997). Conclusion: they like to watch the circle catch fire and to know that we are going crazy. Let us deny them this morbid pleasure.

<!-- seg 09-0008 paragraph h=93c8099e -->
Instead of debating, it is better to take a radical and unexpected attitude that corners her and leaves her disconcerted about us. A very disconcerting attitude that works well is simply to accept the contrary and undesirable opinion. Accepting certain absurd opinions about fidelity, however, is very difficult in certain cases because it demands total freedom from passion. Experience has shown me that when we seriously encourage a woman who is flirting with another guy to go off with him, she becomes desperate if she was only trying to irritate us. This is a good form of revenge because, most of the time, the other one does not want her seriously, leaving her alone in the end, with no one, and we will be able to laugh. On the other hand, if the guy really wants her and accepts her, and she wants him too, this will only mean that you should have treated her as a “tramp[^3]” from the start, and that, if you considered her your girlfriend, the mistake was yours alone for not having perceived what kind of person you had at your side.

<!-- seg 09-0009 footnote n=3 h=c5a1eab4 -->
In the sense given by the Michaelis (1995) and Aurélio (FERREIRA, 1995) dictionaries.

<!-- seg 09-0010 paragraph h=f110a01d -->
This is the attitude least expected from a man and, precisely for that reason, the most disconcerting. In general, what is expected is that in such situations we will protest and fall into emotional upsets of various kinds. If, on the contrary, we encourage them to carry the absurd fantasy forward, they will be emotionally cornered[^4].

<!-- seg 09-0011 footnote n=4 h=e86283ea -->
All the forms of psychological cornering described in this work have only the effect of leading the woman of ambiguous behavior to reveal what she truly feels for us and her real intentions, and they are totally ineffective as a form of manipulation aimed at satisfying the man’s personal desires. Those who try to use them for this shady purpose will suffer the consequences of the shot that backfires and will land in ridiculous situations. They will be caught on their own hook and will unknowingly drink the poison they distilled. Since this is counter-manipulation (the dismantling of manipulative wiles) and not manipulation, its effectiveness is borne out only in cases of legitimate emotional defense, in which reason is on our side. In other cases it is ineffective, since woman is more skilled at manipulating feelings than man. Trying to outdo woman in the manipulative wiles of love is almost the same as demanding that they outdo us in physical strength, that is, an absurdity.

<!-- seg 09-0012 paragraph h=cb503c79 -->
However, so as not to be predictable, it is advisable now and then to go to the opposite extreme, implacably unmasking their disguises (without arguing, but only making confident, clear, direct, and conclusive observations) without the slightest fear of losing her and without wavering. For the unmasking to reach the feelings and have the desired effect, the words used must be extremely easy to understand, suited to little rational intelligence[^5], and at the same time absolutely exact, in order to bring about an unerring cornering. Be prepared, because in these cases feminine reactions tend to be violent, and you will need to be forewarned in order to hold things together with a female in a fit of madness for having been unmasked by force and suddenly feeling naked. But this will soon pass if you are the stronger and colder of the two and remain centered. Do not fear clamor, shouting, or crying. Do not be affected by storms of words. Stand firm and resolute in your position. The flow of energy you set off will soon be exhausted.

<!-- seg 09-0013 footnote n=5 h=941f4e3d -->
For discernment disappears in moments of flooding by negative emotions. This holds for both sexes. Trying to reason with a person who is possessed by negative feelings is a waste of time.

<!-- seg 09-0014 paragraph h=bd851439 -->
When faced with shady attitudes on your companion’s part that cast doubt on her fidelity, do not waste time arguing, but simply communicate, without wavering, what such acts mean to you and the consequences they will have. Do not try to negotiate or make her understand your point of view, because it will be useless, and on top of that you will be considered weak and unsure about your own goals in life. In general, a second chance can be given, provided the lapse was not serious.

<!-- seg 09-0015 paragraph h=22d5c182 -->
In matters of behavior that shakes our belief in fidelity, a very common mistake is to insist that our dear ones acknowledge their shady attitudes. We do it in the vain hope that they may understand our noble motives, expecting our point of view to be taken into account. This has the opposite effect and causes us to be seen as weak rather than democratic. On the other hand, we are seen as strong and resolute when we corner them by communicating unilaterally what we perceive and the measures we will take as a consequence, refusing to argue. However, if you bluff, promising a “punishment”[^6] without carrying it out, you are lost. Promise only what you can carry out.

<!-- seg 09-0016 footnote n=6 h=b8324086 -->
By “punishment,” understand the breaking off of the relationship or, at least, of the man’s commitment to fidelity.

<!-- seg 09-0017 paragraph h=4b29e7ad -->
What matters is to close all the exits. The door to stubbornness and resistance stays open when we argue. Render stubbornness useless by giving free rein to opinions contrary to yours. Regard other people’s mistaken opinions as a problem that is not yours[^7] but belongs to the very person who voices and defends them (and so do the consequences).

<!-- seg 09-0018 footnote n=7 h=422b82a6 -->
According to Freire (2000), the mature man accepts opinions contrary to his own and does not try to do violence to the other’s point of view; the capacity to accept divergence corresponds to the fourth stage in the maturing of consciousness.

<!-- seg 09-0019 paragraph h=9e37b543 -->
Obviously, you should not try to do this if you are in love, or you will fall headfirst into the abyss. The man in love is in a servile and miserable state, incapable of mastering the relationship. That is why women insistently try to induce us to surrender.

<!-- seg 09-0020 paragraph h=10c47fbb -->
Do not try to force her to be coherent, sensible, or logical. Accept her as she is, understand her, and adapt. Have no form; kill your egos. Observe her and take things as they are, without wishing they were different.

<!-- seg 09-0021 paragraph h=d41b3f1b -->
Our adorable companions are naturally conditioned to concealment, and that is why they are sometimes such liars[^8]. They do very well in roles that require the ability to hide, to dissemble. They need to feel that they are deceiving, and when they cannot hide anything from us, they become sad and depressed, feeling incompetent[^9]. But so it must be; let us not rebel. We have to adapt to their ambiguous languages, learning to find our way amid the chaos they create, instead of fighting, arguing, and debating.

<!-- seg 09-0022 footnote n=8 h=b028984d -->
I thus return the provocations of the authors who characterize the masculine gender as inherently mendacious.

<!-- seg 09-0023 footnote n=9 h=b9a1878f -->
Although they are not always aware of it. This anguish gestates at deep levels of the psyche and becomes visible in the form of feelings of vulnerability. It seems to me that the act of concealing gives them a sense of security, as if they were sheltered from something they fear. Most likely this fear of the masculine is the fear of the father figure pointed out so many times by Freud in his works.

<!-- seg 09-0024 paragraph h=37d63151 -->
The time and effort spent on arguments are lost, because we cannot reach them ahead of the facts. We reach them only with real facts in progress and never with warnings, alerts, pleas, etc. There are no emotional impacts[^10] <i>a priori</i>, only <i>a posteriori</i>.

<!-- seg 09-0025 footnote n=10 h=23edd924 -->
These impacts should not be understood as harm, much less as aggression, but as sensitizations that mobilize feelings.

<!-- seg 09-0026 paragraph h=3d62ebab -->
The only case in which an argument can be considered useful is when it is taken as an opportunity for our psychological training. We can develop resistance if we gradually expose ourselves to the deleterious hypnotic influences of the formidable and fatal feminine magnetism. In an argument[^11], the battle does not take place on the rational plane, as it seems at first sight, but on the emotional plane. Be much colder, more incisive, more direct, more aggressive[^12], more curt, and blunter than your opponent in order to exercise dominance[^13], or you will be the one dominated. Do not argue: communicate, running over, trampling, and crushing every fascinating influence[^14]. Look her in the eyes. At the same time, be kind and accept her as she is, leaving her free to think and do whatever she wants.

<!-- seg 09-0027 footnote n=11 h=76df1a19 -->
Although it is common to claim the contrary, I believe this is almost universal. Arguments cloud understanding, whether rational or emotional.

<!-- seg 09-0028 footnote n=12 h=0e1f86a3 -->
Within the limits of good manners and civility, obviously. Do not regress to the Paleolithic.

<!-- seg 09-0029 footnote n=13 h=9081a0f0 -->
I mean dominance of the situation and not of the other person’s mind or body.

<!-- seg 09-0030 footnote n=14 h=fb4fd91a -->
In other words: whip yourself with the lash of the will to tame the inner animal and avoid possession by negative feelings and thoughts. On this point, see Nietzsche (1884-1885/1985).

<!-- seg 09-0031 paragraph h=678a1d54 -->
To render useless the mental hells of stubbornness and debate, it is enough not to force your partner’s opinions. Respect absolutely her opinions, worldviews, conceptions, etc., even when they are mistaken, false, ill-intentioned, absurd, selfish, and completely harmful to the relationship. However, communicate to her one-way and kindly, without debating, what you see regarding them and the consequences they have. Never try to oblige her to admit her own mistakes or to own up explicitly, verbally, to anything whatsoever. The acknowledgment of mistakes and the acceptance of responsibilities are achieved by letting them be what they are and handing the consequences back to them[^15]. We hand back the responsibility and the consequences simply by not assuming them, by not taking them upon ourselves. Always reinforce that her opinions will be respected. The emotional realities of husband and wife are distinct and parallel (GOLEMAN, 1997), which is why it is a waste of time to try to force them to understand our point of view and, even less, to try to oblige them to assimilate our worldview and our idiosyncrasy.

<!-- seg 09-0032 footnote n=15 h=8e6f74de -->
In this way, in some cases, they come to become aware of their negative characteristics. I have observed that some even change their attitude of their own accord, after the effects of their reckless attitudes come back upon themselves. Becoming aware of one’s own mistakes is something very individual, and one cannot oblige one’s neighbor to do it. In truth, this is the central pillar of my theory: absolute adaptive acceptance. There is nothing more we can do except let them do whatever they want with their lives (but not with ours, obviously) and wait for them to savor the good and bad consequences of the paths they choose. Let us not delude ourselves: they will not return to the home, nor will they ever again feel pride in having their domestic work recognized and remunerated by their husbands. Nor will they feel pride in their traditional and distinguishing feminine characteristics: skirts, dresses, delicacy, beauty, softness, superior emotionality, etc. The trend that points toward the days to come is for them to become more and more similar to men and, therefore, more and more uninteresting. The future is bleak. I hope I am wrong.

<!-- seg 09-0033 paragraph h=f61404f3 -->
The secret here consists in not opposing, that is, in allying ourselves with the continuous movements, following the fluctuations, oscillations, and alternations. For this, it is essential not to identify ourselves with the relationship, separating ourselves and seeing events from outside, like a spectator detached from the facts who does not consider them his own. In other words, we have to attain an inner state in which our partner’s opinions and attitudes are no longer considered our problem but only hers, releasing ourselves from any responsibility regarding them, since it is not ours to bear, as it does not belong to us. Both the companion and the relationship must be taken as strange entities[^16].

<!-- seg 09-0034 footnote n=16 h=09c43cb8 -->
That is, entities that are not known and that one needs to observe, get to know, and understand.

<!-- seg 09-0035 paragraph h=3812e20c -->
Learning to separate oneself in order to hold a dialogue in emotional storms is not easy. Fatal magnetism tends to drag us into fights and disagreements[^17]. It is necessary to resist charms and spells (LÉVI, 2001), and provocations of every nature, good as well as bad, keeping the lucidity and calm that the other party will lack in those moments: to be superior in understanding, patience, coldness, and kindness, conditions attained only by those who have dissolved their egos.

<!-- seg 09-0036 footnote n=17 h=0fa62d96 -->
Which Goleman (1997) calls “floods of feeling.”

<!-- seg 09-0037 paragraph h=d2accdc1 -->
Learn to control your mind so as to keep quiet in the worst emotional hells. Endure the tortures and confusions in silence, like the Buddha. Resist all your partner’s provocations aimed at drawing you into a debate. Be distant and mysterious. Speak as little as possible. Hold your tongue even if inside you are about to burst. The silence of the man who disappears within himself bothers them a great deal (ALBERONI, 1986/n.d.), and it is an excellent defense against emotional aggression because it strikes them unerringly. The act of shutting down, refusing to argue, destabilizes and disorients them emotionally (GOLEMAN, 1997).

<!-- seg 09-0038 paragraph h=7fec5ad8 -->
The more we talk, the worse it will be. The more we lay out our points of view, the more we will be feeding the conflicts. It is better to listen to her and make only short, well-aimed, and destructive[^18] interventions, for, as Solomon wrote:

<!-- seg 09-0039 footnote n=18 h=d9634111 -->
I mean the destruction of a few deceptions and mistakes that can be cleared up in such difficult moments.
