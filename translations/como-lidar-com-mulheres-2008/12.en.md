11. Why they observe us

<!-- seg 12-0002 paragraph h=e0ed7ab0 -->
Our behavior is the target of feminine curiosity (that is why there are gossips on street corners). When they are involved with a man, everything he does, what he wears, what he eats, etc. is an object of curiosity for these superficial beings[^1].

<!-- seg 12-0003 footnote n=1 h=94016a0f -->
Man’s superficiality manifests itself through other characteristics, which will not be detailed here because that is not the aim of the book.

<!-- seg 12-0004 paragraph h=2bf4c6c9 -->
We men tend to be incompetent at dealing with emotions and interpreting facial expressions (GOLEMAN, 1997), which is why we become disoriented in the war of passion. Women, on the other hand, when they observe men, seek to understand what is going on in their heads and in their hearts. This is how they come to know our emotional limits, so they can play with us to the extreme in total safety.

<!-- seg 12-0005 paragraph h=b1fe0fb8 -->
Our degree of affective-sexual dependence is measured by the woman through continuous observation. Hence the importance of confusing her[^2] with disconcerting attitudes.

<!-- seg 12-0006 footnote n=2 h=0f5d1f00 -->
As they do with us.

<!-- seg 12-0007 paragraph h=634ceccc -->
Observing the other allows one to detect his behavioral patterns, identify his ways of thinking, and predict his reactions. When we are the object of observation, we become predictable and lose our mystery. When we lose our mystery, we lose the capacity to surprise and become vulnerable.

<!-- seg 12-0008 paragraph h=3ff48ad2 -->
A common rule in dealing with the female sex is the need to be on standby, prepared for the improbable. Once they know our psychic structures through observation, the witches become capable of surprising us with unexpected reactions, and they do so precisely because they know what our expectations are. The curious thing is that this is not the product of intellectual analysis but of an instinctive and unconscious tendency to act outside the other’s patterns of expectation.

<!-- seg 12-0009 paragraph h=1ea1bb50 -->
It is a great danger to become mechanical in our expectations, believing that feminine reactions will be the expected ones. As in combat, we will be struck and disconcerted by attacks we did not foresee and against which we have no response-reactions immediately ready to be delivered. At this level, man tends to lose the game because he looks for the answers intellectually, whereas woman delivers them through emotional impulses, which makes them much faster[^3] than us, but not invincible, as we will see.

<!-- seg 12-0010 footnote n=3 h=4bdda054 -->
And therefore superior in this field.

<!-- seg 12-0011 paragraph h=a6a6ae5c -->
Obviously, I am referring to unpleasant surprises and not to pleasant ones. It is common, for example, for our protective, attentive, or affectionate attitudes to be disdained if they are not placed in a correct context of evident lack of interest. If we are mechanical in our expectations, we will be surprised by disdain, which is the opposite of what we would expect in such circumstances. Consequently, the solution is to have a contrary response-reaction available like an ace up our sleeve. This contrary and surprising response-reaction, in this case, consists of phrases or attitudes that strike her self-esteem, wounding her painfully through horrifications or resolute displays of rejection[^4].

<!-- seg 12-0012 footnote n=4 h=c7d369b5 -->
Which should be used only as a means of legitimate emotional defense. This use is justified only when the sly one has demonstrably taken the initiative of attacking us and wounding our feelings.

<!-- seg 12-0013 paragraph h=2dc17837 -->
Disdain indicates absence of the fear of loss, unconcern about pleasing, and the certainty that we are already caught. More deeply, there is possibly an exaggeratedly positive self-image (the woman thinks she is the hottest thing on Earth). We can provoke a strong and disturbing cognitive dissonance if we reject her resolutely, drawing her attention in a terrible way to the fact that there has been an ingratitude whose natural consequence is repulsion.

<!-- seg 12-0014 paragraph h=dc73fe73 -->
Obviously, passion prevents us from having such attitudes. We need a great deal of emotional intelligence to win the little games.

<!-- seg 12-0015 paragraph h=201b12bf -->
Our suffering in love makes them happy because it raises their self-esteem. This suffering may stem from irritation, affective deprivation, sexual deprivation, and longing. It is a need they have, and when it is not satisfied, it leaves them immensely sad and disturbed, because they feel incompetent to attract and hold on to a man.

<!-- seg 12-0016 paragraph h=2814e43c -->
We are dealing with emotional defenses and attacks. Defenses are penetrated by surprising. One surprises by shocking, by acting in the most improbable way possible, which requires freedom of action and deconditioning. However, if we do not calculate the effects correctly, the reactions we provoke may be undesirable, and we will be the ones surprised. It is dangerous to risk shocking indiscriminately and in any old way[^5].

<!-- seg 12-0017 footnote n=5 h=baeae6db -->
This happens when we do not have reason on our side and are wrong, or are not acting honestly in legitimate self-defense. It also happens when we want to cause emotional harm or do not limit ourselves to the goal of merely preserving the integrity of our feelings without attacking gratuitously or for selfish motives. By proceeding in this way, the emotional aggressor hands the moral high ground to the other party and becomes unjust, suffering the consequences of the shot that will backfire.

<!-- seg 12-0018 paragraph h=766119d9 -->
The one who irritates is in command of the relationship, and the one who is irritated is being commanded. The irritating person is the active agent, and the one who suffers the irritation is the passive agent. Through observation, women perceive our irritations, discover our tendencies, beliefs, needs, desires, and wants, and lead us. To turn the game around, it is we who have to irritate them (correctly!), while at the same time observing their reactions and following the whole process without identifying ourselves with it. But we will not be able to irritate them if we are not immune to their provocations. Therefore, there are three important points here: observe, resist, and irritate. It is a war in which the weapons are provocations and the shield is resistance. Over time, our lady friends have learned to control us emotionally, playing with our feelings in infinite ways. They provoke in us, at their pleasure, anger, desire, happiness, enthusiasm, frustration, the feeling of being belittled, etc. To defeat them, we have to fight with the same weapons, being more resistant and more provocative than they are with us.

<!-- seg 12-0019 paragraph h=8b62f296 -->
A very common way in which we are provoked to the point of madness consists in being stimulated (by promises of heavenly encounters, wonderful sex, etc.) and then frustrated. This is a very interesting process in which they usually lure us with promises that are <b>implicit</b> (very rarely explicit) in their conduct, creating certain expectations in us, only to surprise us afterward, frustrating us on the most varied pretexts, generally emotional ones, while they observe us. To turn this game around, all we need to do is apply the same procedure back, offering and frustrating, or, if this does not work, creating situations that leave her with no way out. Observing the whole process in order to unmask it also tends to produce results.

<!-- seg 12-0020 paragraph h=a348f46c -->
Since this pattern is common to the overwhelming majority of the sly ones, it follows that, deep down, they are predictable and not unpredictable as they seem. However, they hide their predictability in order to disconcert us.

<!-- seg 12-0021 paragraph h=d7b4bee8 -->
In sum, we can say that we are observed continuously so that our limits, desires, and feelings may be identified. Identifying them is necessary so that they can be aroused and frustrated in sudden games of emotional hell-raising.
