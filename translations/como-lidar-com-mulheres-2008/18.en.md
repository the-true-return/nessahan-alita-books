17. The Stupid Social Circle

<!-- seg 18-0002 paragraph h=efb01067 -->
There is a very efficient way to win back an old girlfriend, an ex-wife, or simply a female who interests us: it consists in getting close to as many as possible of the people she admires and likes and who belong to that stupid circle of friends that irritates us so much. If you manage to win a prominent place in that friendly circle and, at the same time, show yourself somewhat uninterested specifically in the woman you want to win back, she will come after you.

<!-- seg 18-0003 paragraph h=30d9fecf -->
A woman normally has an idiotic circle of friends and relatives who steal her attention and draw her away from us. In general, we feel a justified anger because these people steal her time, and often she may even end up giving sex to some imbecile from that circle, camouflaging it all as friendship. However, if we jump into this circle instead of running away from it, and win these people over, especially the most magnetic ones, we will have two advantages: 1) the woman will admire us; 2) if she, unfortunately, has already gotten involved with some suspicious “harmless little friend” from there, we can win over one of her girlfriends, preferably the closest one, and this will be a good punishment that will hurt quite a bit... Then we will feel avenged and can laugh in the sly one’s face. We will have imploded the bubble that sheltered her, removed her points of emotional support, and, on top of that, received a well-deserved prize!
