20. On the Kind of Security They Seek

<!-- seg 21-0002 paragraph h=86f15e84 -->
It is common to hear that women want security, but almost no one can say exactly what kind of security this is. Some men, in desperation, think it is security regarding the feelings they have for the woman, and they hurry to give them flowers, often even on their knees. They are wretches.

<!-- seg 21-0003 paragraph h=056744d3 -->
The masculine security they seek is not the security of the man’s feelings for the woman but rather of the man about himself. The secure man the ladies talk about so much is the man who fears no one and needs no one, who does not crawl and does not hurry to please, who pleases by his mere existence. He is also the one who is sure of his goals in life, who does not give up his aims, and who knows what kind of love and what profile of woman he is looking for, making no concessions. He is a special man who does not bow to the charm of any female, who resists all spells, including attempts at conflict, at creating unfriendly atmospheres, and the infernal tests. This profile gives the female intense security. The self-assured man conveys a feeling of protection to whoever is with him.

<!-- seg 21-0004 paragraph h=10c64f1a -->
Paradoxically, such a man must temper this security about himself by inserting into the feminine mind an insecurity about what he feels for her, making her oscillate between hope and despair, between being welcomed and the fear of losing him, just as she does, or tries to do, with him. If he lets her become polarized, he will lose her.

<!-- seg 21-0005 paragraph h=75001702 -->
This security has nothing to do with giving flowers, little notes, or chocolates. Although we may do this once in a while, it is not advisable to do it all the time, so as to avoid subliminally communicating emotional weakness.

<!-- seg 21-0006 paragraph h=f8aaf4de -->
The characteristics that convey the idea of a man’s security about himself are: firmness, determination, objectivity, courage, detachment, independence, leadership, insensitivity, protection, severity, cruelty (which must be directed in favor of good things and against bad things)[^1], lack of pity, strength, and concentration, among others. On the other hand, the following convey to the woman the idea that the man is insecure: romanticism, sensitivity, passivity, emotionality, fragility, emotional neediness, infatuation, dependence, harassment, attachment, fear, shyness, kindness, fear of losing, and submission. The crisis of masculine values that humanity is currently going through, and that affects women so strongly, originates in the confusion of roles. The confusion, in turn, comes from the weakening of the masculine as early as childhood.

<!-- seg 21-0007 footnote n=1 h=56fc436e -->
For what we have agreed to call “evil” consists of the instinctive and natural forces of the unconscious that do not find their place in the consciousness of modern man (SANFORD, 1988). “Civilized” life does not allow the expression of all the impulses with which nature endowed us. These impulses, banished to the unconscious, will make up the shadow and will express themselves in an autonomous and compulsive form. One and the same behavioral trait will be good in its place and bad out of it.

<!-- seg 21-0008 paragraph h=99d93645 -->
Although they will hardly admit it, observation reveals that human females seek emotionally strong men who will guide, dominate, and protect them, in the senses already so exhaustively explained in this book. It is no use being tall, strong, rich, and handsome if you do not have a brave heart. Nor is it any use being a tough guy with other men, carrying knives, and physically threatening rival males. She will get tired of you all the same, will get sick of you, and will give you horns. And it will serve you right, because you deserved it...

<!-- seg 21-0009 paragraph h=e5899050 -->
Another thing: never speak in a submissive tone, and do not be bossy either. Speak with concentration, from the heart, and without wavering. Use a deep tone of voice, not a high one. Do not keep asking for opinions, asking questions, giving explanations all the time, etc. Simply make sound decisions and announce them. If some explanation about your conduct is requested, confine yourself to giving it briefly and objectively, preserving the mystery. Of course, when you make a mistake you must acknowledge it and hurry to correct it before your partner starts complaining, opportunities they never miss. Women are great complainers and, if you are a softie, they will make you run after their absurd and contradictory complaints until you go completely mad. I also suggest that you never shout and that you not let her shout at you. Do not make threats you cannot carry out, and never bluff. Lose all fear. Do not consider her invulnerable. If you say you will not go after her anymore, really do not go, and kill the urge to see her inside yourself.

<!-- seg 21-0010 paragraph h=fdcd7386 -->
Observe yourself in front of a beautiful woman and you will immediately discover that you are ridiculously concerned with pleasing her. You will catch yourself looking covetously at her beautiful body. You will try to be pleasant. Perhaps you will try to puff up your muscles to look like a strongman, or else smile at her nicely, stupidly believing that you will get admiration for it. You may try to make idiotic jokes, monkey around, show off money, a car, or other attributes. Perhaps you will start scratching yourself while hanging upside down from some branch... This behavior is the same in all males and will simply make you ridiculous. Instead of conveying security, you will convey the opposite. You will be pathetic and insecure. On the other hand, if you simply ignore her, you will immediately be noticed and will stand out from the rest, but this is only half the work; it is not everything. Besides standing out by not caring, you must approach without fear to establish contact <b>with indifference</b>, but at the same time determined to take her for yourself as something that is your due, without hesitation. Be ready to sense rejection before it begins so that you can take the lead by rejecting her first.

<!-- seg 21-0011 paragraph h=ee080146 -->
Men have not yet understood that woman is not the fragile being she appears to be. Precisely because of her bodily fragility, woman has refined her strategies for dominating and subjugating by means of games of feelings and the manipulation of beliefs and doubts in the masculine mind. The countless benefits and privileges that the females of <i>homo sapiens</i> have enjoyed in relation to the males throughout history (CREVELD, 2004) are due to this ability to awaken in us solidarity and the desire to protect and help them. Fragility is feminine strength. The only possible way to cancel out the negative effects of this strength on our psyche is not to give ourselves up emotionally. Then we make it powerless against us and we master it, directing its flow positively.

<!-- seg 21-0012 paragraph h=c1d9d5e5 -->
It is advisable to discover the real content of the feeling the woman has for us. To do so, it is enough to test her without fear of losing her, because, after all, if you lose her it is because you never had her, and then there is no point in being afraid.

<!-- seg 21-0013 paragraph h=425fde1a -->
All this demands great security about oneself, detachment, and confidence in one’s own potential.

<!-- seg 21-0014 paragraph h=4a0e2372 -->
Since childhood, we have learned that we should please them so that, in return, love would be given to us as a gift. Television, movies, books, etc., drilled these erroneous ideas into us. Now we carry on with the conditioned behavior in adult life, always concerned with pleasing, with being nice, always “walking on eggshells,” afraid of breaking the crystal doll. However, this is the same thing all suitors do, and it does not allow us to stand out. How could the one who does what everyone does stand out, the one who is the same in his attempt to be different? This mistake is shot through with the assumption that feminine love is a reward for masculine attempts to please.

<!-- seg 21-0015 paragraph h=980b1538 -->
Tall, rich, muscular, or handsome men are not desired simply for having these characteristics, but rather for feeling superior to their rivals and, consequently, more secure. As for those who are physically strong but childish, there is also the question of convenience: when they are immature, they serve well as beasts of burden and guard dogs. When domesticated by means of sex and affection, they make excellent animals, pointing their threatening, sharp horns at whomever their mistresses order. If you outdo them in security, you will surpass them and can become the master of their mistresses! Obviously, there is nothing wrong with being tall and strong (in fact it helps a lot), but there is with being stupid and childish. There are many tall, strong, sensible, mature, and intelligent men. But there are others who are not... Among several men who are absolutely the same in everything but different physically, the bigger ones will be preferred. However, strength and size are not enough, and if you are a hulk, I suggest you not neglect your intellectual and emotional development. If you are short, I suggest you invest in developing behaviors that overcome this deficiency. There is no fixed rule, and any type of man can be traded for an opposite type. I have seen rich men traded for poor ones, poor for rich, tall for short, short for tall, old for young, young for old, etc. The reason for this absence of a rule is that the unconscious feminine psyche is chaotic and drives women to continual dissatisfaction, leading them, as van Creveld (2004) observed, to complain all the time, even when all their demands have already been met.

<!-- seg 21-0016 paragraph h=e949e408 -->
Some are desired to be slaves, mere providers. These are the good ones, whom we could also call suckers. Others are desired to be breeding males, to mate. These are the bad ones and the cads. Still others are desired to be the absolute masters of the body, of sex, and of the soul.[^2] These are beyond good and evil. Prefer to be among these last.

<!-- seg 21-0017 footnote n=2 h=33d78284 -->
Let us not forget that this whole dynamic of desires is unconscious or, at most, semiconscious, which is why we should not rebel against it.

<!-- seg 21-0018 paragraph h=2ec16b5b -->
To convey security, get used to speaking in a tone of command. Lead the relationship; exercise protective authority. Set times and deadlines. Do not ask; inform and give orders in a manner that is not arrogant but firm. Leave her no way out when you notice any tendency to behave unpleasantly. Return the effects of negative attitudes. Do not identify yourself with the relationship, but be the head of the relationship.

<!-- seg 21-0019 paragraph h=e4e38bb1 -->
Notice that the villains in stories are more confident, cold, and decisive than the good guys, but they are less intelligent, less sensitive, and less romantic. The complete man has both sides: he is the synthesis of the hero and the villain. He is superior to both because he is beyond good and evil (NIETZSCHE, 1886/1998). Be superior to the cad and to the good homebody. Study them. Keep what is good in each of them and discard what is bad.
