7. Getting past the emotional defenses of women who are closed to approach and contact

<!-- seg 08-0002 paragraph h=a9a246d6 -->
Just as we are vulnerable to erotic assaults by femmes fatales, many women have no resistance whatsoever against a disinterested protective command without sexual intentions, because they are looking for suckers ready to serve them for all eternity. This selfish impulse, however, can be used in our favor, for it is an open flank. Human females are not as invulnerable as they appear to the men who, at first sight, seem uninteresting to them.

<!-- seg 08-0003 paragraph h=b4538a45 -->
When the female is absolutely refractory to contact and approach, it is generally because she believes she is exceedingly desired, or else she wants to induce men to believe it so that they will desire her. Consequently, the more blatantly we display our sexual intention, the more we will close the passage. The more you look at her covetously, make advances, and insist, the more you will be rebuffed. The only alternative left for conquering her is to show yourself in the opposite way, acting as if you could desire every woman in the world except her! If, instead of pretending, you manage to let go and really see her as a normal woman, equal to or even less interesting than the others, so much the better.

<!-- seg 08-0004 paragraph h=4d13df1f -->
I will now set out this feminine weakness in the field of seduction more fully; obviously, I am thinking of absolutely “difficult” women, because the “easy” ones require no work. Difficult women are those who are absolutely refractory, with whom one cannot establish any sympathetic affinity in order to conquer them. They tend to be scowling, and nobody has the courage to come near them or even to look at them. They can also be those beauties who frighten even the most macho of men.[^1]

I mean the arrogant and snobbish ones, not the humble, kind, and sincere ones.

Let us see what happens. Given the double meaning attributed to sex, when faced with masculine desire females envision two possibilities: one that they desire and another against which they feel an instinctive horror. The possibility they desire is that of being loved, and the one they detest is that of being violated (ALBERONI, 1986/n.d.). This latter is what makes them so prone to hysteria. It is the unconscious fear of being violated that leads them to reject the losers, those incapable of seducing them, the shy ones, the clingy ones, the childish ones, and, in general, every harasser or pursuer (ALBERONI, 1986/n.d.). This contradiction makes them disconcerting, for they fear and desire at the same time (ALBERONI, 1986/n.d.). The contradiction of feelings, inherent in the contradiction of the possible consequences of masculine desire, leads them to act in a paradoxical way that does not allow us to know what they really want. The slightest suspicion of any intention of sexual violence can trigger a hysterical crisis that will give rise to a hostile social chain against the harasser. No amount of care is too much to avoid being mistaken for a loser, and therein lies the problem, for we have to approach them, make contact, and conquer them without harassing[^2]. Hence the importance of knowing how to read the signals correctly, of never insisting against resistance, of knowing how to approach with a certain dose of hypocrisy (LÉVI, 1855/2001)[^3], without conveying that we are desperate, and of never forcing absolutely anything. We have to go through only the passages that are opened to us. But the passages will not be opened unless we conceal our desire. Explicit masculine desire causes fear, aversion, and disgust (ALBERONI, 1986/n.d.), contrary to what the ignorant think. It is repulsive. That is why, if you show your penis in public, you will go to jail immediately, whereas your neighbor, if she takes off her clothes in the middle of downtown, will merely be taken tenderly to the doctor[^4]. Explicit masculine desire closes the passage to intimacy.

In the feminine mind there is a constant opening, a passage that never closes. A skilled seducer quickly identifies it and uses it. It is the opening to intimacy “without malice” with a man who convinces her that he is disinterested, without ulterior motives, without sexual aims, but at the same time protective and dominant. Paradoxically, the more we conceal our sexual intention, the more opening to an “innocent” intimacy we obtain. That is why you should be suspicious of your wife’s innocent little friends.

The key to approaching the scowling ones consists in gradually increasing intimacy while at the same time showing indifference, naturalness, and lack of interest, combined with a slightly protective and aggressive posture. Depending on the degree of resistance and antipathy of our “prey”[^5], we need to feign indifference not only toward sex but even toward friendship and toward the lady’s very person.

This is a metaphorical expression.

Gynecologists, for example, are permitted to look inside vaginas simply because they are backed by the belief that their aims are merely therapeutic. The woman who opens her legs for him does so out of an unshakable belief in his honesty and absence of sexual interest. Along the same line, but going further, the gynecologist can touch her clitoris[^6] on the pretext of performing an examination, and even arouse her. As long as the belief is preserved, there will be no feminine reaction against the touch, in the sense of rejecting it. In the nineteenth century, doctors even went so far as to arouse and masturbate women as a form of treatment to try to cure them of frigidity. That is how the vibrator was invented: as a medical tool to replace the hands. Obviously, many were not cured in a single session; others discovered that they felt nothing when they were at home with their husband, but only with the doctor, and returned to the office many times... This is proof that the belief in, and trust in, the absence of sexual intentions allows a woman to open up and give herself little by little. The same happens with psychotherapists, to whom they reveal secrets they would never reveal to anyone, much less to their husbands. Deep down, females want to feel welcomed, understood, and accepted just as they are, without any sexual favor being demanded in return. They want to feel safe, to have a harbor where they can dock.

Our lady friends will run away if you are lustful and blatantly display your intention. To approach them without their running away or rebuffing us, we have to show ourselves uninterested in their erotic attributes and, at the same time, tighten the bonds of intimacy, giving protection and orders[^7], guiding them, and also listening to them or helping them. Sometimes, to disarm them, it is necessary to reprimand them, making it explicit that we do so for their own good, and, in some extreme cases, even to reject her approach or presence, wounding her emotionally[^8].

It is not a matter of being the little confidant friend or the sexless nice guy who desires no one. What I am suggesting here is something entirely different. It is a matter of being a superior male who does not desire her in particular, because he has the chance to get other, better ones, but who reveals himself as a real man in the way he treats her, without fear, without desperation to please, and without fear of losing.

<!-- seg 08-0019 paragraph h=3e1c20fb -->
Note that I am <b>not</b> claiming that friendship is a good path to seduction, as my opponents pretended to understand. What I am claiming is that women withdraw when we blatantly display our desire and open up when they believe we have no desire for them at all. None of this means that women feel attraction to friends or that friendship is an efficient means of seduction. What happens is that they offer their treasure to those who do not want it and refuse it to those who desire it. I am claiming that blatant intention scares them away and indifference attracts.

The need to be accepted with their “morally reprehensible acts”[^9] is very strong and makes them vulnerable to men who show no sexual ulterior motives and do not react with disapproval to the mistakes they make. When they get to know such a man, they gradually reveal to him the “ugliest” or most “wrong” things they have ever done in their lives, observing his reactions. As they confirm that they are accepted, or rather, that these men are morally indifferent, they build more trust, and the confessions deepen as intimacy grows. Then, without realizing it, they are already involved emotionally and sexually.

This is the mental passage that never closes and through which any woman can be conquered, provided we treat her correctly. There is no heterosexual woman who can resist correct advances through this channel, because all of them have a desperate need for complicity and for raising their self-esteem when they do not feel desired. If one still resists, it will be due to some clumsiness on the part of the would-be seducer that resulted in some subliminal communication of intention.

Women are also absolutely vulnerable to friendships, and when they rebuff a friendly attempt at contact, it is because they perceive that the candidate for “friend without malice” wants something more. And they perceive it because he shows himself to be a needy male and, therefore, a second-rate one. Those who avoid contact and behave in an inaccessible way do not do so out of respect or love for the man they live with or are committed to, but because they hold no hope that there are sincere friendly intentions on the part of those who cross their path and try to approach them. It happens that, in the case of the scowling ones, this is the only possible route of approach left, apart from calculated “horrification,” which should be understood as the act of scandalizing and shocking in the right way (and harmlessly, obviously), a resource that is not advisable to use very often, but only when all the others have failed.

This is a form of psychological penetration that is obtained by talking disinterestedly with the woman about herself, making her feel welcomed and safe.

The course of the conversations should revolve around general romantic questions and, later, around the specific romantic questions of the woman we are seducing. The sexual theme can be introduced only after a good while.

The more intense the displays of disinterested, dominant, guiding, and protective care, the more emotionally intoxicated she will become[^11].

Feminine emotional defenses are crossed through attitudes that communicate indifference, specific sexual lack of interest in the “prey,” and, at the same time, guidance, command, and protection. The image to project is more or less that of someone sexually uninterested in the woman he commands, but not asexual in general (be careful!). There can be no hesitation, wavering, or doubt in how you treat her. By this path one enters the world of even the most forbidden and difficult women. There are men who have seduced unthinkable women with this procedure alone.
