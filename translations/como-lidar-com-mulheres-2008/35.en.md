About the author:

<!-- seg 35-0002 paragraph h=a8c7a102 -->
The author of this work IS NOT A PSYCHOLOGIST; his professional training is in another field. He IS NOT A MASTER and DOES NOT WANT DISCIPLES OR FOLLOWERS. He IS NOT THE LEADER OF ANY RELIGION. He simply likes to think freely about the question of love and believes he has that right. His opinions are PROVISIONAL AND INDEPENDENT. The author published his ideas only so that people would study and discuss them critically, and he recommends that people read other books on the subject. His ideas are merely a starting point for deeper study and bridges to other authors and other points of view. He DOES NOT GIVE ORDERS; he only makes suggestions, which should be received critically.

<!-- seg 35-0003 paragraph h=06707a33 -->
The author asks readers NOT TO PESTER HIM with questions, because everything is already very well explained in his books, nor to BOTHER HIM by insisting that he take part in biased groups. He wants neither fans nor admirers; he wants only CRITICAL, REFLECTIVE, AND STUDIOUS READERS.

<!-- seg 35-0004 paragraph h=98d9f60c -->
The author DISAPPROVES of the formation of any groups claiming to represent him or his ideas. There is no group or institution, anywhere in the world, virtual or not, that represents this author. Obviously, there may be groups with points of view similar to his, but, definitely, none of these groups represents him. All those who claim to be his disciples are IMPOSTORS and should be unmasked.
