6. Psychological tortures

<!-- seg 07-0002 paragraph h=6f771fa6 -->
Sly females will “torment” (provoke and irritate)[^1] the males who do not know how to exercise dominance[^2] by means of a protective coldness, a powerful will, and an extreme severity[^3]. They take great satisfaction in creating puzzles and emotional games; they delight in watching us suffer as we try to dismantle them. When they see us in a tight spot, desperate to get out of the psychological plots they create, they are happy and can measure our persistence in order to assess how far they have managed to fascinate us, for they seek the one-sided continuation of the romantic encounter. Always keep reason on your side so as not to fall headfirst into the abyss.

<!-- seg 07-0003 footnote n=1 h=0cdbc1be -->
These “tortures” are one of the expressions of the sadistic love described by Fromm (1976).

<!-- seg 07-0004 footnote n=2 h=3b24e784 -->
I mean the emotional dominance that results naturally from the masculine renunciation of every form of coercive domination.

<!-- seg 07-0005 footnote n=3 h=73e2ac31 -->
I mean the severity that a man must exercise over himself in order to educate his will.

<!-- seg 07-0006 paragraph h=9c5add7e -->
The refinement of this ability to wound emotionally begins at the start of adolescence, when girls tend to replace physical aggression with words:

<!-- seg 07-0007 paragraph h=31ed0703 -->
“At thirteen, a revealing difference between the sexes appears: girls become more capable than boys of planning cunning aggressive tactics, such as isolating others, gossiping, and taking sly little revenges. Boys, in general, remain brawlers, unaware of the use of subtler strategies. This is just one of the many ways in which boys—and, later, men—are less sophisticated than the opposite sex in the byways of emotional life.” (GOLEMAN, 1997, p. 145)

Woman’s emotional intelligence is more developed than man’s, because she is encouraged from childhood to face and talk about her emotions (GOLEMAN, 1997). This gives them a sophisticated ability to strike at our feelings, for good as well as for ill. An example of the bad use of this form of intelligence can be seen when a woman discovers that a man she previously considered special is in truth just an ordinary mortal. She then becomes disenchanted and loses interest. Disillusioned, she begins to detest him and torment him psychologically (ALBERONI, 1986/n.d.):

<!-- seg 07-0009 paragraph h=d8bf8911 -->
“She is then seized by irritation, by anger. She escapes into fantasies. At the same time, she takes revenge with routine gestures that irritate the man and leave him exasperated. Knowing his tastes and desires, she strikes at him continuously, obsessively. It is a ritual of hatred, to which she devotes herself with the same diligence she once devoted to the ritual of love” (p. 78)

Therefore, feminine fragility is undeniable in the physical sphere but not in the emotional sphere as a whole (CREVELD, 2004), contrary to the widespread belief in popular culture. In the field of the couple’s relationship, many human females are not the least bit delicate or fragile; they are powerful, merciless, and they play dirty[^4]. However, we must accept such characteristics as instinctive and natural, without rebelling.

They possess great magnetic power (LÉVI, 1855/2001) to provoke negative feelings in the male. If he is emotionally weak, they easily make him fall into states of jealousy, irritation, and impatience (JUNG, 1996) and, in the same way, they make him feel small, as if he were a brat (ALBERONI, 1986/n.d.). These influences are actions of the animus, the masculine part of the feminine unconscious, upon the anima, which is the feminine part of the masculine unconscious (JUNG, 1979; JUNG, 1995). You must have seen those funny situations in which women in a group laugh at a lone man to make him feel small (ALBERONI, 1986/n.d.). If he is not emotionally strong enough to return the magnetic flow, he will momentarily regress to childhood. I love to dismantle this sentimental manipulation simply by giving them back a sarcastic smile while I stare them in the eyes for a long time, until they become intrigued about my motives and start bombarding me with questions. Then I withdraw without answering, victorious.

<!-- seg 07-0013 paragraph h=13e79a29 -->
Because they are psychological, feminine strategies of attack and retaliation are rarely admitted. They hide very well from ordinary eyes, which can only see the external, the physical. Nevertheless, they are highly effective at inducing suffering in others.

<!-- seg 07-0014 paragraph h=caf5cbbc -->
The secret to defending ourselves against all the female wiles of manipulation and mental/emotional torture consists in not identifying ourselves with the woman’s strategies, isolating her in her own capricious and contradictory acts. For this, it is indispensable not to be in love, which can be achieved only through the death of the egos. Then she will play her games alone and drink in all the madness she tried to introduce into our heart. Such power is attained when we break with identification through the strong work of eliminating sentimentalism. It is also advisable to regard her as a “tramp”[^5] until proven otherwise, since in our modern Western civilization, with its “advanced” customs, few escape. There are sly ones who pretend to be “little saints” for several years.

<!-- seg 07-0015 footnote n=5 h=b40fcd62 -->
In the sense given by the Michaelis (1995) and Aurélio (FERREIRA, 1995) dictionaries.

<!-- seg 07-0016 paragraph h=8e380f72 -->
The capacity to resist feminine bewitchments and enchantments is one of the prerequisites of mythic heroes, who resist fears and attractions, not allowing desires and fears to steal their soul and cloud their consciousness. As a simple matter of spiritual health and emotional survival, man must reconcile himself with the masculine patterns portrayed in myths (JUDY, 1998). Once we have achieved such independence, we must observe the female, waiting to find out how long she will persist in her attempts to bewitch and subjugate us. We have to hand back to her the burden she insistently tries to throw onto our backs, that is, let her do all the heavy work and simply wait until exhaustion overtakes her.

A very common way of torturing is through suspicious behavior that provokes jealousy. The stages of this process of mental torture are the following:

<!-- seg 07-0018 paragraph h=20146ab6 -->
1st phase—The woman behaves like a saint, giving affection and sex until we are emotionally dependent. In this phase she pretends not to be interested in anyone else, not to give attention or the time of day to any other man.

<!-- seg 07-0019 paragraph h=ef9921f6 -->
2nd phase—After making sure that we have taken the bait and are firmly caught by feeling, the “tramp[^6]” begins to behave suspiciously toward other males, so as to cast doubts into our mind and set off suffering from jealousy.

<!-- seg 07-0020 footnote n=6 h=c5a1eab4 -->

<!-- seg 07-0021 paragraph h=158ba035 -->
3rd phase—When we protest, with just cause, she flatly denies the intentions behind such visibly compromising behavior, claiming innocence, indignation, sadness, etc., crying crocodile tears and persisting in the same behavior right afterward.

<!-- seg 07-0022 paragraph h=2b0ddec3 -->
Through this strategy, the female manages to prolong the male’s suffering indefinitely. They use it with greater or lesser intensity, according to their conceptions of the world and the willingness they have to fight against their own evil instincts. Note that the foundation of the torture is the feeling of attachment and passion. Despite all your attempts to break free and your useless thrashing about, she will not stop torturing you with such games unless she feels that you have truly fallen out of love. This is the secret. The more in love you are, the more subjected to the infernal little games you will be.

<!-- seg 07-0023 paragraph h=43c124a1 -->
Try acting intensely jealous and needy on the phone: your partner will make up some pretext or other and hang up right away to keep you in that state for the next few days. The thing is, they like to see us like that, desperate, because it gives them a morbid pleasure associated with the feeling that there is a sucker who will wait for them all his life. However, this kind of pleasure does not fulfill them as women, and you will be regarded as a secondary and uninteresting male if you show yourself this way, a mere spare kept in reserve as a last resort. The first on the list will be the one who does not pay them much attention, without letting himself become polarized in coldness. If you have made this mistake of being jealous, to correct it you need to undo the belief that was created. This feminine behavioral pattern of pulling away when the male is jealous or needy can also be very useful when you are fed up and want some peace for a few days: just stage a scene like that and you will be left in peace. But do not forget: if, as the days go by, you do not confirm with additional signs the belief you induced, your companion will come desperately after you.

<!-- seg 07-0024 paragraph h=418df600 -->
Another common way of making our mind a hell is to make dates and not show up. To smash this little game, never forget to set a limit on the times of the dates, and never stay waiting like an idiot after the deadline has passed. Deadlines disconcert them because they are agreements explicitly defined for both parties, which corner their minds and prevent them from maneuvering in vagueness.

<!-- seg 07-0025 paragraph h=8a501c87 -->
There is also an ingenious feminine strategy that consists in not showing care and withholding affection in order to induce the male to show them.

<!-- seg 07-0026 paragraph h=94ef876b -->
In practically all torturing psychological games we will find vagueness and contradictions intended to confuse. We see them, for example, in the woman who flirts and then runs away, in the one who starts an argument by raising critical points and slips away before they are cleared up, in the one who takes the initiative of calling and then behaves as if she wanted to hang up quickly, etc. The intention is to leave important questions hanging in the air, feelings unresolved.

<!-- seg 07-0027 paragraph h=10f86377 -->
In short, the mechanisms of torture consist in stirring up our doubt, our sexual drive, and our amorous feelings to the utmost but never satisfying them. When they decide to satisfy them, they do so because they feel threatened[^7], moved by the idea that they are losing dominance, but keeping the expectation that further on they will be able to throw us into prolonged dissatisfaction again. Erotic desire and the feeling of love (understood here as being in love and attachment) are normally the main tools used, the others being rarely employed except in direct association with these or in exceptional cases. Unsatisfied arousal produces a state of discomfort that can be prolonged to the utmost. This is why real hatred, rejection, or indifference on the man’s part frightens them: it renders them powerless. Hatred is not advisable[^8]. Indifference[^9] is, and it can be attained when we eliminate all the egos related to passion, attachment, lust, affection, etc.

The best way of unmasking I have found is simply to point them out with conviction at the very instant they are being applied, so as to take her by surprise and allow no denial. Your inattention will be used against you. Therefore, be alert to catch and denounce, mercilessly, cruelly[^10], and implacably[^11], all wiles, lies, and manipulations. The fundamental thing is to be alert, ready to unmask with lightning speed. If the denunciation is postponed, it will turn into a mere argument and the opportunity will have been lost. Never leave the unmasking for later, because it will not have the same effect, owing to the feminine wiles for evading the problems of the romantic relationship. The problem here lies in the fact that we are slow, because we are more rational, while our lady friends are fast because they move and are motivated only by feelings[^12]. To overcome this deficiency in speed, all we need to do is get used to expecting the worst[^13]. In this way, we will be one step ahead, early in perceiving the wiles of others.
