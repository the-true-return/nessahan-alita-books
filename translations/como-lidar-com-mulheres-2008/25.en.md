24. Observing Them Realistically

<!-- seg 25-0002 paragraph h=6a4e92be -->
Many of Machiavelli’s precepts (1513/1977; 1513/2001) are valid in dealing with women: being at the same time loved and “feared” (respected), doing good little by little, “punishing them” (returning consequences) all at once, not seducing the wives of other males so as not to make unnecessary enemies, etc.

<!-- seg 25-0003 paragraph h=e7377f58 -->
You will be loved only on the basis of the emotional suffering you give back. Do not love her passionately, but treat her well. Learn to reach her in her emotions.

<!-- seg 25-0004 paragraph h=5a3a46b5 -->
For a woman to admire us, we need to “wound” (strike) her correctly in her feelings so that she feels the power of our will and determination. The fear of displeasing and of losing reveals weakness, and a man must take every care not to be taken for a weakling, because weaklings are unattractive.

<!-- seg 25-0005 paragraph h=c9ba8e6f -->
Learn to observe the feelings your attitudes, gestures, and words provoke. But beware of your partner’s skillful simulations.

<!-- seg 25-0006 paragraph h=34ee6192 -->
A woman does not know much about herself. Do not take your bearings from the idiotic masculine profile of the heroes of love movies and romance novels, nor from the type of “interesting man” that women describe. The man who dominates them[^1] emotionally does not correspond in any way to what they say. In fact, such descriptions only serve to draw the weakest men into subservience and mark them for rejection, since these imbeciles hurry to try to fit into those stupid models. In general, what strikes them in their emotions and makes them fall in love is precisely the opposite of what we hear them say all the time. Hence the importance of not being afraid of losing them, so that we can contradict them at will.

<!-- seg 25-0007 footnote n=1 h=4f687205 -->
I am referring to consensual domination, free of violence of any kind. See the note on dominance.

<!-- seg 25-0008 paragraph h=00b26f6c -->
The so-called feminine love, freely offered, is selfish because it does not take into account the emotional suffering it causes. It is absolutely calculating in its purpose: to select the male most resistant to the fatal magnetism of females. It is garbage; reject it.

<!-- seg 25-0009 paragraph h=64db6de7 -->
Feminine understanding in a couple’s relationship usually comes after the emotional impact of events, not before. Hence the uselessness of attempts to argue. They are reached <i>a posteriori</i>.

<!-- seg 25-0010 paragraph h=daf80d39 -->
Do not try to reach them with logical arguments but rather with the emotional impact of your words and conduct. Pay attention to the feelings your words and conduct provoke. The element that guides them will be feeling, not linear rational logic. The opinions they adopt, the ideas they defend, the value they attribute to things, etc., are due to the emotions provoked. The same holds for the value that will be attributed to their partner. You will be considered a man, a crybaby, a demon, a Prince Charming, a frog, a servile dog, or a rat according to the feelings you provoke and not according to the reasoning you set off. However, this does not mean that imagination will not be at work. Do not try to make them reason; accept them as they are. Be adaptable and malleable; have no form.

<!-- seg 25-0011 paragraph h=eb0fdd8b -->
Do not expect sincerity. Whoever needs affection and love to be happy in a relationship is a wretch, in my view (obviously, you do not have to agree!). The noblest, most sublime, and most altruistic intentions are generally trampled and go unrecognized. The human being is asleep and moves about in incoherence and ingratitude.

<!-- seg 25-0012 paragraph h=db539669 -->
If you are suffering at the hands of some lady, this simply means that you are not seeing the real content of the relationship. Your suffering is arising from infernal behavioral contradictions. They are very skillful at deceiving and disguising what they really want, do, and feel. Watch her in action and discover what she really feels and wants. If she does not give you good-quality sex, and often, if she does not show up for dates, if she keeps postponing the commitments she has made, if she does not call or offers unconvincing justifications for her absence, these are unequivocal signs that the relationship is superficial and good for nothing except casual and widely spaced encounters. Regardless of what she says, it is the facts and the attitudes that show the truth, and we must always yield to them.

<!-- seg 25-0013 paragraph h=69df1956 -->
Because they feel inferior, our fatal little lady friends feel great satisfaction in knowing that they deceive us by hiding their intentions and feelings. It is a kind of unconscious revenge for not being able to surpass us in any field other than that of emotional resistance against passion. It is the symbolic penis envy. If we surpass them in this field, we surpass them in all the others.

<!-- seg 25-0014 paragraph h=93a3a4ae -->
Emotional resistance makes us able to accept lies and attempts at deception naturally. It is a strength and not a weakness; cultivate it.

<!-- seg 25-0015 paragraph h=f03e6142 -->
She will not love you for free. She will love the intense feelings you can provide. Reject the false love that is offered to you for free and wrest from the feminine soul the love reserved for supreme moments. This is true love: the love that is normally refused us but is given when the sly one is in despair at having lost the man of her life forever.

<!-- seg 25-0016 paragraph h=ac3fda5a -->
Our hope that they will always be affectionate is vain. Equally vain is the hope that they will confirm through their attitudes the fidelity of feeling that they demand so much of us and proclaim they have.

<!-- seg 25-0017 paragraph h=c361d56a -->
When we study and understand the dark aspect of the feminine, we create against its fatal magnetism a resistance that comes from aversion. It is a resistance similar to the one they have against us. This resistance protects us and allows us to enjoy the pleasures of sex and love without risk.

<!-- seg 25-0018 paragraph h=375ce2dc -->
When we put them first in our lives, we are considered suckers, smothering, repellent, and clingy. When we put them second, we are accused of “not paying attention.” This means that it is no use worrying about pleasing them and that love, as it is normally understood, is nothing but nonsense. There will always be some invented excuse to justify and hide the real fact that we are not needed outside a utilitarian context.

<!-- seg 25-0019 paragraph h=9dd0dbf0 -->
Their capacity to displease us without fear of losing us is remarkable. They do it because they know exactly the limits imposed by our needs and attachments.
