12. How to Deal with Women Who Run Away

<!-- seg 13-0002 paragraph h=a6ef43a1 -->
I have seen many men suffering at the hands of women who attract them and then run away. There are also women who run away when the man wants a definitive answer about a love affair that ended unresolved.[^1] I have discovered a very good way for us to reach and capture these runaways with ease.

<!-- seg 13-0003 footnote n=1 h=f6751a54 -->
Alberoni (1986/n.d.) tells us that this sudden flight is intended to imprison the man, keeping him loving her and desiring her for his whole life.

<!-- seg 13-0004 paragraph h=efa91311 -->
The runaways induce us to pursue them through the continuous subliminal suggestion that they are prizes we do not deserve. Their deep-rooted belief that they are desirable overflows subliminally and induces us to chase after them, which must be avoided at all costs.

<!-- seg 13-0005 paragraph h=3b01336f -->
What we must do with the runaways is corner them mentally. How? By giving them an <i>ultimatum</i> so as to throw the responsibility into their hands, forcing them to make a decision within a very short deadline, creating situations that leave them with no way out. Let us look at this more closely.

<!-- seg 13-0006 paragraph h=3ae660c1 -->
The runaways’ strategies vary a great deal. Sometimes they show interest at the beginning but, as soon as you begin to show that you reciprocate, they avoid contact. They stop answering phone calls, stop writing, send word that they are not in when we look for them, etc. All with the intention[^2] of forcing you to pursue her. They may also make dates and not show up. The more you go after her, the more you confirm that you are interested and the more the runaway avoids you, happy as can be! The intention is to measure your degree of persistence, excite your desire, and keep you hooked. Some take pleasure in the act of rejecting.

<!-- seg 13-0007 footnote n=2 h=2f6d88ac -->
The unconscious has goals and intentions.

<!-- seg 13-0008 paragraph h=0e6a3568 -->
By way of example, and not as an encouragement to adultery, I will mention the case of a young man who flirted with a married woman only by telephone. Whenever they saw each other on the street, they both flirted, but the adulteress said nothing, claiming to be afraid of her husband. Nevertheless, she was always calling him and saying she was in love, etc., to attract him and confuse him. Suddenly, at the moment when the poor wretch was showing himself most interested and in love, the little rascal stopped answering his calls. Whenever the poor guy called and identified himself, the “tramp[^3]” hung up immediately. She was measuring his degree of persistence.

<!-- seg 13-0009 footnote n=3 h=c5a1eab4 -->
In the sense given by the Michaelis (1995) and Aurélio (FERREIRA, 1995) dictionaries.

<!-- seg 13-0010 paragraph h=24f6ce3d -->
Then, one day, the lovesick fellow manned up and called her. But before the lady could think, he said in a firm and decisive voice: <i>“If you don’t answer the next time I call, you will have given me the certainty that you don’t love me, and I will forget you forever.”</i> The next day, he called again and was answered kindly. He managed to turn the runaway into a good girl, because he cornered her in her own feelings. Unfortunately, she was a runaway who was cheating on her good husband. I am not praising the act of flirting with other men’s wives, but only using the example to illustrate how the psyche of the runaways works and how we must act to catch them.

<!-- seg 13-0011 paragraph h=7592ac40 -->
The runaways want to keep us emotionally hooked through doubt. Many just want to string us along, keeping us chasing after them without giving sex in return. They know that when they suddenly avoid us, we become dominated by our own feelings. They are very fond of making us waste our time, and they have fun watching us run after them like a bunch of imbeciles. They like to run away, run away, and run away; they take pleasure in this act because they know, instinctively, that they will leave unresolved doubts and questions in the man’s mind, and a person who has unresolved love or sexual issues with someone remains “tied.” The runaways’ intention is to keep us bound to them by means of doubt,[^4] preferably for all eternity. To turn the tables, all you need to do is give them an <i>ultimatum</i>. The <i>ultimatum</i> must be the notification of a situation that corners her, making her flights and evasions work as a decision in favor of ending the relationship. Let us look at a hypothetical example:

<!-- seg 13-0012 footnote n=4 h=861dc91e -->
For doubt creates an unbearable emotional irritation in the human being (PEIRCE, 1887/n.d.).

<!-- seg 13-0013 paragraph h=45495716 -->
1) The runaway attracts you, pretending to be interested or in love;

<!-- seg 13-0014 paragraph h=dba50f0b -->
2) You show interest and begin to be avoided by the runaway;

<!-- seg 13-0015 paragraph h=f63e993c -->
3) You reach her somehow, by letter or telephone, and tell her in a short, blunt, and decisive way, without the slightest room for discussion, more or less the following: <i>“If you don’t give me a clear answer by...</i>(deadline set by you)<i>, you will have given me the certainty that you don’t want anything more to do with me, and I will forget you forever.”</i>

<!-- seg 13-0016 paragraph h=4e257859 -->
That way you will have cornered her. The sly one may even keep running away for a while but, as the end of the deadline approaches, her flights become clear answers to your doubt, and she falls into despair as she realizes she has no way out. In this way we reach the unconscious desire that motivates her, and we will truly know whether the runaway wants something with us or not. It is an <i>ultimatum</i> with a chronological countdown that transforms even the most evasive and contradictory indecisions, attitudes, and flights into clearly defined situations that eliminate all doubt from our mind and from the mind of the slippery runaway.[^5]

<!-- seg 13-0017 footnote n=5 h=90b12984 -->
The intention of this ultimatum is not to force the woman to desire us, nor to change her feelings about us, but rather to discover the reality hidden behind the apparently contradictory behavior so that we can give our lives a suitable direction. It is not a strategy of manipulation but of counter-manipulation, that is, of dismantling manipulative wiles.

<!-- seg 13-0018 paragraph h=48692eaf -->
When we reach them by telephone, the runaway usually hangs up. What she wants is simply to have the pleasure of slamming the phone down on you. Get ahead of her: say objectively what you have to say and hang up first, robbing her of the pleasure.

<!-- seg 13-0019 paragraph h=aa3d4f3b -->
The runaways raise a lot of hell over the telephone. Being the most widely used means of personal communication nowadays, the telephone is the technological tool most used by the sly ones in their little games. Just to make it clearer, the runaways’ telephone torments are usually the following:

<!-- seg 13-0020 paragraph h=feac7ceb -->
• asking for or accepting your number, promising to call but not keeping the promise;

<!-- seg 13-0021 paragraph h=2f28a456 -->
• not returning your messages or not answering when you call, even while sitting right there next to the phone;

<!-- seg 13-0022 paragraph h=8338ac46 -->
• leaving the phone switched off full-time for a long period.

<!-- seg 13-0023 paragraph h=701f159b -->
In all the cases above, the damned woman wants to keep you after her, chasing her. She is getting satisfaction from the chase. What motivates her is the certainty that she is being sought and that she is rejecting the one who seeks her. When you finally reach her and ask for an explanation, the excuses are flimsy, ridiculous, and would not convince even a donkey.

<!-- seg 13-0024 paragraph h=ec728f7f -->
To break this hell and corner the sly one, you must hit her exactly at the point that motivates her: the certainty that, by avoiding you, she makes you want her more and more. By pursuing her with ever greater intensity, you are giving her certainties that you are more and more in love in the same proportion as you are avoided. Therefore, it is at this point that you must wound her[^6] squarely, breaking down all her motivations. How? By reaching her by some means (letter, telephone, a message through a friend, etc.) and communicating a sentence to her: that her next flight will give you the definitive certainty that she was a lying, fraudulent “tramp[^7]” from the very beginning, and will thus bring about a total and definitive break. In this way, you hit her at the nerve center, because the damned woman believes that, by running away, she is intensifying your passionate suffering and your doubts. If she suddenly learns that this attitude will unleash the opposite effects, she will be shocked, confused, and left with no way out. You will have created a cognitive dissonance. The sentence must be clear, direct, and terrible, leaving no room for any other interpretation. It must leave the runaway with no alternative other than to seek you out within a short deadline. No other alternative should remain because, if one does, she will not seek you out. If even so the runaway does not come back, then it is because she was really never any good and should have been scorned as leftovers from the beginning. Some examples of messages that can be sent by telephone, letter, or in person in such cases are the following:

<!-- seg 13-0025 footnote n=6 h=21a44264 -->
For she is wounding you in advance.

<!-- seg 13-0026 footnote n=7 h=c5a1eab4 -->
In the sense given by the Michaelis (1995) and Aurélio (FERREIRA, 1995) dictionaries.

<!-- seg 13-0027 paragraph h=9502ba93 -->
“If you don’t look for me by (date set by you), it’s because you were never any good, and I will never look for you again!”

<!-- seg 13-0028 paragraph h=41c67c6e -->
“If you don’t look for me by (date), don’t ever look for me again.”

<!-- seg 13-0029 paragraph h=40d346b7 -->
“I’m giving you one last chance to come back to me by (date); if you don’t, disappear from my life forever.”

<!-- seg 13-0030 paragraph h=6cb7b0fa -->
“Look for me by (date) or else disappear forever.”

<!-- seg 13-0031 paragraph h=d7f11da4 -->
She must feel the weight of your determination and the power of your sentence. It is almost never possible to reach them to speak to them in person, since they hang up the phone and usually, ridiculously, hide and avoid you in the street so that you feel as if you were a stalker. So one must have alternative means at one’s disposal. What matters is to reach them and shock them, hitting them hard in their feelings. This requires a lot of courage and willingness to lose.

<!-- seg 13-0032 paragraph h=d70c5c48 -->
Some runaways also like to torment their husbands and boyfriends by promising and avoiding sex. In this case, they avoid going to bed when the poor wretch needs it, or they promise to give it and refuse at the crucial moment. They usually promise him paradise during the day and invent excuses at night. The best thing to do in these cases is to find a way to throw the bomb back into her hands. One way to do this is to measure how long the refusal lasts and make this rhythm official, announcing that on the other days nothing will be expected, presenting this as her decision. It is usually also very effective to state explicitly that, by refusing sex, the runaway is morally authorizing us to exchange her for another, even if she denies it and does not formally articulate such authorization. Then the feminine imagination will go to work with jealousy, and perhaps the situation will be reversed.

<!-- seg 13-0033 paragraph h=5fd476d6 -->
Do not forget: when you make an appointment (a date, a phone call), you must corner her by means of deadlines. If you leave the agreement open, you will probably be cheated.

<!-- seg 13-0034 paragraph h=b9141980 -->
What feeds the runaways’ behavior is the unconscious idea that you will be available, even after many years, like a spare tire (they are so shameless that they even call this wile “keeping the spare”). They lean on this idea and do not feel the slightest need to face you.

<!-- seg 13-0035 paragraph h=1267a69c -->
The idea of being desired makes women happy:

<!-- seg 13-0036 paragraph h=6c8a5e09 -->
“The happiness of man is called ‘I want.’ The happiness of woman is called ‘He wants.’” (NIETZSCHE, 1884-1885/1995)

<!-- seg 13-0037 paragraph h=ddc6951d -->
Knowing they are desired is more than enough for the runaways. They nourish themselves unconsciously on the pursuit. They want to be pursued so that they can reject the pursuer. The possibility of rejecting gives them the feeling of being the hottest, the most desirable of all women on Earth. When they run away, they do so to induce pursuit and even, sometimes, to make a fuss, drawing the attention of everyone around them. Sometimes they initially send signals of interest to induce the male to seek them out but then reject him, telling their friends about their triumph. To get to them, first we must not pursue, and second, we must transform their flights into unmistakable decisions in favor of ending the relationship, into clear communications of disinterest. In this way, we destroy the doubts they try to instill in our mind, turning the spell back on them. The dynamite is thrown back into the hands of whoever lit the fuse.

<!-- seg 13-0038 paragraph h=1eeca42f -->
It is all a matter of psychological cornering. What matters is to leave her with no way out, so as to force her either to come running straight to you or to put an end, once and for all, to any doubts in your mind. The work consists in isolating the runaway in her own mental dungeon, making her confront her own contradictory feelings and desires. By creating a definitive situation that allows no doubt whatsoever, the real content of her feelings will show itself. Then you will know what you really mean to her, how you are seen, and what you are for, because there are many women who just want to keep us in reserve as a guarantee for old age or for some material or emotional emergency (the famous “spare,” or spare tire). I know of a case in which a woman kept a young man in reserve and later accepted him as her boyfriend when she became pregnant by another man, who had run off, in order to pin the paternity on him. Cases like this are frequent.

<!-- seg 13-0039 paragraph h=4963b87e -->
I have observed that the feminine unconscious seems to want to be cornered, to ask for a confinement that does not allow escape (evasions or excuses). As long as you allow any mental openings that make it possible to avoid responsibilities, the runaway will avoid you, blaming you for everything and considering you uninteresting. On the other hand, if you corner her mentally, you will be considered superior to the other males in intelligence, emotional strength, confidence, and determination. You will also communicate subliminally that you will not remain available for all eternity and that you have access to other, better females (more beautiful and more sincere) or more important things to do.
