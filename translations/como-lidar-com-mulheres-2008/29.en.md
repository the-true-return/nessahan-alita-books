28. On the Telephone

<!-- seg 29-0002 paragraph h=4f87f5cc -->
Women love a great deal through the ear, unlike us, who overvalue the visual side. They appreciate songs and whispers of love, and they get aroused on the telephone when we know how to use our voice and speech the right way.

<!-- seg 29-0003 paragraph h=4e88bb70 -->
With a telephone in hand, you will have only two weapons: your tone of voice and the content of your speech, the subjects you are going to talk about.

<!-- seg 29-0004 paragraph h=9a72b287 -->
Do not call before you have clearly and decisively in mind what you are going to say. Be kind but firm. Say what you have to say and withdraw. If you keep stretching out the contact unnecessarily, you will be seen as weak and needy. Plan what you are going to say, call, say it clearly and directly, and withdraw.

<!-- seg 29-0005 paragraph h=dbd4a1c0 -->
Beware of psychic standstills, that is, of hesitation. Psychic standstills are moments when our action is frozen by uncertainty about what we should or should not say, leaving us with nothing to talk about. It is better to finish what has to be said and hang up the telephone than to drag out the conversation and fall into a ridiculous silence because we do not know what to say. Having nothing to talk about in a telephone contact causes a loss of prestige because it indicates that we do not know what we want, that we are hesitant, wavering, indecisive men and, therefore, uninteresting.

<!-- seg 29-0006 paragraph h=4f2cc1b1 -->
One way to prevent a psychic standstill is to draw up a plan for the conversation in advance, carefully choosing the subjects. To make a mark on the feminine imagination, leading her to see you as a different kind of male, avoid at all costs the mechanical repetition of the same subjects that all the idiots bring up.

<!-- seg 29-0007 paragraph h=5c9ebc7f -->
Use a commanding tone of voice; be imperative.

<!-- seg 29-0008 paragraph h=bb02b279 -->
Do not wait for her to end the conversation. Take the initiative and hang up first. Save the “wish to talk more” for another occasion. The sly ones want to hang up on us, so rob her of the feeling of victory by hanging up first.

<!-- seg 29-0009 paragraph h=5ad698fb -->
Do not keep plying her with questions. This shows excessive interest and causes aversion, because only weaklings and needy men, incapable of conquering interesting females, show excessive interest in one particular woman.

<!-- seg 29-0010 paragraph h=085b3bb2 -->
Command the conversation; be the leader. At the same time, be protective. Show determination and a slight concern for her. As Riddick says to Jack: <i>“Maybe I care.”[^1]</i> Do not show excessive concern.

Do not return calls immediately. Let her call once or twice and only then call back. Surprise her by calling unexpectedly from time to time.

<!-- seg 29-0013 paragraph h=dc8ce28f -->
To keep feminine excitement at the highest possible levels and for most of the time, activate her imagination by saying the things that reach her. However, alternate, absenting yourself until she seeks you out. Continuous action in a single direction causes boredom. Observe how they alternate their conduct with us.

<!-- seg 29-0014 paragraph h=cfd7a00a -->
When we deal with women, whether on the telephone or in person, we need an arsenal of means that lead them to reveal their real intentions. We must have reactions at hand that prevent them from dodging clarity. It is common, for example, for certain women to take the initiative of calling or to send out signs of interest to draw you into contact by telephone or in person but, as soon as they are with you or listening to you, to fall silent or hit you with the disconcerting question: “What do you want?” Other times they simply order: “Talk.” In acting this way, they subliminally suggest that the interested party is you and not her. In suggesting this, they are setting themselves up as a prize. This subliminal suggestion must not be accepted and needs to be dismantled. To disarm it, all you need to do is create a situation that forces her to reveal whether she is really interested or not, so as to eliminate any shadow of doubt. If she starts toying with you, sending contradictory signals to confuse you, resolutely and without the slightest hesitation or fear create a definitive situation by telling her something more or less like this: <i>“Call only when you are really interested in me. If you</i> (the sly one) <i>do not call me within</i> <b><i>n</i></b> <i>days</i> (a deadline set by you) <i>I will know that you were never interested, and I will not wait any longer.”</i> Then hang up on her. With this procedure you force her to reveal her true intentions and you disarm the little game, because the situation allows no kind of confusion. The very attempt to confuse will unmask her. If the woman does not call, she will have revealed herself, and if she does call, so will she! By acting this way, you will be showing yourself to be a decisive and determined man who does not hesitate in his goals. Obviously, it will backfire if you are in love or attached, because this means “setting off a bomb” that hits only the one who is more in love, more attached, and more in need of the other.
