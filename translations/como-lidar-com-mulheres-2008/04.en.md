3. Precautions to take when dealing with sly women who try to cheat in love

<!-- seg 04-0002 paragraph h=744a3775 -->
Note 1. Never use this knowledge for evil (seducing several women at the same time, deceiving young virgins, seducing minors, etc.). Do not try to play the stud “alpha male” who bangs them all, because his fate is to be murdered, to catch venereal diseases, or to become impotent in every sense, including the sexual one, and to be replaced by up-and-coming beta males.

<!-- seg 04-0003 paragraph h=bb75337f -->
Note 2. This information is meant only to help well-intentioned men who are at a disadvantage in the fierce competition for females, and not to encourage male promiscuity. If you use it the wrong way, the fault will be entirely yours.

<!-- seg 04-0004 paragraph h=6f11675c -->
1. Never try to kiss her if she looks away as you approach.

<!-- seg 04-0005 paragraph h=6bab3142 -->
2. Excite her imagination by making her think about you constantly, preferably as a man absolutely different from the others[^1].

<!-- seg 04-0006 footnote n=1 h=12e37de5 -->
Without deceiving her, however. Truly acquire these characteristics.

<!-- seg 04-0007 paragraph h=c37a09de -->
3. Make a strong impression on her without showing off.

<!-- seg 04-0008 paragraph h=0725f306 -->
4. Be mysterious.

<!-- seg 04-0009 paragraph h=b5f8cfc3 -->
5. Hide your sexual intention until the moment to “strike.”

<!-- seg 04-0010 paragraph h=8dc54ceb -->
6. Steer the conversation toward her emotional problems, not yours. Do not talk about idiotic things.

<!-- seg 04-0011 paragraph h=ddc06860 -->
7. Wait patiently for trust to take hold[^2].

<!-- seg 04-0012 footnote n=2 h=d3fb5704 -->
And do not betray it. Live up to the trust placed in you, so as to keep right on your side in case she betrays your feelings.

<!-- seg 04-0013 paragraph h=e8beb305 -->
8. Keep the frequency of your conversations regular.

<!-- seg 04-0014 paragraph h=039d766d -->
9. Let her set the length of the conversations and of the intervals between one conversation and the next.

<!-- seg 04-0015 paragraph h=b2305427 -->
10. Never show haste or sexual urgency.

<!-- seg 04-0016 paragraph h=fc5507bf -->
11. Let her talk about sex, if she wants to, and show great knowledge of the subject.

<!-- seg 04-0017 paragraph h=205d4f40 -->
12. Make her dependent on your conversations.

<!-- seg 04-0018 paragraph h=345df139 -->
13. Agree with her often, but not always.

<!-- seg 04-0019 paragraph h=c3167562 -->
14. Do not monopolize the conversation. Let her talk freely. You should only listen and nudge the topics in the directions that matter, encouraging her to keep talking so as not to leave her with nothing to say.

<!-- seg 04-0020 paragraph h=659dc817 -->
Important: It is essential to perceive the type and depth of the openings given in order to make your advances accordingly. An advance that goes beyond or falls short of what is permitted results in failure.
