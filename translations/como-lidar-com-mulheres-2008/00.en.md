Abstract:

<!-- seg 00-0008 paragraph h=c164654d -->
The art of dealing with women in love demands of a man an appropriate inner state, one that allows him to resist feminine charms and fascinations, and a strategic knowledge that allows him to dismantle romantic trickery and attempts to induce him to fall in love.

This work should be read in a spirit of humor and solidarity, never in a spirit of revolt.

<!-- seg 00-0018 paragraph h=bac0be2a -->
This book teaches the art of dismantling and neutralizing female wiles in love and of protecting oneself against the emotional damage of passion, and it cannot be invoked as an incentive for, or as support for, any form of negative feeling. Its critical, direct, ironic, and incisive tone reflects only the pointing out of flaws, errors, and wiles.

This work does not support the formation of any sectarian group. The wiles denounced, unmasked, and described here correspond to feminine expressions, largely unconscious, of behavioral traits common to both genders. The profile outlined corresponds only to a specific type of woman: the one who is ruled by sentimental selfishness. The author makes no pronouncement on the percentage of incidence of this profile among the female population of the various countries.
