25. Binding Them to Us Through Their Feelings

<!-- seg 26-0002 paragraph h=64ac54da -->
A woman does not soften or bend in response to a man’s affection. Nor does she bend in response to brutality. To reach her and make her dependent, you must first of all give her security.

<!-- seg 26-0003 paragraph h=047ce74f -->
Your partner does not need affection and love first and foremost, but rather your power to protect her. Try offering only affection and love and you will see them trampled and rejected. If we are very (and only) affectionate, we will be seen as second-class males, incapable of giving protection. Be firm, speak in a deep tone of voice, treat her like a little girl. Exercise a protective authority and take command. Forbid unnecessary contact with other males or, if she resists, release her once and for all into a relationship with absolutely no commitment for either party. Do not allow the sly one to remain in indefiniteness. Do not be afraid of losing her. Be affectionate constantly, but not only affectionate.

<!-- seg 26-0004 paragraph h=c4a80c7c -->
We are currently living through a terrible crisis of masculine values. Men have become demasculinized, turning sensitive, romantic, sentimental, and clingy. Women sorely miss masculinity. This is where we must take hold of them and bind them to us.

<!-- seg 26-0005 paragraph h=fb7c7ba9 -->
Sentimentality, passion, attachment, romanticism, affectionateness, and sensitivity are feminine attributes. On the other hand, coldness, impetuosity, objectivity, firmness, cruelty, pitilessness, calm, determination, and security are masculine values. Such characteristics can be used for good or for evil. If you use them for evil, oppressing and exploiting your partner, you will be detested and you will wear horns. If you employ them for good, giving protection and guidance, you will receive love and fidelity. Use your masculinity for good. Emphasize the masculine in your nature in a conscious and directed way so as to completely master the situation.

<!-- seg 26-0006 paragraph h=94c69563 -->
Be passive in the relationship and you will also wear horns. Be active for evil and you will be betrayed just the same. Be active for good—firm, dominant, guiding, leading, protective—and you will have a great chance of receiving love, good-quality sex, and fidelity.

<!-- seg 26-0007 paragraph h=8ac828a3 -->
While remaining unattached and free of passion, give affection, protection, and care (but keeping your distance) to make her dependent, as she does with you. Do what no other man would do and become special. That way, her fear of losing you will be greater when you distance yourself in retaliation for some mistake (as happens with you when she distances herself to punish you). Furthermore, have ardent and wild sex, with no fussiness, no sentimentality, and no fear of impressing her. Females, even the anorgasmic ones, need to feel that they are desired.
<!-- QUERY: "sem o temor de impressioná-la" literally "without the fear of impressing her"; probably "without worrying about (failing to) impress her" or "without fear of shocking her". Kept the literal reading. -->

<!-- seg 26-0008 paragraph h=3c49209a -->
Cars and material possessions are not the only things that make the female dependent: care and protection do so as well. Compensate for your poverty and other deficiencies with distinctive behavior, superior to that of all other males. If you go about on foot, are poor, ugly, scrawny, fat, short, or potbellied, and if this cannot be remedied, look for other attributes through which you can develop. Be unique and superior in everything you can.

<!-- seg 26-0009 paragraph h=4340272a -->
Be capable of disliking your partner and at the same time caring for her as no other man would.

<!-- seg 26-0010 paragraph h=8a2ab6af -->
To bind them through their feelings, it is essential to establish the right kind of sympathy. The mistake most men make is to suppose that erotic sympathy will arise from hurrying to please and impress or from the fear of hurting her tastes by displeasing her. In the case of women, what actually happens is the opposite: sympathy for sex originates from a stance that is affectionate but active, protective, firm, distant, mysterious, and leading. Be the head of the relationship, the boss, the leader. Do not confuse erotic sympathy with friendly sympathy.

<!-- seg 26-0011 paragraph h=10a3f30d -->
Females like to look up when they talk. It is no accident that they like big men: they would give themselves to men fifteen or twenty meters tall, if such men existed. They want to be carried, to feel small. But there are several ways for us to be big, and not only in bodily stature. There are tall and short men who are stupid and childish; others are inert, lacking initiative. Such attributes have nothing to do with size. If you are tall, that is an advantage and should be taken advantage of. But this same advantage will be wasted and will turn into a disadvantage if you neglect your overall development. On the other hand, if you are short, old, potbellied, bald, poor, and on top of that without a car, you will have to develop other behavioral attributes to compensate for these deficiencies. Surpass your rivals in the right characteristics and you will take the lead. In the field of living together, the main attributes to develop are behavioral ones, although physical attributes also count. There is, unequivocally, a widespread prejudice against less physically gifted people of both sexes, but this prejudice can be overcome by developing the right behavioral characteristics.

<!-- seg 26-0012 paragraph h=4abcc05e -->
They want to be subdued by their own passion, and that is why they raise so much hell, defy, provoke, and rebel so much against coercive dominance. They do not want to be subdued by means of our passions, which would force them to satisfy those passions, but rather by means of their own passions. From the feminine point of view, the whole world must revolve around personal passions and feelings. Our feelings, passions, desires, and wishes do not interest them except insofar as they can be used to satisfy theirs. A great many people of the female sex see only themselves:

<!-- seg 26-0013 paragraph h=9b13e334 -->
“Narcissism and megalomania are characteristics common to women of all cultures. Certainly, they wear different disguises from one people to another.” (PACHECO, 1987, p. 40)

<!-- seg 26-0014 paragraph h=41782b50 -->
The greatest feminine difficulty is going against herself; that does emotional violence to them. Never push in that direction. When you come up against resistance, do not insist. Instead, excite the imagination and wait for the natural results, within the dynamics of her desires and not yours. Wait patiently and you will see the obstacles give way little by little. Imaginative excitement is similar to sexual excitement: it is slow but can be deep.

<!-- seg 26-0015 paragraph h=14fba4f2 -->
As Francesco Alberoni (1986) states, feminine eroticism is continuous and masculine eroticism discontinuous. Schopenhauer (2004) says the same:

<!-- seg 26-0016 paragraph h=4032d841 -->
“Man is by nature inclined to inconstancy in love; woman, to constancy. A man’s love diminishes markedly from the moment it attains satisfaction. Almost any other woman excites him more than the one he already possesses: he longs for variety. A woman’s love, on the contrary, increases precisely from that moment. This is a consequence of nature’s aim, which is directed toward the preservation of the species and is therefore directed as intensely as possible toward reproduction. A man can in fact easily beget more than a hundred children a year, if he had a large number of women at his disposal; a woman, by contrast, even with many men, can bring only a <i>single child</i> into the world per year (twins excepted). That is why <i>he</i> always has his eye on other women, while <i>she</i> fixes on a single man, for nature leads her instinctively and spontaneously to stay with the provider and protector of the future offspring.” (pp. 45–46)

This means that we like to begin, conclude, and start over, while our dear manipulators want the opposite: the permanence of masculine interest. They want to be permanently loved, desired, and pursued; they fight to maintain permanence and feel an aversion to ending, to conclusion. Indefiniteness is the means they resort to in order to achieve permanence: permanence of masculine passion, of pursuit, of the servility of males for all eternity. They want continuity out of fear of the future.

<!-- seg 26-0018 paragraph h=768a3206 -->
Our dear manipulators have three basic needs, which they cannot do without and for which they fight their whole lives: to be loved, desired, and protected. Note well: this does not mean that they want to love or desire the man, as some believe. They do not want to give back; they want only to receive and enjoy. And one more idiot who gives himself up will be welcome. They want to build a matriarchal clan made up of countless lovesick wretches eternally willing to give protection and love without receiving anything in return. They take pleasure in knowing that they are desired (Nietzsche, 1884-1885/1985) because through masculine desire they obtain love and protection, in addition to the countless advantages that unfold from them.

<!-- seg 26-0019 paragraph h=fa1d6cae -->
To keep the servility going, they excite our love and our desire without ever satisfying them fully, only partially, with the aim of keeping them alive indefinitely. They avoid satisfaction because they know that to satisfy is to conclude and that to conclude desire is to end dependence. Since what matters to them are their own loving feelings and not ours, they see no reason for any feeling of guilt or pity.

<!-- seg 26-0020 paragraph h=75886eb0 -->
To “counterattack,” we need only excite the three basic needs (to be desired, protected, and loved) without ever satisfying them fully, as they do with us, turning continuity back in our favor. If you let feminine desires be completely satisfied, your partner will feel secure, become snobbish, and stop giving you affection as she should. Believing that you are already caught, she will move on to the emotional imprisonment of others, and so on. The solution is to be equally contradictory, exciting, promising, but satisfying only partially. That way we preserve the feelings we want. The same strategies used by the sly ones against us can be redirected back at them, in this case as self-defense.

<!-- seg 26-0021 paragraph h=99f7dd05 -->
This logic makes an old and disturbing contradiction understandable. It explains why our love is rejected when we want them to love us and why we are sought out only by those we reject. What happens is that females come out of their inertia and devote themselves to taking care of the relationship only when they feel that their object of use is not very accessible or is drifting away. When the object is accessible, there is no problem, and the tendency is to relax, to grow careless. If you offer your love or interest to a woman for free, there will be no need for any work to obtain it, since what is most interesting will already have been handed over. Continued dedication requires continued indefiniteness, doubt, and insecurity. Leave her insecure and you will be the object of caresses, attempts at seduction, etc., aimed at subduing you. Enjoy it, and do not allow polarization.

<!-- seg 26-0022 paragraph h=51aa3c00 -->
Passion makes us repulsive because it conveys, among other things and at times, the message that we do not want to offer love but only to receive it. It also conveys the message that we are spineless. Since the feminine need is to be loved and protected, but never to love, if you show yourself needy or dependent you will be rejected, because softies cannot protect anyone and, what is more, they want to receive love and protection. A needy man is a man in need of love. A man in need of love is someone who wants to receive love and not to give love. He wants an emotional lifeline. That is precisely what scares them off.

<!-- seg 26-0023 paragraph h=fe617bf9 -->
Do not want to receive love, and do not want to receive sex. Become independent. Just offer love, protection, and support without actually giving them. Then your partner will try to “buy” them with her charms, and you will be able to enjoy yourself for as long as you manage to confuse her by remaining in indefiniteness. The very common idea that one receives love by giving love is a lie; it does not hold for humanoids with a subjective psyche. In truth, one receives love by offering love without actually giving it. This is the logic that really governs ridiculous “love.” We are animals and want only to satisfy our instincts, among them the need to receive protection, care, and affection. Nobody wants to give them, only to receive them. When people do give them, they do so with some other intention, even if a hidden one.

<!-- seg 26-0024 paragraph h=42f567ca -->
The man in love lacks the love of others and wants to make up for that lack. He is repulsive, on the one hand, because he does not offer what females need, but attractive, on the other, because he is a possible emotional slave.

<!-- seg 26-0025 paragraph h=bdcf9ab6 -->
Feminine love is not a reciprocation; it is a strategy for winning the three benefits mentioned. If the benefits are easily available, there will be no need whatsoever for dedication or for conquest. If they are absolutely inaccessible, on the other hand, there will likewise be no point at all in the latter.

<!-- seg 26-0026 paragraph h=84a28a15 -->
We can say that, for men, there are two possibilities in love: 1) receiving the partner’s heart; 2) handing one’s heart over to the partner. The strong man receives and the weak man hands over.

<!-- seg 26-0027 paragraph h=be3d9956 -->
The more we want our partners to desire us, love us, treat us well, etc., the less worried we will leave them and the less dedication we will receive. Feminine love is refractory to pressure. Pressure your partner to love you and she will detest you; she will develop an aversion. She will keep you captive only as a slave and will look for another man who ignores and despises her, to offer and give herself to. Trying to force women to love us is a waste of time:

<!-- seg 26-0028 paragraph h=911a8af2 -->
“Love is like faith: it will not be forced.” (SCHOPENHAUER, 2004, p. 41)

<!-- seg 26-0029 paragraph h=02cad72e -->
When we demand that they love and desire us, we are indirectly communicating that we have nothing to offer, since we want only to receive and not to give. Going the other way, when we hasten to flatter and please, we are indirectly communicating that we are submissive and that there is no need for anything to be done to hold on to us, no affection to be given, etc. The solution is not to demand, to offer and not to give. Offer a lot, give almost nothing, and demand nothing.

<!-- seg 26-0030 paragraph h=9e4d672f -->
Except in the sexual field, it is a mistake to satisfy them fully. The ideal is to excite their dreams and desires, filling them with hopes, promising and never fully keeping the promise, as they do with us. To preserve desire, we must not satisfy it fully.

<!-- seg 26-0031 paragraph h=d67c7025 -->
The fact that they wish to be loved and protected does not mean that they will automatically love those who hasten to love and protect them, but quite the opposite: they will love those who excite their imagination by holding out such promises without ever fully keeping them. The skill of the great seducer consists precisely in exciting the imagination, in convincing them, in making them believe, and then immobilizing them in doubt.

<!-- seg 26-0032 paragraph h=6d4eedad -->
We complain about the absurdity of our lady friends loving only the cads who do not love them, but in truth there is nothing absurd about it at all; it is perfectly logical. People only worry about things when they are losing them. Women are born with this knowledge, since they are instinctively governed by the logic of feelings.

<!-- seg 26-0033 paragraph h=74dd1aa2 -->
There is, moreover, a very simple and highly efficient way to bind very refractory, cold, and difficult women: it consists in seeking them out only for sex and ignoring them the rest of the time (without admitting it, of course). Try to have wild sex and then forget her for a while. Do not keep calling, watching, chasing after her, etc. Simply ignore her until she seeks you out again, and then receive her with the ardor and intensity of an animal. Make her feel like a wild female in heat. It usually works very well.

<!-- seg 26-0034 paragraph h=5bbff55c -->
The affection and love offered to you are meant to soften you, like the wave that slowly erodes and wears down the rock. They are tests: the loved and desired ones are the firm men, who never let themselves be bewitched. If you let yourself be fascinated, you will immediately be considered weak and seen as a bottom-rank male who is easily bent. This explains why feminine love does not meet masculine love and is directed at those who do not love them. Therefore, the more resistant we are to the spells of affection and love, the more affection and love (not infrequently hypocritical, because they carry an ulterior motive) we will receive, which can be used strategically so that we have the emotional servility of the sly ones at our disposal when we need it (inversion), as happens to us in relation to them. This strange logic is explained by the fact that females need protection and only tough guys are able to offer it. What security or protection could be offered by the kind, romantic, and sensitive men who are satisfied with a hypocritical “spiritual” love? They are not even capable of protecting themselves; they need the love of others to be happy and bring happiness to no one.

<!-- seg 26-0035 paragraph h=f8148a64 -->
The profile of the ideal man who stands up to feminine spells can be summed up as cold, distant, mysterious, impenetrable, silent, concentrated, active, leading, daring, courageous, indifferent, and protective. He is like nothingness, like the void, or like water, in which all attacks cancel themselves out.[^1]
