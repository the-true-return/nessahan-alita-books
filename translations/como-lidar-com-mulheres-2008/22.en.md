21. Lies

<!-- seg 22-0002 paragraph h=9dea98fb -->
Men are not the only liars, as everyone believes. Human beings of both sexes lie. Simply as a matter of focus, we will concentrate on the manifestation of this universal tendency in the women who interest us.

<!-- seg 22-0003 paragraph h=b2253372 -->
The intelligence of the sly ones is directed toward, and perfected in, the art of deceiving, lying, dissembling, convincing, manipulating, and simulating, with the aim of domesticating the male. This often absorbs their intelligence to the point of making them mediocre in other fields of human activity (VILAR, 1998), making them need masculine support in order to feel secure in difficult and dangerous situations. However, these women find their way easily amid the chaos of confused feelings, because the emotional aspect of relationships is the only one they pay attention to. Their judgments, decisions, choices, etc., are determined by the emotions that situations provoke and not by the objective external reality those situations consist of. Schopenhauer (2004) states the following:

<!-- seg 22-0004 paragraph h=c7817dc2 -->
“Just like the squid, woman too likes to hide in dissimulation and to swim in lies.

<!-- seg 22-0005 paragraph h=d0a14dd9 -->
Just as nature equipped lions with claws and teeth, elephants with tusks, wild boars with tusks, bulls with horns, and the cuttlefish with the ink that clouds the water, so too it provided woman with the art of dissimulation, for her protection and defense; and all the strength it conferred on man in the form of physical vigor and reason, it bestowed on woman in the form of this gift. Dissimulation is therefore inherent in her, which is why it suits foolish women almost as well as clever ones. For the same reason, making use of it on any occasion is as natural to her as it is for animals to use their weapons suddenly in an attack, and women feel that using it is, so to speak, their right.

<!-- seg 22-0006 paragraph h=83a9b07c -->
A completely truthful woman who does not dissemble is perhaps an impossibility. Precisely for this reason they easily perceive dissimulation in others, so that it is not advisable to try to use it in front of them (sic).” (p. 24)
<!-- QUERY: Schopenhauer, “On Women” (Parerga and Paralipomena), translated from the author’s Portuguese; the public-domain English version is T. <!-- seg 22-0007 paragraph h=be84079a -->
In part, the tendency of the sly ones to avoid the truth by taking refuge in lies and illusion is due to the natural feminine disposition to conceal, a symbolic reflection of their sexual anatomy. While the female sexual organs are internalized in the body, the male ones project outward. It is no accident that we feel pleasure in showing our “phallus erectus,” in exhibiting it, while they feel satisfaction in the opposite act, in concealing the vagina by closing their legs or covering it with their hands. If they notice that this bothers us, that we are dying to see what they are hiding, they get even more excited and hide it more. For the same reason, we want to make them open up, spread themselves completely open, in the sexual act and in their emotional life, because this is a victory over the resistance of the heart. We want them to turn inside out and show themselves.
<!-- QUERY: The original has “phalus erectus”; spelling corrected to “phallus erectus” (pseudo-Latin, kept as in the original). -->

<!-- seg 22-0008 paragraph h=70a6da22 -->
Men have only a story when they lie[^1]. Women have a story, tears, dramatic scenes, and simulated indignation when we do not believe their lies. Do not be moved by crocodile tears. You will never really know whether that lame excuse for something poorly explained is the truth or a lie. You will never be sure whether that melting does not hide an attempt to manipulate you into giving yourself up. Therefore, never believe anything.

<!-- seg 22-0009 footnote n=1 h=ebd9fc01 -->
There is a limit to the ability of human beings, including those of the female sex, to bear and express reality in acts and words. Beyond this limit, lies reign. However, as with almost everything in life, we are much more unconscious than conscious of this tendency. In this chapter we are dealing with the feminine form of expression of the universal human tendency to lie.

<!-- seg 22-0010 paragraph h=f2291e73 -->
Through falseness and lies, the weakest males, that is, the easiest to convince and tame, and the strongest, who believe nothing and scorn all attempts at deception, are identified and marked for the functions that correspond to them by vocation.

<!-- seg 22-0011 paragraph h=fd583862 -->
Generally, it is not advisable to run after lies in order to unmask them. The drain on energy can be high, and the little witch’s satisfaction will be complete when she sees you being manipulated like an imbecile. Prefer to accept them and encourage them up to a point so untenable that it becomes ridiculous, making it evident that you always knew everything and never let yourself be deceived, or else up to a point where that lie becomes useful.

<!-- seg 22-0012 paragraph h=82df0558 -->
It is not advisable to assault their nature by trying to force them to be sincere. It is best to accept them as they are and take advantage of the situation:

<!-- seg 22-0013 paragraph h=829d0952 -->
“Women above all, who are essentially and always actresses, who like to impress themselves by impressing others, and who are the first to be deceived when they play out their nervous melodramas, women—we repeat—are the true black magic of magnetism. Thus it will be impossible for magnetizers who are not initiated into the supreme arcana and not assisted by the lights of the Kabbalah always to master this fleeting and refractory element. To be master of a woman, one must distract her and deceive her skillfully, letting her suppose that it is she herself who is deceiving. This advice, which we offer here especially to magnetizing physicians, might also, perhaps, be useful in married life.” (LÉVI, 1855/2001, p. 225)
<!-- QUERY: Lévi, Dogma and Ritual of High Magic, translated from the author’s Portuguese; compare Waite’s public-domain “Transcendental Magic” (1896). -->

<!-- seg 22-0014 paragraph h=d541a231 -->
When we accept attempts at deception and pretend to believe the lies, or when we pretend to want exactly what we do not want in order to be contradicted, this anti-manipulation defense is in action.

<!-- seg 22-0015 paragraph h=9dfdd731 -->
Accept being “put one over on” consciously a few times. Although it seems like a weakness, it is a strength that few possess. Let her think she is deceiving you. The need to lie and deceive is inherent in sly females and is part of their instinctive selective strategies for mating[^2]. Superior males regard such attempts at trickery and deception as silly, childish games that in no way belong in their lives: they see them as a problem that is not theirs. Then the women seek them out without knowing why. The males who are emotionally affected by this show themselves to be weaker and tend to be traded in. The one who tries least to deceive you should be the most suitable for a stable relationship.

<!-- seg 22-0016 footnote n=2 h=9c88a41a -->
For in this way they, or rather their unconscious, discover who the most intelligent ones are, those who do not let themselves be deceived, and select them.

<!-- seg 22-0017 paragraph h=17e859db -->
It is difficult to find women who can bear the weight of the truth:

<!-- seg 22-0018 paragraph h=61823efd -->
“Even considering that there are kinder women, they hardly want to displease anyone by telling the truth, because the truth often shows the harshness of human madness.” (PACHECO, 1987, p. 76)

<!-- seg 22-0019 paragraph h=5132b83d -->
Rebelling against other people’s inevitable lies is a weakness. Rebel against the lies you have told yourself and against your naiveté in believing in enchanting feminine magic.

<!-- seg 22-0020 paragraph h=e5392f66 -->
It is extremely difficult to accept lies and attempts at deception from someone we love. Once, a friend of mine discovered that a woman he loved very much was lying on the phone. He detected hesitations and inconsistencies in her speech that clearly indicated something strange was going on. He felt unbearable pain because, until then, he still believed in human beings, particularly in women. He fought in vain against the pain of being deceived, without any result. He was desperate.

<!-- seg 22-0021 paragraph h=46ec0f35 -->
Suddenly, he discovered that the pain came not from the lie itself but from his inability to accept it as such. Then he understood that we have to accept lies as inherent in human nature, including feminine nature. And more: we have to accept the fact, when it is incontestable, that our noblest, purest, and most sublime feelings will be trampled on and scorned. The suffering came from several mistaken assumptions and expectations on his part regarding the opposite sex. When he discovered them, he felt great relief. The woman he liked was there, most likely with another guy; she had just called putting on an act, and he had simply accepted the fact and ignored it, regarding it as something that was none of his business. And in fact it no longer was.

<!-- seg 22-0022 paragraph h=25798ac0 -->
We nurture many false expectations regarding the female sex. They are expectations that were drilled into us from childhood and that only do us harm. We have to tear the root of evil out of our hearts. The main root is passion, but there are many others.

<!-- seg 22-0023 paragraph h=35516997 -->
There is in the female sex a continual pleasure in deceiving and dissembling in the field of love. Amorous deception gives them satisfaction[^3]. I cannot state that this is universal, but I am sure it is a frequent characteristic. Therefore, skepticism is man’s greatest weapon for defending himself, and credulity his greatest weakness. Cultivate extreme skepticism and beware of credulity.

<!-- seg 22-0024 footnote n=3 h=974087a4 -->
In men, this tendency to deceive one’s neighbor occurs less frequently in the field of love and more frequently in other fields.

<!-- seg 22-0025 paragraph h=d4bedbbb -->
Women are usually very patient in inducing credulity. Always resist and, on top of that, encourage them to lie even more. Pretend to believe, unmasking them only after you have in hand several proven lies with which to surprise and unmask them. Never let her know whether or not you are aware that you are the target of attempts at deception.

<!-- seg 22-0026 paragraph h=91518254 -->
The study of feminine lies and of the corresponding behavioral patterns is usually very useful. But to do so, we have to accept lies as they are, without rebelling.

<!-- seg 22-0027 paragraph h=cd65572c -->
A notable lie that does a lot of damage is that companionable, sensitive men are desirable and drive women wild. Observation reveals that such men are in fact tiresome because they do not provoke intense emotions. Victimized by this lie, many men try to fit this deceptive pattern of the ideal man and are astonished when they get results opposite to the ones they were aiming for.

<!-- seg 22-0028 paragraph h=014a9987 -->
Never forget: they lie when they describe the ideal man.
