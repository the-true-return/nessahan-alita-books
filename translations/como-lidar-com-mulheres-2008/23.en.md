22. Infidelity

<!-- seg 23-0002 paragraph h=0a4e2005 -->
Infidelity is a universal characteristic. All human beings, including those of the female sex, are unfaithful, even if they are not at all aware of it. Let us now look at how infidelity takes on a feminine form of manifestation.

<!-- seg 23-0003 paragraph h=27d731b2 -->
In present times, the situation is serious. It is very hard to find partners who are any good for marriage. Many women are acquiring the habit of exposing themselves to betrayal in subtle ways, facilitating it through ambiguous, innocent-looking situations, which they usually describe as “meaning no harm” and which confuse us completely when we are not experienced enough to unmask them. Such situations are in fact the beginnings of involvement with other males or, at the very least, of voluntary and conscious exposure to their desires. Because of their ambiguous character, they provide unfaithful women with a comfortable refuge in which to expose themselves and camouflage their true intentions, confusing their partners and dodging their possible and just anger.

<!-- seg 23-0004 paragraph h=8d39d339 -->
An effective camouflage for feminine infidelity consists in playing innocent, pretending not to notice or understand the meaning of certain acts that unequivocally betray subtle infidelities[^1]. The immediate effect of such acts is to provoke doubts in our minds that very effectively hinder unmasking them through accusations, driving us mad in the midst of the confusion. The obvious and the evident are denied to the very last moment. Hence the importance of not wasting our time trying to force them through arguments to admit the shady character of what they do, and of confining ourselves to announcing unilaterally the attitudes we disapprove of and the consequences they entail, then resolutely taking the appropriate measures.

The reasons that motivate them to get involved with us are many, and not only love, as they usually claim falsely. Generally, love is the last of the reasons for which they enter into a commitment, an engagement, or a marriage. Let us analyze this more closely.

Husbands/fiancés/boyfriends serve only to provide material and/or emotional support through the subservience of infatuation. This is the reason why they are not normally loved and must be sincere, honest, and hardworking. Present-day wives will not infrequently prefer to truly love insensitive men who are not their husbands. In modern Western society, marriage is an almost bankrupt institution, in a rapid process of extinction. I know several women who married one man while truly loving another. They do it with complete naturalness, as if this unspeakable crime against true love were absolutely legitimate and just. They do not see it as an aggression against the love of the soul.[^2] Schopenhauer (2004) states the following about marriage:

It is worth remembering that adultery satisfies a feminine fantasy (CALIGARIS, 2005 and CALIGARIS, 2006). Husbands, in our present-day society, have three purposes:

1) to provide material and emotional security;

<!-- seg 23-0013 paragraph h=8b8c2d6e -->

Let us now deal with this last function.

<!-- seg 23-0016 paragraph h=693ff075 -->
In general, marriage is a trap for the man (SCHOPENHAUER, 2004). After being drawn in, hooked, and caught, the husband serves certain desires of the feminine unconscious, the main one being the fantasy of being a mixture of courtesan and defenseless princess awaiting a knight. It should be noted that outbursts of passion and libido usually happen not within marriage but outside it. And one of the reasons for this is that the wife needs to feel like a princess abducted by a villain or a dragon. The lover, then, embodies the archetype of Prince Charming, of the knight who rescues her from pain, suffering, and imprisonment. Obviously, after the princess marries the prince, he turns into a husband and, therefore, into a new villain, and the cycle repeats itself. The intense emotions of adultery, or of the betrayals in novels in general, are provided by the husband/boyfriend/fiancé, with his constantly threatening presence, and not only by the lover himself, as it seems at first sight. That is the reason why the lover, when he marries the adulteress, stands a good chance of later being betrayed by her. Once he is married, the roles change, and the feminine fantasy can no longer be satisfied without a new extramarital passion.[^3]

They want to add to their matriarchal nest as many males as possible, in a hierarchical scale defined by the intensity of the passions each one arouses. This is a prehistoric inheritance that runs counter to the patriarchal tendency, equally rooted in a remote past.

Therefore, it is absolutely normal that we do not want anyone near our territory except those we authorize. Do not be ashamed, and do not accept being told that you are jealous or insecure when you want your female to keep her potential rivals a hundred kilometers away. Do not accept for no reason, without satisfactory explanations, that she let males come close. It is a legitimate masculine right.

Nowadays, marriage is more and more a partnership in which the husband puts in the labor and the wife puts in the horns. The promise to give good-quality love and sex is never kept. There is no advantage in being married:

<!-- seg 23-0029 paragraph h=4ff40002 -->
“The usual goal of the so-called career of young men is none other than to become a woman’s beast of burden. Among the best of them, woman appears as a rule as a sin of youth” (SCHOPENHAUER, 2004, p.86)

The situation today is no different from the one that existed in Schopenhauer’s time. Experience shows that good, honest, hardworking men are usually considered dull and flavorless, and they end up sharing the female with males considered more interesting while they fulfill the function of giving material support, of providers. In other words: they buy horns believing they are buying love. The horns are the payment for the subservience that comes from giving one’s whole heart. And on top of that, the blame is usually thrown on the husband himself:

We are not judging the fact that a woman flirts with or has sexual relations with several men. Such behavior is none of our business and is not our problem. It is not up to us to judge other people’s behavior, except as regards the emotional harm it may cause us. We are in no way interested in suppressing other people’s freedom or violating feminine free will. Of course the woman has every right to do whatever she wants, as long as she acts with sincerity. The wile denounced here consists only in deceiving, dissembling, and pretending to be a saint in order to enjoy the benefits a monogamous woman would deserve (something rare nowadays), and in wanting to induce us to believe that visibly compromising behaviors are innocent, underestimating our intelligence. The problem lies in amorous cheating and not in the fact that a woman changes partners or keeps more than one relationship at the same time. The sly ones do this to avoid the bad consequences of their own actions and to enjoy the intensification of emotions in carrying out a forbidden act.

Then you will see her dodging at all costs.
