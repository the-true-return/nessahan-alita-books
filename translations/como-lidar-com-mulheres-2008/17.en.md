16. The Tests

<!-- seg 17-0002 paragraph h=707406cb -->
The human female is essentially treacherous in love:[^1] she incessantly asks the male to give himself over but, at the same time, considers those who do so feeble and uninteresting, betraying them with others who are stronger and do not love them.[^2]

<!-- seg 17-0003 footnote n=1 h=0d0bfc9d -->
Just like the male, but in another form. I am referring here to the treacherous character contained in the act of demanding love while intending not to give it in return. It is worth remembering once again that we are dealing with characteristics of which most women do not show themselves to be conscious.

<!-- seg 17-0004 footnote n=2 h=906d942c -->
In masculine betrayal, this incessant demand for the surrender of feelings followed by abandonment and disinterest is much less frequent. Masculine betrayal revolves around sexual surrender and not sentimental surrender. A man cheats because he wants sex in itself. A woman cheats because she wants to experience intense feelings.

<!-- seg 17-0005 paragraph h=a63dbdef -->
This treacherous amorous essence arises from the need to test masculine worth and from the duplicity of her desire.[^3] The demands for surrender, as well as the recriminations and the games of jealousy, aim to test the quality of the breeder and protector of her offspring. Her intention is to verify how sure the man is of himself, of his strength, and of his worth.

<!-- seg 17-0006 footnote n=3 h=1a878218 -->
Feminine desire has two faces. One face corresponds to the desire to be fertilized by the carrier of the best genes, that is, to have sex. The other face corresponds to being preserved from everything that is unpleasant and, in general, dangerous. In this second case, the desire is to be protected, to have a provider, an emotional slave, etc. The man will be assigned one role or the other according to his profile and conduct.

<!-- seg 17-0007 paragraph h=b269f411 -->
Women usually test us by pretending to be disappointed with us, treating us as if we were brats, little boys guilty of reprehensible mischief, with the aim of activating childhood memories in our mind and thus forcing us to see them as stern mothers. It is also common for them to attack our points of view and conceptions, often labeling them childish, aiming to shake our morale so that we doubt our own worth. Through these procedures they will compare us to other males and will conclude that we are superior to those who wavered and doubted themselves.

<!-- seg 17-0008 paragraph h=7fe8b01d -->
Attentions and kindnesses toward other males are another type of test they use. In this way, they find out whether or not we feel inferior to other men. If we react with jealousy, they conclude that we are feeble, and this will show them two things: 1) that we believe the other man can fascinate her more than we can; 2) that we are afraid of not finding another, better female and are therefore incompetent as men. Therefore, it is necessary for us not to be jealous. But this will not be possible as long as we feel passionate love. For this reason, and for this reason only, we must totally avoid love and infatuation. Such feelings are debilitating and make a man uninteresting, even though the opposite is said.

<!-- seg 17-0009 paragraph h=1dc5a3e2 -->
The good ones are seen as feeble and insecure. Unfortunately, women love bad and strong men, without love and without feelings,[^4] because it is precisely these men who convey to them the security they need (or at least that is what they feel, since that is how the feminine unconscious reads this fact). They reason unconsciously: <i>“If I manage to attract the affection of this demon, I will be protected.”</i> That is why mobsters and powerful men have so many women. The feminine unconscious is irresistibly attracted by power and by wickedness[^5] as moths are attracted to light. Of course, these guys do not treat them badly; they are absolutely fake and affectionate. They promise them heaven without ever giving it to them, and they excite their imagination. And they season the relationship with fear. Even so, a woman normally does not fear the fearsome man, because she trusts in her power to manipulate him:

<!-- seg 17-0010 footnote n=4 h=13f1cf93 -->
Alberoni (1986/n.d.) says that “To achieve his goals, the seducer cannot have sincere feelings; he must always pretend.” (p. 167) and adds: “Men do not understand, in general, why women feel so attracted to scoundrels, why they are so intolerant of them and so indulgent toward the great seducer.” (p. 74)

<!-- seg 17-0011 footnote n=5 h=7d702564 -->
I defend the hypothesis that this tendency is due to an inverted perception of reality that leads them to confuse good with evil and right with wrong. Judgment becomes clouded by the invasion of instincts and feelings.

<!-- seg 17-0012 paragraph h=b288b703 -->
“A fragile woman can easily dominate a muscular murderer through sexual seduction, a fact that is well known to all.” (PACHECO, 1987, p. 119)

<!-- seg 17-0013 paragraph h=699fb387 -->
That is why love is offered to the fearsome:

<!-- seg 17-0014 paragraph h=4af1d58f -->
Let there be valor in your love! With the weapon of your love you must confront the one who inspires fear in you. (NIETZSCHE, 1884-1885/1985, emphasis mine)
<!-- QUERY: Zarathustra, “Of Old and Young Women,” from the author’s Portuguese; Common: “Let your love be with valour! With your love ye shall assail him who inspireth you with fear!” “Grifo meu” (emphasis mine), but no emphasis is marked in the extracted text; check the PDF. -->

<!-- seg 17-0015 paragraph h=089a2a05 -->
Instinctively, they sense that the fearsome man will make a good protector if he is dominated by means of seduction. LÉVI (1855/2001) wrote:

<!-- seg 17-0016 paragraph h=bab1f9f5 -->
“If the angels were also women, as modern mysticism represents them, Jehovah acted as a very prudent and very wise father when he put Satan at the gate of heaven. A great disappointment for the self-love of honorable women is to discover that the man with whom they had fallen in love is, at heart, good and irreproachable, when they had taken him for a bandit. The angel then abandons the good man with contempt, saying to him: ‘You are not the Devil!’ Disguise yourself, then, as the Devil, as perfectly as possible, you who want to seduce an angel. Nothing is allowed to a virtuous man. ‘Who, after all, does this man take us for?’ say the women. ‘Does he believe, perhaps, that we have less morality than he does?’ Everything, however, is forgiven a libertine. ‘What better can you expect from such a being?’ The role of the man of great principles and unassailable character can only constitute a power with women who have never needed to be seduced [the desperate ones whom men reject]; all the others, without exception, adore bad men.” (p. 337).
<!-- QUERY: Lévi, Dogme et rituel de la haute magie, translated from the author’s Portuguese; A. E. Waite’s public-domain version (Transcendental Magic) could be used instead. The bracketed comment is the author’s. -->

<!-- seg 17-0017 paragraph h=1624e4d0 -->
Unfortunately, experience has confirmed this many times up to the present. I hope one day to confirm the opposite.

<!-- seg 17-0018 paragraph h=0a724fb6 -->
I am not praising the behavior of the wicked, but only pointing out some characteristics of their personalities that the good, domesticated, and civilized man lacks. Being bad is as senseless and self-destructive as being good (NIETZSCHE, 1886/1998). The solution is not to be a real monster but, to paraphrase Eliphas Lévi (1855/2001), to disguise ourselves as demons as perfectly as possible in order to seduce these angelic beings.

<!-- seg 17-0019 paragraph h=cbedf851 -->
If you think that being a nice guy is enough to be loved, change your mind. Otherwise, hell on earth will be waiting for you. If you are truly wicked, you will have many problems and a short life. Be beyond good and evil. Extract the good that there is in evil and take it for yourself. Remove the evil that there is in good and throw it away.

<!-- seg 17-0020 paragraph h=b623a5e5 -->
The psychological tortures aim to test and select the best breeder and protector of offspring, even in the case of those women who insist on saying that they do not want to marry. The most fearless, cruel, and unfeeling[^6] is the chosen one. Those who fear losing their companion, who hurry to please her and submit to her whims, are considered useless for sex because they are emotionally feeble and, if they are not discarded immediately, they are marked out to perform the mere function of providers or emotional slaves.

<!-- seg 17-0021 footnote n=6 h=ef8e15b2 -->
Even the wickedness of cruelty and unfeelingness is relative, since these attributes can be directed toward fighting evil. Therein lies the origin of the inverted selective criterion that governs the feminine mind in a confused way. They know by instinct, and not by reasoning, that such characteristics can be used in their favor, but they do not let themselves be held back by the fact that, at the same time, these characteristics can also be highly harmful to them.

<!-- seg 17-0022 paragraph h=2a88dc54 -->
The more you pressure her to love you, give you sex, and stay by your side, the more repulsive you will be. The point is that a woman’s dynamics are governed by the following principle: her loves are directed only at those who do not need her, preferably in no sense at all, because women want the best genes. The more you run after her, the worse it will be.

<!-- seg 17-0023 paragraph h=f5b64626 -->
When the human female discovers a male who does not need her, her unconscious works on the idea that he is very good, very worthy and strong, that he must have many beautiful women available, etc. Then she will desire him, but the matter does not end there. The guy will be tested.

<!-- seg 17-0024 paragraph h=6adeee22 -->
Only the tough and unfeeling pass these hellish tests. The key to this is to feel nothing, not to love, not to be in love. Then the tests will seem absolutely ridiculous to us and will not affect us. The woman will go away, wait a few days, and then come back. She will go without calling you for a long time and finally give in. She will refuse sex to the extreme limit only to then throw herself naked on top of you, devouring you. She will offer herself insistently, not out of tenderness, as you would like, but because she will feel aroused without understanding why. And you must never tell her this, obviously.

<!-- seg 17-0025 paragraph h=95491983 -->
The closer the couple’s relationship, the more terrible the mental hells will be and the more promising the opportunities for inner training. If you defeat the little she-devil you live with, it will be easier to defeat the others who cross your path in the future.

<!-- seg 17-0026 paragraph h=5dddcc71 -->
Owing to a certain unconscious resentment against males,[^7] the insincere ones may “torment” them mercilessly, unless they are severely dominated.[^8] The strategies of “torment” (Erich Fromm’s sadistic love) are subtle and hard to detect, but they are normally based on the same element: submission through the passion that arises from the need for affection. Resist the charm of fragility and you will be unbeatable.

<!-- seg 17-0027 footnote n=7 h=96d17dbc -->
It is a hatred whose origins are linked to unpleasant experiences with the masculine throughout life, especially in childhood. I do not rule out the hypothesis that it is an archetypal trait activated by contact with the father. If it did not exist as a latent possibility prior to its manifestation, it would not arise in the feminine psyche.

Not only our emotional strength but also our intelligence is tested by means of fallacious and naive arguments that serve to cover up shady attitudes and little games.
