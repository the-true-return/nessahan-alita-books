4. A little about approach and conquest

<!-- seg 05-0002 paragraph h=f20b736b -->
With the aim of helping our male friends find partners with whom they have great affinity, and not of encouraging promiscuity, let us go a little deeper into the subjects of approach and conquest.

<!-- seg 05-0003 paragraph h=a43a20f3 -->
Most studies devoted to this field are meant only to teach men to conquer the greatest possible number of women in order to fornicate, and nothing more. That is the goal of most studies in seductology. Let us occupy this field in order to provide a different alternative: not to conquer many women but to win the best partners (or, if you prefer, the least bad ones) for a stable relationship. Logically, if we are able to conquer a woman whom we like very much and with whom we have great compatibility and affinity, we will have less of a tendency to be promiscuous than if we are dissatisfied with the companion we have at our side.

<!-- seg 05-0004 heading h=fc469844 -->
The impulse to possess

<!-- seg 05-0005 paragraph h=2af73b91 -->
When we see a desirable woman, our first and strongest impulse is to possess her immediately. We would like her to undress at that very moment and offer herself totally to us. Nothing else crosses our mind. We are seized by a kind of suffering, the suffering of lust, which often borders on despair. We become blind to everything else; we only want to possess that delicious female, we want to enter her, to unite, to be together, to fuse and disappear inside that marvelous body. We know that this suffering will be relieved only if we possess her; otherwise, the dissatisfaction will accompany us for a long time, until we forget that woman completely. It is precisely this unthinking impulse that ruins everything.

<!-- seg 05-0006 heading h=3590927c -->
A first mistake

<!-- seg 05-0007 paragraph h=16cf3648 -->
Driven by this impulse, we immediately show our intention. We let what we want show through. We lose control over ourselves, and our acts no longer belong to us; they become autonomous. This is our first mistake, because it has the opposite effect to the one desired, making the desired woman see us as a mere harasser and consider us, unconsciously, a mere beta male (I am not recommending that we be alpha males) desperate from having been rejected by females. Besides that, her unconscious goals of being loved and desired will already have been achieved. Why would she, who does not much like sex or men, need to get involved with us if we have already surrendered and offered ourselves on a silver platter? For the woman, the situation is settled; there is no problem that needs solving, and that is why she shows no interest, since the mere confirmation of male interest is enough to satisfy her. The sly one senses that the donkey will be tied to the tree and that she can find it whenever she wants; she feels that the dog will always come when she snaps her fingers...

<!-- seg 05-0008 paragraph h=ed7dea2d -->
Most of the time, the woman and the man are not conscious of this process and simply act and react automatically, by instinct.

<!-- seg 05-0009 heading h=cd9a306f -->
What should be done

<!-- seg 05-0010 paragraph h=afe5cb3f -->
We would get better results if, instead of flaunting our brutal sexual interest, we simply aroused in the woman some interest in us and approached her only much later, after this interest had taken hold. Here is where the problem begins.

Lustful blindness leads to error

<!-- seg 05-0012 paragraph h=c484647d -->
Before the desired woman, the man seized by lust finds no other way than to throw himself at her in order to obtain copulation as quickly, objectively, and directly as he possibly can. And it is precisely this desperation to take a shortcut that ruins everything.

<!-- seg 05-0013 paragraph h=865780f5 -->
The man in such a condition does not want to violate the woman. He believes, in his passionate madness, that his feelings are already being returned or are about to be, when in fact he is causing growing repulsion. If he persists in this insanity, he will soon be committing sexual harassment without realizing it.

<!-- seg 05-0014 paragraph h=506e1e24 -->
If the woman in question is a tramp (a woman of bad character, insincere, who likes to harm others, a trait that does not depend on how many partners she has), she will encourage him more and more with ambiguous behavior in order to have the pleasure of harming him in the end. If she belongs to the circle of the few honest people who exist on Earth, she will quickly eliminate all the man’s doubts so that he cannot hold on to any further hope. Unfortunately, the first case is much more frequent than the second.

Directions in which they are not armored

<!-- seg 05-0016 paragraph h=1722e18c -->
A woman’s interest is aroused by the right kind of impressionism. To impress, here, means to leave a mark, to get oneself noticed, to stand out, and to make oneself remembered. Leave your mark on her imagination, just as she does with you.

They may well be insensitive to letters, to declarations of love and of sexual interest, but they are not insensitive to money, to mystery, to the resolute defiance of their convictions, to being relegated to the background in favor of their rivals, to a man’s singular inattention, to scorn for their beauty, to fear of a storm or other dangers, etc. In these fields, their vulnerability is total, just as ours is when it comes to the offer of tenderness and sex and to voluptuous beauty. On reaching female feelings

<!-- seg 05-0021 paragraph h=0d7d2922 -->

<!-- seg 05-0026 paragraph h=7fa9b646 -->
2. Look at her in a penetrating, serious, and fearless way;

<!-- seg 05-0027 paragraph h=c601edc3 -->

<!-- seg 05-0028 paragraph h=d32c9621 -->

<!-- seg 05-0029 paragraph h=ab553ab8 -->

How to arouse interest

<!-- seg 05-0038 paragraph h=2ba0c901 -->

Losing the fear of the first contact

How to approach
