6. Some hellish female wiles
<!-- QUERY: This chapter does not appear in the original’s table of contents, and its number "6." coincides with the number the contents give to the previous chapter. <!-- seg 08-0002 heading h=72a0fa06 -->
The wile of suddenly disappearing

<!-- seg 08-0003 paragraph h=197cb215 -->
You are as happy as can be with your girlfriend. Everything is going well, and she is behaving marvelously. So you lower your guard, confident that she is being absolutely sincere and that she will not betray you.

<!-- seg 08-0004 paragraph h=938bdcce -->
Suddenly, without the slightest warning, she disappears, stops looking for you, and/or stops answering your calls and does not call. You have fallen into a trap: she was only waiting for the right moment to pull away so that you would suffer.

<!-- seg 08-0005 paragraph h=0f405d50 -->
For days she watched you and behaved so as to build trust. You were baited like a fish, and now the hook has been pulled. The sly one is testing you; she wants to be sure the fish is well hooked. If you chase after her, you will fall even deeper into the servitude of passion. If you do not chase after her, you feel you will lose her. What was your mistake? Letting yourself get drunk on the good moments.

<!-- seg 08-0006 paragraph h=8a5223ce -->
What to do now? I see two possibilities. The first is to face inwardly the anguish and torment that are eating you alive. The emotional pain that oppresses you comes from passion, and passion is totally internal. Whoever overcomes passion within himself and develops the will manages to win these “tugs-of-war” simply by disappearing. The second is to reach her by some means and corner her with an ultimatum. Both are painful, and neither can guarantee the return of your beloved. I in no way suggest that you humiliate yourself by pursuing her desperately, because that will make everything worse.

<!-- seg 08-0007 paragraph h=89788f61 -->
If you had behaved correctly, she would not have sabotaged you treacherously in this way. She pulled this crooked trick on you because she noticed that you had begun to surrender. If you had not surrendered, the sly one would still be trying to convince you to do so and would be at your side, since that is the only thing that keeps them attached to us: the attempt to induce us to surrender our hearts.

<!-- seg 08-0008 heading h=5c0be49f -->
The wile of sabotaging and pretending nothing is happening

<!-- seg 08-0009 paragraph h=f7afc6ec -->
All of a sudden your girlfriend becomes strange, cold, and distant and treats you differently. You get worked up, and your mind races, thinking a thousand things. The sly one denies that there is anything strange, plays dumb, and acts as if nothing were happening. You question her and want to wring an explanation out of her by force. The more you argue, the hotter the hell gets. Observe yourself: with this behavior, you are communicating that she has hooked you through your feelings. Without realizing it, you are saying: “Look, I am desperate with passion; I need your tenderness and your attention more than anything on this Earth. You are the hottest woman in the world.” It so happens that this is exactly what she wants to hear so that she can pull away even more. The sly one wants to test you, once again. She wants to see whether you get upset, whether you miss her tenderness and attention. What to do?

<!-- seg 08-0010 paragraph h=c39e9c3d -->
In your place, I would simply treat the sly one the same way she was treating me. There would be only one difference in my treatment: I would be a little worse than she was. I would become even stranger, more distant, colder, more indifferent, and would deny everything, giving the hell back to her. I would refuse the emotional hell that was offered. But this requires freedom from passion and a powerful will.

<!-- seg 08-0011 heading h=03e799fe -->
The wile of suddenly stopping calling

<!-- seg 08-0012 paragraph h=0bb71890 -->
Everything is fine between the two of you and, suddenly, out of nowhere, she stops calling you. She wants to test your passion; she wants to see whether you keep calling insistently like a desperate man. First solution: do the same to her, but for much longer. Second solution: give her an ultimatum. Third solution: ignore her forever and find another, better woman. Your mistake: not having set beforehand a maximum number of days for her to call you, after which it would be settled that SHE had decided to end the relationship.

<!-- seg 08-0013 heading h=599b82d9 -->
The wile of ending the relationship unresolved

<!-- seg 08-0014 paragraph h=052183dc -->
Because they are illogical and contradictory, the irritation of doubt does not affect them as much as it affects us. In fact, it really seems that unresolved and confused situations please them.

<!-- seg 08-0015 paragraph h=4308b8fa -->
Without giving any account or explanation, and without anything having gone wrong, she simply loses interest and leaves you. There is no logic at all in this behavior, apparently... But there is a hidden, unconscious logic: you will remain imprisoned to her precisely because you do not understand what happened. The question mark will remain in your head. This will make you keep thinking about the sly one for many months or even years, asking yourself and speculating about it... Intuitively, your suffering is sensed from a distance, in a way that borders on the paranormal.

<!-- seg 08-0016 paragraph h=e75e3bea -->
I see no other solution to this hell than foreknowledge of this fatal and inevitable tendency. A man must anticipate it and remain continually expecting this form of emotional betrayal, which usually comes sooner or later. The only case in which it seems not to occur is when the man already presupposes such abandonment and expects it, or else when the man really wants the woman to go away forever... Once again, your mistake was passion, the fear of losing her, the strong desire to have her for yourself and close to you.

<!-- seg 08-0017 paragraph h=963c0ce1 -->
If this misfortune has already befallen you, a first alternative, for the bravest, is to reach her and give her an ultimatum: “Either you come back by tomorrow or never look for me again!” Then the truth about the sly one’s feelings will be revealed, and no doubts will remain. But do not do this if you are not prepared for the worst. Getting another, even prettier girlfriend usually works too, because the sly one will conclude that she misjudged you and dumped an interesting guy. Then she may come after you again. If there is a chance that she will see you fairly often, as when both of you work or study together, it will also be an excellent opportunity to change your conduct, showing yourself different and interesting.

<!-- seg 08-0018 paragraph h=cdcb53ba -->
If you do not want to give the ultimatum, then you should act as if nothing had happened, to give the hell back to her. But this too is very hard to bear. Under no circumstances become a harasser or pursue her trying to wring answers to your questions out of her. Pursuit polarizes the situation even more in the sly one’s favor and against you.

<!-- seg 08-0019 paragraph h=ce6df74c -->
Remember: in love they are absurd, so do not look for coherence. Accept the absurd and adapt. Not even they themselves can explain themselves without confusing the situation even more.

<!-- seg 08-0020 paragraph h=503baa7c -->
If there is no passion, there will be no suffering. When she treacherously disappears, you will simply turn your back and move on to another. I think that no being who betrays another’s sincere feelings deserves any form of consideration or importance, unless that being repents and changes conduct. Whoever betrays the sincere feelings of another is simply confessing that he is a person without worth.

<!-- seg 08-0021 heading h=a0edcdc2 -->
The wile of provoking love and hatred

<!-- seg 08-0022 paragraph h=e17a637b -->
She treats you like a ping-pong ball. She pleases you and enrages you alternately or even simultaneously. She does what you hate and also what you adore. Her intentions are to learn your limits and measure how far you can be manipulated. The sly one wants to know the limits of your fury; she wants to “take your temperature” and also to test ways of calming you down. As Karen Salmansohn taught, she is training the dog, taming the beast. The solution: place yourself “beyond good and evil,” as Nietzsche said, not letting yourself be emotionally manipulated either to the left or to the right; be master of yourself, neither love nor hate, transcend. This will frustrate her, because she will not be able to get to know you.
<!-- QUERY: "Salmanshon" in the original corrected to Salmansohn (as in 01-0003). -->

<!-- seg 08-0023 heading h=6ed59170 -->
The wile of going against our wishes

<!-- seg 08-0024 paragraph h=206847c8 -->
She behaves well while she watches you. When she confirms that you like her a lot and discovers what pleases and displeases you, she begins to do exactly what you do not want. The solution: accept it and encourage her to keep doing exactly that until she comes into conflict with herself.

<!-- seg 08-0025 heading h=7ec8c20c -->
The wile of lying

<!-- seg 08-0026 paragraph h=2539a925 -->
She says marvelous things, and you sense some inconsistency. What she is saying is too good to be true. The solution: pretend to believe the lie and encourage her to lie even more, to the extreme.

<!-- seg 08-0027 heading h=65c08988 -->
The wile of offering sex and not giving it

<!-- seg 08-0028 paragraph h=add7c2f1 -->
She fills you with hope, promising what you like best: intense sex. She makes advances and behaves as if she really were a fatal female, but she does so only at moments when it is impossible to actually fulfill the desire she has kindled in you. When a chance finally comes along, the sly one invents an excuse and leaves you frustrated.
<!-- QUERY: "fêmea fatal" rendered "fatal female" (approved glossary term "female"), as in How to Deal with Women; alternative: "femme fatale". -->

<!-- seg 08-0029 paragraph h=909f8c47 -->
The solution: do not get excited about the advances and offers; ignore them, and unmask the farce before it happens. It also helps never again to approach her sexually, but only to accept her when she comes to you, so that she strongly suspects she has been dismissed from her sexual role.

<!-- seg 08-0030 heading h=075e0e48 -->
The wile of provoking physical aggression

<!-- seg 08-0031 paragraph h=568ae542 -->
This is one of the worst of all. The woman provokes the man in various ways, doing everything he loathes, with the intention of irritating him more and more, to induce him to lose control and attack her. The motivations may be various: wanting to draw people’s attention, testing the man’s physical strength or self-control, feeling the thrill of seeing him lose his head, playing the poor little victim before neighbors and family, having grounds to demand the end of the relationship, feeling protected by other men induced to defend her because of the scandal, enjoying the adrenaline rush, etc.

<!-- seg 08-0032 paragraph h=8938efc6 -->
Never fall into this trap. Stay far away from this kind of woman. If you run into one of these witches, simply withdraw and leave her silently, without arguing. Such cases are beyond recovery.
