2. The types of imprisonment to woman, according to the centers of the machine.
<!-- QUERY: "aprisionamento à mulher" = a man’s being imprisoned (bound) to a woman. Rendered "imprisonment to woman" to keep the author’s prison imagery ("A ligação é uma prisão"). Alternatives: "The ways a man is held captive by a woman", "The types of bondage to woman". -->

<!-- seg 03-0002 heading h=c50c271c -->
Bond and affinity at the intellectual level

<!-- seg 03-0003 paragraph h=c5efd0f5 -->
A man becomes attached to a woman through the centers of the machine. If he is attached to her only through the intellectual center, he will take pleasure in talking with her, in exchanging ideas, but he will feel little or no sexual attraction and/or romantic-amorous bond, because he is not linked to the woman in the motor-instinctive-sexual and emotional centers, respectively.

<!-- seg 03-0004 heading h=db75de03 -->
Imprisonment at the sexual level

<!-- seg 03-0005 paragraph h=6986cf63 -->
It may also happen that we are attached to a woman only or mainly through the sexual center. In this case, we feel immense sexual attraction but no intellectual or emotional affinity with her. The bond will occur only at the level of the motor-instinctive-sexual brain, to which the sexual center belongs. The subjects she talks about will be tiresome and even irritating to us. Nor will we feel any kind of affection or romantic sentimentality. It is this type of bond that a man usually seeks with prostitutes and with women who seem highly attractive sexually to him at first sight, although sometimes he later ends up becoming emotionally attached to them and getting screwed. This is the bond that exists between the porn actress and her admirers. It is the first of the forms of bond with the opposite sex that a man fantasizes about. Pornographic films belong to this type of bond. Men dream of imprisoning women through this center.

<!-- seg 03-0006 heading h=c1bfb761 -->
Imprisonment at the emotional level

<!-- seg 03-0007 paragraph h=15f6c4ef -->
There is still the third and fatal case, in which we become attached to a woman only or mainly through the emotional center. It is this form of bond that leads to tragedies, crimes of passion, and the so-called “crazy things done for love” that the sly ones appreciate so much. Romanticism and affections belong to the emotional center. When the bond, or imprisonment, occurs at this level, the man weaves little or no pornographic fantasy about the object of his adoration. She is seen more as a goddess, whose will cannot be crossed even slightly. Crossing her and defying her are considered sacrileges. It is a form of servility: the miserable state of the man in love. The sly ones try incessantly to throw us into this state, because of the material and psychological guarantees it gives them, but at the same time they feel aversion if we let ourselves fall so low. So here there is a curious contradiction: they feel aversion precisely toward those who give in to their pressure to fall in love; they pressure the man to fall in love but feel repelled as soon as he gives in to this pressure and surrenders. In other words, they feel repelled by those who do everything they want (or consciously believe they want) and meet their demands. Here is a contradiction: how can we pressure someone to do something and loathe him as soon as he complies? There is an obvious ingratitude here, visible to the man and vehemently denied by the woman. All of this belongs to the emotional center; these are games of feelings. Since female emotional intelligence is usually greater than male, males usually lose this war or game and fall into despair. Hence the outbursts of anger and fury and the crimes of passion.

Normally, the female fantasy revolves around bonds through this center. They dream of binding, through the emotional center, the males who are at the top of the male hierarchy. Of course, the intention is not altruistic. In their dreams, it is not they who submit but the men. They dream of the dominance exercised in this field.

<!-- seg 03-0009 heading h=4db9525d -->
Discovering through which center we are bound

<!-- seg 03-0010 paragraph h=efebe975 -->
Thus, the bond is usually more pronounced in one center than in the others. We have to observe in ourselves what type of bond we establish with a woman so that we can free ourselves. We must discover through which center we are bound primarily, secondarily, etc.

<!-- seg 03-0011 heading h=c3d358f0 -->
The bond is a prison

<!-- seg 03-0012 paragraph h=677070d8 -->
The bond is a dependence, since the woman’s absence causes suffering. It is a prison, because the suffering of abstinence is relieved only by her approach and by the satisfaction of the impulses felt in the centers.

<!-- seg 03-0013 heading h=2d82a6eb -->
One man and several women

<!-- seg 03-0014 paragraph h=22d83272 -->
A polygamous man is usually bound by different centers to each of his women. This one cooks well, takes care of him like a son, and has imprisoned him through the instinctive center (filial instinct). That one is a sex goddess and has imprisoned him through the sexual center. A third will be affectionate, sweet, and tender, imprisoning him through the emotional center. The fourth may have great intellectual affinity and will be his great friend, even though now and then they have sex, for there is always some attraction, however small, in the centers other than the main one from which the involvement originates.

<!-- seg 03-0015 heading h=2e1fa5eb -->
The type of bond can be seen in dreams

<!-- seg 03-0016 paragraph h=c77ce6fb -->
The nature of the dreams we have about women can reveal through which center we are bound. If we dream that we hug them, kiss them, or simply see them, but everything is charged with intense emotion, the dream will indicate that the bond is emotional. If we dream that we are having wild sex, the dream will indicate that the bond is through the sexual center. If we dream that we are merely talking deeply about some subject, it will indicate that the bond is through the intellectual center.

<!-- seg 03-0017 heading h=4dde2fe2 -->
Opposite bonds in different centers

<!-- seg 03-0018 paragraph h=467d5775 -->
Sometimes the curious case may occur in which one person hates another and, at the same time, feels sexually attracted to that person. The aversion will be felt in the emotional center and the attraction in the sexual center. In situations like this, the sexual attraction is usually violent. A man may feel intense anger toward a woman but, in spite of that, desire her strongly for sex. If he is a misogynist, he will hate all women. On the other hand, if the person who hates is an androphobic/misandrist woman, she will hate all men, perhaps because she does not feel desired. If the contradiction is extreme, sex even comes to be used as a form of aggression against the other. That is why many misogynistic men and misandrist women are heterosexual and not homosexual.
