3. Sexual attraction in woman

<!-- seg 04-0002 heading h=a0340766 -->
How female sexual attraction operates

<!-- seg 04-0003 paragraph h=6f36e324 -->
Woman also has a sexual center and feels sexual attraction, although its keynote is much more emotional and less sexualized and genitalized than in man. The female sexual impulse is triggered much more by the action of the emotional center than by direct action on the sexual center. That is why, if a man touches a woman’s sexual organ without being authorized, he will be violently rebuffed, unlike a woman who touches a man’s sexual organ without being authorized. What for him is a pleasant surprise is, for her, a grave offense.

<!-- seg 04-0004 paragraph h=d44ddc58 -->
A woman’s attraction to a man is motivated much more by needs and impulses of her emotional center than by a taste for sex itself. In other words, males like sex in and of itself more than women do, who seek sex for other reasons. The emotional needs that motivate them to seek sex are: material security, feeling protected, a boost in self-esteem, and victory over rivals.

<!-- seg 04-0005 paragraph h=e3bc93aa -->
Therefore, woman becomes attached to man by way of the emotional center. When her emotional needs are excited but not satisfied, she pursues him and does everything she can to keep him imprisoned to her. When the man lets himself be imprisoned, these emotional needs are satisfied and she loses interest, in a manner analogous to a man after being sexually satisfied in intercourse.

<!-- seg 04-0006 heading h=5ff18621 -->
Unconsciously they want a father

<!-- seg 04-0007 paragraph h=0ff71192 -->
The model of man that the female unconscious calls for is linked to the father figure. In a woman’s memories, usually, the father led, commanded, protected, ordered her to go to bed and to take her medicine at the right time, forbade her to associate with bad company, and took many other measures for her own good. The father figure was fearsome but offered security.

<!-- seg 04-0008 paragraph h=4f51b260 -->
It is these same sensations that she seeks, now as an adult, in a man. Whoever, instead of taking the symbolic place of the father in the woman’s imagination, tries to do the opposite, submitting to her command and readily offering himself to serve her, as foolish men did in the Middle Ages, will not provide the intense sensations needed for her to fall in love. If he is accepted as a companion, it will be solely in the role of an emotional slave.

<!-- seg 04-0009 paragraph h=252256e9 -->
Since the father is woman’s first male reference, he directly shapes her selective criterion for choosing the men destined to be seen as ideal models of fertilizing males. Was the father not the ideal male of her mother, at least in theory and according to idealized standards?

<!-- seg 04-0010 heading h=872704c5 -->
Woman needs to feel desired and loved

<!-- seg 04-0011 paragraph h=784b871b -->
Beyond the selective criterion, however, there is in them an immense selfish need to feel strongly desired and loved by the greatest possible number of men, so that they can reject them. This sensation works as a thermometer by which they can measure and regulate their self-esteem, since female self-esteem depends on social approval and on victory over rival women. The more desired a woman is, the better she will feel and the higher her self-esteem will be. The more suitors she can reject, the happier she will be. The converse is also true. Therefore, this does not mean that they want to have sex with all men, nor even that they like sex, but only that the sensation of being desired and loved puffs them up, because they feel like the hottest women on Earth and better than their rivals. It is in no way an altruistic need, since the impulse to automatically reciprocate male desire and love does not exist. In fact, it is the opposite: the first impulse is to reject the pursuers and tell everyone about it, especially the other women.

<!-- seg 04-0012 paragraph h=62aa59a1 -->
This hypothesis explains why the sly ones run away and reject us when we pursue them but pursue us when we reject them resolutely and decisively because we consider them unattractive or unbearable. Regarding this detail, Eliphas Lévi wrote:

<!-- seg 04-0013 paragraph h=66ce3048 -->
“Given such transcendental knowledge of woman, there is a further maneuver to be carried out in order to attract her attention: this maneuver consists in not concerning oneself with her, or in doing so in a way that humbles her self-love, treating her like a little girl and not letting even a glimpse of the idea of courting her be seen. Then the roles will be reversed: she will do everything [to] tempt thee, she will initiate thee into the secrets that women keep to themselves, she will dress and undress before thee, saying things such as these: ‘[the two of us are] among women—[here we are] among old friends—I do not fear you—you are not a man to me,’ etc., etc. Then she will watch thy glances, and if she catches them calm and indifferent, she will feel outraged; she will come near thee on some pretext or other, brush thee with her hair, let her peignoir fall half open... “Treating her like a little girl” means: paying no heed to her capricious opinions and taking no account of her complaints, impertinences, and judgments, in addition to leading her for her own good and seriously reprimanding her for her mischief. When she is treated like a little girl by a man who does not pay her much attention, she is struck in her vanity and pride by the absence of male sexual interest, and she is also struck in her selective criterion, developed since childhood through observing her father, which is then activated. Moved by multiple simultaneous feelings (desire for revenge, curiosity, the need to reject, the search for the fertilizing male, the need for security and protection, etc.), the woman then makes advances without quite understanding why she does so. By the time she comes to her senses, she will already be offering herself.

Why they prefer the bad ones

<!-- seg 04-0016 paragraph h=f83e183e -->
The bad ones are preferred for the following reasons: 1) in female eyes, they seem stronger and more masculine than the good ones; 2) they are more unscrupulous in the art of dissembling, lying to them, and deceiving them; 3) they impress them excessively; 4) they allow women to perform the sacrificial role and be seen as “women who love too much,” despite being mistreated, and to arouse pity in society.

The predilection for fearsome men is related to the intuition that they make good protectors, since they make people tremble with fear (an ancestral, prehistoric, caveman, animal female instinct). Let us remember, however, that fearsome men have short lives.

<!-- seg 04-0018 paragraph h=e904d425 -->
I do not recommend that we be bad, but that we extract what is good in them for our own benefit and take their place in women’s hearts.

The rare cases of sexual pursuit by women

<!-- seg 04-0020 paragraph h=e5182a64 -->
It is more frequent for a man to approach a woman with explicit sexual intentions than the reverse. Women pursue and approach a man with explicit sexual intent only when they are extremely hurt in their feelings, but in that case the motivation is emotional, often even vengeful, and not the desire to have sex.

<!-- seg 04-0021 paragraph h=26d65e28 -->
The cases in which women explicitly throw themselves at men, with undisguised intentions of seducing them, are those in which they lose control over themselves because of an invasion of lower emotions related to their needs. In general, it is because they feel defeated by rival females and scorned or simply ignored by the man they all desire. This fact wounds them violently in their feelings. The girls who faint at concerts and tear the clothes off performers, as well as the crazed women who want to tear the briefs off dancers at “women’s clubs” to swallow their <i>phalus</i>, show a motivation of the same order.

This hypothesis explains why they run away from those who pursue them and pursue those who reject them. It also explains why they pursue those who reject them but run away from them as soon as they change their conduct and begin to desire them. The solution for dealing with this hellish contradiction is to be even more dissembling than they are with us, pretending not to want them very much even when they, driven by emotional disturbance, are approaching.

<!-- seg 04-0023 paragraph h=d682c146 -->
This hypothesis further explains why polygamy is more frequent than polyandry as a socially accepted institution (because women prefer those who have several women and, when a man has several women, they become imprisoned to him through rivalry). And more: it explains why they become attached to those who only practice wild sex without any trace of sentimentality and abandon or cheat on affectionate romantics.

<!-- seg 04-0024 paragraph h=e84a8582 -->
The man who shows interest after the woman has begun her pursuit will make her turn around and try to run away, with the intention of reversing the roles. On the other hand, the man who shows himself totally uninterested will make the sly one also give up pursuing him, driven by pride (she will say: “I’m not going to lower myself” or “who does he think he is?”). However, the one who lets a certain faint acceptance show through will keep the pursuit going until the moment he shows affection. If he stays in the state of uninterested acceptance up to the point of having sex with her, he may keep her imprisoned to him indefinitely.

<!-- seg 04-0025 paragraph h=ef41c138 -->
Therefore, a man who wants to arouse interest in a woman must “attack” her correctly on the emotional side and not on the sexual side, as the wretched, clumsy Matrixian harassers do.

The rare cases in which men are pursued are not motivated by the desire for sex in and of itself, nor by love, as everyone believes, but by various other motives that disguise themselves and creep into amorous and sexual conduct. These motives, always with an emotional keynote, correspond to intentions secondary to sex and love. In other words, the sexual pursuits carried out by women are motivated by hidden interests of a nonsexual nature, such as the desire to obtain money, to enjoy fame, prominence, and power; the desire to arouse envy in rivals, to feel attractive, to be the center of attention, to have an emotional slave at her disposal, to obtain alimony, to take revenge for some slight, to secure guarantees for old age, to have the pleasure of attracting and repudiating, to enjoy the sensation of being clever by deceiving, and many other shady interests. Love does not appear on this list or, if it does, it is in last place.

Why they like to be led

<!-- seg 04-0028 paragraph h=fd0b2030 -->
Female attitudes belie the current idea, and women’s assertion, that they do not appreciate male leadership, including when it is exercised over the romantic relationship. Here is one of the reasons for this taste: it is much more comfortable, safe, and pleasant to be led, and to be able to torment the leader with criticism when he makes mistakes, than to lead. Since the true leader always leads for others and not for himself, he cannot afford to be selfish and to conduct his leadership exclusively for his own benefit. The selfish leader is quickly dethroned and banished, for there is no leadership without the support of those who are led. There is yet another reason: to be a leader, the male must destroy the opposition of the other rival males, who also aspire to reach the position of the one most desired by the females. In doing so, he shows that he is superior to his enemies and the bearer of the best genes of the species.

<!-- seg 04-0029 heading h=3b22a6cc -->
A being of implicit intentions

<!-- seg 04-0030 paragraph h=bcf95e65 -->
The natural dissimulation inherent in woman makes her a being who expresses her sexual interest in a man only implicitly. In the overwhelming majority of cases, she will only send implicit signals that you must be able to interpret, always with the risk that it is just a trap to mock your good faith.

<!-- seg 04-0031 paragraph h=6a75d32d -->
Only very rarely will a woman show the sexual attraction she feels explicitly and unequivocally. No, my friend, she will never come up to you to invite you to sleep with her. The sly one will never say “I want you to take me to bed,” except in exceptional and rare situations. To believe otherwise or to expect it is tantamount to being crazy.
