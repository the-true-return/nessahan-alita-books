7. The obsessive female quest for the continuity of male interest

<!-- seg 09-0002 paragraph h=a9ee600a -->
Let us now develop some implications of Francesco Alberoni’s (n.d.) confused and poorly explained theory of continuity.
<!-- QUERY: "s/d" (sem data) rendered "n.d." (no date). The work is Alberoni’s "L’erotismo" (1986), listed in the bibliography as "Eroticism" (Brazilian edition, n.d.). -->

<!-- seg 09-0003 paragraph h=af73d3ee -->
Alberoni was a genius in detecting continuity in female eroticism but, unfortunately, his explanations fall short when it comes to clarity. The way he explains his theory of continuity, the reader is led to believe that woman is permanently interested in giving sex and giving love, which is false. The reality is precisely the opposite: woman wants both to be offered to her (and not taken) continually so that they can be rejected; that is, she longs for the continuity of male interest; she wants to receive constant proof that the man is interested in her. She does not want to offer sex and love full-time.

<!-- seg 09-0004 heading h=5b57e79a -->
Behavioral contradiction aims at continuity

<!-- seg 09-0005 paragraph h=9f1a23db -->
Ambiguous female behavior, which communicates interest and lack of interest simultaneously, aims to maintain the continuity of male interest. The preservation of the continuity of interest, and not the satisfaction of this interest, is the most important, if not the only, goal of all this disconcerting conduct based on sending contradictory signals.

<!-- seg 09-0006 heading h=20625ea8 -->
Contradiction preserves desire

<!-- seg 09-0007 paragraph h=3d55ee62 -->
Male interest is preserved by contradictory female conduct because it does not allow desire to be satisfied and, at the same time, keeps it from being directed toward a rival female. Contradictory conduct binds desire to whoever adopts it.

<!-- seg 09-0008 paragraph h=71251a8f -->
We can reverse the game of pursuit by adopting contradictory postures instead of the usual coherent male conduct.

<!-- seg 09-0009 heading h=4910458e -->
Coherent conduct extinguishes desire

<!-- seg 09-0010 paragraph h=bdd590d3 -->
When a woman acts in an absolutely coherent and definite way, showing only interest or only lack of interest, clearly, explicitly, and totally unequivocally, one of two things may happen:

<!-- seg 09-0011 paragraph h=a932c111 -->
1) If the woman is interested, the man’s advances will be accepted, his desire will be satisfied, and male interest will be momentarily lost;

<!-- seg 09-0012 paragraph h=f93d9514 -->
2) If the woman is not interested, the man’s advances will be clearly rejected, no doubts will remain, he will no longer waste his time like a sucker, and he will go after another woman.

<!-- seg 09-0013 paragraph h=a1c5b369 -->
In both cases, the continuity of desire will be lost. That is why the sly ones avoid definite conduct at all costs. And that is also why it is we who pursue them in search of sex and not the reverse. If we knew how to play with indefiniteness, it would be they who pursued us. It is a selfish and negative emotional intelligence.

<!-- seg 09-0014 heading h=4aaee8bb -->
There is no limit

<!-- seg 09-0015 paragraph h=6a041569 -->
In the obsessive quest for continuity, anything goes: lies, cheating, mental and emotional tortures, torments of doubt, betrayals, adulteries, etc. What matters here is preserving the continuity of the man’s interest, regardless of the suffering this causes him. Dissimulation lies at the very core of the contradictory conduct that preserves continuity.

<!-- seg 09-0016 paragraph h=5c06d9fc -->
The continuity sought is not that of the interest of a single man but, preferably, of all the men on Earth, if that were possible, so that all of them could be rejected. They do not want to sleep with all of them, but they want to have the power to reject all those they can.

<!-- seg 09-0017 paragraph h=0e670185 -->
Even when they break off a relationship or ask for help from the law or from men to keep away an obsessed suitor, ex-lover, or ex-husband, the sly ones want, deep down, to preserve the continuity of his interest, for some future possibility. Even though they want him far away, they want him interested all the same. That is why they send subliminal signals that keep the wretch’s hope alive while openly stating that they no longer want him.

<!-- seg 09-0018 paragraph h=c2d18ee4 -->
Of course, this whole mechanism is unconscious most of the time, but it is real all the same.

<!-- seg 09-0019 heading h=24646e90 -->
Fighting fire with fire

<!-- seg 09-0020 paragraph h=0d387fc3 -->
When it is absolutely impossible to convince them to behave in a coherent and clear way, an impossibility that is very frequent, the only solution left to us will be to outdo them in this field, being even more contradictory than they are with us. But for that, total detachment and total freedom from passion are necessary.

<!-- seg 09-0021 paragraph h=c5b6c2d2 -->
Male conduct is usually clear and definite, which allows the sly ones to know with mathematical certainty what our real interests and feelings are. The ideal would be for the signals we send to be contradictory, leaning more toward showing lack of interest than interest, but still alternating according to the situations and needs that arise.

<!-- seg 09-0022 heading h=25b4cee1 -->
The desire for continuity can mobilize them

<!-- seg 09-0023 paragraph h=28ffcdf7 -->
It is the desire for continuity that drives a woman to mobilize herself and go after a man who has rejected her or who stands out and does not notice her presence.

<!-- seg 09-0024 paragraph h=28af0256 -->
It is also this same desire that makes them “run away” from those who “pursue” them and show no interest at all in those who have already shown themselves interested in them in advance, such as devoted husbands, sincere boyfriends, and every well-intentioned man in general. In this case, female inertia and “flight” are due to the fact that this desire has already been satisfied by the inexperienced man. If the man has already shown himself interested, for her things are settled. Why should she go to the trouble of arousing his interest?
