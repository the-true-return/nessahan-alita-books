1. Original principles and concepts that guide the works of Nessahan Alita

<!-- seg 02-0002 paragraph h=69e57aea -->
Since the first versions of the books, it has been made clear and explicit that:

<!-- seg 02-0003 paragraph h=cf93c0f6 -->
1. Evil and goodness exist in both sexes; my attention to female evil is merely a question of focus and of necessity for these decadent times;

<!-- seg 02-0004 paragraph h=f4eae323 -->
2. I do not condemn women but rather their attitudes and behaviors in the present day;

<!-- seg 02-0005 paragraph h=d63a9834 -->
3. My criticisms are limited to the women I have had the opportunity to observe in my life, and do not extend to all women in existence (so who authorized any careless reader to conclude hastily that I generalize on this point?);

<!-- seg 02-0006 paragraph h=97a82d85 -->
4. The female behaviors I criticize are, for the most part, unconscious (therefore the criticism aims to shake women up and shock them to see whether, who knows, one of them might wake up and become conscious);

<!-- seg 02-0007 paragraph h=31743462 -->
5. The goal of my texts is to help men and not to harm women, for the two things are totally distinct and separate (only sly misogynists and sly androphobic misandrists try to confuse the two on purpose);
<!-- QUERY: "misóginos e androfóbicos-misândricos espertinhos": the author’s compound "androfóbico-misândrico" rendered "androphobic misandrists"; "espertinhos" (masculine of the glossary’s "espertinha(s)" → "sly one(s)") rendered "sly". -->

<!-- seg 02-0008 paragraph h=f55225be -->
6. We must have correct and upright attitudes so that reason is always on our side (this applies not only to dating but also to ideological wars);

<!-- seg 02-0009 paragraph h=08d48ed8 -->
7. I am in favor of what is right and not of what is wrong, and I will not give that up;

<!-- seg 02-0010 paragraph h=d8bc69cf -->
8. I do not approve of evil;

<!-- seg 02-0011 paragraph h=ba6a3e3f -->
9. We should be neither evil nor promiscuous but merely acquire certain behavioral characteristics that the wicked possess without, however, becoming like them, for the path they follow is destructive for everyone, including themselves;

<!-- seg 02-0012 paragraph h=ef393b0f -->
10. I defend the family, marital fidelity, and the voluntary submission of wives and children to the authority of the man;

<!-- seg 02-0013 paragraph h=5f8d4b35 -->
11. The man has the responsibility to exercise his authority for good and not for evil, and he must pay dearly if he uses his authority to commit any abuse;

<!-- seg 02-0014 paragraph h=b51e7678 -->
12. We must accept women’s defects without rebelling;

<!-- seg 02-0015 paragraph h=46129a44 -->
13. We must leave women absolutely free to do whatever they want, merely giving them back the consequences of their attitudes if these are abusive (a giving back that must not be held back by fear of what might happen, not even by fear that the relationship or marriage will go down the drain);

<!-- seg 02-0016 paragraph h=02c77fa6 -->
14. A “tramp” is a woman who toys with the dearest feelings of a sincere man, and not the others (of course there are male tramps, but I deal with them only marginally);

<!-- seg 02-0017 paragraph h=2a5e3327 -->
15. I am in favor of conscious and enlightened machismo, and not of irrational and violent machismo (extremist dogmatic machismo), which is an abusive plague that reinforces androphobic-misandrist feminism;

<!-- seg 02-0018 paragraph h=bb8e41a1 -->
17. I am an adherent of Gnosticism, and my philosophical conception of love and woman derives from my religion, which accepts the Koran, the Bible, and the apocryphal books as rules of life because they are messages from Heaven.

None of this is new. Everything has been explicit in the texts since the first book.
