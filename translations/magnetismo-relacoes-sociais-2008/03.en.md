2. Magnetic chains

<!-- seg 03-0002 paragraph h=60563d2b -->
When wills unite, they form magnetic chains (egregores). Through sympathetic affinity, spontaneous chains of feeling form and spread through society, commanded from the center by manipulative individuals. These chains may be political, commercial, artistic, religious, etc., in nature. Their reach in time and space varies.

<!-- seg 03-0003 paragraph h=8e34d1d1 -->
Wars are examples of highly destructive magnetic chains and are due to the clash of antagonistic chains.

<!-- seg 03-0004 paragraph h=95466afc -->
In today’s globalized world, sympathetic chains of international geographic reach are formed, and their nucleus may be a company, a government, a piece of news, or a major work of cinema or art.

<!-- seg 03-0005 paragraph h=22a18d2f -->
The more extensive a sympathetic chain is, the greater its strength. Sympathetic force spreads through continuous enthusiastic communication and is set in motion by solid practices.

<!-- seg 03-0006 paragraph h=f4a34e95 -->
Ordinary human beings need leadership. A man of genius forms his own chain to achieve his aims. He acquires a point of fixity, psychic immobility, and then sets off a persevering circular action of initiatives. He possesses great power of action and direction. If he is a genius of good, he will use his power to help his fellow men. If he is a genius of evil, he will lead them to ruin and will have to answer for it. Hitler was a genius of evil. Today there are many geniuses of evil at work.

<!-- seg 03-0007 paragraph h=b98cfee3 -->
The movement of the magnetic agent is twofold and multiplies in the opposite direction, since every action has an equivalent reaction (for example, a privilege granted to someone will awaken the beneficiary’s sympathy for the one who granted it but, at the same time, will provoke the antipathy of the beneficiary’s enemies toward the benefactor). The secret lies in calculating reactions in advance and avoiding acting on impulse. Whoever does not isolate himself from magnetic currents is struck down, because he cannot resist the temptation to satisfy his desire in spite of the adverse and dangerous reactions its satisfaction may set off. Every act creates a linked sequence of effects in a network.

<!-- seg 03-0008 paragraph h=d8eb6338 -->
The opinions in circulation directly influence the strength of the magnetic agent that forms the chain, which is why the scope and depth of existing predispositions must be carefully assessed, at the risk of unleashing a catastrophe against ourselves or against the world.

<!-- seg 03-0009 paragraph h=c517fdfa -->
Love is superior to hatred because it is intrinsically sympathetic. Christ was crucified because he was progressively influencing the multitude in a direction contrary to the interests of the centers of the strongest chains of his time.

<!-- seg 03-0010 paragraph h=8bedd379 -->
Chains are subject to a pendular movement; they evolve and involve. A chain that is coming to an end is succeeded by an opposite chain.
<!-- QUERY: "evoluem e involuem" rendered as "evolve and involve", keeping the author's esoteric pairing (involution as the opposite of evolution). Alternative: "they rise and decline". -->

<!-- seg 03-0011 paragraph h=e64d4459 -->
At present (the year 2003), the worldwide sympathetic chain that has the USA at its center has entered a slow regressive movement. The Democrats delay this historical process through their flexibility, and the Republicans, their antagonists, hasten it through unilateral actions. George Bush, without meaning to, is accelerating the spread of anti-Americanism around the world and is therefore hastening the downfall of the empire, within the time scale of the ages of nations.
