Conclusions

<!-- seg 14-0002 paragraph h=9e45fdc7 -->
The passions make human beings vulnerable. Desires are passions, and they sweep us away.

<!-- seg 14-0003 paragraph h=db5f8bcf -->
The mind of the impassioned person is obsessed by elementaries (larvae or thought-forms).

<!-- seg 14-0004 paragraph h=81ee0c80 -->
The impassioned person is not master of himself; his actions do not belong to him.

<!-- seg 14-0005 paragraph h=bb6ed459 -->
It is not legitimate to manipulate others.

<!-- seg 14-0006 paragraph h=98a9cd69 -->
It is legitimate to dismantle other people’s attempts to manipulate our psyche.

<!-- seg 14-0007 paragraph h=c4e7b622 -->
It is advisable to overcome our fears and weaknesses in order to protect ourselves from attempts at manipulation.

<!-- seg 14-0008 paragraph h=5149d921 -->
Sympathetic people do not always wish us well, nor do they always wish us harm.

<!-- seg 14-0009 paragraph h=79249b61 -->
Antipathetic people do not always wish us harm, nor do they always wish us well.

<!-- seg 14-0010 paragraph h=0d687227 -->
Manipulative force flows through everyday life.

<!-- seg 14-0011 paragraph h=9f79354e -->
The free will of others must be respected.

<!-- seg 14-0012 paragraph h=55709887 -->
The free will of others is disrespected by the manipulator.

<!-- seg 14-0013 paragraph h=4880c78a -->
Mirror action (refractory action) returns the consequences of spells to the manipulator.

<!-- seg 14-0014 paragraph h=3f200691 -->
We keep our beliefs from being manipulated through proper skepticism.

<!-- seg 14-0015 paragraph h=12849e41 -->
Temperance, balance, serenity, and sobriety are far more advisable than passional exaltation.
