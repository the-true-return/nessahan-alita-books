7. Taking possession of another’s will

<!-- seg 08-0002 paragraph h=3af65948 -->
March 26, 2003

<!-- seg 08-0003 paragraph h=be33a4a7 -->
All human behavior has repercussions or reactions in others. Everything a person does carries the intention of provoking feelings, thoughts, and actions in other people. When we greet someone, we intend to make them believe we are friendly and to make them feel sympathy, or at least to keep them from feeling antipathy. Whoever mocks a person wants to induce that person to become enraged or to feel belittled. The bandit who points a revolver at his victim wants to induce the victim to feel fear. We want to flaunt luxury so that others feel admiration or envy. The woman who displays her body wants to provoke desire. All these behaviors, and any other social behaviors, are intentional and manipulative, since they aim to force others into specific emotional states. Human beings, even if unconsciously, do not act without ulterior motives. This is not, in itself, bad, as long as it does not serve as a means to harm others or to achieve selfish ends. Human abilities should be used for legitimate defense or for the benefit of others.

<!-- seg 08-0004 paragraph h=a3c85d00 -->
Identifying the directions taken by someone’s libidinal flows makes it possible to draw closer in sympathetic affinity. The libidinal flows are the weaknesses: loves, hatreds, longings, and terrors. Once identified, the weaknesses can be instrumentalized for domination.

<!-- seg 08-0005 paragraph h=f8c78670 -->
Instrumentalization happens mainly through speech, but also through facial expression and attitudes.

<!-- seg 08-0006 paragraph h=dc1566fb -->
To steal another’s will, the manipulator needs to speak ill of what the victim detests, praise what the victim loves, and give the victim security against what he fears. In this way the libidinal flow is intensified by the joining of libidinal flows running in the same direction, and a magnetic chain is created.

<!-- seg 08-0007 paragraph h=fee0dd5e -->
The effectiveness of magnetic power is directly proportional to the will impressed upon the act. If the manipulator acts hesitantly, the passive element will not be sufficiently magnetized.

<!-- seg 08-0008 paragraph h=39db58fb -->
When one speaks with intense feeling and concentration, force is impressed upon the magnetic current, and it absorbs the flows of the other’s will.

<!-- seg 08-0009 paragraph h=a32d13af -->
In general, whoever wants to take possession of someone’s will usually first draws closer in sympathetic affinity with that person, as some salesmen do. To bring about this closeness, the passions need to be identified. Then the sly operator blesses what the person loves and curses what the person detests, in order to fit perfectly into the structure of that person’s passions. In this way the sympathetic affinity grows closer and deeper, up to a dangerous point.

<!-- seg 08-0010 paragraph h=9449c3b5 -->
Once the chain is active, that is, once the sympathy has deepened, the manipulator faces the difficulty of controlling it.

<!-- seg 08-0011 paragraph h=78b29bfc -->
Control is obtained by making the other person believe that he will fulfill his desires by adopting the behaviors the scoundrel wants. The spoken word carries great weight at this stage.

<!-- seg 08-0012 paragraph h=d5ed5107 -->
Once the victim is open, with his guard down, he is induced through dialogue to believe in the advantages of the attitudes the manipulator wants him to take.

<!-- seg 08-0013 paragraph h=49e431b5 -->
But none of this will be possible if the victim is closed to influence. Sympathetic isolation completely hinders this work and is therefore a way of defending ourselves against charlatans.

<!-- seg 08-0014 paragraph h=162c7ffc -->
The most intense and deep-seated desire of a man’s soul, however sublime, altruistic, and wonderful it may be, is his main weak point, the key to his undoing. If an enemy dangles the possibility of satisfying it, he will set his passion ablaze and can lead him wherever he wants, maddened. For this reason, the people who please us can be just as dangerous as those who displease us. Ask yourself: “What are the most intense desires I have?” The answer will reveal the means by which your will can be captured and manipulated by an enemy, making you a slave.
