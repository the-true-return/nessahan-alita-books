5. The tendencies through which belief takes hold

<!-- seg 06-0002 paragraph h=206fd108 -->
Men are so innocent that they quickly believe in any behavior that crooks simulate. They also tend to believe, without doubting, what people they admire or love tell them.

<!-- seg 06-0003 paragraph h=b719a4fa -->
When something is said to someone in direct words, the person is more readily inclined to distrust it than when it is said indirectly, through words that have the consequences the manipulator desires or through simulated behaviors. This is the mechanism of the subliminal advertising used by companies and developed by certain specialists in the art of fooling suckers.

<!-- seg 06-0004 paragraph h=e4e112ab -->
It seems to me exceedingly difficult for men to doubt simulated behaviors. It is enough for someone to pretend to challenge a man, to ridicule him, or to be interested in his wife to quickly divert his attention from targets unrelated to these points. He will then become a defenseless victim.

<!-- seg 06-0005 paragraph h=ef8a4321 -->
A woman is incapable of believing that a man is pretending when he feigns looking at her cleavage or her legs. An arrogant individual cannot suspect the authenticity of the behavior of someone who pretends to be submissive or to be embarrassed by his attacks.

<!-- seg 06-0006 paragraph h=4a4b8966 -->
Other people’s belief is easily led when one tries to steer it in the direction of their natural tendencies: their desires and fears. People readily believe in what they fear and in what they desire. In this way their passions are aroused.

<!-- seg 06-0007 paragraph h=c232dff8 -->
To tame his victim, it is essential for the charlatan to deceive her, making her believe through manipulative attitudes. But the manipulator can also make use of indirect speech, or of direct speech addressed to a third party who is dear to the victim.

<!-- seg 06-0008 paragraph h=649de680 -->
One of the secrets of manipulation consists in being able to identify the possibilities of inducing belief in the other person and instrumentalizing them. The manipulator needs to know in which field to apply them and, above all, when the need arises, to know which is the best belief that can be induced. If the manipulator fails in this calculation, he will fall into discredit and his magnetic power will be diminished.

<!-- seg 06-0009 paragraph h=c2efcafe -->
The manipulator’s antipathetic reaction to the other person’s peculiar way of expressing himself hinders manipulation. Instead of setting force against force, trying to force the victim to give up the antipathetic attitude, the most astute consider it strategic to receive and accept the person just as he is and as he comes to them, steering his beliefs within the possibilities offered by his natural passional tendencies. It is not possible to create new passions, but it is possible to stir up and arouse latent passions that already exist. The difficulty lies in finding the other person’s spontaneous tendencies that are useful for manipulative purposes.

<!-- seg 06-0010 paragraph h=b3bf9bf9 -->
In manipulation, the ability to find meaning in the victim’s preexisting passional weaknesses matters more than the ability to force his will. But for that, it is necessary to anticipate the results that arousing the passions will bring about and to choose the right passion, the one that will produce the desired result. It is a calculation in which a small error can be fatal.

<!-- seg 06-0011 paragraph h=e84257d6 -->
Shortsightedness in detecting the effects of an aroused passion can cause those effects to be turned back against whoever tried to unleash them. Hence the importance, in cases of self-defense in which we return spells and dismantle manipulations, of having a penetrating and all-encompassing consciousness, one able to grasp the facts in breadth and depth so as to minimize the risk of side effects, and, at the same time, of being highly resistant to the magnetic counter-impact of the manipulator who is suffering the effects of the mirror-like return.

<!-- seg 06-0012 paragraph h=5dc901a9 -->
The magnetic counter-impact is the side effect of the attempt at fascination and may arise as a conscious reaction of legitimate defense on the part of the one who is receiving the counter-spell. Only the strengthening of the will through the death of the egos confers resistance against it.

<!-- seg 06-0013 paragraph h=e0e0ab48 -->
When the manipulator comes up against a person who is resistant to his magnetism, he feels powerless and is struck by antipathy. The way to keep our beliefs from being manipulated is to isolate the manipulator in his attempts at manipulation, so that he wastes his time on vain, solitary attempts.
