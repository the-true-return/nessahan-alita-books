10. The use by vile men of the sympathy of most of the links in a chain

<!-- seg 11-0002 paragraph h=1f2ac246 -->
Magnetic currents are skillfully instrumentalized by a special category of vile men: the cowards who flee every individual, one-on-one confrontation with a rival and fight only in full view of several people. I have come across them in many settings.

<!-- seg 11-0003 paragraph h=9f9c452e -->
The presence of an audience that has been co-opted beforehand, and unconsciously, provides the coward with refuge and energetic nourishment. The meaning his figure holds for the collective works as a weapon that can be instrumentalized to endorse his attacks against some unfortunate victim unknown among the crowd. His privileged position (as a leader or an authority, even if not openly acknowledged) allows him to draw on the energetic flow of most of those present to back the force of his blows.

<!-- seg 11-0004 paragraph h=a49af5e8 -->
This reinforcement is achieved through sympathy. The coward who specializes in fighting under the eyes of others suggests to the crowd, imperceptibly, that the ideas he defends converge perfectly with the ideas of the collective present. In this way, the emotional energetic substrate of his speech is reinforced by the unconscious identification of those present with what the scoundrel defends. A quick observation is enough to catch him in the act of giving the ignorant to understand that his ideas and theirs are exactly the same. Thus comes an artificial increase in energy.

<!-- seg 11-0005 paragraph h=c4a6fe97 -->
Moreover, they take advantage of the solitary victim’s concern for his own self-image. They play on this weakness all the time and dominate him by diverting his attention to the preservation of his self-image, making him feel that it is being scratched or destroyed. They may use various tools to induce their opponents into timidity and fear (erudition, mention of the names of literary works or authors esteemed by the group present, mention of titles, positions, the names of some important family they belong to, friendships they have with important figures in society, etc., etc., etc.), and they rely on the collective consensus that these are signs of true superiority, wisdom, and knowledge.
<!-- QUERY: The final clause in the original ("e no consenso coletivo de que as mesmas são sinais...") lacks a verb; "they rely on" was supplied to complete the sentence, which is the most plausible reading. -->

<!-- seg 11-0006 paragraph h=48d6924d -->
In general, this class of eunuchs of the understanding flees in terror from individual encounters. Alone, they are puny and defenseless. When we challenge them, they try at all costs to bring the fight into the collective sphere, their own terrain, since they have no strength of their own and rely only on the strength of others to deal with rivals.

<!-- seg 11-0007 paragraph h=d516eada -->
One way to disempower them is to be more sympathetic than they are to the mass of hypnotized people. In addition, we can force them into discredit by luring them, through their passions, into some attitude that breaks the sympathy of the image on which their powers rest (inducing them to lose control and attack us furiously, for example).

<!-- seg 11-0008 paragraph h=bf3ae844 -->
One way to torment them to unbearable levels is to be superior to them in depth and nobility of spirit, in flow of ideas, and in sophistication of speech. If we act this way, and if these attributes are sympathetic to the community that sustains them, we will drive them into despair through the loss of a point of support and source of nourishment. They then begin to strike themselves.

<!-- seg 11-0009 paragraph h=40ac2de0 -->
We find them in every magnetic chain. They are always the ones who manage to seize the will of others quickly without being detected. The puppets, manipulated, believe they have a will and attitudes of their own, but in fact they simply serve the manipulator’s interests.
