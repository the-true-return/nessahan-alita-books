Introduction

<!-- seg 01-0002 paragraph h=43350e6c -->
The Profane Feminine is the reverse of the wonderful Sacred Feminine, its opposite pole.

<!-- seg 01-0003 paragraph h=af937d25 -->
I did not intend to write any more about the animal side of human females, but the messages I have been receiving have shown the need to clarify a few more points. That is why this work is short.

<!-- seg 01-0004 paragraph h=46467b90 -->
I value the continuous construction of knowledge. The ideas contained here are not permanent. They correspond to my present thinking and may be changed as experience requires.

<!-- seg 01-0005 paragraph h=8441b470 -->
I want to make clear that I do not judge women: it is they themselves who show and reveal themselves in the way I describe them. The existence of exceptions does not invalidate the outlining of a profile.

<!-- seg 01-0006 paragraph h=0ae638c4 -->
I make a point of stressing, once again, that my studies of the female gender are philosophical satires, at times tragicomic, and not sets of simple neurolinguistic techniques, which I do not like at all. I am not on the side of the lie but of the truth. I do not side with deception but with sincerity. My articles are not meant to manipulate but the opposite: to provide knowledge that makes possible self-defense against the fatal feminine magnetism that operates in deceitful manipulations of mind and feeling. We study ways of dismantling female manipulations, not of manipulating women selfishly.

<!-- seg 01-0007 paragraph h=f3676438 -->
Do not confuse my philosophical essays with seduction manuals. These studies deal with living together and winning a woman over with a view to stable relationships.
<!-- QUERY: "convivência e à conquista" → "living together and winning a woman over" (as in the 2008 edition; wording tightened so the Introduction fits on one page). Alternative: "coexistence and courtship". -->

<!-- seg 01-0008 paragraph h=3d4f5217 -->
Once again I reiterate that I do not absolve man of the blame that is his; I simply do not concern myself with denouncing male wickedness in detail, because that is done every day, everywhere. Why throw one more stone when everyone is already throwing them?
