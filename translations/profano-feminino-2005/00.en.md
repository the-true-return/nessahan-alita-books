Its critical, direct, ironic, and incisive tone reflects only the pointing out of flaws, errors, and wiles, and does not signify support for any attempts to cause harm to human beings. It is one thing to dismantle the acts of women who attack us in love and to criticize them in that field. It is something entirely different to cause them harm.

The author makes no pronouncement on the percentage of incidence of this profile among the female population of the various countries.
