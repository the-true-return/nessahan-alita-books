4. Mothers, wives, and daughters

<!-- seg 05-0002 paragraph h=6cbbff1d -->
When dealing with their male children, many mothers talk as if they were mentally handicapped: they swap their R’s for L’s, call everything by a diminutive, and use a specific high-pitched tone of voice[^14]. Besides that, instead of toy tigers, sharks, crocodiles, dragons, and swords, they give them little animals, teddy bears, little ducks, etc., so that they will not grow up.
<!-- QUERY: "trocam os 'R' pelos 'L'" describes Portuguese baby talk; kept literally ("swap their R’s for L’s"). An English equivalent would be swapping R’s for W’s ("wabbit"). -->

<!-- seg 05-0003 footnote n=14 h=1fcfb12f -->
In the first months of life, this conduct may even be a way of establishing emotional affinity with the child. But when it becomes a habit and stretches over several years, it becomes an obstacle that delays the development of masculinity. This is my opinion.

<!-- seg 05-0004 paragraph h=690ad293 -->
As the poor things grow up, their dependence on female affection grows with them. When they become physically adult, they are incapable of living without female affection and the presence of mommy or a substitute. When they fall in love, they transfer the <b>maternal imago</b> to their girlfriend or wife. The transference carries with it the expectations, obligations, and demands. The wretch expects from girls what he was used to expecting from his mother: love. The man in love pleases, obeys, gives presents, and flatters, hoping to receive affection in return, because that worked when he was little. But the result now is always disastrous. Females react in the opposite way from what he expects and disconcert him.

<!-- seg 05-0005 paragraph h=3eca6bd2 -->
Sly ones that they are, women take advantage of this neediness. Far from feeling compassion for the emotional state of males, they use it as a tool to defend themselves and dominate them.

<!-- seg 05-0006 paragraph h=3da29d29 -->
To complete the evil work, the sly ones proclaim to the four winds that the chosen ones will be the sensitive, affectionate, romantic, kind, honest, sincere, and hardworking men. It is as if they were saying: <i>“If you are good boys, we will give you love and sex the way you want them.”</i> It is all nothing but a lie. Experience and direct observation always reveal the opposite.

<!-- seg 05-0007 paragraph h=f699a0c8 -->
Faced with a beautiful woman, men submit and do everything they are told, resembling dogs and beasts of burden. They give the maximum and receive the minimum. They expect to be rewarded with top-quality sex and affection, but they are always frustrated.

<!-- seg 05-0008 paragraph h=66ecdf92 -->
Men in love, when they marry their goddesses, are exploited, deceived, and betrayed. However, the thing began long before...

<!-- seg 05-0009 paragraph h=3bad0286 -->
Females learn to dissemble, deceive, hoodwink, and double-cross from the day they are born. They are protected and taught by their mothers, even if the mothers do not realize it. They practice the art of knavery first on their poor father: lying, getting around his orders and rules, dating in secret, etc. Once they are well trained, the skill will be used with boyfriends and later with husbands. The dynamic is always the same: to enjoy the pleasant aspects of life and to shift the side effects and unpleasant consequences onto someone else. Daughters want to do freely whatever they feel like without ceasing to have their expenses paid by their father. For dating or for promiscuous “hooking up,” girls demand to be treated as adults, but when it comes to paying expenses and the obligations of supporting themselves, they demand to be treated as children. Adult women want to live “freely,” exempt from marital obligations, but without losing the rights of a wife/girlfriend/fiancée. The chumps must hold the bag so that the clever girls can have their fun.

As long as they do not know how to give back the consequences of other people’s attitudes, the wretched males will suffer a great deal at the hands of the sly ones.

He who does not transcend the conception of woman that originates in the maternal relationship will always fall victim to infatuation. Inherent in attributing a maternal meaning to the animal female is attributing a sacred meaning to her, which constitutes a profanation. And one does not cast pearls before swine with impunity.
