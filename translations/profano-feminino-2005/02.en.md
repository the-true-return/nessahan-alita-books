1. The danger of getting it all wrong

<!-- seg 02-0002 paragraph h=d7cfed41 -->
None of the observations, recommendations, suggestions, and reflections contained in my books should be taken dogmatically and uncritically. I do not write for ignoramuses looking for ready-made formulas and finished, closed conceptions. I only provide a provisional model for a better understanding of the contradictions we come up against in love.

<!-- seg 02-0003 paragraph h=562a90ad -->
The messages I have been receiving have shown that many readers did not understand the need to administer contrary attitudes in suitable doses and according to circumstances. On being told of the need to develop strength, confidence, and coldness, they fell into the mistake of polarizing themselves exclusively in distance and indifference, and obtained disastrous results.

<!-- seg 02-0004 paragraph h=d4f9d0b3 -->
The more sensitive ones, who had had bitter experiences, concluded that they should simply be the opposite of what they had always been, and later complained about the lack of results. But they did not hold themselves responsible, as they should have.

<!-- seg 02-0005 paragraph h=7305a640 -->
The fact is that dealing with women is not that easy. It is not enough to adopt the posture of the tough guy, one-sided, and leave it at that. Much more is needed: knowing how to reward good behavior, having firm protective attitudes, being flexible, alternating one’s conduct, etc.

<!-- seg 02-0006 paragraph h=91805005 -->
One point that caused confusion was that of rejection. We should not simply reject a woman when she comes to us and offers herself; instead we should seize the opportunity. We should reject her only when we notice signs of some trap, trick, or emotional knavery, as, for example, when they offer themselves in order to attract us and then scorn us or exchange us for someone else. As long as there are no such signs, we should accept the approach and make the most of it[^1]. However, when we realize that the sly one wants to play the pursued one, we need to be quicker and reject her or give her a “dressing-down” first, robbing her of the feeling of triumph and “shocking” her.

<!-- seg 02-0007 footnote n=1 h=57e194b6 -->
I understand that we should never be unjust or ungrateful toward women who offer us their sincerity without ulterior motives or who seek to understand us. The defensive, dismantling posture is justified only in cases of unmistakable attempts at emotional abuse.

<!-- seg 02-0008 paragraph h=54c9e4bc -->
What is advisable is not simply to push her away gratuitously, indiscriminately, and any old way, but only when we sense the smell of the irresponsible game, of the little game of attracting and repelling. In cases where the signs are explicitly favorable to an approach, the right thing is to seize the moment and draw closer, receiving them naturally, but without lowering one’s guard completely.

<!-- seg 02-0009 paragraph h=9d219de8 -->
At the root of the mistakes I am pointing out lie the belief that it would be enough to replace certain behavioral conditionings with others, and also the conviction that there are general rules that can be applied mechanically to every situation. There is no such thing. The female psyche is complex, and the behavioral and analytical parameters I provide are only guiding principles, effective only when dosed and adapted to the context. Therefore, those who do not know how to identify situations in which to apply them correctly will always obtain results opposite to those expected. One and the same act can produce multiple effects depending on the situations or contexts. Those who believe a set of ready-made recipes is possible that will always work in every situation, regardless of the moment at which they are applied, are very far from understanding this science and would do better to abandon it. Dealing with women is not for the ignorant, the stupid, misogynists, and witless fools of little intelligence[^2].

<!-- seg 02-0010 footnote n=2 h=41e1ef7c -->
Unfortunately, this category of men chases after my books without having been invited, like gate-crashers who barge into a party meant for well-mannered people.

<!-- seg 02-0011 paragraph h=2c6bf8d9 -->
One of our fundamental mistakes consists in not accepting the cold, selfish, and indifferent nature of women toward us. We insist on not accepting reality and on nourishing the absurd hope that they might offer their love to those who ask for it. Against all evidence, we vehemently deny to ourselves the incontestable fact that female love is offered only to those who do not want it, or for as long as we do not want it. This refusal to surrender to reality is the cause of our torment.

<!-- seg 02-0012 paragraph h=5390132e -->
It turns out, then, that the struggle is against ourselves, against the madness of our absurd illusions, dreams, and hopes, and not against the opposite sex, as some who wrote to me believed. It is a waste of time to set oneself against women. Trying to force them to change their conduct is useless. Passion is our great enemy. The need to be loved by those we love is killing us[^3].

<!-- seg 02-0013 footnote n=3 h=7650e83e -->
Because it is excessively intense in men. If it were something sober, it would do good, but the male need to be loved by women tends to be exaggerated in our Western culture, owing to ideological indoctrination begun in childhood and violently reinforced in adolescence. Women seem to me more balanced in this respect, and that is precisely why they dominate men, not always altruistically.

<!-- seg 02-0014 paragraph h=e92d9fee -->
We have to work inwardly toward accepting reality, even if it is painful. Reality will not change; it will not submit to our desires. Human females will not stop being what they are and always have been. So we have to eliminate our desire for them to be coherent, logical, and sensible in love and sex.

<!-- seg 02-0015 paragraph h=d679fc85 -->
Notice that women do not accept that we are human. This happens because they wish to give themselves to a “Beyond-Man.” When they discover that the one beside them is human, suffers, and feels, they become furious instead of feeling compassion. Know that your emotional suffering will provoke irritation instead of pity. It is almost impossible for them to feel pity for the emotional suffering of the one who was supposed to be their man. That is why, if you ask to be loved, you are more likely to provoke revulsion than pity.

<!-- seg 02-0016 paragraph h=1d5c7d79 -->
To love those who do not love us and to feel aversion for those who love us is illogical. However, the female unconscious follows its own principles, and ladies, as a rule, do not control it, do not confront it, and often do not even suspect that it exists[^4]. So it is we who have to study them, observe them, understand them, comprehend them, and accept them, because they will hardly be able to do the same with us. But this understanding will not be possible as long as we are maddened by passion.

<!-- seg 02-0017 footnote n=4 h=60b01e20 -->
When a person, whether woman or man, admits to having a dark side, he or she begins to observe it and understand it, transcending it and becoming happier.

<!-- seg 02-0018 paragraph h=e3c5ad7e -->
When we are in love, we want females to love us, to be with us all the time, as in a continuous sexual union. This continuous demand for contact causes aversion. The worst of it is that they themselves are the ones who ask us for and demand our being in love, but when we give it to them, they reject us and start avoiding us.

<!-- seg 02-0019 paragraph h=42fee898 -->
Every time a man tries to live a “great love”[^5], a terrible misfortune befalls him. Passionate love is very close to hatred because it is irrational, instinctive, and animal. That is why the two forms of madness tend to follow each other.[^6]

<!-- seg 02-0020 footnote n=5 h=2bded291 -->
I am referring to romantic-passionate love, which is insane and is nothing but a sentimental disguise for raw animal instinct, and not to true altruistic love.

<!-- seg 02-0021 footnote n=6 h=5650943e -->
Here is one of the many reasons why I do not write for those who give themselves over to negative feelings. Hatred and resentment worsen a man’s situation; they make him ill.

<!-- seg 02-0022 paragraph h=a8816e2e -->
There is, therefore, no alternative other than to destroy in our hearts all the shadows of attachment, passion, and sentimentalism[^7].

<!-- seg 02-0023 footnote n=7 h=b1066f7f -->
This will not turn us into monsters but, on the contrary, will allow sublime and higher inner states to blossom. I insist so much on the dissolution of sentimentalism because it is dangerous, given its immense magnetic and hypnotic power to seduce and deceive by passing itself off as something sublime and spiritual.

<!-- seg 02-0024 paragraph h=cb75306f -->
If you are suffering over some sly one, you could die and you would not arouse any pity. You will be seen as a weak man, useless, good-for-nothing, incapable of taking care of himself, a rag, a dog, a rat. So do not waste time suffering over any “tramp”[^8]. Love yourself and do not dream of ridiculous hypocritical romances. The danger and falseness of rose-colored loves exist in direct proportion to their beauty and fascination. Learn to resist what is lovely, wonderful, and fascinating.

<!-- seg 02-0025 footnote n=8 h=4210f767 -->
By “tramp,” understand an idle shrew who spends her time playing with what is most serious to a man: his sincere feelings. Not all women, however, do this. I have observed several women who, because they deal very well with their dark side instead of denying it, do not play nasty jokes with male love.

<!-- seg 02-0026 paragraph h=8d24f278 -->
First of all, know that you will die and be forgotten. Therefore, seek happiness within your soul and not outside it. Give yourself over to your Spirit. Only He will be with you after death.

<!-- seg 02-0027 paragraph h=4065a4a8 -->
Your partner will be less cold if she believes you are “the man,” “the one,” “the real deal,” but she will never love you as you would like, that is, for what you are: a human being who suffers and feels. Above all, she will never feel tenderness for you. However, if she is treated as a mere female, she will feel attraction. She will complain endlessly, but the interesting results will make themselves felt.
<!-- QUERY: "'o cara', 'o tal', 'o bom'" (colloquial praise) → “the man,” “the one,” “the real deal,” as in the 2008 edition. Alternatives: “the guy,” “the big shot,” “the good one.” -->

<!-- seg 02-0028 paragraph h=f9c9418a -->
Do not simply treat her with coldness and indifference: be her mirror most of the time. Tell her you love her only when she says it, give presents when you receive them, but be distant when she is cold. Always return what you receive in the same way. If you receive affection, return it (not too much). If you receive coldness, return it with coldness. If she avoids you, unmask her and avoid her. If she complains about all this, throw it back in her face. This requires freedom from passion and behavioral deconditioning. Pay her back in the same coin and, at times, even more intensely than you received. Train her[^9]. Nevertheless, have your point of support in masculinity: be more fearsome than lovable, colder than affectionate, more cruel than merciful, more distant than close. Even so, always be absolutely mysterious, protective, and domineering. Obviously, never fail to wear her out with intense sex.

Isn’t this what Karen Salmansohn and Amy Sutherland suggest all the time, which is why they are applauded?

<!-- seg 02-0030 paragraph h=808acccc -->
Let us now look at some intentional and malicious distortions. Certain idiots, male and female, have said that my writings encourage male promiscuity. That is a lie. What I do is denounce the female preference for promiscuous men. In truth, I am against promiscuity and sexual degeneration. If at some point I wrote that women set up socio-psychic structures that corner men and push them into promiscuity, I did so as a denunciation, so that the wile might be shattered. Moreover, the man who knows the female psyche and is able to bind to himself a woman he considers interesting and with whom he has great affinity will be much more satisfied and will tend less toward promiscuity and changing partners.

According to other imbeciles, I supposedly attack women, and this would therefore be evidence that we, the students of male love crises and of female wiles, do not appreciate them, or sex either. Here is another slanderous piece of knavery. What we teach is precisely how to defeat them in the war of passion so as to obtain what is always refused: certainties, definitions, clarity, and transparency in love and sex. Obviously, nobody would look for such elements in romantic relationships if he did not like the best that women have to offer. However, when we identify with these three attractions and begin to pursue them passionately like madmen, we lose them. So, to have them, one must first not desire them.

Some lunatics judge that I am simply a rebel trying to defame women, and they do so because this is the conclusion most accessible to their deteriorated brains. The short imagination they possess does not allow them to conclude that there may be lofty motives for someone who unveils bad intentions and tricks in love. In the case of this ridiculous accusation that I am unmasking now, what logical dissection reveals is, besides plain and simple stupidity, a naive attempt at defensive manipulation against truths whose revelation and demonstration are uncomfortable. The underlying idea these manipulators work with is that males could not criticize female postures, nor become indignant or defend themselves against psychological wiles that destroy sincerity in relationships. In short, they argue, fallaciously, that we should remain passive in the face of female tricks because such passivity would be, in their view, proof that we like females.[^10] They want to induce the following belief in the unwary: that it is impossible to find defects in something delicious. They suggest that when we like something, we do not criticize it, and when we do not like something, we criticize it; in other words, they defend irrationality. They hide the fact that delicious females have developed sophisticated wiles to cheat us and not to hand over their treasures to us (sex, affection, and love). No one has ever heard of men who wanted to build a society without women or to banish them from the Earth, but that is exactly the discourse of many women, including feminists. We know very well that we cannot live without females, and we acknowledge that fact[^11], while they generally adopt the opposite posture.
