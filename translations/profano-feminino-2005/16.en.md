Conclusions

<!-- seg 16-0002 paragraph h=b8d2022e -->
The knowledge developed in this book, and in the previous ones, constitutes only a provisional theoretical model that aims to help men by making the stressful behavioral contradictions of women in the realm of love comprehensible. Theoretical models must always be replaced by more refined theoretical models, and I hope this one will be too. It is not my wish to form fanatical, dogmatic, and extremist readers, devotees of stupidity and fixed conceptions, which would be anti-philosophical. If you have this tendency toward ignorance, I suggest you throw this work in the wastebasket and never read it again. I do not write for the stupid.

<!-- seg 16-0003 paragraph h=1c8aadd6 -->
All the criticisms developed here are limited to the terrain of love and do not extend to others. I would very much like to be wrong about the reality of love but, unfortunately, my commitment to the truth does not allow me to write the opposite of what I observe, verify, and confirm daily.
