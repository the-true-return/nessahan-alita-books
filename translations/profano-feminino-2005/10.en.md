9. Shattering the little emotional hells

<!-- seg 10-0002 paragraph h=5997d008 -->
You must have noticed by now that females tend to surprise us by suddenly disappearing or abruptly not returning our phone calls. They also tend to promise and not deliver, to oscillate between coming closer and pulling away, to feed expectations and frustrate them, to suggest that they will call and then not do it, to provoke our fury on purpose, to do exactly what we do not want or what we hate, etc. These are the well-known little emotional hells that they create voluntarily and consciously. They do this to torment us with doubt and to test us. They want to find out whether we will despair, whether we will go looking for them like madmen, etc.

<!-- seg 10-0003 paragraph h=77c84371 -->
The sudden disappearance is sometimes preceded by clues and signs that make it possible to detect it: silence, bad moods, or mild disagreements, on some occasions, or, most of the time, misleading clues that suggest the opposite of what is about to happen: an excess of affection, of kindness, of eagerness, and of goodwill. If your woman is being very sweet, helpful, affectionate, etc., watch out, because you may be being prepared to take the impact of the withdrawal syndrome caused by the sudden disappearance. When you realize that she is going to disappear, stop calling, or stop answering the phone on purpose to torment you, get ahead of her and steal her triumph by ordering her not to look for you, not to call, and to wait for an indefinite time until you do. This way you disarm this little emotional game, because you order her to do precisely what she was planning to do. If that is too difficult, then do the opposite: get ahead of her by announcing a punitive decision that will turn the consequences of the sudden vanishing act heavily back on the sly one.

<!-- seg 10-0004 paragraph h=22dbd7e3 -->
As a general and overriding rule, the sudden vanishing act only happens after we have been “hooked.” The female vanishes when she begins to suspect that you will be affected by her absence. The intention is to provoke growing suffering in order to intensify the passion.

<!-- seg 10-0005 paragraph h=31f1173c -->
Hellish little games such as suddenly disappearing or not calling work by contrariness: the sly ones do what they believe will displease, bother, or hurt us. Therefore, they are disarmed when we lead them to believe that the planned act will in fact have the opposite effect and will please us or serve our interests instead of thwarting us. Then, in order to displease and hurt us, opposite attitudes will be necessary.

<!-- seg 10-0006 paragraph h=55206759 -->
Order her to do precisely what was planned to torment you. Obviously, this strategy of emotional defense must be put in context and measured out according to the circumstances, like all the others. Do not go ordering her to do something you cannot stand.

<!-- seg 10-0007 paragraph h=600d1c5a -->
The indefiniteness that torments us so much originates in attitudes, postures, words, and facial expressions that contradict one another. In this way, we are left with absolutely no idea of the truth, and doubt torments us. What confuses us is contradictory behavior: at some moments the sly one implies that she loves us, and at others she implies precisely the opposite. There are moments when our girlfriends seem to want an eternal union with us and moments when they act as if they did not want to see us anywhere near them. Obviously, this hellish contradiction is always denied, and the more you try to force her to acknowledge it, the worse everything will get. The more you argue and interrogate, the deeper you will sink into confusion and doubt, until it reaches unbearable levels of madness. Then horrible conflicts and fights will arise and, if the man is emotionally uncontrolled and weak, physical aggression. In the end, we will always be the villains of the story. No one will be interested in our reasons, even if they are solidly grounded and coherent.

<!-- seg 10-0008 paragraph h=8f0cd355 -->
The first thing to do during such hells is to identify clearly which contradictory behaviors are bothering you and creating the confusion. Once they are identified, do not waste time arguing; simply corner her by giving her a very short deadline to correct herself, under penalty of bearing the unpleasant consequences of the situation that will be created if she does not. If the girl does not change, it means that she is no good and only wanted to deceive you. If she changes, accept her, but always leave the punishment pending. Above all, what matters is to discover the truth about female feelings and intentions, so that we are free of later worries. To wrest out this truth, which is never revealed spontaneously, we need to find the right attitudes, ones that can be taken unilaterally, without any need for cooperation from the other party, and that have the effect of turning the unpleasant consequences of the little games heavily back around, punishing the one who tries to play with her own little game. This does not mean that we have to stay passive in the face of the hellish little games, merely observing them, but rather that we must quickly find the mirror actions that reverse them and wound the one who launched them.

<!-- seg 10-0009 paragraph h=9e4e0516 -->
The manipulator needs to believe that you are unaware of what is going on in order to persist in the damned little games, because it is precisely this belief that motivates her, since it gives the nasty prank its meaning. While she is toying with the male’s happiness and feelings, the female takes delight in imagining that she is manipulating his mind and his belief system. She wants to induce him to imagine a thousand things so that he will suffer! Driven by the desire for emotional revenge, she tries to keep herself inaccessible at all costs to prevent the doubts from dissipating. However, if you manage to reach her somehow (which is very difficult when they take it into their heads to withdraw into their shell) and unmask her in a short, direct, clear, and accurate way, showing that you are aware of what is going on and walking away quickly before an argument begins, you will be able to turn the game around. It is important to hit her emotionally and walk away quickly, not giving a fight time to begin, because that way you will manage to leave an anxiety hanging in the air. All that matters is to shatter the wretched little game, not to win the argument, nor to force her to confess that she plays dirty. For that, it is enough to show that the wile has been perceived. When we let ourselves be drawn into the argument, we are supplying information, revealing everything we think and feel. Instead of doubts, we send certainties and confirmations.

When we deal with incorrigibly infantilized people, who enjoy playing with other people’s feelings, there is no alternative but to manage the relationship by means of terrible regulations and dictatorial laws[^21]. Before the emotional hell sets in, get ahead of it and announce, without allowing any discussion, the consequences that each dishonest attitude will bring. Hand the responsibility back to the sly one; make her understand that she has the obligation to answer for her actions, since she is not a child. The crook needs to learn to bear the consequences of what she does. Let us look at some undesirable attitudes that may have the definitive end of the relationship as their consequence (always be careful to make it very clear that it will be <b>she</b> who is responsible for the end of the relationship, and not you):

<!-- seg 10-0011 footnote n=21 h=6b73b1bc -->
Note that I am recommending managing the relationship, not subjugating the partner against her will. The “terrible” regulations I refer to are non-negotiable rules for maintaining honesty in love and justice, not selfish norms that would put the man in the wrong by favoring him exclusively. They are “terrible” in the sense of being non-negotiable and relentless. The dictatorship I refer to is the dictatorship of reason. Obviously, the partner must be free to leave the relationship if she does not agree with the rigor of the rules and prefers to opt for dishonesty in love. There is no sense in trying to force a person to be honest or to remain in a relationship if she explicitly wishes the opposite. As Schopenhauer said: “Love is like faith; it does not let itself be forced.”

The “punishment” for all these sentimental crooked tricks must be the definitive end of the relationship, but the sly one must be informed in advance so that she cannot claim ignorance. If we leave any loophole for this claim to be used, the tormenting will continue. If we leave the slightest loophole for the decision to end it to seem to have come from us, the plan will backfire, because she will feel free of guilt. Therefore, it is important not to argue but merely to communicate, unilaterally and with absolute clarity, that the decision is in her hands, since it will be her attitudes that will define the course of the relationship. In cases of sentimental fraud, a terrible letter of warning that informs properly has more effect than a thousand love letters, since females are not susceptible to good sense.

<!-- seg 10-0020 paragraph h=53bfc6e1 -->
If you are skillful and your maneuver has been correct, the anxiety and doubt that had been meant for you will immediately be transferred back to the other side and will begin to torment her. For this maneuver to work, reason and justice must be on your side. Whoever tries to carry out this counter-manipulation unjustly will suffer the consequences of the backfire. Do not try to be a manipulator; just defend yourself relentlessly when necessary.

<!-- seg 10-0021 paragraph h=219aefeb -->
Here is an invariable characteristic that sums up female cunning: the wiles and little games aim to conceal the woman’s real feelings and intentions, while at the same time manipulating and testing the man’s real feelings and intentions. They want to find out what we feel and to hide what they feel. That is why they wrest defined positions from us by force but give us back indefiniteness. That is also why they swim in certainty while we remain in confusion. That is why for them everything is clear and defined while for us everything is always one great cloud of tormenting indefiniteness.

<!-- seg 10-0022 paragraph h=679bb844 -->
The central point from which everything originates is the inner state. Therein lies the greatest difficulty of all. The question is not external but internal. Attitudes and behaviors are outward expressions of inner states. The woman’s inner states reflect the man’s inner states and vice versa. The great problem, we might say the supreme problem, consists in finding the specific inner state that produces in us the behaviors and attitudes that protect us from hell. This state cannot be described exactly in its qualia, because it lies beyond the reach of language. It can be experienced directly but not defined satisfactorily. Roughly speaking, we could try, precariously, to describe it as an absolutely quiet and impenetrable mind, a total absence of negative feelings, and a penetrating consciousness. Outwardly, the woman may see a silent, disconcerting, calm, distant, wise, mysterious, intriguing man, etc., and she will not know whether we are concentrated, distracted, attentive, tranquil, sad, or furious. But even so, this description is deficient. The correct state is a state of soul higher than the ordinary states, in which identification, fascination, passionate chaos, and mental acceleration are easily visible. In other words: we have to acquire a state of consciousness higher than that of the person with whom we interact, resisting the magnetism of all her passionate provocations, good and bad. The attempt at a merely outward change is doomed to failure; it is simple pretense.

<!-- seg 10-0023 paragraph h=6eedcd79 -->
In the correct inner state there is not the slightest desire to argue. There is no desire to impose a point of view or for the companion to understand what she does not want to understand. There is no desire to convince, and one takes no part in the damned little games. The witch with a fairy’s face plays alone and condemns herself to frustration. The solution lies in desiring nothing, demanding nothing, and expecting nothing. What matters is to make her play alone. It is the shortest path I know. Instead of taking part in the little emotional hells by trying to reverse them, we distance ourselves and isolate ourselves. We shatter an emotional hell when we die to it. Then we are able to agree with everything and at the same time cooperate with nothing, sabotaging and frustrating by means of distance, silence, and non-action. Do not try to win the war of passion; sabotage it by taking no part.

The woman defeats the man’s muscular and rational strength through her power to drive him to madness and make him irrational. The irrational and the insane are the terrain on which they feel at ease and act with assurance, handling situations very well, because what they possess of legitimate understanding, outside the field of sentimental selfishness, is almost nil.

<!-- seg 10-0025 paragraph h=e53133a1 -->
When the emotional hell reaches an exaggeratedly critical level, the man’s greatest urge, as he loses control, may even be to give them a few good slaps, because of the excessive stress. However, if he does so, he will put himself in the wrong and will have “given weapons to the enemy.” A good way to vent this anger is to reach them and say those truths stuck in our throats that we never have the courage to say.

<!-- seg 10-0026 paragraph h=3be9acb3 -->
The weak point through which the man is first taken, weakened, and brought down is his vain desire and hope that his companion will understand the obvious and act coherently. This is a beginning of passion, because it is a desire that clouds the perception and acceptance of inevitable reality.

<!-- seg 10-0027 paragraph h=5b674331 -->
The irrationality to which we are magnetically drawn, and against which we have to resist, is the irrationality of love, hatred, desire, and repulsion. She will always act contradictorily, provoking contrary feelings in you, and she will create a hell whenever you attempt any kind of agreement, discussion, or bilateral, democratic, and sensible dialogue, because the world she lives in is the world of senselessness.

<!-- seg 10-0028 paragraph h=9b33f046 -->
Trying to make them understand the obvious is a mistake, and the man who proceeds this way is falling into a trap whose result is catastrophic. More efficient results are achieved with the reverse procedure: silently making sound decisions. The puzzle is truly a demonic game. A wrong decision will cause a disaster. The key is to find the correct decisions and attitudes, which is not easy. Warning, cautioning, asking for understanding, trying to demonstrate mistakes, etc., is a complete waste of time. They are only susceptible to the realistic impact of facts felt in real time or <i>a posteriori</i>.

<!-- seg 10-0029 paragraph h=c9d9a0f4 -->
What matters is not forcing them to be what we want, nor forcing them to admit their mistakes. Whoever goes down these two paths will have fallen into a trap and will hit rock bottom, completely mad. What matters is to create, by means of a free and powerful will, situations that oblige them to reveal what they really want and feel. What matters is to oblige them to define themselves and to show what they hide behind their contradictory behavior. Obviously, the incoherent attitudes are meant to cover something up, to hide intentions. They aim to install and keep doubts, questions, and confusions in our heads. Disconcerting female behavior has as its goal creating and maintaining questions that strike us violently in the heart by remaining unresolved. The answers to the questions that torment you will not be given for free, even if you beg in every way. They will only be obtained by means of radical and definitive actions that wrest them out. It is like hunting prey: you must close off every passage so that the sly one does not escape and, on top of that, you must be slyer and anticipate the wiles of the deception, frustrating the attempt at frustration.

<!-- seg 10-0030 paragraph h=be4348ed -->
The capacity to react correctly to female attempts to induce madness does not develop overnight. It takes a long time, and one goes through a great deal of suffering, before reaching a satisfactory level.

<!-- seg 10-0031 paragraph h=ecf1f6dc -->
Any dishonest or improper behavior by your partner must be immediately followed by behaviors of yours that retaliate, give it back, and corner her: silence, distance, and no contact for an indefinite time, in milder cases, or surprising attitudes that hit them directly in the emotions, in more serious cases. Instead of arguing, act.

<!-- seg 10-0032 paragraph h=e764082b -->
Observe her and discover her weak points, what she fears within the relationship (e.g., being abandoned, being traded for another, not receiving favors or money from you, etc.). Find the most uncomfortable punishments, ones that hit her directly in her feelings, and keep them at hand to use when it is just and legitimate, that is, when she tries to wound you first with these same weapons.

It is very hard to find a man who understands this, and even harder for one to appear who manages to accomplish it in himself. Women almost always win this war of hells.

<!-- seg 10-0034 paragraph h=43412ac3 -->
When we face the difficult situations forged by human beings, we are simultaneously facing outer and inner events. Every difficult outer situation creates an astral hell: betrayals by friends, persecutions by enemies, disappointments on the part of loved ones, and also provocations from women. Only by fighting within ourselves can we overcome the difficulty that strikes us from outside. The hell must be shattered inwardly, not fought on its outer side. It is of no use to think up strategies to resolve difficult situations if we neglect their psychic aspect, that is, the horrible feelings and thoughts that they unleash within us.
