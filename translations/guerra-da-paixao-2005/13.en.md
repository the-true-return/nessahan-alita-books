12. The ideal male profile

<!-- seg 13-0002 paragraph h=889dc9ea -->
If your relationship is worn out, your partner ignores you, refuses sex, does not want to see you, etc., this means that your person, as it has always been, no longer interests her. Therefore, it is time to “die” and become someone else. Understand this well: “dying,” here, means becoming another person completely different from who you have always been, changing yourself to the point of causing strangeness, a sense of loss. It is a symbolic “death”: the real death of your egos, that is, of the psychological person you are.

<!-- seg 13-0003 paragraph h=4388c25a -->
If you are in a panic, desperate, or depressed because your beloved has betrayed you or despises you, and you are thinking of suicide, I suggest you do not do it. Choose to “die” psychologically rather than make an attempt on your own life or against your own physical body. It is better to transform yourself psychologically than to commit suicide, don’t you think?

<!-- seg 13-0004 paragraph h=475e9722 -->
If you truly “die” within yourself, you will become another person in fact, and not by mere supposition or simulation. You will not simply be simulating a behavior but will have truly transformed yourself. You will no longer be recognized; the woman who made you suffer will find you strange and will despair because she has lost you.

<!-- seg 13-0005 paragraph h=3d7d897d -->
If you are being despised, this indicates that you may be making the following mistakes:

<!-- seg 13-0006 paragraph h=0889a807 -->
• Being excessively affectionate;

<!-- seg 13-0007 paragraph h=881a0540 -->
• Talking too much;

<!-- seg 13-0008 paragraph h=eeed7dc6 -->
• Trying to please her all the time;

<!-- seg 13-0009 paragraph h=df364892 -->
• Showing fear of losing her;

<!-- seg 13-0010 paragraph h=87a75c17 -->
• Demanding attention, affection, and sex;

<!-- seg 13-0011 paragraph h=301989dc -->
• Demanding her presence and company;

<!-- seg 13-0012 paragraph h=aebacf92 -->
• Running after her all the time, calling nonstop, etc.

<!-- seg 13-0013 paragraph h=7486e880 -->
Despite the lies the sly ones tell, the fact is that a very affectionate man becomes tiresome and serves only to be rejected and treated like a slave or a mongrel dog. Affection must be well dosed, rationed. Be affectionate only now and then and at the right moments: as a reward for good conduct. Be more cold than affectionate, but not totally cold.

<!-- seg 13-0014 paragraph h=2b03d36c -->
Generally, a man strives and sacrifices himself intensely, flattering and pleasing, in order to receive in return a minimal amount of affection and sex. This tendency is general and you will be able to confirm it through observation. Women are so used to this that whenever you show yourself affectionate you will be seen as a harasser in search of the sex-affection-love triad. Since they are not very fond of sex, the result is that affectionate men are considered uninteresting and are used as mere emotional slaves who give everything of themselves and receive little or nothing in return. The curious thing is that it is precisely the insensitive ones, who are very few, who receive for free and without effort what the affectionate men and the harassers struggle so hard to obtain. This happens because women do not understand the logic of love in depth. In the field of love relationships, the minds of the sly ones work like the minds of swindlers: they are incapable of understanding events from any point of view other than their own. The only point of reference in love that exists is themselves, for they live immersed in a natural selfishness. It is a waste of time, therefore, to pressure and demand affection, love, and sex from those who refuse them, because such an act will always produce the diametrically opposite effect.

<!-- seg 13-0015 paragraph h=6fb7889e -->
Do not be a chatterbox and do not talk too much. If you observe, you will see that these women’s conversations are usually superficial and subjective. If you enter into this frivolous subjectivity, taking part in useless conversations, listening to litanies, psychological songs, gossip, soap opera endings, malicious talk about other people’s lives, etc., their unconscious will react by considering you not very masculine, since among the ideal male characteristics are rational objectivity, firmness, depth, superiority, and dominance (that is why they pursue leaders). The disastrous result will be the following: your partner will consider you “nice,” “kind,” and “pleasant,” but very unattractive as a male. Likewise, you will fall into an equally ridiculous situation if you keep trying to be funny, to make her laugh, to make funny faces, to hang upside down from branches, etc., for you will be treated as a cheerful and well-meaning clown. Also, do not get into arguments; resist the fatal magnetism of the viperish tongue and ignore useless complaints. Do not keep shouting, because you will be considered not very masculine. We want them to see us as males, and not as friends, pets, or clowns, right? Therefore, the ideal is to be silent, serious, stern, and to talk little. However, these few words must be well judged, always in a tone of command and in a protective and guiding manner, directed toward good and never toward evil. Never legitimize the accusation that men are oppressors and enemies of women.

<!-- seg 13-0016 paragraph h=037e55a0 -->
Do not run after her fantasies, trying to satisfy them, because you will be considered a mere submissive slave. We may even do this now and then, but not always, because it communicates submission.

<!-- seg 13-0017 paragraph h=866d95fb -->
This is the hardest part: do not be afraid of losing her. If you have this fear, it will show through your attitudes or during the relentless tests to discover who we are. Be continually willing to lose her for real, forever. If you feel afraid of losing her, you will be showing that you do not have other, better, more faithful, more dedicated, more sincere, and more beautiful women at your disposal and that, therefore, you are a second-class male, hardly capable of getting females. You will also communicate that you want to pressure her, smothering her with your feelings and attachments. Women greatly value detachment and indifference when combined with a protective-guiding posture that is beneficial to them. Detachment, sentimental coldness, and insensitivity are considered ideal male characteristics. Let it be understood that such attributes are not intrinsically bad, for they may very well be used to fight wrong things, to protect, and to avoid dangers. Coldness is bad when it is turned toward selfishness, but not when it translates into calm directed altruistically. That is why women value attributes like these.

<!-- seg 13-0018 paragraph h=894f5b3b -->
Harassing them and pursuing them is also a serious mistake. The harasser is always rejected because he communicates that he is incapable of obtaining something more important in life. Harassment communicates weakness, submission, desperation, urgency, etc. Therefore, do not keep phoning nonstop, pursuing her all the time, etc. Let her do this with you, and if she does not... too bad! Do not seek her out; let her seek you out as often as she likes. That way you will know who she really is and what she truly feels.

<!-- seg 13-0019 paragraph h=cdad396b -->
When she wants to see you, do not resist; but when she suddenly disappears, simply forget her, ignore her, and busy yourself with other things, disappearing for even longer, normally for twice as long.

<!-- seg 13-0020 paragraph h=b7e5cf84 -->
Women are used to being flattered all the time in exchange for sex, affection, and love. They have adapted so thoroughly to flattery, gifts, compliments, special treatment, privileges, etc., that they get a shock when a man ignores them. They feel diminished, small; they believe they are losing the competition with their rivals, and their self-esteem drops terribly. As a result, they often pursue him out of revenge, in an attempt to reject him as soon as they can bend him.

<!-- seg 13-0021 paragraph h=dcb34d8f -->
Do not fall into the trap of the good, democratic, and pliable boyfriend. Be democratic if she deserves it, but be firm in your points of view and change them only if the errors are objectively demonstrated. If she resists, demolish all her arguments, run over them and crush them (note well: the arguments, is that clear?) without pity and without hesitation. The act of giving in is seen as a sign of weakness of spirit, because it indicates little firmness of purpose and little willpower. Pliability is never recognized and repaid but, on the contrary, is exploited as a chance to abuse the other. The pliable man is considered a sucker and not a wonderful man. They seek males who will guide, dominate, and protect them, and not servants who will satisfy their capricious whims.

<!-- seg 13-0022 paragraph h=b1a32e6d -->
The ideal man, modeled according to our objectives, speaks little and to the point (this is just a model for reference). He uses a deep and imperative tone of voice. He speaks in a tone of command. He does not ask his partner for permission: he orders, but does not force her to obey, leaving her the right to refuse. He does not talk about himself. He does not complain. He does not confess his weaknesses. He does not cry in his partner’s presence. He is not a chatterbox. He looks into her eyes suddenly, fixedly and firmly. He does not watch her all the time, only now and then. He does not hover over her: he almost ignores her existence. He does not argue. He does not debate: he simply informs. He is a king in his domain and not a servant. He does not miss her, does not pine for her. He does not harass. He does not keep looking at other women’s bodies, because he is neither lustful nor a fornicator. Despite this, when the female finally seeks him out for sex, he shows his strength in wild, overwhelming sex like a hurricane. He is an earthquake in bed. He does not use pickup lines: he pleases without effort. He does not shout. He does not let dirty games go unnoticed: he knows how to hand them back. He is not a clown. He is not funny. He does not laugh often: he only smiles slightly now and then. When he finally laughs, his laughter seems to have something strange about it. He takes the lead in situations. He dominates the relationship for good and not for evil, treating the woman like a little girl. He does not pester his partner by asking her opinion all the time. He is not irritated by provocations: he knows how to hand the consequences back to whoever launched them. He is impenetrable, distant, and mysterious. He neither forbids nor takes revenge: he hands back the consequences, rewarding the sincere women and making the insincere ones who try to deceive him bear the burden of their own acts. He does not chase after lies, for it does not matter to him whether he is being deceived or not. He does not commit himself for free: he charges a high price. He is a prize. He values himself. He is not affectedly sensitive. He is not delicate. He may have a lot of money, but he despises it. He is above social prejudices. He is neither a moralist nor a “goody-goody” fellow, a friend of good manners. When he enters a room, he attracts women’s attention because he ignores them. He does not beg to be loved. He does not need passionate affection to be happy: he despises it, knowing it to be false and hypocritical; he prefers true love. He helps. He advises. He cares. He protects. He guides. He commits no injustices against his partner. He always keeps reason on his side. He uses hardness and firmness for good and not for evil. He is disconcerting. He surprises. He is not predictable. He is not moved by onion tears, he ignores crocodile tears, he is moved only by real tears, which he knows very well how to identify. He does not chase after capricious complaints. He fuses opposite characteristics. He is at the same time good and, in a certain sense, “bad,” indifferent and protective. He punishes adultery with a definitive, unappealable break, or with contempt. He never commits a crime of passion. If he is betrayed or deceived, his mere absence and contempt will be enough to punish the traitress, who will suffer because she cannot find another like him to replace him. He is the best of all because he does what none of the others do: he treats her like a little girl, making her feel like a child, small, reminding her of her childhood, instead of deifying her and placing offerings on her altar. His heart is worth gold; he charges a high price to commit himself: total, complete, and transparent fidelity. He is an incomprehensible mystery. In short: he is a real Man.

<!-- seg 13-0023 paragraph h=826bb83b -->
Of course, no mortal man would fit this model with mathematical exactness and in its entirety. But the model serves as a reference for us to approach it.

<!-- seg 13-0024 paragraph h=7fe3a69d -->
When a man is not being noticed, he usually monkeys around, harasses, uses pickup lines and compliments, looks with a covetous and hungry gaze, etc.; this indicates that he is ignorant of this science and that he is not behaving as he should. If he changed the way he treats her, replacing harassment with the right attitudes, attraction would be activated. The need to harass shows ignorance of the behaviors that generate attraction. He who acts correctly has no need to harass. The only women who accept hungry, desperate, and anxious harassers who throw out lame pickup lines with famished looks are the desperate ones: those who have children going hungry and urgently need a provider, the old maids who have not yet lost hope, the unbearable bores, etc. If, despite everything, an interesting woman accepts such repulsive behavior, she will do so for some other reason, such as money or status, but never because she has felt attracted.

<!-- seg 13-0025 paragraph h=cc19bf1b -->
The fact that we are not harassers does not mean that we should remain passive, standing still and waiting for some woman to fall from the sky. You can, and indeed should, take the initiative, acting as a male who makes an impact, strikes psychically, astonishes, and even shocks positively (positively, let that be clear!), but never as a feeble, desperate harasser.

<!-- seg 13-0026 paragraph h=61cb199d -->
The act of “horrifying” positively, already explained in the first volume, consists of breaking established ideas, behaving in a way absolutely opposite to the usual one but well calculated, that is, with behavior that shows one is different from the feeble. It demands great skill, for the slightest mistake can produce the opposite of the desired effect. “Horrification” must be positive and not negative (keep that well in mind!). An example of positive “horrification”: giving an order in a serious tone that runs counter to what a beautiful sly one is doing but that, ultimately, benefits and protects her. This attitude runs contrary to the tendency of all the feeble, who hasten to please her and submit instead of treating her “with the sword”[^40], as Ulysses did with Circe. Those who are incapable of contradicting her are enslaved by animal passion and turn into swine like Ulysses’ companions. The superior male not only commands her but contradicts her and could not care less whether she will like it or not. He does not worry about recriminations, disappointments, etc., because he does not want to impress; yet precisely by renouncing the wish to impress, he impresses her.

When people speak of the ideal male profile, the one we are trying to model, a dangerous mistake usually occurs. I will denounce it: there are two ideal male profiles. One of these profiles is ideal for achieving selfish female objectives, and the other is ideal for achieving male objectives. Normally, the ideal male profile described and portrayed in movies, magazines, soap operas, interviews, etc. is false, because it corresponds only to selfish female objectives: it would be the sensitive, cuddly guy who sends flowers, hardworking, honest, affectionate, and with money, always at her disposal. Since this objective is entirely calculating and selfish in its ends and its means, it turns out to be contrary to our objectives and becomes devastating for our lives when we adopt it. Almost all women unanimously claim that such men are ideal and that they would like to have them by their side, but they do not say what they are ideal for, nor what they want them for. I will tell you: they are ideal for being emotional slaves, giving love and receiving coldness, cuckold’s horns, disdain, abuse, etc. in return.

<!-- seg 13-0029 paragraph h=c7bf9fbe -->
The ideal male profile I have described here and tried to model is by no means this soap-opera profile that they describe. It is an ideal profile for protecting oneself against domination in love, manipulation, deception, lies, dissimulation, disrespect, and being cuckolded. Although it seems contradictory, it is a profile that also benefits women, even though they protest against it when they are thoughtless.

<!-- seg 13-0030 paragraph h=0b397a24 -->
It is essential to resist the influences of fascination in all their forms. Fascination is hypnotic, and we can define it as an identification of our person with external facts. Contrary to common sense, fascination operates not only when there is liking and dazzlement but also in negative situations of conflict. Hostile words, offenses, insults, provocations, scorn, etc. provoke as much fascination as compliments, affection, promises, etc. Fascination by negative attitudes provokes negative emotional states. If you are mad with rage because you were double-crossed, overjoyed because you got what you wanted, sad because you were abandoned, etc., you are fascinated by these events. We must not, therefore, be fascinated either by good or by evil.

<!-- seg 13-0031 paragraph h=03ff9db7 -->
You will not be able to simulate this ideal male profile I have modeled here. If you merely try to pretend to be like this without really being so, your plan will backfire: you will develop emotional illnesses and will be unmasked in the selective mating tests directed by their animal instinct (we all have animal instincts; no one should be offended by this), falling into ridiculous situations. The female instinct possesses an ancestral wisdom, developed since prehistoric times, and quickly allows them to identify a fraud who wants to mate. Be a real Man, with a capital M. But for that you will have to die within yourself and become someone else. It is a hard, arduous task. Many fail in this attempt.

<!-- seg 13-0032 paragraph h=b70190dc -->
Men today seem to be ashamed of being what they are. The fashion is to be affectedly sensitive, and anyone who raises the banner of masculinity and heterosexuality is considered prehistoric, a troglodyte, backward, and a male chauvinist. The male is cornered. It is commonly said that we are good for nothing. However, all women remember us in times of danger and of difficult tasks. No one dares to say we are useless when floods, earthquakes, and fires occur. And if it were not for us, the males, our species would not have survived natural dangers and wild beasts since prehistoric times. Who was it that hunted mammoths and faced saber-toothed tigers so that women would have protein to eat? Who was it that waded into rivers infested with crocodiles, piranhas, and snakes to bring them fish? Therefore, it is not indispensable to have a uterus and shelter life in the womb in order for someone to be indispensable. Of course, without women we do not exist, and their importance has never been denied by real men. No man would idealize a world without women. In the same way, the intrinsically masculine characteristics I described above are, and always have been, indispensable to women, regardless of what they say. It was these characteristics that allowed men to wage wars and hunt wild beasts to defend them.
