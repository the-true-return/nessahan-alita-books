10. Irritating provocations

<!-- seg 11-0002 paragraph h=8df36c1e -->
Let us now study the hysterical provocations carried out by some shrews, which confuse us so much.

<!-- seg 11-0003 paragraph h=c9117d22 -->
It is common for some men to be challenged, or for us to have our anger provoked, by the attitudes, behaviors, or words of their companions. These provocations are tests aimed at measuring a man’s self-control, his degree of being in love, and his ability to react correctly to difficult situations.

<!-- seg 11-0004 paragraph h=2af0ae78 -->
When mixed with anger or wrath, the feeling of passion acts as a brake on the destructive aggressive attitudes of the male who is irritated at having suffered a provocation. Therefore, according to the degree of aggressiveness of your reaction, the woman will find out whether you are very much or only a little in love, and also whether you are impulsive or have self-control. If you lose your head and fly into a rage, you will be showing that you do not control yourself and, therefore, that you are an inferior-category male, incapable of keeping cool in tense situations to protect her in case of need. If you assault her verbally, you will be indicating that you are not very submissive but, at the same time, that you do not control yourself. In both cases, you will be lowered in the eyes of the sly one, who will feel superior to you. If you do nothing, on the other hand, you will be indicating that you are passive, submissive, and, likewise, not very interesting. What to do, then?

<!-- seg 11-0005 paragraph h=2ab16c53 -->
The situation is difficult, almost a dead end. It is yet another trap that tests and measures male worth. If we react aggressively to the provocation, we will lose the game. If we passively accept the provocation, we will also lose it. But there is a solution: calmly unmask the provocation at the exact moment it is happening, denouncing the fact before her eyes and to her face so as to make her feel found out and ashamed. Never walk into the trap. Do not assault, do not shout at, and do not curse your companion in any way, ever! Under no circumstances hurt her physically. These attitudes will make you lose the right on your side and come out defeated in the war of nerves being waged. She will look like a poor defenseless little thing and you will be seen as the villain of the story. That is exactly what the sly shrews want and try to induce.

<!-- seg 11-0006 paragraph h=da9c66f0 -->
When we denounce, without fighting or arguing, directly and clearly, exactly what is happening, what the provocative attitudes are, the reasons why they are challenging, etc., we immobilize our partner because we make her feel caught red-handed.

<!-- seg 11-0007 paragraph h=aca09aa6 -->
The act of challenging and provoking aims not only to test us but also to manipulate situations so as to put the person who provokes in the spotlight as a victim. Historically, females have always used this role as a social weapon for dominance and for obtaining protection and favors. In some cases of self-defense against perverse men, the use of this strategy is just, but not in all.

<!-- seg 11-0008 paragraph h=a15a1fa4 -->
When we allow the woman to appear as a victim (without really being one) before ourselves, before women themselves, and before other people, we become morally indebted. Then we will feel an intense emotional need to flatter, please, run after her, etc., in order to be “forgiven.” The curious thing is that, when the manipulation is perfect, the one who seeks forgiveness is precisely the innocent one and the one who holds the power to forgive is the guilty one. It is an ingenious wile of mental manipulation that inverts each person’s position and is typical of hysterical shrews[^30].

<!-- seg 11-0009 footnote n=30 h=135e3b25 -->
Put another way: man is manipulated by his feelings of guilt, as Esther Villar explained.

<!-- seg 11-0010 paragraph h=223414a6 -->
The key to dealing with such hysterical stratagems is to catch the provocation in progress and denounce it immediately, without leaving any room for discussion and, of course, without fighting. Use a firm, convinced, and grave tone of voice, but speak little, in a short, blunt, and direct way, looking her in the eyes. Then fall silent or withdraw until she makes ashamed overtures toward reconciliation[^31]. If she makes no overtures, leave her for good, trade her in for a better one, for you will have lost nothing: shrews who are not ashamed even when caught red-handed are incorrigible.

<!-- seg 11-0011 footnote n=31 h=2778cec6 -->
The moment immediately following the reconciliation after a fight or breakup is very suitable for establishing a new pattern of relationship based on distance, coldness, dedication, protective leadership, intense eroticism, sincere affection, and impartiality, all consciously measured out and articulated.

<!-- seg 11-0012 paragraph h=e3e82db3 -->
Do not be a chatterbox, long-winded. He who reduces his speech and dialogues to a minimum protects himself against female provocations. Speech gives away our feelings, limitations, and weaknesses.

<!-- seg 11-0013 paragraph h=9d8d6d5d -->
The more you argue with your partner, the more complicated everything will become, because in such cases female arguments are capricious and illogical. Instead of seeking clarity and understanding when they argue, they seek to irritate us, calm us, appease us, and enrage us by turns.

<!-- seg 11-0014 paragraph h=091e7634 -->
A common form of provocation consists in stating or asking something obviously absurd but that has the power to touch exactly on our weak point, enraging us, while at the same time pretending not to realize what they are doing. Then, when they perceive our anger, they withdraw from the discussion on the grounds that we are being rude, as if our anger were unjustified. It is a very common procedure among the sly ones, whose intention is to leave us in a bad emotional state, but it becomes effective only because we argue and talk.

<!-- seg 11-0015 paragraph h=8b8c6544 -->
Since women are emotionally oriented beings, their attacks target the feelings of those they aim to wound, bend, and subdue. This happens because they have no other form of defense: attacks on feelings are a way of compensating for physical delicacy and little intellectual incisiveness[^32]. Their ability to argue in a focused way is lesser, and that is why they attack us by the emotional route. Since we, human males, are puny and feeble in emotional intelligence and therefore childish in this respect, they have a field day. They attack in various and unpredictable ways, avoiding logical-rational confrontation as much as possible and trying to provoke specific feelings, because this is the field in which they dominate and feel at ease. Most men fall into the trap and, desperate, thrash about trying to force them to argue, sinking deeper and deeper and losing the war. And they lose it simply through a strategic error, as I will explain next.

<!-- seg 11-0016 footnote n=32 h=68343e57 -->
I understand the female intellect to be very broad and comprehensive but not very incisive. It is easier for a woman to think about many things simultaneously than to think about only one thing with extreme depth. This gives women advantages in certain aspects of life and disadvantages in others.

<!-- seg 11-0017 paragraph h=2ae521e9 -->
One way of dealing with these disguised provocations is, first of all, to be distant and untouchable, never coming too close.

<!-- seg 11-0018 paragraph h=f7d6214b -->
For fulfilling our duties as men in what benefits them, we are always welcome, but for receiving from them the rights that would benefit us, we are always considered excessive, backward, male chauvinists, etc., and we receive, in return, provocations, complaints, deceptions, and lies. Hence, the solution is to discipline ourselves inwardly so as to achieve total silence.

<!-- seg 11-0019 paragraph h=b178c695 -->
Silence is armor, and if some witch is provoking you and driving you mad, this is simply due to some earlier opening that you left through speech. Do not let yourself be known, because he who lets himself be known becomes predictable.

<!-- seg 11-0020 paragraph h=724caa0e -->
Once we have shown ourselves and revealed who we are, we give them material to abuse our tolerance within our limits. Our limits are very carefully calculated through what we reveal when we speak, converse, act, etc. They detect them and rarely overstep them.

<!-- seg 11-0021 paragraph h=b94b65b0 -->
Whenever a man gives importance to what a sly woman says, he is usually dragged into various negative inner states and undesirable behaviors. This influence is hypnotic and takes place through fascination and identification. The ideal state is one in which we are indifferent because we insulate ourselves from the violent magnetic power of speech and voice (remember the song of the sirens[^33]). Although they are physically delicate, many women possess an extremely strong hypnotic power that acts in various directions through the voice and the gaze, easily leading us now to joy, now to anger, now to despair. Hence the importance of not taking what they say seriously and of ignoring their words when they are deceitful. The simple act of paying attention to an attempt at deception may be enough to trigger a hypnotic crisis, and it is not wise to underestimate this power.

<!-- seg 11-0022 footnote n=33 h=8c16f4bd -->
The power the female voice has to enchant man is very well illustrated in the stories of the sirens who dragged sailors down to the depths of the sea.

<!-- seg 11-0023 paragraph h=90e6c20d -->
Therefore, a second precaution to take is not to let yourself be fascinated by provocations of good or bad feelings. This means: do not become enraged, do not be flattered, do not become enthusiastic, do not admire, do not hate, do not be made happy, etc. Resist attempts to induce liking as much as attempts to induce dislike. Do not let yourself be seduced by compliments, loving looks, displays of cleavage, love letters, gifts, etc. Equally resist the effect of cynical smiles, cutting remarks, attempts to belittle you, disparage you, make you jealous[^34], etc. Do not waver; stay firm in yourself. Keep your mind silent and serene, looking her steadily in the eyes. Be calm. She will tirelessly try to provoke good and bad feelings by turns. The good feelings aim to disarm you, to make you lower your guard; the bad feelings aim to make you suffer and to retaliate against you for your rebelliousness.

<!-- seg 11-0024 footnote n=34 h=884b72d5 -->
By resisting bad provocations, we do not become violent. By resisting good provocations, we are not deceived. The ideal is always temperance: avoiding extremes. Let us not fall to one side or to the other.

<!-- seg 11-0025 paragraph h=15421698 -->
Third, outdo her in the art of provoking varied feelings. It is of little use to be emotionally armored if, in addition, you do not attack correctly by the same route. Observe her and learn to strike at feelings as women do. Instead of uselessly trying to force her to argue, simply unmask each emotional provocation and hand everything back to her.

<!-- seg 11-0026 paragraph h=c769c377 -->
In short, the solution for dealing with irritating provocations is to be more resistant to provocations than they are and, at the same time, to be more provocative[^35], defeating them in their own domain. He who surpasses them does not let himself be irritated but, on the contrary, is impervious to their multiple provocations. He does not let himself be manipulated because he resists good and bad temptations, the fascination of affection, flattery, challenge, insult, voluptuousness, sentimentality, attachment, arrogance, indifference, etc. The path is calm, non-action.

<!-- seg 11-0027 footnote n=35 h=0b2ac8e7 -->
Through a natural effect of mirror-like conduct.

<!-- seg 11-0028 paragraph h=dca4a82c -->
As a complement, it is also advisable to “punish” irritating or dishonest attitudes with others of the same kind, within a rigorous criterion of justice, of course, so that the sly ones feel how delightful it is to suffer emotional abuse. If you turn each irritating attitude into a rule for the relationship, instead of taking revenge, you will have tied up the little smart aleck and will be left in peace for a while, for to strike at your feelings she will first have to strike at her own, and she will keep quiet. For example: if your girlfriend makes a date and fails to show up without reason, do not show up for the next dates you make and do not hide what you are doing. Let her know that, since she did not keep the appointment, you too have no obligation to show up, and that, the next time she breaks an appointment, there will be an even worse “punishment” (<i>“if you did not want to come, why did you commit yourself?”</i>); if you catch her in a lie, tell her never to tell the truth again from then on. Obviously, you can reverse this decision if there is sincere repentance, shown through attitudes. If the repentance is not feigned, it is fair to give another chance. We have to forgive if we want to be forgiven.

<!-- seg 11-0029 paragraph h=17a8053c -->
One of the great difficulties in dealing with human beings lies in the fact that, normally, if we accept and forgive mistakes and dishonest attitudes for free, we are considered suckers instead of kind. He who forgives a mistake for free is considered an accomplice. Therefore, there is no other solution than to “punish.” The “punishment” I am referring to here differs completely from simple revenge because it preserves justice, honesty, sobriety, and reason, preventing worse things from happening. And it is nothing that will harm the other person; it will only hand her own acts back to her so that she may reflect.
