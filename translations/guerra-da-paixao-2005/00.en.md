<!-- seg 00-0016 paragraph h=65e0fcdb -->
This book teaches men the art of dismantling and neutralizing female wiles in love and of protecting themselves against the emotional damage of passion, and it cannot be invoked as an incentive for, or as support for, any form of aggression.

<!-- seg 00-0017 paragraph h=2dde91f5 -->
Its critical, direct, ironic, and incisive tone reflects only the pointing out of flaws, errors, and wiles, and does not signify support for any attempt to cause harm to human beings. It is one thing to dismantle the acts of women who attack us in love and to criticize them in that domain. It is something else entirely to cause them harm.

<!-- seg 00-0018 paragraph h=a1d58809 -->
The wiles denounced, unmasked, and described here correspond to feminine expressions, largely unconscious, of behavioral traits common to both genders. The profile outlined corresponds to a specific type of woman: the one who is ruled by sentimental selfishness. The author makes no pronouncement on the percentage of incidence of this profile among the female population of the various countries.
