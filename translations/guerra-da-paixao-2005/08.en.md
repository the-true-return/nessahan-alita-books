7. How not to fall in love

<!-- seg 08-0002 paragraph h=63039841 -->
Male passion can be defined as a hypnotic fascination with woman’s voice, delicacy, beauty, perfume, touch, caresses, softness, whispers, and fragility. It is felt mainly in periods of abstinence.

<!-- seg 08-0003 paragraph h=c6b532b6 -->
Woman’s fascinating characteristics attract us, hold us, intoxicate us, make us hallucinate, and drive us mad. They subdue us, degrading us to the level of a servile dog, without self-respect and without honor. Like a drug, they cloud our judgment, preventing us from thinking clearly.

<!-- seg 08-0004 paragraph h=c6d571d6 -->
In such a condition, we become exactly the opposite of the dominant male model that would intoxicate them with passion, and we always obtain results contrary to those we aim for. We become submissive, dependent on love being granted to us so that we can enjoy a few minutes of happiness. We see woman as a lifeline for our pains. Passion is a form of dementia.

<!-- seg 08-0005 paragraph h=0190d3a9 -->
To protect ourselves against this danger, or to rid ourselves of this emotional illness once it has set in, we need to employ will, spiritual discipline, and mental discipline correctly. It is no wonder that many spiritualist ascetics of various religions avoided women and sex. The hell of passion is truly unbearable, and few triumph over it.

<!-- seg 08-0006 paragraph h=6e04c5d1 -->
The first thing to do is to learn to subdue the mind, avoiding mechanical imagination. All mental images, good or bad, related to the object of passion (the goddess of our dreams) must be suppressed by means of the will. Those that cannot be stopped need to be analyzed. It is necessary to reach mental silence. It is also necessary to work on the death of the psychic aggregates involved in amorous fascination.

<!-- seg 08-0007 paragraph h=c5d72622 -->
The more feminine a woman is, the more fascinating and potentially dangerous she will be. From the beginning of the relationship, we must resist the fascination and combat the memories, fantasies, and thoughts related to this passionate love. It is the enchantment of Lilith-Nahemah[^14] that leads the innocent astray and takes them to the abyss. Woe to the innocent, to the weak who let themselves be hypnotized by the charms of Circe, Delilah, or Helen of Troy[^15]! They will plunge headfirst into the abyss, like the inverted pentagram. Woe to those who believe in earthly happiness and seek it outside themselves, in passionate love, because there they will find only suffering and madness.

<!-- seg 08-0008 footnote n=14 h=0f9e6b8d -->
In certain branches of ancient Hebrew mythology, it was Lilith and Nahemah, and not Eve, who led Adam astray.

<!-- seg 08-0009 footnote n=15 h=946a5f7c -->
Mythological representations of women who are at once fascinating, beautiful, and perverse.

<!-- seg 08-0010 paragraph h=9506eeee -->
By its own fatal, absurd logic, passionate female love[^16] is condemned to eternal dissatisfaction, since its selection criterion is, inherently, male indifference and distance. This means that whenever we desire woman’s love it will flee from us, and that it will come to meet us only when we do not want it, when we reject it[^17]. There is no way to fool it by feigning indifference, because the unconscious expresses the real tenor of our feelings through subliminal channels.

<!-- seg 08-0011 footnote n=16 h=27bbeee3 -->
Note that here I am referring to passionate love and not to the conscious and sober love of women, nor to the insane, passionate, and fatal love of men.

<!-- seg 08-0012 footnote n=17 h=e833c71e -->
Something identical happens with woman: man’s love comes only when she does not want it. This fatality prevents people from being happy in passionate love, since it drives them toward those who do not love them.

<!-- seg 08-0013 paragraph h=c794bdfd -->
Passion is a set of defects that we carry in the soul, in our psyche. To be overcome, it must first be understood through self-analysis.

<!-- seg 08-0014 paragraph h=f0664633 -->
The analysis of passion is carried out by collecting as much information as possible about the feelings, thoughts, and actions involved in it. It is not theoretical but practical. To theorize about a psychological element is to move away from understanding it by inventing hypothetical ideas about its characteristics. The information is collected first of all through self-observation at the moments when passion manifests itself; that is, it is a self-study <i>in loco</i>. No theorizing should be allowed. All the details of the movements, thoughts, and feelings are important and need to be captured. In serious cases, the work can be supplemented with a self-analysis after the manifestation, but based exclusively on facts observed and remembered, without theorizing or hypothesizing. The information about passion is present at the moment of its manifestation and can be captured if we are vigilant.

<!-- seg 08-0015 paragraph h=8708f5c6 -->
Passion expresses itself in the mind in the form of multiple mental images: thoughts, recollections, memories, fantasies, and plans. At this level it is an automatic, mechanical, and autonomous imagination that does not obey our will. We can repel it, and it will come right back.

<!-- seg 08-0016 paragraph h=690cd1b3 -->
Besides the mind, the danger is present in the heart in the form of feelings, which are closely linked to the mental images that are in the head. It is felt as blows that actually hurt. The feelings that compose it are longing, jealousy, missing the person, the pleasure of being together, and a great many others that we could not list here for lack of space. At this level it becomes very subtle and disguises itself a great deal.

<!-- seg 08-0017 paragraph h=8def7fb1 -->
At the level of bodily movements, we can catch passionate weakness in the act when we turn our head or eyes to gaze at the beloved, when we stretch out our arm to make a phone call, when we walk toward her, and in countless other movements that vary from one case to another and from one person to another.

<!-- seg 08-0018 paragraph h=46c41e4c -->
We can also observe and study passion in the form of instinctive and sexual manifestations. As instinct, that is, as everything related to the preservation of the species and of the person himself, we can see it quicken the heartbeat and the breathing rate, diminish hunger, etc.

<!-- seg 08-0019 paragraph h=ee511eb6 -->
The pain of passion is real, piercing, and deep. It is felt clearly in the heart and is objectively detectable. It causes visible and indisputable damage.

<!-- seg 08-0020 paragraph h=f87bd680 -->
There are those who say that we should adore woman. This is something controversial. An authentic woman, who has struggled against herself, engendered her soul, and overcome her base instincts, is truly worthy of veneration; she should be cared for like a treasure, protected, and rewarded. On the other hand, a mere sly cheat does not deserve the same “adoration,” since she is no less wicked than anyone else, despite having a fragile and angelic appearance.

<!-- seg 08-0021 paragraph h=53fefedd -->
The more you think about the sly one (well or badly), the worse it will be. The ideal is simply to forget her, to give no importance to her whims, feelings, desires, thoughts, and absurd fantasies. Never take her seriously; remain distant and mysterious.

<!-- seg 08-0022 paragraph h=8aac16fe -->
If you “drink the poison” of passion[^18], the spell will steer your thoughts by force, autonomously. You will try to think about other people but will not be able to. Your beloved will inhabit your dreams, your imagination, and your mind against your will. She will be an invader in your heart. You will try to turn your attention away from her, but whenever you do so you will fall again into the same abyss; you will once more be paying attention to the witch, occupying yourself and worrying about her; you will not be able to ignore her.

<!-- seg 08-0023 footnote n=18 h=66e58217 -->
The “poison” is drunk through credulity. When we set skepticism aside and surrender, from then on we will be intoxicated. It is much easier to prevent passion from setting in than to try to tear it out of the heart once it has set in.

<!-- seg 08-0024 paragraph h=4a62021f -->
Nevertheless, she is not your enemy: your enemy is yourself. It is against yourself that you must fight: against your weaknesses, follies, affections, fears, desires, yearnings, dreams, fantasies, pains, attachments, etc. A man’s greatest enemy is himself. When we conquer ourselves, we conquer the sly women by extension, for ultimately it is not they who strike and wound us but rather our own feelings. Our partners merely use our weaknesses against us and, in doing so, are in truth showing us who we are and even, in a certain way, helping us. Therefore, do not revolt against anyone, much less against women, because it is a pure waste of time and nobody will pay any attention. Revolting against women is nonsense.

<!-- seg 08-0025 paragraph h=86cebbc2 -->
Be watchful of the habit of finding her similar to your mother. The tendency to see one’s partner as a mother is one of the roots of falling in love. It is the same feeling we had in childhood, and it makes us see women as lifelines.

<!-- seg 08-0026 paragraph h=f3698e79 -->
The procedure for curing passion is the same one by which any other illness of the soul is cured: understanding.
