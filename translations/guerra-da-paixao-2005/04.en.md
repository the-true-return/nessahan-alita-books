3. The shifting of decisions

<!-- seg 04-0002 paragraph h=f8d639f1 -->
Whoever decides something is always responsible for the consequences of his decision. Knowing this, your partner, if she is one of the sly ones we are dealing with, will refuse to take definite stances in the relationship, preferring to remain in the ambiguity of contradictory, double-meaning behaviors. Incoherence in the way they treat us, but not in the way we men treat them (sly ones!), is very much in their interest because it keeps them in control while we sink into the hell of doubt.

<!-- seg 04-0003 paragraph h=3a3b8023 -->
To preserve indefiniteness and thus safeguard the mystery, perpetuating our confusion and doubts, the sly ones flatly refuse to make decisions that have a definitive repercussion on the relationship. They never want to choose definitively between two paths, preferring to oscillate between both so as to enjoy the benefits of each while trying to dodge the unpleasant consequences inherent in them. That is why their attitudes never decisively define whether they want to be wives, lovers, casual flings, or cheats, for they want to enjoy the benefits that each of these positions offers without paying the corresponding price. When we protest, they try to induce us to make a decision we might regret later, because then they will be able to throw it in our face. The solution for these cases is this: create a definitive situation that obliges them to reveal unequivocally what they feel and how much they value us. Trying to force them through discussions to define themselves is a waste of time. The right thing is to find a right decision on our part whose result inevitably places them in a definitive situation, with no way out, obliging them to define themselves even if they do not want to. Then we must communicate that decision unilaterally, refusing discussion altogether.

<!-- seg 04-0004 paragraph h=6534f929 -->
As a general rule, sly women tend to withdraw from the relationship without cutting the man off definitively, because they want to keep him trapped afterward. To this end, they avoid explicitly taking on the responsibility that is theirs for the failure, implying that they are withdrawing through our fault. They perform ingenious maneuvers to get out while keeping the sucker imprisoned. This is the reason why they almost never have the courage to tell us to our face, clearly, objectively, and definitively, that they no longer want us, that they no longer feel anything, etc. They know that, if they do, we will benefit, because we will be able to give our lives another direction. It is a dishonest attitude, for it prevents us from turning the page and calmly going our way. They want to be remembered afterward; they want to feel, and to be able to say, that there is a rejected idiot who still loves them. Shifting decisions onto the other is an excellent mechanism for satisfying this sadistic selfishness.
