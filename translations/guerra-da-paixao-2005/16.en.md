15. The quest for continuity leads them to insinuation

<!-- seg 16-0002 paragraph h=0f52d80a -->
Only when they sense that male interest is about to disappear do certain women insinuate themselves on men. Insinuation, especially explicitly sexual insinuation, is the last and most desperate resource they turn to when they see no other alternative. Since they are not very fond of sex, they resort to it only as a last resort.
<!-- QUERY: "se insinuam sobre os homens" / "insinuação": rendered "insinuate themselves" / "insinuation" (as in the table of contents), in the sense of making advances. Alternative: "make advances to men" / "advances." -->

<!-- seg 16-0003 paragraph h=0439d0cd -->
What motivates them to insinuate themselves is the desire for continuity of male interest, which is extremely strong and whose satisfaction directly affects female self-esteem. The desire for continuity seems to be the strongest female desire and the factor that leads them to offer themselves to those who reject them, despise them, or simply do not notice them. Any man who has ever, at some point in his life, been involved with a woman who arouses no attraction in him will know this. Instead of punishing the one who despises her with the same contempt, as would be logical, sensible, and correct, the all-but-rejected woman insistently pursues him, tries to bend him and make him reverse the way he treats her, desperately trying to be loved. The most interesting thing is that, if she really does achieve the reversal, she begins to avoid him in a measured and calculated way, for her goal of being an object of interest has been reached and, for this interest to be continuous, she now needs to excite his desires without satisfying them, but always preserving his hopes. The sly one plays with the man’s hope, and her goal is not to love him but to be loved.

<!-- seg 16-0004 paragraph h=a187e74a -->
The same woman who pursues a man who rejected her because she aroused aversion in him will very probably reject another who pursues her because she arouses passion, interest, and desire in him. What will determine pursuit or flight will be whether or not the violent female desire to preserve the continuity of male interest is satisfied. She only wants to keep him captive, desiring her, and nothing more.

<!-- seg 16-0005 paragraph h=e2ed819b -->
The desire for continuity is the desire to exercise power over a man’s soul by means of his sexual and affective needs, which must be inflamed to the utmost and never satisfied.

<!-- seg 16-0006 paragraph h=023b3d90 -->
It is this desire for continuity that the seducer activates so that women will pursue him. Women seem to be highly vulnerable to male rejection, just as we are vulnerable to cleavage, miniskirts, and voluptuous female conduct.

<!-- seg 16-0007 paragraph h=3064e402 -->
The main female vulnerability is the desire for continuity of male interest. Obviously, there are other strong vulnerabilities, such as the desires for protection against what they fear, for having money, for humiliating their rivals, for taking revenge on those who reject them, and for satisfying their curiosity, but the desire for continuity seems to be the dominant vulnerability, to which the others are added. Envy of their rivals is what drives them to conquer a man who is accompanied by a beautiful woman. Curiosity drives them to try to attract a mysterious man. The desire for power leads them to insinuate themselves on the richest. Fear leads them to seek out protectors and leaders. But all these cases are permeated by the desire for continuity. The various female vulnerabilities reinforce one another, sketching a panorama of the emotional weaknesses through which women can be taken by idle, useless, ill-intentioned men, but also through which they could be swept away by good men, if these paid more attention to what we are pointing out.

<!-- seg 16-0008 paragraph h=c87f86b3 -->
Normally, a woman unconsciously presupposes that there is always some male desire, however faint, directed toward her. She believes, without realizing it, that the stranger walking by on the other side of the street would not refuse a sexual offer if one were made. She believes herself desired. She imagines that, if she offered herself sexually, her offer would be promptly accepted by the stranger. Some even go so far as to believe that every man is a potential rapist and, therefore, someone who desires them. This unconscious belief gives them emotional stability. It is a belief in the continuity of male interest and a need of the female psyche.

<!-- seg 16-0009 paragraph h=503f90e5 -->
When the unconscious belief in continuity is shaken, an emotional unease drives the woman to restore it, moving toward the man who shook it.

<!-- seg 16-0010 paragraph h=579d33fe -->
Why do they want continuity so much? Simply because continuity gives them present and future guarantees in favor of what they desire and against what they fear or detest. A woman sees a man from a pragmatic and functional point of view. Her coldness, as regards showing solidarity with male emotional suffering, does not imply indifference to the benefits and guarantees that this very suffering provides and, to tell the truth, the more we suffer for love, the more guarantees of continuity we are giving her. Our torment in love and sexual frustration show that we are captive, chained by passion and in continuous interest in the woman who chained us.
