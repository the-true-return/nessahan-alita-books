14. Getting them to reveal themselves

<!-- seg 15-0002 paragraph h=6830c0b1 -->
Let us return once more to the thorny point related to the true and hidden nature of woman. There is much that is wonderful and sublime in this nature, but there is also much perfidy, which is expressed in varying degrees according to the personality. While in some women this perfidy is hardly noticeable, in the sly ones we are concerned with in this book it is quite evident.

<!-- seg 15-0003 paragraph h=d9d92b68 -->
The general tendency is for them to dissemble, feigning shyness, modesty, innocence, inexperience, decency, purity, and naivety in the sexual field. Behind the mask, however, there may hide a sensual female imprisoned by fear of male authoritarianism, jealousy, and possessiveness, which aim to preserve exclusivity. This hidden sensual female may express herself clandestinely to a greater or lesser degree, according to the courage the woman has to break the imposed norms that create in her the need to keep up an appearance of saintliness and chastity. This hidden side, in some cases, is so repressed and extinguished that it can be detected only in dreams. In any case, there will always be repressed aspects of the personality in the realm of sensuality.

<!-- seg 15-0004 paragraph h=89741b6c -->
It is extremely difficult to make this hidden part show itself if you are her husband or boyfriend, because of the ancestral and justified fear of male reactions. Since the general rule is that males are territorial and possessive, demanding exclusivity[^41], women pretend to be that way to please us. The result is that we date, commit ourselves to, and marry a mask, a person who does not exist, through our own fault.

<!-- seg 15-0005 footnote n=41 h=fcea20d4 -->
Man errs when he demands exclusivity. Exclusivity must not be demanded; it must be received. Man’s territorial instincts, which make him possessive, are one of the reasons that force women to dissemble their conduct. Therefore, he who overcomes them receives exclusivity in sex and frees the woman from the need to dissemble.

<!-- seg 15-0006 paragraph h=f0b7e46e -->
This is the reason why husbands rarely know their wives truly and, obviously, suffer from it their whole lives. There always seems to be a mystery, a question mark in their heads: <i>“How would this woman react if she were left alone with another man? What is the limit of her fidelity? To what extent am I the exclusive holder of her desires and her sexuality?”</i>

<!-- seg 15-0007 paragraph h=61e95278 -->
Unlike husbands, who tend to be rigid and moralistic with their wives, libertines and immoral men know and enjoy precisely the female side that is hidden at home and lived in limbo, in the half-light, and clandestinely. This happens because women believe that their forbidden acts will not be condemned by the libertine but, on the contrary, approved, encouraged, and directed by those who place themselves diametrically opposite to the marital role. It follows, therefore, that only if we are immoral and encourage socially forbidden female behaviors will we know who the woman really is.

<!-- seg 15-0008 paragraph h=0dfc6baa -->
At the slightest sign of conservatism or prohibitionism on our part, the woman will withdraw and begin to simulate politically correct and socially praiseworthy behavior because we demand it. We see, then, that the best way to get to know them is by showing that we are precisely the opposite.

<!-- seg 15-0009 paragraph h=a3609134 -->
If you acquire within yourself the capacity for total acceptance, revealing yourself to be a completely liberal man, one of those who like their women to travel alone, to have male friends “with no ill intent,” to visit women’s clubs, bars, etc., and if you manage to make her really perceive this, you will find out who your partner truly is and what can be expected of her. But you must demonstrate it perfectly, or you will be deceived. For that, it is necessary not to be in love.

<!-- seg 15-0010 paragraph h=21dc41ff -->
As Eliane Calligaris correctly showed, women have a strong need to live the side of life that has been forbidden to them. This is one of the reasons why immoral men, libertines, cads, playboys, Don Juans, etc. attract them so much. They are the living possibility of experiencing what their fathers and husbands deny them.

<!-- seg 15-0011 paragraph h=ce9396bd -->
Your partner will never reveal herself if she perceives that you are a moralist. However, we men are, by instinct, territorial. We obviously want our female for ourselves alone, and that is a legitimate right. But it is not right to force or to forbid anyone from doing what they want, because each person must have the right to run his or her own life. If, even believing that we are absolutely amoral, our partner still remains firm in her exclusive dedication, rejecting “modern” behaviors, this will indicate that she possibly has a vocation for being a good wife, for she has offered us her fidelity without our demanding it, without our pressuring her.

<!-- seg 15-0012 paragraph h=5f2e7745 -->
Almost every heterosexual man is, deep down, a moralist. Even the most immoral libertines usually prefer as wives women who keep other males well away. The thing is, libertines pretend, passing themselves off as very understanding and tolerant. In truth, human males are exclusivist by nature. They always tend to forbid, which gives females very fair reasons to deceive them and get around their ridiculous prohibitions, mocking them inwardly. The solution is not to be prohibitionists but to accept whatever comes, so as to discover who the person by our side truly is. Once the reality has been discovered, we can make whatever decision seems most correct to us.

<!-- seg 15-0013 paragraph h=87f1394a -->
When they lie, sly women do so by asserting the opposite of what we know. For example, if the husband looks for his wife at location A at a time when, by habit, she should be there, and later tells her so, she will possibly defend herself with the following lie: <i>“No, my routine changed today and I stayed at location B.”</i> Therefore, to induce her into a blatant lie that cannot be disguised, that cannot be denied, and from which there is no escape, it is enough for the husband, instead of telling her the truth that he was at location A (a true fact), to hide this information and convey convincingly in their conversation the idea that he did not find her at location B or at other locations (false facts), even though he was not there. Then, to deceive him and escape, the dissembler will try to lie by saying she was at location A (the place where she thinks the man was not) and will be caught red-handed. In other words, we must deliberately lead the sly ones to lie about something whose truth we already know beforehand, in order to catch them in the act. This will only be possible if we do not let them discover what we actually know and what we actually do not know. The sly wife in question must believe that the husband checked for her presence at location B and not at location A. Once she believes that he did not check for her presence at location A, that will be precisely the place used in her lie. This is just one example among thousands.

<!-- seg 15-0014 paragraph h=ee5b4729 -->
The difficulty in catching lies lies in women’s natural specialization in the art of lying, fooling, and dissembling. Therefore, we have to outdo them even in this art[^42] if we want to know them. Female intelligence in the field of concealment is immense and allows them to move with ease between false and true facts, and very few men outdo them and corner them. The best way to catch a lie is by inducing the lying sly one to lie more. By the very logic of the deceptive act, the falsification must concern points she believes are unknown to us. In other words, we have to outdo the liar in the act of deceiving, fooling her in such a way as to induce her to believe that she is deceiving us. Only when we accept the lies and are more brazen than the person trying to deceive us do we discover the truth behind her intentions. Unfortunately, as Schopenhauer says, lying has become a law in this world. Therefore, to uncover a lie, it is enough to accept it instead of becoming enraged.

<!-- seg 15-0015 footnote n=42 h=d15d4b12 -->
Without applying it unjustly or for selfish ends.

<!-- seg 15-0016 paragraph h=b2838c7a -->
The act of lying and deceiving is a psychological game. The most skillful one wins. The skill consists in steering the other’s beliefs. In the case of the sly ones, he who wants to discover the truth encourages, stimulates, and induces the other party to lie precisely about what is already known, carrying the deceptive act to a point where the false statements become ridiculous in their obviousness. Obviously, if the person is being sincere from beginning to end, which is difficult, you must repay such nobility of character in kind.

<!-- seg 15-0017 paragraph h=eb93d1bf -->
This tactic for dismantling lies by accepting them works for both sexes and can also be used by wives who want to unmask lying husbands. I am dealing with women who lie only as a matter of focus, since the book is about man’s suffering in love.

<!-- seg 15-0018 paragraph h=fbfcb192 -->
Lying is not something praiseworthy, but I see no better way to peacefully unmask lying people than to lead them to lie more. Even so, however, we will be on the side of truth: the truths we leave out in this counter-manipulative maneuver will be revealed to the lying person when she discovers she has been caught in the lie. So, ultimately, we do not sink so low into the vile abyss of lying when we seek to reveal falsehoods.

<!-- seg 15-0019 paragraph h=6f4ff7b7 -->
A characteristic common to lies, and one that often allows them to be detected quickly, is the tendency to avoid a certain subject and the resistance to addressing it, giving explanations, etc. If you mention a certain fact, place, or person and the sly one quickly tries to change the subject or steer the conversation away, this indicates that something is wrong in connection with it and that something is being hidden. Insistence on avoiding a point is a strong sign that a lie is under way or about to be told.

<!-- seg 15-0020 paragraph h=82396a1a -->
Female dissimulation is, and always has been, the great problem for man, because it does not allow him to know whom he is dealing with, what he should expect, what expectations to nurture, etc. By the path pointed out here, however, it can be overcome if we have the necessary calm. I must add that men who lose control end up giving reasons to be deceived. Fortunately, the sly ones are more specialized in allaying suspicions than in sustaining lies.
