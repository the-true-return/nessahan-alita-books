4. The main psychological hell

<!-- seg 05-0002 paragraph h=6c4d2bf9 -->
We can define this psychological hell as a situation of intense emotional suffering arising from doubt and confusion regarding the feelings and fidelity of the person we love. Emotional suffering is something real; it exists objectively and can be verified by anyone.

<!-- seg 05-0003 paragraph h=20c7e4de -->
What torments us most in love is not the possibility of being traded in, of being considered inferior to other males, etc., but rather the hell of doubt arising from ambiguous behaviors. What makes living together unbearable is not the “immoral” desires of the partner but rather the lack of honesty. A woman’s right to decide what to do with her own life, her own feelings, and her own body is untouchable and must always be respected. What is dishonest is the attempt to exercise these rights without bearing the inevitable consequences.

<!-- seg 05-0004 paragraph h=139065ff -->
To dodge the natural consequences of a free sexuality, the sly females have specialized in the art of lying, dissimulating, and deceiving so as to enjoy certain benefits without giving up others. The result of this specialization was that they turned into walking lies, into people who can no longer live without hiding something from their father, boyfriend, fiancé, or husband. The only phase of life in which women like this are transparent and do not dissimulate is childhood. As soon as adolescence begins, the first concealments of behavior from the father begin, often even with the mother’s connivance. The first concealments prepare the adolescent girl to deceive, later on, the other men who will enter her life. They mark a preparatory phase in which the mother plays the role of initiator.

<!-- seg 05-0005 paragraph h=06fc663a -->
It is extremely painful to discover that the beloved woman is an insincere sly one who tries to put one over on us merely in order to feel better, cleverer, and hotter than her rivals. The act of taking pleasure in hoodwinking, manipulating, and deceiving a person who loves sincerely is extremely horrible because it abuses the noblest of feelings: love.

<!-- seg 05-0006 paragraph h=42c90fd1 -->
The main female strategy in these wars of passion is behavioral ambiguity. They speak and act contradictorily to confuse us and keep us from knowing what they really feel for us and what they want (for example, they usually say that they want marriage and commitment on our part but at the same time want freedom, or else, on the contrary, they say that they want an open relationship but demand love, affection, and feelings). They do this on purpose to disconcert us.

<!-- seg 05-0007 paragraph h=ff70a300 -->
The solution for winning these battles is to create decisive situations that oblige them to reveal through actions what they truly feel, think, and want. Do not expect confessions or sincerity in words.

<!-- seg 05-0008 paragraph h=1d307562 -->
In love, what counts is not what is said but what is revealed through attitudes and concrete actions. Learn to see what is going on without needing to ask, without needing a confession. In cases of insurmountable indefiniteness, we have to be realistic and opt for the most probable conclusion: that the human being tends more readily toward selfishness and toward using others to obtain the maximum benefit. She, the sly one, will never admit that she flirted with, felt attracted to, or had sex with another man. Therefore, do without the confession and make your decisions on the basis of the first signs. If she really loves you, she will run to make up for the damage and try to prove her innocence (!). If she does not stir herself, then you will have lost nothing, since she was no good anyway. A woman who does not love is not worth a dime, and there are heaps of them everywhere. Cheats never deserve to be cried over and they do not even come close to sincere women, who are indeed worthy and precious.

<!-- seg 05-0009 paragraph h=44d36008 -->
If your partner is irrevocably strange, different, distant, arrogant, or cold, treating you badly, consider the relationship lost and take as much advantage of it as you can while it is possible. Do not waste time interrogating, wanting to know what is going on. Simply enjoy whatever good remains, until it ends. When she no longer wants to give you anything, simply leave her without giving any explanation, as this type of woman likes to do with us. Above all, do not argue, do not stir up controversy, do not fight, do not take revenge, do not try to prove that you are right, do not insist on your reasons, and do not explain your motives, because this will only make the situation worse.

<!-- seg 05-0010 paragraph h=59d1a23c -->
Considering that dissimulation is the main tool of the sly ones, experience has been showing us that the guiding line that must lead good, sincere, and honest men in dealing with these women is the ability to discover what is hidden behind confusing, double-meaning behaviors. The whole strategy seems to come down to the ability to create decisive situations that allow no evasions or dissimulations. Doubt is our greatest enemy, and we must create situations to eliminate it, which demands a great deal of determination.

<!-- seg 05-0011 paragraph h=786d7964 -->
The hellish little games involving doubt aim to force us to show that we suffer terrible pangs of passion, crises of absence or of jealousy, and they are never acknowledged by the women who practice them. They take place in the half-light, in obscurity, while the sly woman acts as if nothing were happening or flatly denies everything when questioned, with the most brazen face. Confrontation is always avoided because it is clarifying. When we try to trigger it, the dialogue is diverted into subjective discussions, controversies, or capricious stubbornness that preserve the doubts, confusions, and indefiniteness. She will never tell the truth about what she feels, thinks, and does. Regardless of any consequences, she will never admit the obvious, which is why it is absolutely useless to engage in dialogue or to attempt open, explicit, sincere, and honest agreements. It is likewise a waste of time to demand clarifications or transparent, defined, coherent conduct, etc. The best thing is simply to observe them and make decisions on our own account.

<!-- seg 05-0012 paragraph h=3ee20de2 -->
It is very common that, after several days of stable treatment without conflicts, she suddenly withdraws some displays of affection to which her “victim” was accustomed. At the same time, she will keep up other affectionate acts to create an indefiniteness that confuses her partner. This is always done when we are not expecting it, at moments when things are going well, so that we are caught by surprise. The intention of this manipulative action is to force the man to show that he is suffering and is still in love. It is a periodic test aimed at measuring the degree of dependence and assessing passionate submission. If you become upset, you will show your suffering through body language and make her as happy as can be. The best solution for shattering this hellish little game is simply to withdraw in silence or to break off contact immediately after detecting the slightest sign of strange behavior. Then wait, wait, and wait. If she seeks you out, unmask her and definitively exclude from the relationship those very affectionate gestures that were denied you before. If she does not seek you out, be glad, for this means that by your side there was only a creature who was good for nothing but lying.

<!-- seg 05-0013 paragraph h=4e757b5a -->
Precisely because they take place in obscurity, the hellish little games of feeling are difficult to detect, foresee, and combat. The difficulty is aggravated by the fact that our credulity (vote of confidence) in words is not recognized as a virtue to be repaid with sincerity. On the contrary, credulity is seen and exploited as an opportunity for them to deny everything that is happening and in this way to hoodwink us and keep us confused.

<!-- seg 05-0014 paragraph h=36533ff8 -->
There are cases in which the man becomes irritated with his sly partner because of her superficiality, her insistence on taking up his precious time by offering “spiritual” affection, chatting uselessly about banal subjects instead of having intense, ardent, wild sex, etc. Sometimes it even happens that the man grows impatient with the affectionate way this type of partner looks at him. These bouts of impatience are due to freedom from passion and are felt as rejection. The curious thing is that, almost always, she insists on offering her love and remains in love as long as she is offered some glimpse of hope of reversing the situation. Whoever supposes that this insistence on breaking down rejection by offering affection is proof of the altruistic superiority of her feminine love is mistaken. What is really going on is that the manipulative female cannot bear to lose the wars of passion and tries to break down the male’s resistance so as to take revenge afterward, for what she seeks is simply to come out on top, to take possession of the situation. To keep her under control, it is enough to reject her and, at the same time, to offer her faint hope.

<!-- seg 05-0015 paragraph h=185927ab -->
The games in the war of passion come down to concealing one’s true intentions while discovering the other’s real intentions. Whoever is more mysterious will confuse and, by being more realistic and observant, will win.

<!-- seg 05-0016 paragraph h=b493dda5 -->
Besides the main psychological hell there are also other psychological hells in love. One of them is the well-known situation in which the person in love is pushed aside by the person he loves, while she has fun, as happy as can be, with other company and completely ignores his suffering. Only a complete revolution against the curse of passion can overturn the positions in these cases. The poor victim of the spell feels his strength slipping away, failing him, and does not feel the slightest will to fight against his decline. He suffers terribly, and there are cases in which he even gives himself over to drugs or alcohol or commits suicide. However, if he manages to gather his strength and fight until he is truly free of passion, with the help of God (may my atheist readers forgive me), he sees reality again, understands the monstrosity of which he was a victim, and unmasks the person who oppressed his heart, handing her back the very hell she created. This time, however, he usually does so definitively, because he has reason on his side, backed by facts.

<!-- seg 05-0017 paragraph h=8925ccee -->
All these emotional and mental hells are possible only because we make the mistake of taking manipulative women seriously instead of seeing them as mere mischievous children. One minute of distraction is enough for us to begin letting ourselves be carried away by the talk, being drawn into multiple negative states. If you take seriously the nonsense of this feminine world we are dealing with, discussing trivialities as if they were serious and very important matters, you will be lost. You will soon be dragged into states of confusion, anger, fury, sadness, doubt, etc.
