8. Decisions that “corner”

<!-- seg 09-0002 paragraph h=8b60a8ca -->
To immobilize them and keep them from toying with our noble feelings, we need to find the right decisions. The right decisions are those that turn the tide in our favor, keeping reason on our side, and oblige them to act transparently, revealing what they truly feel.

<!-- seg 09-0003 paragraph h=762fea97 -->
We can say that the paramount strategy in such defensive emotional maneuvers consists in creating definitive, radical, and “cornering” objective conditions from which the sly woman cannot escape and which oblige her to reveal what she truly wants and feels toward us, since dissimulation, indefiniteness, and deception are her main psychological wiles. They try to hide what they truly feel, desire, and want in order to confuse us. They conceal their real intentions and simulate false intentions to disorient us. <i>Decisions that “corner”</i> must be understood and defined as follows: courageous and unappealable attitudes that leave woman no alternative other than to reveal unequivocally her true intentions toward us. They are attitudes that demand total detachment and freedom from passion, without which the plan will backfire violently and hit us. One must be truly willing to lose her forever for such radical strategies to work. Therefore, do not attempt such maneuvers if you are in love or attached, or if you are not willing to truly risk losing her. By means of “cornering” decisions, which I will describe shortly, you will find out what is really hidden behind the confusing and disconcerting behavior. Once you have discovered the truth, it will be much easier to decide what to do with your life and what course to give your relationship with your manipulative woman.

<!-- seg 09-0004 paragraph h=914ad86f -->
First of all, do not make empty threats, promising what you will not have the strength to carry out, for if you do, your credibility will be lost and your threats to leave her, to trade her in for another, never to seek them out again, etc., will become ridiculous[^19]. They usually play to the very limit to find out whether we are bluffing.

This is the very funny case of the man who says he is going to “punish” the woman with his absence but never manages to keep his word.

Some “cornering” and “punitive” decisions such as the following can help:

<!-- seg 09-0007 paragraph h=e7553dcd -->
• Absenting yourself long enough for her to suffer a good deal;

<!-- seg 09-0008 paragraph h=9fb9f641 -->
• Falling silent, reducing dialogue to zero or almost zero;

<!-- seg 09-0009 paragraph h=4cfd62ea -->
• Establishing consequences (ending the relationship, getting involved with another woman, definitively breaking off contact, terminating the commitment, etc.) for the next time the undesirable behavior is repeated.

<!-- seg 09-0010 paragraph h=cfa3aeac -->
“Cornering” and “punitive” decisions can be expressed in various ways, but they normally must contain an anticipatory component (<i>“the next time you do such and such”</i>) and a consequence that hands back what was done, morally commensurate with and corresponding to the undesirable attitude that motivates it.

<!-- seg 09-0011 paragraph h=fb440a28 -->
When your girlfriend or wife goes long periods without calling and invents lame excuses, go mute, fall silent, and do not talk until she insists on knowing what is going on. Then tell her that you will talk again only on the day she commits herself to calling with the frequency of a woman in love. Apply the same measure to long absences or to situations in which you are left confused, not knowing whom she is with or what she is doing.

<!-- seg 09-0012 paragraph h=c80e1979 -->
If your companion likes to keep her cell phone turned off in order to torment you with doubt, let her know that you will accept her calls again only when she commits herself to leaving the device on permanently.

<!-- seg 09-0013 paragraph h=a959529d -->
If your partner likes to refuse sex on absurd grounds or gives herself grudgingly, without enthusiasm, forbid her to seek you out again until she is mad with desire, letting her know that you will accept her again only when she commits herself to always having sex with great eagerness, in the way and with the frequency you need, without fussing.

<!-- seg 09-0014 paragraph h=6bd6ce8c -->
If your companion insists on offering and not giving, on promising and not delivering, withdraw, letting her know that you will resume contact only on the day she corrects herself.

<!-- seg 09-0015 paragraph h=f8958d9c -->
If your girlfriend treats men “with no ulterior intent” in a suspicious way that disturbs you, unmask her by bluntly pointing out the suspicious behavior that bothered you and let her know that the next time it happens you will trade her in for another immediately. The technique of withdrawing and demanding a correction of conduct before resuming contact also works in these cases.

<!-- seg 09-0016 paragraph h=802a3bce -->
If your girl insists on excusing shady attitudes and flatly refuses to acknowledge the malicious ulterior motives of the males around her, or stubbornly persists in being unnecessarily attentive, excessively nice, or friendly with “nice” guys[^20], offering lame excuses, tell her to seek you out again only on the day she changes her mind and acknowledges her mistake.

Before your female suddenly disappears without a word, as the sly ones usually do to leave us desperate with love, let her know that, if she is absent for more than two or three days without giving explanations, you will simply conclude that <b>she</b> has decided to end the relationship. They like to do this so that we will be worried, imagining that something serious has happened to them, that perhaps someone has kidnapped them, or that they are simply at a motel with someone!

In short, if your partner behaves improperly, refuses sex, avoids your kisses and embraces, mistreats you, avoids you, provokes, challenges, irritates, frustrates you, does not call, is arrogant, etc., simply paralyze the relationship, distancing yourself suddenly and closing yourself off completely. Do not pursue her, do not seek her out for sex, do not talk, do not call, do not interrogate, do not try to force her to say what is going on, and, above all, keep your head, preserve your coolness, and do not fight, do not fight, do not fight! Break off contact and wait, wait, wait, and wait. Hold out to the end and do not give in (this is why we must never fall in love). There will come a moment when she will not be able to bear the emotional tension she herself created and will come to you to find out what is going on, because doubt and curiosity will be swallowing her alive. Then impose your conditions without softening them; make the necessary and correct demands for resuming the relationship: frequency and quality of sex[^21], frequency of phone calls and of dates, the right way of being treated, exclusivity of attention, distancing herself from fake suitors, etc.

Formulate your demands in an absolutely clear way to avoid the customary feigned misunderstandings. Obviously, you must be polite, calm, and kind, but at the same time direct, decisive, realistic, and blunt.

<!-- seg 09-0023 paragraph h=c17cea40 -->
Rigorously carry out all the aforementioned retaliatory promises. Do not threaten to do what you cannot carry out. However, do not try any of this if you are in love or attached, because you will be the one struck down.

The decisions must eliminate all possibilities of dissimulation and deception, leading to only one result: her revealing herself. For this, they must contain only two alternatives or ways out for the sly one: meeting your demand or ending the relationship. If she loves you, she will do what you demand (and demand something fair, for otherwise your girlfriend will feel wronged and will take revenge, with every reason; always keep reason on your side). If the girl prefers to end the relationship, it is because she never loved you and was no good for you anyway, so she will not be missed.

Once this wicked and cheating nature of many animal females is understood, as well as the impossibility of falling in love without suffering bad consequences, the following question inevitably arises in the male mind: Will we have to give up having a companion? How can we bring a woman into our home and live with her under the same roof?

<!-- seg 09-0026 paragraph h=dd3d249c -->
To solve this problem, it seems to me that the most viable path is to maintain temporary relationships, without the eternal commitments on paper[^22], extended for as long as the partner provides certainty of fidelity, honesty, and transparency[^23]. When this certainty is irremediably shaken[^24], it means that the moment has come to try a new venture. Suspicion of betrayal or adultery, even faint[^25], when not dispelled, is enough to settle the total rupture of emotional commitments without appeal. It is not necessary to wait for certainty. A serious mistake I see in many suffering men consists precisely in waiting for certainty that they are being betrayed by their partners before breaking off the relationship or, at least, ending the commitments, instead of handing the consequences back to them at the first slight suspicion. Such weakness has its origin in being in love. If we are free of passion, we will not have to wait for the moment of catching them naked with their lovers; it will be enough to detect lies, incoherences, or simply something poorly explained for us to decree the end of the commitment. Let her know, without arguing, that at the first sign that something is wrong the consequences will be handed back.

Examples of subtle betrayals are: lying, continuing to listen to pointless courtship when there is no need to, being friendly or caring toward males who desire them, etc. The lame excuses are always the same: they claim that they feel sorry for the “poor guy,” that he had no “bad intentions,” or that they had not noticed his ulterior motives, etc. In truth, what they really want is to preserve the desires and hopes of the fake admirer, avoiding getting rid of him and pushing him away.

Obviously, the companion must be informed beforehand about the attitudes that shake trust and end the commitment, so that she cannot claim ignorance and accuse you of injustice. Draw up a list of the suspicious attitudes that bother you (obviously, do not be absurd; make a cold and rational judgment) and notify her decisively. If she has been warned beforehand and senses certainty in your voice and in your eyes, she will keep trying to seem faithful for longer and will prolong the relationship, perhaps indefinitely. But she must always be kept “on a tightrope.” What is the purpose of all this? To try to revive the nearly extinct role of wife, practically nonexistent in our day.

Man’s lesser emotional intelligence has made him less manipulative and more vulnerable to the effects of emotional attacks. Throughout history, man has never been capable of answering emotional aggressions with equally emotional responses. Stupidly, he has always answered them with physical reactions, which took all reason away from him and allowed countless accusations, besides feeding an unconscious ancestral hatred against his gender. Since they have never been struck back in their feelings for their little games, torments, manipulations, deceptions, etc., the sly ones believe themselves invulnerable in this field and rarely suffer the pains we suffer. We do not strike back in the same way but in different ways, and therein lies our mistake. Besides being proof of cowardice and weakness, the acts of assaulting them physically, insulting them, cursing them, shouting, etc., are a harmful waste of time that, in the long run, favors them by handing them the role of victim. Therefore, we have to learn to strike back at their feelings, so that they feel what it is like to have their noblest feelings turned into objects of irresponsible games. Toying with feelings and abusing another’s sincerity is almost the same as toying with life[^26]. Unfortunately, most men are too weak to hand back to them the appropriate emotional consequences. If such consequences always came, the use of wiles would certainly diminish.
