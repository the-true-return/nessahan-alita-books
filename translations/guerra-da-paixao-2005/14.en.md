13. A violent war of nerves

<!-- seg 14-0002 paragraph h=2e1756fb -->
You are alone, or with someone whose company is pleasant but does not fulfill you as you would need. Meanwhile, your beloved is perfectly happy with other people.

<!-- seg 14-0003 paragraph h=adbc5cf6 -->
You miss her, but she does not miss you. It is always you who takes the initiative to seek her out, and the opposite never happens. You always take the initiative in calling: you call several times until she finally answers. You drag out the phone conversations until she begins to make lame excuses to end the conversation. She always hangs up first.

<!-- seg 14-0004 paragraph h=6d568a91 -->
You are always curious about what she has to tell. She is never interested in what you have to say.

<!-- seg 14-0005 paragraph h=8940e500 -->
You give countless assurances that you are faithful and that you love her, but you receive as payment only doubts, inconsistent facts, and fishy stories.

<!-- seg 14-0006 paragraph h=2d7fd18e -->
She promises to see you; you wait anxiously for a long time for the date, but she lets you down. The excuse she offers would not convince even an idiot.

<!-- seg 14-0007 paragraph h=15dc7570 -->
In desperation, you stay away, but your absence is not felt in the least. On the contrary, it seems that your absence pleases her even more. Then you discover that you need her very much, but she does not need you in the least. For you there is nothing on Earth more important than she is, but for her there are many things more interesting than you. You are traded for girlfriends, “male friends,” relatives, parties, trips, bars, or even a simple television program.

<!-- seg 14-0008 paragraph h=8a9a6a19 -->
You are an open book: she always knows where you are and with whom. On the other hand, you never quite know whom she is with and what she is up to.

<!-- seg 14-0009 paragraph h=c728e923 -->
You feel you are in hell and she feels she is in heaven; that is why she is a goddess and you are one of the damned. She is your goddess because you put her on the altar.

<!-- seg 14-0010 paragraph h=79366aba -->
When she finally shows interest, there you are, available, as if you had waited for that moment for all eternity. She momentarily graces you with a little of her wonderful presence but soon withdraws, so that you tumble out of the dream again and fall into the nightmare.

<!-- seg 14-0011 paragraph h=b6b5c3b5 -->
Welcome to Hell! The merciless war of passion is under way and you are being defeated day after day. You may die of sadness, somatizing illnesses, or go mad. You may commit a crime. Your mental torture makes her immensely happy, for she feeds on your misfortune. When she is far away, in the painful absence, she knows you are suffering. She feels she is the best, the hottest, the most beautiful (even if she is not), a super-female, because she has the power to reject and trample.

<!-- seg 14-0012 paragraph h=5b249b83 -->
You try to defend yourself but discover that you are incapable; you have no strength. The only strengths you have are brute muscular physical strength and reason, which are useless in this war. Muscles are of no use, and reasoning is of even less use.

<!-- seg 14-0013 paragraph h=e50c444c -->
The more you argue, the worse things get and the more the problems become tangled and aggravated. You try to make her understand your obvious point of view, but she pretends not to understand and turns the conversation into chaos. You complain and receive in reply: “You’re insecure,” “You don’t trust me,” etc. It is no use appealing to logic, for everything is crazy, insane, illogical, absurd, calculatedly stupid, and irrational.

<!-- seg 14-0014 paragraph h=7356b6f7 -->
If you lose your head and hit her after so many provocations, you will have fallen into a trap: you will undeniably reveal yourself to be a cowardly villain by assaulting a “frail being who only knows how to give love.” You are tied up and dominated.

<!-- seg 14-0015 paragraph h=61dfcc93 -->
You know that disappearing is not the solution, for she will not come after you and you will lose her forever. She knows that, even after several years without seeing you, you will be there, available. You are worth nothing because you have nothing interesting, and therefore your absence will not be felt. But you do not want to lose her. The thing is, you have nothing that she really wants or needs, while she has many attributes without which you could not live: her particular way of being, her gaze, her walk, her voice, her touch, etc.

<!-- seg 14-0016 paragraph h=b7942d5a -->
You were cheated, you fell into an emotional trap, you were double-crossed. It was all a lie: the loving gaze, the guileless smile, the delicacy, the purity of feelings, the frailty, the defenseless look, the affection, the words of love. She deceived you, toyed and gambled with your happiness, with your sincerity, and with your noblest feelings. Your mistake was falling in love, considering her unique, special.

<!-- seg 14-0017 paragraph h=1badd441 -->
Now the only solution for you is to give up everything and fall apart. So fall apart and “die” psychologically, eliminating from yourself this poisonous feeling that has thrown you into such a miserable state. Do this and you will see that little by little everything will change. You will understand that your goddess was made of clay and that her divinity was a sham. You will hurl the disenchanted statue far away.

<!-- seg 14-0018 paragraph h=a9b9f699 -->
I tell you that you have been deceived all this time. You were fooled by passion and are seeing the world upside down, inside out. There are many important and interesting virtues within your person, but you ignore them; you do not know them. Dig them up. There is another man in there; wake him up. Stop loving passionately. Stop longing for her. Stop flattering. Stop pursuing. Do not spend your precious time thinking about her; unmask her without pity. Treat her as she really is: a female. Ignore her crocodile tears. Denounce the lies and unmask the pretenses at the very moment they are under way. Ignore her onion crying. Make her see who she really is: a sly one with an angel’s face. Do not let her run away from herself. Give her a good lesson in feelings so that she never forgets it and never again toys with anyone else’s happiness.

<!-- seg 14-0019 paragraph h=93a87c2c -->
Yes, she needs you, but neither of you knows it. What attributes do you have without which she could not live? Many. What does she need madly? Your guidance, your protection, your command, your initiative, your logical rigor, your lack of inhibition, your self-assurance, your determination, your certainty, your coldness, your firmness, among others.

<!-- seg 14-0020 paragraph h=4c5b4a22 -->
She wants you to be greater, to dominate the situation so as to lead her safely; that is why she resists. She wants to have reasons to respect you and to feel protected at the same time; that is why she provokes, attacks, and challenges. She wants to gauge your capacity not to be deceived; that is why she lies, plays dirty, and tries to cheat you. She wants to gauge your capacity not to be persuaded; that is why she offers false affection. She creates psychological hells and takes pleasure in watching you dance in the fire. It is the most brutal animal instinct in action: the instinct of the female’s selection of the male. If you fail, you will be discarded from the genetic history of your species, and it will be no use explaining your reasons to her, because they will not move her. She will take no pity, for you are a man and were therefore born to be tough and to suffer, to “put up with everything,” and if you are weak, you are good for nothing. She, and not you, is the male chauvinist.

<!-- seg 14-0021 paragraph h=44087c62 -->
Many women cannot feel attraction and pity for the same man. But, unfortunately, some can feel attraction and fear simultaneously. Others can also feel attraction and sadness. These women do not love those who wish to make them happy, but those who make them cry, making them unhappy. Such is nature. And unless they struggle hard against themselves and against their instincts, so it will continue to be, unfortunately.
