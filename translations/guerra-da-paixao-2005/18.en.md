Conclusions

<!-- seg 18-0002 paragraph h=5974076e -->
After reading this volume, I hope the reader has noticed that we (the author of this book and the authors who have inspired him since the first volume) propose peaceful resistance and inner tranquility as strategies for dismantling female perfidy. We propose a boycott of the little games, manipulations, wiles, and ruses. This boycott can only be carried out through non-action, which will demand of us the capacity to accept everything and hand back the consequences fairly. The strategy we propose is the same as Gandhi’s: boycotting through silence and refusal.

<!-- seg 18-0003 paragraph h=4cc4f0d8 -->
As the attentive reader will already have noticed, we do not argue in favor of negative feelings or of misogynistic sentimentalism of any kind. We argue against passion and in favor of questioning and skepticism toward the myth of the defenseless, frail, inferior, and harmless woman. Those who conclude that we favor negative feelings will have distorted the work. Being free of passion is not being selfish, arrogant, manipulative, vindictive, wrathful, or furious. Negative feelings are passions too.

<!-- seg 18-0004 paragraph h=ad544807 -->
When a hypothesis or a polemical idea causes discomfort, it should be properly refuted, by demonstrating the flaws on which it is based, its internal logical inconsistencies, and its low explanatory power, instead of simply being disparaged. There is a difference between refuting a set of ideas and disparaging it. Mere disparagement is something subjective and vague.

<!-- seg 18-0005 paragraph h=cb6ea0d6 -->
Our conclusions do not go beyond the scope of love relationships, as many have mistakenly judged. Everything said here applies exclusively to the field of love and to no other. I hope I have made it clear that the proposals are valid only for stable relationships and are therefore intended only for adults.

<!-- seg 18-0006 paragraph h=f53d85fa -->
The reader should further conclude that, in dealing with female perfidy, we are dealing with only one aspect of the whole of human perfidy, which is much broader and takes qualitatively different forms in men and in women. As was pointed out in the book, men also have their “shadow,” and there are women who do not let themselves be dominated by what they call their “dark side,” truly expressing the face of the Sacred Feminine. We have not gone deeper into these two aspects simply as a matter of focus, but nothing prevents us from doing so in the future. Thus, generalizing would be absurd, since we could never know all the people on Earth. Let it be understood that when we use the expressions “such women,” “these women,” “women,” “sly ones,” “manipulative women,” etc., we are referring exclusively to insincere women who cheat in love and not to the others. I will say nothing about the percentage incidence of the profile outlined here in the populations of the various countries, leaving this question to the reader.
