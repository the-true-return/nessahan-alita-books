11. Female vices and weaknesses

<!-- seg 12-0002 paragraph h=adf7e01e -->
People usually speak of female wickedness reluctantly. The common tendency is to avoid it, to dodge the subject. On the other hand, denouncing the various and undeniable cruelties of men is commonplace, seen by people as natural since, as one often hears, <i>“men are no good anyway.”</i> Good men, then, give a forced smile and pretend to find it funny, even though deep down they know things are not so simple. Hence the need for us to take the opposite path in order to clarify what is missing, facing head-on the problem that everyone avoids and denouncing it as fair-minded feminists[^36] do with male vices. If it is true that animal humanoid males[^37] are wicked toward females, coveting them, valuing them for their outward beauty, and always having sexual ulterior motives, it is no less true that females are wicked as well, valuing us for our social position, our attractiveness to beautiful women, our money, our fame, etc., and not loving us disinterestedly. They are wholly utilitarian and do not love us for what we are, but only for the practical and emotional benefits we can provide. Male ulterior motives are sexual: the male wants to copulate. Female ulterior motives are practical and calculating: to be envied by her rivals, to be turned into a princess, to have a slave, to draw attention, to be protected, to be known, etc. Therefore, men are not the only villains of the story.

<!-- seg 12-0003 footnote n=36 h=7cf23136 -->
Let it be clear that I am referring to conscious and enlightened feminists and not to the misandrist Nazi-feminists who blame men alone for all the misfortunes of the world.

<!-- seg 12-0004 footnote n=37 h=325d53ac -->
We are all animal humanoid males. However, not everyone resigns himself to being one, and there are those who struggle to surpass themselves. We are not only human, as we like to think; we are also animals, and there is nothing wrong with that; it is merely the current stage of our evolution. Our animality, in turn, is not always in harmony with the instincts and with nature. It seems unnecessary to me to mention proofs that confirm this idea.

<!-- seg 12-0005 paragraph h=deb6543e -->
Vices are emotional weaknesses. Emotional weaknesses are the most intense desires of the soul, against which the person has no resistance. They are secret springs that lead to action. These springs are activated when the right psychological buttons are pressed. The right psychological buttons are pressed by means of attitudes that excite and kindle the passions, the desires, the fears, and the intense emotions. Hence the importance of human beings becoming conscious of their weaknesses and overcoming them.

<!-- seg 12-0006 paragraph h=7549ed81 -->
Despite their sometimes apparent virtue and nobility of character, the sly women we are dealing with here have desires they are incapable of resisting. These are tools by means of which they can be taken, captured, and manipulated. I will point them out:

<!-- seg 12-0007 paragraph h=e09611b9 -->
• being protected against her natural fears (fear of the dark, of loneliness, of abandonment, of death, of disease, of the cold, of the rain, of old age, of certain small and repugnant animals, etc.); this is the main weakness;

<!-- seg 12-0008 paragraph h=3fc22ebc -->
• a sweet tooth for candy, ice cream, and chocolate;

<!-- seg 12-0009 paragraph h=731279cb -->
• being socially recognized, admired, and envied by all;

<!-- seg 12-0010 paragraph h=7a73f441 -->
• getting ahead of her rivals, managing to be noticed by a famous man desired by many;

<!-- seg 12-0011 paragraph h=3346e87a -->
• discovering secrets that excite her curiosity;

<!-- seg 12-0012 paragraph h=d211282c -->
• being desired and rejecting whoever desires her;

<!-- seg 12-0013 paragraph h=7f9116cf -->
• dissembling, lying, and feeling that she manages to deceive possessive men;

<!-- seg 12-0014 paragraph h=3a050a9a -->
• continually proving that she can make someone fall in love and suffer for love.

<!-- seg 12-0015 paragraph h=16313aec -->
The desires mentioned are never fully satisfied during a relationship with more experienced men: they sabotage them. Women become attached to these men without understanding why. And the reasons come down to this: these men hold out the possibility of satisfying those absurd passions and at the same time never satisfy them fully, preserving the female thirst.

<!-- seg 12-0016 paragraph h=57d514b4 -->
The desire to offer sex, affection, and love <b>IS NOT</b> one of the main female weaknesses. These three elements are merely tools used to attract men and dominate them in a manner analogous to the one described above. In the war of passion, the other’s desire, whether man or woman, is never fully satisfied. He who completely satisfies the other’s desire loses it. He who never satisfies any of the other’s desires likewise loses it. He who most skillfully meets all of the partner’s desires partially, always allowing those desires to continue, will win. That is why the war of passion is foolish; it has nothing to do with true love. The war of passion is pure sentimental selfishness.

<!-- seg 12-0017 paragraph h=9e4472ed -->
Every desire is a weakness through which a person can be taken. Sly women do not wish to love a man unconditionally, disinterestedly, and altruistically, nor do they desire sex solely in and of itself, as the uninformed customarily think. Their desires are as petty and selfish as men’s desires, differing from them only qualitatively. The kinds of desire differ, but the selfishness contained in them does not. That is why what is commonly called love has no use whatsoever. It is something dispensable and has nothing to do with true and divine LOVE.

<!-- seg 12-0018 paragraph h=58bf5feb -->
Unless she has a history of dedication to the victorious struggle over herself and seeks to surpass herself day after day, becoming conscious of her weaknesses, a woman will betray her husband without much difficulty if left alone with another man who corresponds to the ideal male model in her soul. This is the proof that romantic and passionate love is a hoax to deceive innocent people. Passion has a fatal face that no one likes to talk about. The irresistible ideal male model is the one that synthesizes all the desires, dreams, fantasies, vices, fears, and longings that imprison the will. The men onto whom these models are projected will not necessarily deserve it. Fascinating men will not always draw a woman toward salvation.

<!-- seg 12-0019 paragraph h=6ec9bcf4 -->
If you are being ignored by some lady, that fact indicates, with total accuracy and without the slightest shadow of a doubt, that you are not pressing the right psychological buttons, whether out of ignorance or out of inability. But it is quite possible that a more experienced man is doing so. Most of the time, uninformed men press the wrong buttons; that is, they act mistakenly, believing they will get one result and getting another. Then they are surprised and become confused, without understanding the reason for the rejection or lack of interest. And the reason is simply ignorance: the attitudes he believes would impress them do not impress them, and the attitudes that would apparently drive them away do not drive them away. The effects are always the opposite of those expected, and the would-be seducer may fall into ridiculous situations without even realizing how ridiculous they are. Then we witness tragicomedies in which we laugh and cry at the same time. These are ridiculous attitudes that the uninformed believe have a seductive effect but that in fact produce the opposite effect: shouting, clowning around, talking loudly, using pickup lines, being extroverted, being a tough guy, being an exhibitionist, monkeying around, playing the nice guy, trying to please, pretending to be Prince Charming, acting in love, harassing, pursuing, keeping watch, appearing anxious and desperate for sex or for dates. Conversely, these are attitudes that produce a positive effect: taking no notice of beauty, looking fixedly into her eyes until they are lowered, surprising her by suddenly making contact, but as if no great importance were attached to it, being serious, speaking in a firm tone of voice, being short and blunt, speaking little, surprising her with long, well-judged speeches, ignoring her presence and then surprising her with sudden contact, disagreeing, contradicting her opinions, leading beneficially, advising severely, “horrifying” in a calculated way, “cornering,” “pushing the evasive sly ones up against the wall,” creating definitive situations, being distant, reserved, and mysterious, speaking little and correctly, being protective, touching physically in a quick, light way as if there were no intention, surprising her by speaking at length about well-judged things and then immediately returning to silence, not talking nonsense, not being long-winded, and, above all, being fair, sincere, and upright.

<!-- seg 12-0020 paragraph h=8d8af888 -->
Before a man who leads, stands out from the rest, and impresses with his uncommon firmness and self-assurance, females swoon and cannot resist. It is a weakness analogous to the one we feel before beautiful, delicate, and voluptuous women who display their legs, their cleavage, and their curves, inviting us to love (even if they refuse it immediately afterward).

<!-- seg 12-0021 paragraph h=7549bb8f -->
Much like children, many adult women need to feel the presence of someone more powerful and wiser who will guide and protect them (that is why the powerful are the most pursued). Outside this position, they feel vulnerable, exposed to the natural dangers of our species. It is this weakness that drives them to throw themselves madly at famous men, singers, and artists, because these communicate to the unconscious that they are more powerful than ordinary men (<i>“If he stands out so much, he really must be very powerful and have something good about him!”</i>). In such situations, the fantasies of the female unconscious are activated, which makes women come out of their immobility and offer themselves to, pursue, and chase the men who are in the spotlight. But once they have seduced and conquered them, they will suddenly be disappointed if these men do not live up to the demands of their unconscious fantasies. They will then lose interest and exchange them for others, or keep them as mere trophies, emotional slaves, or something of the sort.

<!-- seg 12-0022 paragraph h=c31a60c7 -->
Indifference to sex and reluctance to show acts of love and affection compensate for physical frailty and confer emotional dominance over the other gender; these are factors that make women emotionally resilient and hard to defeat in the wars of passion. The females of mammals and birds, in general, are not eager to copulate, unlike the males, who fall into intense stress when forced into abstinence, even developing pathologies. The desperate need for the sex-affection-love triad felt by human males makes them vulnerable and forces them to pursue, please, chase, flatter, and submit like subjects to a queen. Like princesses and felines, human females receive security and comfort as something naturally due to them, whose corresponding price need not be paid, since their mere existence is already seen as a more than fair payment. We males, on the contrary, generally resemble slaves and dogs, for we consider it natural to sacrifice ourselves, giving them much or everything and receiving little in return. Therefore, they are strong in a field in which we are weak. To make matters worse, knowing that they are delicious and necessary for our emotional health, they take advantage of this weakness to exercise dominance. Man, as a rule, is a puppet who manages everything and commands everything, except the felines who command him. Male superiority is a myth.

<!-- seg 12-0023 paragraph h=0696efd4 -->
Some uninformed men project their psychological characteristics onto women and believe that women are like them, eager for sex, love, and affection. They believe that the love-sex-affection they offer could impress women, who would then fall in love with their <i>phallus erectus</i>. This is an absurd idea that does not hold up under observation and experience, because women function in the opposite way to men; they are men’s opposite pole.

<!-- seg 12-0024 paragraph h=3ec86e5b -->
Knowing our weaknesses as they do, it becomes easy, for example, to tame through affection a husband enraged by jealousy, or to invite harassment by exposing themselves and then accuse the harasser, etc., for everything stems from men’s weakness (and men, therefore, are also to blame for it). Therefore, we have to fight our own weaknesses instead of setting ourselves against our delicious felines. When we subdue our animal part, our needs, our desires, our craziness for sex, etc., we involuntarily subdue them by extension, even if we do not want to, because we eliminate the buttons or weak points through which we were manipulated. In other words, we subdue them when we subdue ourselves, giving up subjecting them to our interests and to our passionate weaknesses, namely: desire, affections, lust[^38].

<!-- seg 12-0025 footnote n=38 h=e69d52b9 -->
Of course, when women also become conscious of their weaknesses, relationships become more harmonious. The animal stage then begins to be transcended and the contradictions move to other levels.

<!-- seg 12-0026 paragraph h=097f3c9c -->
The behavior of the sly ones is often governed by a principle I call “sentimental selfishness.” For them, what matters is not our feelings but theirs, and theirs alone. They are absolutely blind to anything else. They consider “logical” whatever provides desirable feelings and “illogical” whatever provides undesirable feelings. Here another complication arises and the pot boils over for good: pleasant feelings are not always the desirable ones, because the unconscious reacts differently from, and even contrary to, consciousness.

<!-- seg 12-0027 paragraph h=601b76d3 -->
Sentimental selfishness possesses them and drives them to constantly satisfy the need to confirm that we are suffering from love. When they cannot detect signs of emotional suffering in their partners, they become sad and say to themselves: <i>“He doesn’t suffer for me anymore; I must be getting uninteresting and unattractive, etc.”</i> They take pleasure in seeing us suffer from anger, irritation, jealousy, longing, sadness, absence, attachment, confusion, doubt, etc. It is this very need that strikes back at them when they come up against a refractory man, for he does not allow it to be satisfied. Since the desire to confirm our emotional suffering is very strong, it turns into an internal parasite that swallows them alive when it is not satisfied, for the pain of dissatisfaction is proportional to the intensity of the desire[^39].

<!-- seg 12-0028 footnote n=39 h=aec1956f -->
It is for this reason that women should recognize and transcend their weaknesses instead of hurling invective at whoever describes them.

<!-- seg 12-0029 paragraph h=2856d81f -->
Therefore, the refractory partner will strike at the sly one’s feelings a first time by refusing to suffer from passion, and a second time by “punishing” her with her own attitudes and unsatisfied desires. If even so she does not change, he will have no alternative but to leave her.

<!-- seg 12-0030 paragraph h=1fdcceb5 -->
There are extreme cases of violent predatory females, highly hysterical and aggressive, who dare us to assault them physically (<i>“Hit me, if you’re a man!”</i>). In such cases there is no alternative but to walk away forever.

<!-- seg 12-0031 paragraph h=432a88c7 -->
Very few males manage to deal with this female vice of provocation. Most despair and succumb, for the learning is difficult, slow, and painful. The weak shout, assault, insult, and lose the war.

<!-- seg 12-0032 paragraph h=1f34d7e4 -->
Men may be strong physically and intellectually, but emotionally they are feeble. They easily lose their temper when provoked. They are impatient and have a short fuse. This emotional feebleness causes defeats in the wars of passion. Women’s greater emotional intelligence confronts and disrupts men’s physical and intellectual strength and defeats it, taking men apart and knocking them down. This is the reason why those who resist influences and provocations on the emotional level become invulnerable.

<!-- seg 12-0033 paragraph h=20b96e30 -->
The minds of the sly ones have difficulty distinguishing kindness from weakness, just as they do cruelty from strength. This confusion leads them not to feel safe in the company of democratic and kind men. However, there are good and strong men, just as there are cruel and feeble men. Therefore, the preference for the worst is based on a mistake. And this mistake results from yet another vice: superficiality in judgments.
