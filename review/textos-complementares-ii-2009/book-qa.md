## Open questions

- **00-0001**: Title "Textos Complementares II" → "Supplementary Texts II" (config title_en; consistent with "Supplementary Texts" for the 2008 volume). Alternative: "Complementary Texts II".
- **00-0004**: The citation gives the English title; the Portuguese original is "Textos Complementares II". "Edição virtual independente" → "Independent online edition", as in the other volumes.
- **00-0027**: The original contents line reads "20 Indo além" (no period after the number); the period was added to match the chapter heading and the other entries.
- **02-0002**: "pegada" (Brazilian slang for a man's firm, confident physical handling of a woman) → "firm grip." Alternatives: "a strong touch," "a firm hand."
- **03-0002**: "magia sexual" → "sexual magic" (the usual rendering in English translations of Samael Aun Weor). Alternative: "sex magic."
- **03-0004**: "agüentar o tranco" (colloquial: to withstand the jolt, to hold up under strain) → "take the pounding." Alternatives: "withstand the jolt," "hold up."
- **04-0010**: This paragraph repeats 04-0003 word for word in the original; translated identically.
- **07-0009**: "miguxo" (Brazilian slang for a cutesy, affectionate friend, used here for the sexless male friend) → "bestie." Alternatives: "buddy," "BFF," "pal."
- **08-0028**: "chifres" (horns) is the Portuguese idiom for cuckolding; the image was kept, as in How to Deal with Women. Alternative: "with cheating."
- **10-0001**: "Desarticulação antecipada" → "Preemptive dismantling" ("desarticular" is rendered "dismantle" throughout). Alternatives: "Advance dismantling," "Dismantling in advance."
- **13-0002**: "Dor de amor" → "Lovesickness" in the title; "dores de amor" in the text → "the pains of love." Alternatives: "Heartache," "Love pain."
- **14-0007**: "(rs)" is the Brazilian internet abbreviation for "risos" (laughter); rendered "(ha ha)," as in The Profane Feminine. Alternative: "(lol)."
- **16-0001**: "galanteios" (gallant compliments, flirtatious flattery) → "compliments"; "cantadas" → "pickup lines." Alternatives: "gallantries," "flattery."
- **20-0013**: "morte dos detalhes" → "death of details" and "lei de recorrência" → "law of recurrence" (Gnostic terms from Samael Aun Weor, as usually rendered in English). Alternative for the first: "death of the small details."
- **20-0022**: "ginásio psicológico" → "psychological gymnasium" (Samael Aun Weor's term for daily life as a field for self-observation, as usually rendered in English).
- **21-0013**: "no(a) outro(a)" marks both genders in Portuguese; English "the other person" is already neutral, so the marking was not reproduced.
- **22-0019**: "para nos tomarem por aí" is unusual; read as "to take us for a ride" (to exploit/fool us). Alternatives: "to take hold of us," "to get the better of us."
- **22-0033**: "revolta" rendered "resentment" to keep it distinct from "rebeldia" ("rebelliousness") in 22-0034, which the author sets on the other side. Alternatives: "revolt," "outrage."
- **22-0098**: Heading "Irritação da dúvida" rendered "The irritation of doubt," the phrase used in Charles S. Peirce’s "The Fixation of Belief" (1877, public domain), which the author points to here.
- **22-0102**: "o uso de relatos e perguntas" read as relying on women’s own accounts and on asking them questions; rendered "accounts and questionnaires." Alternative: "accounts and questions."
- **22-0110**: "desencarnação" (spiritist term for death, the soul leaving the body) rendered "discarnation." Alternative: "hastens death."
