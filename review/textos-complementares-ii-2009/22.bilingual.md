### 22-0001 · heading

Aphorisms

### 22-0002 · heading

> Atos de vontade

Acts of will

### 22-0003 · paragraph

> A vontade se confirma por atos e não por simples pensamentos ou intenções. Não conseguiremos modificar nossos comportamentos, pensamentos e sentimentos simplesmente fazendo esforços sobre nós mesmos sem confirmá-los com atitudes que correspondam aos novos padrões. Fazer esforços corretos é importante, mas cooperar com tais esforços atuando conforme um novo comportamento é imprescindível.

The will is confirmed by acts and not by mere thoughts or intentions. We will not manage to change our behaviors, thoughts and feelings simply by making efforts upon ourselves without confirming them with attitudes that correspond to the new patterns. Making right efforts is important, but cooperating with those efforts by acting in accordance with a new behavior is indispensable.

### 22-0004 · paragraph

> Quanto mais reiteramos um novo comportamento, tanto mais ele se consolida em nossa natureza.

The more we repeat a new behavior, the more it becomes consolidated in our nature.

### 22-0005 · paragraph

> A modificação do comportamento é algo gradativo. Exige paciência e persistência. Ninguém vira o oposto do que é de um momento para outro.

Changing one’s behavior is something gradual. It demands patience and persistence. No one becomes the opposite of what they are from one moment to the next.

### 22-0006 · heading

> Solidariedade

### 22-0007 · paragraph

We must show solidarity with the weak, not scorn them, and understand that each one is at their own level. What is easy to achieve for one may be extremely difficult for another.

### 22-0008 · heading

> Atenuando a irritação

### 22-0009 · paragraph

Com isso, nos colocamos acima da situação, nos protegemos e até podemos auxiliá-la. Esta é uma boa forma de evitar brigas com a parceira.

When a person irritates us, we can ease the irritation by keeping alive in our consciousness the remembrance that there is in them another part, different from the one now manifesting. In this way, we place ourselves above the situation, we protect ourselves, and we may even help them. This is a good way to avoid fights with one’s partner.

### 22-0010 · paragraph

Let us see beyond the person who acts in a way we do not like, seeking their true Being. Every person has a higher part that can manifest through them in various degrees.

The world is lost, and human wickedness seems to have no end anymore. Nevertheless, there are still people of both sexes with goodness within them. The human being is ignorant and does not see that, by harming his neighbor, he is harming himself.
