### 11-0010 · paragraph

Use um tom de voz firme, convicto e grave mas fale pouco, de forma curta, grossa e direta, mirando nos olhos. Então cale-se ou se afaste até que ela se insinue envergonhada para reconciliação[^31]. The key to dealing with such hysterical stratagems is to catch the provocation in progress and denounce it immediately, without leaving any room for discussion and, of course, without fighting. If she makes no overtures, leave her for good, trade her in for a better one, for you will have lost nothing: shrews who are not ashamed even when caught red-handed are incorrigible.

### 11-0014 · paragraph

It is a very common procedure among the sly ones, whose intention is to leave us in a bad emotional state, but it becomes effective only because we argue and talk.

### 11-0029 · paragraph
