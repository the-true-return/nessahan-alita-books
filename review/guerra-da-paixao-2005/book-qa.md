## Open questions

- **00-0001**: Series title follows the glossary proposal "Man’s Suffering in Love" (O Sofrimento Amoroso do Homem), as in the other volumes.
- **00-0003**: Subtitle "As artimanhas e os truques ardilosos das mulheres no amor". "artimanhas" → "wiles" (glossary). Alternative: "Women’s Wiles and Cunning Tricks in Love".
- **00-0006**: The citation gives the English titles; the Portuguese originals are "O Sofrimento Amoroso do Homem" and "A Guerra da Paixão (As artimanhas e os truques ardilosos das mulheres no amor)".
- **00-0012**: The author’s notice forbids any alteration of the book’s "language or title". Translated faithfully; a human should confirm that this English edition is authorized before publication (as for the other volumes).
- **01-0009**: "assimilam este instinto" translated literally as "assimilate this instinct" (i.e., integrate/master it, as opposed to remaining passive before it). Alternatives: "come to terms with", "master".
- **01-0011**: "muit(o)as" marks both genders (muitos/muitas); rendered "many women (and men)". The pronouns "as estou ajudando", "elas próprias" refer to women; made explicit as "women" for clarity.
- **02-0008**: "prova multisignificante" (author’s coinage) rendered "a proof, rich in meaning". Alternatives: "a highly significant proof", "a proof with many meanings".
- **04-0003**: "ficantes casuais": "ficante" is Brazilian slang for a casual, uncommitted romantic/sexual partner; rendered "casual flings". Alternative: "casual hookups".
- **05-0003**: "da(o) parceiro(a)" marks both genders; English "the partner" is already gender-neutral, so the marking was not reproduced.
- **06-0003**: "Cláudia Pacheco" cited without a title; name kept as in the original (possibly the Brazilian author of books on relationships). A human may want to identify the work.
- **06-0005**: "Elane Calligaris" kept as in the original. Possibly a misspelling of "Eliana Calligaris" or a reference to psychoanalyst Contardo Calligaris, who wrote on the prostitution fantasy; not corrected because the intended author is uncertain.
- **08-0007**: "pentalfa invertida" (inverted pentalpha, i.e., the five-pointed star with its point downward, as in Eliphas Lévi) rendered "inverted pentagram". Alternative: "inverted pentalpha".
- **10-0007**: The original spells "Karen Salmanshon" and "Ammy Shuterland"; corrected to Karen Salmansohn (as in Masculine Reflections on Woman and Love) and Amy Sutherland (author of "What Shamu Taught Me About Life, Love, and Marriage", on applying animal-training techniques to a husband). Confirm the second identification.
- **11-0028**: "diga-lhe para nunca mais dizer a verdade dali em diante" translated literally ("tell her never to tell the truth again from then on"), i.e., an ironic rule mirroring her lie. Possibly a slip for "never again to lie" or "that you will never again believe her"; kept as written.
- **12-0016**: "Aquele que satisfaz completamente o desejo do outro a perde": the feminine "a" most likely refers to "a guerra da paixão" (he loses the war); rendered "loses it." Alternative reading: he loses "a outra pessoa" (the partner).
- **13-0015**: "canções psicológicas" rendered literally as "psychological songs" (the Gurdjieffian sense of repetitive inner complaints); alternative: "inner songs" / "the same old song."
- **13-0026**: "ao renunciar ao impressionismo": the author’s playful use of "impressionismo" (the urge to impress); rendered "renouncing the wish to impress" (as "power to impress" in Magnetism in Social Relations 13-0013). Alternative: "renouncing impressionism," the proposed glossary term, currently limited to Masculine Reflections on Woman and Love.
- **15-0010**: "Eliane Calligaris" kept as in the original; in 06-0005 the same author appears as "Elane Calligaris." Probably the psychoanalyst Eliana Calligaris; spelling not corrected because the identification is uncertain.
- **15-0013**: The original reads "Uma vez que acredite que o mesmo não verificou sua presença no local B" ("did not check ... at location B"), which contradicts the logic of the example; translated as "location A" on the evident sense. If the original was intended, read "location B."
- **16-0002**: "se insinuam sobre os homens" / "insinuação": rendered "insinuate themselves" / "insinuation" (as in the table of contents), in the sense of making advances. Alternative: "make advances to men" / "advances."
