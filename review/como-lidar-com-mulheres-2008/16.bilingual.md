### 16-0004 · paragraph

Obviously, Schopenhauer is referring to romantic love, directed exclusively at one single woman, and not to universal love. When a love crisis is exceedingly intense, it can unleash an existential and spiritual crisis so deep that it leads the individual to re-evaluate his whole life and emerge renewed from this dark and dangerous passage (KORNFIELD, 1997 and GROF & GROF, 1989/1997). Unfortunately, the danger of getting lost on this journey forever also exists and is real. Crimes of passion are proof of this danger. Passionate love is hell after heaven.

### 16-0005 · paragraph

> Aquilo que definimos aqui como amor romântico ou passional (paixão romântica) é o que Erich Frommm (1976) denomina "amor neurótico" e também "pseudo-amor", um dos males que afeta a civilização ocidental moderna, ávida pela posse e pelo consumo. What we define here as romantic or passionate love (romantic passion) is what Erich Fromm (1976) calls “neurotic love” and also “pseudo-love,” one of the ills that afflict modern Western civilization, avid for possession and consumption. Still according to Fromm, neurotic love takes several forms: sentimental love, sadistic love, idolatrous love, and narcissistic love.

Since the whole of modern Western civilization is sick, not a few people are affected by amorous insanities.
