16. The Tests

### 17-0002 · paragraph

> A fêmea humana é essencialmente traidora no amor[^1]: solicita incessantemente que o macho se entregue mas, simultaneamente, considera aqueles que o fazem débeis e desinteressantes, traindo-os com outros mais fortes, que não as amam[^2].

The human female is essentially treacherous in love:[^1] she incessantly asks the male to give himself over but, at the same time, considers those who do so feeble and uninteresting, betraying them with others who are stronger and do not love them.[^2]

### 17-0003 · footnote 1

Just like the male, but in another form. I am referring here to the treacherous character contained in the act of demanding love while intending not to give it in return. ### 17-0004 · footnote 2

A traição masculina tem como eixo a entrega sexual e não a entrega sentimental. O homem trai porque quer o sexo em si. A mulher trai porque quer experimentar sentimentos intensos.

In masculine betrayal, this incessant demand for the surrender of feelings followed by abandonment and disinterest is much less frequent. Masculine betrayal revolves around sexual surrender and not sentimental surrender. A man cheats because he wants sex in itself. A woman cheats because she wants to experience intense feelings.

### 17-0005 · paragraph

> Esta essência amorosa traidora se origina da necessidade de testar o valor masculino e da duplicidade de seu desejo[^3]. As solicitações de entrega, bem como as recriminações e os jogos de ciúmes, visam testar a qualidade do reprodutor e protetor de sua prole. Sua intenção é verificar o quanto o homem está seguro de si, de sua força e de seu valor.

This treacherous amorous essence arises from the need to test masculine worth and from the duplicity of her desire.[^3] The demands for surrender, as well as the recriminations and the games of jealousy, aim to test the quality of the breeder and protector of her offspring. Her intention is to verify how sure the man is of himself, of his strength, and of his worth.

### 17-0006 · footnote 3

A outra face corresponde a ser preservada contra tudo o que seja desagradável e, de modo geral, perigoso. Feminine desire has two faces. One face corresponds to the desire to be fertilized by the carrier of the best genes, that is, to have sex. The other face corresponds to being preserved from everything that is unpleasant and, in general, dangerous. In this second case, the desire is to be protected, to have a provider, an emotional slave, etc. The man will be assigned one role or the other according to his profile and conduct.

### 17-0007 · paragraph

> As mulheres costumam nos testar simulando estarem decepcionadas conosco, tratando-nos como se fôssemos pirralhos, moleques culpados por travessuras condenáveis, com o intuito de ativar em nossa mente lembranças da infância e, deste modo, nos forçar a vê-las como mães severas. Também é comum que ataquem nossos pontos de vista e concepções, muitas vezes qualificando-os de infantis, visando abalar nosso moral para que duvidemos do nosso valor. Women usually test us by pretending to be disappointed with us, treating us as if we were brats, little boys guilty of reprehensible mischief, with the aim of activating childhood memories in our mind and thus forcing us to see them as stern mothers. It is also common for them to attack our points of view and conceptions, often labeling them childish, aiming to shake our morale so that we doubt our own worth. Through these procedures they will compare us to other males and will conclude that we are superior to those who wavered and doubted themselves.

### 17-0008 · paragraph

> Atenções e gentilezas a outros machos são outra modalidade de teste que empregam. Por este caminho, descobrem se nos sentimos inferiores aos outros homens ou não. Se reagirmos com ciúmes, concluem que somos débeis e isto lhes mostrará duas coisas: 1) que acreditamos que o outro pode fasciná-la mais do que nós; 2) que temos medo de não encontrar outra fêmea melhor e, portanto, somos incompetentes enquanto homens. Logo, é necessário não termos ciúmes. Mas isso não será possível enquanto sentirmos amor passional. Por este motivo, e somente por isto, devemos evitar totalmente o amor e o apaixonamento. Tais sentimentos são debilitantes e tornam o homem desinteressante, ainda que seja dito o contrário.

Attentions and kindnesses toward other males are another type of test they use. In this way, they find out whether or not we feel inferior to other men. If we react with jealousy, they conclude that we are feeble, and this will show them two things: 1) that we believe the other man can fascinate her more than we can; 2) that we are afraid of not finding another, better female and are therefore incompetent as men. Therefore, it is necessary for us not to be jealous. But this will not be possible as long as we feel passionate love. For this reason, and for this reason only, we must totally avoid love and infatuation. Such feelings are debilitating and make a man uninteresting, even though the opposite is said.
