# Bilingual review: como-lidar-com-mulheres-2008 / chapter 09

**8. Porque não devemos discutir e nem polemizar**

### 09-0001 · heading

> 8. Porque não devemos discutir e nem polemizar

8. Why we should neither argue nor debate

### 09-0002 · paragraph

> As mulheres costumam ter muitas atitudes que prejudicam seu relacionamento conosco. Entre tais atitudes, posso citar o gosto por amizades masculinas, o hábito de admirar ou elogiar outros homens, famosos ou não etc. Quando as apanhamos em flagrante, negam terminantemente e dizem que foi tudo algo inocente e sem más intenções, <i>"sem maldade".</i>

Women tend to have many attitudes that harm their relationship with us. Among such attitudes, I can mention a taste for male friendships, the habit of admiring or praising other men, famous or not, etc. When we catch them red-handed, they flatly deny it and say it was all something innocent and without bad intentions, <i>“nothing malicious.”</i>

### 09-0003 · paragraph

> Por serem baseados em sentimentos e não na razão, estas idéias e comportamentos femininos indesejáveis continuam incólumes após destruirmos intelectualmente seus argumentos.

Because they are based on feelings and not on reason, these undesirable feminine ideas and behaviors remain unscathed after we have intellectually destroyed their arguments.

### 09-0004 · paragraph

> Em geral, os argumentos femininos em favor das atitudes que destroem a relação são muito frágeis. Entretanto, de nada adianta discutir ou polemizar pois, mesmo após destruídos, seus motivos prevalecem por serem emocionais. Elas então elaboram outros caminhos psicológicos para justificar suas atitudes excusas sem nunca assumí-las.

In general, feminine arguments in favor of the attitudes that destroy the relationship are very flimsy. However, there is no point in arguing or debating, for even after they have been destroyed, their motives prevail because they are emotional. They then work out other psychological paths to justify their shady attitudes without ever owning up to them.

### 09-0005 · paragraph

> Por tais razões, é uma total perda de tempo discutir ou polemizar quando as apanhamos em pilantragens[^1]. Este hábito, que vejo em muitos homens, apenas cria um clima desagradável na relação e nos conduz à loucura, para a felicidade feminina[^2].

For these reasons, it is a total waste of time to argue or debate when we catch them in crooked tricks[^1]. This habit, which I see in many men, only creates an unpleasant atmosphere in the relationship and drives us mad, to feminine delight[^2].

### 09-0006 · footnote 1

> Quanto mais o homem tentar violentar o livre-arbítrio da companheira, na vã tentativa de forçá-la a alterar o comportamento ou a admitir seus erros, mais força estará lhe dando. Abrirá espaço para ser acusado de coerção, violência emocional, ditatoriedade etc. Perderá a razão e será visto como um monstro por si mesmo, pela espertinha e por todas as pessoas que estiverem presenciando o conflito. Como escreveu Esther Vilar (1972): “a mulher tem o poder que o homem lhe dá.” (trad. minha). Por este e outros motivos é que conferir total liberdade à mulher é muito mais conveniente do ponto de vista da defesa emocional. O mais indicado é que o homem a deixe absolutamente livre para fazer o que queira, sem jamais proibir nada, mas devolvendo-lhe todas as conseqüências e responsabilidades que lhe cabem. Isso exige desapaixonamento e adaptabilidade totais ou, pelo menos, em níveis mais altos do que aqueles que correspondem ao homem comum, violento, passional e descontrolado.

The more a man tries to do violence to his companion’s free will, in the vain attempt to force her to change her behavior or admit her mistakes, the more strength he will be giving her. He will open himself to being accused of coercion, emotional violence, dictatorial behavior, etc. He will lose the moral high ground and be seen as a monster by himself, by the sly one, and by everyone who witnesses the conflict. As Esther Vilar (1972) wrote: “woman has the power that man gives her.” (my trans.). For this and other reasons, granting the woman total freedom is much more advisable from the standpoint of emotional defense. The most advisable course is for the man to leave her absolutely free to do whatever she wants, never forbidding anything, but handing back to her all the consequences and responsibilities that are hers. This demands total freedom from passion and total adaptability or, at least, at levels higher than those that correspond to the ordinary man, violent, passionate, and uncontrolled.

### 09-0007 · footnote 2

### 09-0008 · paragraph

> Ao invés de polemizar, é melhor tomarmos uma atitude radical e inesperada que a encurrale e deixe desconcertada a nosso respeito. Uma atitude muito desconcertante que funciona bem é simplesmente aceitar a opinião contrária e indesejável. A aceitação de certas opiniões absurdas a respeito de fidelidade, entretanto, é muito difícil em certos casos por exigir desapaixonamento total. A experiência me mostrou que quando incentivamos seriamente à mulher que está flertando com outro cara a ficar com ele, a mesma fica desesperada se estiver apenas tentando nos irritar. Esta é uma boa forma de vingança porque, na maioria das vezes, o outro não a quer seriamente, deixando-a no final sozinha, sem ninguém e poderemos rir. Por outro lado, se o cara a quiser de verdade, a aceitar e ela também, isto apenas significará que você já deveria tê-la tratado como uma “vadia[^3]” desde o início e que, caso a tenha considerado sua namorada, o erro foi somente seu por não ter percebido que tipo de pessoa tinha ao lado.

Instead of debating, it is better to take a radical and unexpected attitude that corners her and leaves her disconcerted about us. A very disconcerting attitude that works well is simply to accept the contrary and undesirable opinion. Accepting certain absurd opinions about fidelity, however, is very difficult in certain cases because it demands total freedom from passion. Experience has shown me that when we seriously encourage a woman who is flirting with another guy to go off with him, she becomes desperate if she was only trying to irritate us. This is a good form of revenge because, most of the time, the other one does not want her seriously, leaving her alone in the end, with no one, and we will be able to laugh. On the other hand, if the guy really wants her and accepts her, and she wants him too, this will only mean that you should have treated her as a “tramp[^3]” from the start, and that, if you considered her your girlfriend, the mistake was yours alone for not having perceived what kind of person you had at your side.

They will be caught on their own hook and will unknowingly drink the poison they distilled. Since this is counter-manipulation (the dismantling of manipulative wiles) and not manipulation, its effectiveness is borne out only in cases of legitimate emotional defense, in which reason is on our side. In other cases it is ineffective, since woman is more skilled at manipulating feelings than man. Trying to outdo woman in the manipulative wiles of love is almost the same as demanding that they outdo us in physical strength, that is, an absurdity.

### 09-0012 · paragraph

Para que o desmascaramento atinja o sentimento e surta o efeito desejado, as palavras utilizadas devem ser de facílimo entendimento, adequadas à pouca inteligência racional[^5], e ao mesmo tempo absolutamente exatas, para promover o encurralamento certeiro. However, so as not to be predictable, it is advisable now and then to go to the opposite extreme, implacably unmasking their disguises (without arguing, but only making confident, clear, direct, and conclusive observations) without the slightest fear of losing her and without wavering. For the unmasking to reach the feelings and have the desired effect, the words used must be extremely easy to understand, suited to little rational intelligence[^5], and at the same time absolutely exact, in order to bring about an unerring cornering. Be prepared, because in these cases feminine reactions tend to be violent, and you will need to be forewarned in order to hold things together with a female in a fit of madness for having been unmasked by force and suddenly feeling naked. But this will soon pass if you are the stronger and colder of the two and remain centered. Do not fear clamor, shouting, or crying. Do not be affected by storms of words. Stand firm and resolute in your position. The flow of energy you set off will soon be exhausted.

### 09-0014 · paragraph

Do not try to negotiate or make her understand your point of view, because it will be useless, and on top of that you will be considered weak and unsure about your own goals in life. In general, a second chance can be given, provided the lapse was not serious.

### 09-0015 · paragraph

O fazemos pela vã esperança de que possam compreender nossos nobres motivos, esperando que nosso ponto de vista seja considerado. Isto surte o efeito contrário e faz com que sejamos vistos como fracos ao invés de democráticos. Por outro lado, somos vistos como fortes e decididos quando as encurralamos comunicando unilateralmente o que percebemos e as atitudes que tomaremos em conseqüência, recusando-nos a discutir. In matters of behavior that shakes our belief in fidelity, a very common mistake is to insist that our dear ones acknowledge their shady attitudes. We do it in the vain hope that they may understand our noble motives, expecting our point of view to be taken into account. This has the opposite effect and causes us to be seen as weak rather than democratic. On the other hand, we are seen as strong and resolute when we corner them by communicating unilaterally what we perceive and the measures we will take as a consequence, refusing to argue. However, if you bluff, promising a “punishment”[^6] without carrying it out, you are lost. Promise only what you can carry out.

### 09-0017 · paragraph

Regard other people’s mistaken opinions as a problem that is not yours[^7] but belongs to the very person who voices and defends them (and so do the consequences).

### 09-0031 · paragraph

Respeite absolutamente suas opiniões, visões de mundo, concepções etc mesmo quando forem equivocadas, falsas, mal intencionadas, absurdas, egoístas e completamente prejudiciais para a relação. Entretanto, comunique-lhe por via única e amavelmente, sem polemizar, o que você enxerga a respeito das mesmas e as conseqüências que possuem. As realidades emocionais do marido e da esposa são distintas e paralelas (GOLEMAN, 1997), motivo pelo qual é uma perda de tempo tentar forçá-las a compreender o nosso ponto de vista e, menos ainda, tentar obrigá-las a assimilar nossa visão de mundo e nossa idiossincrassia.

To render useless the mental hells of stubbornness and debate, it is enough not to force your partner’s opinions. Respect absolutely her opinions, worldviews, conceptions, etc., even when they are mistaken, false, ill-intentioned, absurd, selfish, and completely harmful to the relationship. However, communicate to her one-way and kindly, without debating, what you see regarding them and the consequences they have. Never try to oblige her to admit her own mistakes or to own up explicitly, verbally, to anything whatsoever. The acknowledgment of mistakes and the acceptance of responsibilities are achieved by letting them be what they are and handing the consequences back to them[^15]. We hand back the responsibility and the consequences simply by not assuming them, by not taking them upon ourselves. Always reinforce that her opinions will be respected. The emotional realities of husband and wife are distinct and parallel (GOLEMAN, 1997), which is why it is a waste of time to try to force them to understand our point of view and, even less, to try to oblige them to assimilate our worldview and our idiosyncrasy.

### 09-0032 · footnote 15

Nada mais podemos fazer a não ser deixá-las fazer tudo o querem de suas vidas (mas não da nossa, obviamente) e esperar que saboreiem as conseqüências boas e más dos caminhos que escolhem. Não nos iludamos: elas não voltarão ao lar e nem sentirão, nunca mais, orgulho em ter os seus serviços domésticos reconhecidos e remunerados pelos maridos. I have observed that some even change their attitude of their own accord, after the effects of their reckless attitudes come back upon themselves. Becoming aware of one’s own mistakes is something very individual, and one cannot oblige one’s neighbor to do it. In truth, this is the central pillar of my theory: absolute adaptive acceptance.
