## Proposed translator's notes

- **01-0021**: Wordplay in the original: "preconceito" (prejudice) is read as "pré-conceito" (pre-concept), opposed to "pós-conceito" (post-concept). English "prejudice" (pre-judgment) keeps part of the play; the hyphenated forms are kept literally.
- **08-0014**: Nelson Rodrigues (1912–1980), Brazilian playwright and columnist known for provocative aphorisms; little known to English-language readers.
- **24-0008**: Saci, caipora, curupira, and iara (spelled “yara” in the original) are mischievous or dangerous beings of Brazilian folklore (a one-legged trickster, forest guardians, a river siren). Kept as names; an English reader may need a note.
- **28-0034**: In Brazil a "motel" is typically a love hotel rented by the hour; the English reader may miss this connotation.
