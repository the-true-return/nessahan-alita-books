Uma dor emocional não é irreal, a prova disso é a insuportável sensação que fere o coração quando perdemos alguém que amamos.

The pain of rejection is a kind of withdrawal syndrome: the sensations caused by the loved one are absent and leave in their place a void that is filled with inner suffering. There are two kinds of suffering: inner and outer. Outer suffering is physical pain. Inner suffering is psychological pain, which is ingeniously turned into an instrument in relationships as a mechanism of domination. An emotional pain is not unreal; the proof of this is the unbearable sensation that wounds the heart when we lose someone we love.
