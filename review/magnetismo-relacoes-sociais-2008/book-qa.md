## Open questions

- **00-0001**: Book title follows the glossary proposal "Magnetism in Social Relations". Alternative: "Magnetism in Social Relationships".
- **00-0002**: "Submissão" rendered as "Subjugation" (glossary proposal). Alternatives: "Submission", "Subjection".
- **00-0005**: The citation now gives the English title; the Portuguese original is "O Magnetismo nas Relações Sociais". Consider adding the English edition's year once published.
- **00-0015**: The table of contents reads "A natureza da paixão humana", while the chapter heading (p. 21) reads "A natureza da paixão". The difference is kept as in the original; decide whether to unify.
- **01-0005**: "elementários" rendered as "elementaries" (occultist term, glossary proposal), and "eus" as "I's" (Gurdjieff). Confirm both.
- **03-0010**: "evoluem e involuem" rendered as "evolve and involve", keeping the author's esoteric pairing (involution as the opposite of evolution). Alternative: "they rise and decline".
- **04-0006**: "recordação de si" rendered as "self-remembering," the established English term from Gurdjieff. Proposed for the glossary.
- **04-0008**: "simpatizar ou antipatizar com o outro" literally means "to feel sympathy or antipathy toward the other"; the context (behaving in a chosen way) suggests the ability to create sympathy or antipathy at will. Rendered as "to win the other's sympathy or antipathy"; alternative: "to be in sympathy or antipathy with the other."
- **04-0015**: "um espelho que refrata os feitiços": a mirror reflects rather than refracts; the author may have meant "reflete." Kept "refracts" to preserve the original; consider "reflects."
- **07-0009**: The author coins "apaixonador" (the one who makes others fall in love; the active element) and uses "apaixonante" for the victim who falls in love (07-0012, 07-0016, 07-0020). Rendered as "enamorer" and "enamored victim/party." Alternatives: "the one who inspires passion" / "the infatuated party." Proposed for the glossary.
- **09-0013**: Lévi's phrases are translated from the author's Portuguese (Edson Bini's translation, 2001). A. E. Waite's public-domain English translation ("Transcendental Magic: Its Doctrine and Ritual," 1896) may contain the matching passage; consider quoting it instead.
- **09-0017**: The original spells "Ganhdi" and "Budha"; standard spellings used.
- **11-0005**: The final clause in the original ("e no consenso coletivo de que as mesmas são sinais...") lacks a verb; "they rely on" was supplied to complete the sentence, which is the most plausible reading.
- **12-0044**: Stray period alone at the end of the chapter in the original (p. 45). Kept here so the segment map stays complete; build_pdf.py skips punctuation-only segments, so it does not appear in the PDF.
- **13-0002**: Dates converted to the American MM/DD/YY order (original: 25/07/00, 31/07/00, 09/11/00). Alternative: spell out ("July 25, July 31, and November 9, 2000"), as done for the other chapter dates.
- **13-0016**: "perish of soul" is in English (italics) in the original and kept as is. Jung's terms are "loss of soul" and "perils of the soul"; the author probably meant one of these. Decide whether to correct.
- **15-0002**: Title translated from the Brazilian edition the author used ("Dogma e Ritual de Alta Magia"). The French original is "Dogme et rituel de la haute magie"; Waite's English translation is "Transcendental Magic: Its Doctrine and Ritual" (1896).
